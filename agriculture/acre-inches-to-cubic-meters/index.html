About Converting Acre-inches to Cubic Meters

Acre-inch and cubic meter are both units of volume. An acre-inch is the volume of water covering one acre to a depth of one inch: 102.790153128 cubic meters. The cubic meter is the SI unit of volume.

Formula

m³ = ac in × 102.7901531

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 acre-inch equals 102.7901531 cubic meters, and the reverse factor is 0.009728558325.

Simple example

1 ac in × 102.7901531 = 102.7901531 m³

1 acre-inch = 102.7901531 cubic meters.

Real-world example

10 ac in × 102.7901531 = 1027.901531 m³

10 acre-inches = 1027.901531 cubic meters.

Understanding the Acre-inch

Convention: Volume of one inch of water over one acre. Over exactly one acre it equals a depth of 25.4 mm.

Standard and definition

The acre-inch is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the acre-inch today

  • Irrigation scheduling and water-application records in the United States
  • Rainfall and runoff volume estimates

Understanding the Cubic meter
