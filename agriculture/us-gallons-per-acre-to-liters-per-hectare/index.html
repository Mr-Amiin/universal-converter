About Converting US Gallons per Acre to Liters per Hectare

US gallon per acre and liter per hectare are both units of liquid application rate. One US gallon per acre is 3.785411784 liters over 4,046.8564224 m², about 9.35 L/ha. One liter per hectare is 0.0001 L/m².

Formula

L/ha = gal/ac × 9.353956229

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 US gallon per acre equals 9.353956229 liters per hectare, and the reverse factor is 0.1069066367.
