Frequently asked questions

How do I convert gallons per acre to ounces per square kilometer?

Multiply the gallons per acre value by 32.99510961 to get ounces per square kilometer. For example, 1 gal/ac = 32.99510961 oz/km2.

What is the formula for gallons per acre to ounces per square kilometer?

Use the formula: value in oz/km2 = value in gal/ac × 32.99510961. To convert back, value in gal/ac = value in oz/km2 ÷ 32.99510961.

Is the gallons per acre to ounces per square kilometer conversion exact?

The numeric factor shown (32.99510961) is calculated from each unit's defined scale, but Gallons per acre and Ounces per square kilometer belong to different agriculture measurement families (liquid application rate vs mass application rate). A direct physical conversion between the two requires them to share the same measurement family; use this figure as a scale reference rather than a like-for-like agronomic equivalence.

Where is the gallons per acre unit commonly used?

It is commonly used in agriculture and agronomy for liquid application rate calculations such as field records, fertilizer and seed planning, or irrigation and yield tracking.

What is gallons per acre used for in farming?

It's most often seen in liquid fertilizer and pesticide application, for example sprayer tank calibration. Converting it to ounces per square kilometer makes it possible to compare that figure against records, labels, or equipment calibrated in a different unit system.

Why does this conversion involve acre?

Acre is the US customary land-area unit most familiar to American and Canadian growers, so it shows up whenever a rate needs to be reported per unit of land area rather than as a total quantity.
