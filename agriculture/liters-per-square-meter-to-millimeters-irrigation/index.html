About Converting Liters per Square Meter to Millimeters (irrigation)

Liter per square meter and millimeter (irrigation) are both units of liquid application rate. One liter per square meter equals 1 millimeter of water depth. A millimeter of irrigation water is a depth of 1 mm, equal to 1 liter per square meter.

Formula

mm (irrig.) = L/m² × 1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 liter per square meter equals 1 millimeters (irrigation), and the reverse factor is 1.

Simple example

1 L/m² × 1 = 1 mm (irrig.)

1 liter per square meter = 1 millimeters (irrigation).

Real-world example

10 L/m² × 1 = 10 mm (irrig.)

10 liters per square meter = 10 millimeters (irrigation).
