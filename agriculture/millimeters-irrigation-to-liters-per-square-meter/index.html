About Converting Millimeters (irrigation) to Liters per Square Meter

Millimeter (irrigation) and liter per square meter are both units of liquid application rate. A millimeter of irrigation water is a depth of 1 mm, equal to 1 liter per square meter. One liter per square meter equals 1 millimeter of water depth.

Formula

L/m² = mm (irrig.) × 1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 millimeter (irrigation) equals 1 liters per square meter, and the reverse factor is 1.

Simple example

1 mm (irrig.) × 1 = 1 L/m²

1 millimeter (irrigation) = 1 liters per square meter.

Real-world example
