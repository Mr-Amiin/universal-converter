About Converting Quintals per Hectare to Kilograms per Hectare

Quintal per hectare and kilogram per hectare are both units of mass application rate. One quintal per hectare is 100 kilograms per hectare (10⁻² kg/m²). One kilogram per hectare is 0.0001 kg/m².

Formula

kg/ha = q/ha × 100

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 quintal per hectare equals 100 kilograms per hectare, and the reverse factor is 0.01.

Simple example

1 q/ha × 100 = 100 kg/ha

1 quintal per hectare = 100 kilograms per hectare.

Real-world example

10 q/ha × 100 = 1000 kg/ha

10 quintals per hectare = 1000 kilograms per hectare.

Understanding the Quintal per hectare

Quintal per hectare (q/ha) measures mass application rate. One quintal per hectare is 100 kilograms per hectare (10⁻² kg/m²).

Convention: metric quintal of exactly 100 kg.

Standard and definition

The quintal per hectare is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the quintal per hectare today

  • Crop-yield statistics in India, France, Spain, Russia and parts of Africa
  • Fertilizer and seed application rates
