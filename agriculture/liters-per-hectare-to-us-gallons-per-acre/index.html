About Converting Liters per Hectare to US Gallons per Acre

Liter per hectare and US gallon per acre are both units of liquid application rate. One liter per hectare is 0.0001 L/m². One US gallon per acre is 3.785411784 liters over 4,046.8564224 m², about 9.35 L/ha.
