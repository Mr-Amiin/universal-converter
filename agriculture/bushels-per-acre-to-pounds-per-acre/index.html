About Converting Bushels per Acre to Pounds per Acre

Bushel per acre and pound per acre are both units of mass application rate. A bushel per acre is a yield rate. At the wheat and soybean standard of 60 pounds per bushel, 1 bu/ac = 27.2155422 kg ÷ 4,046.8564224 m² ≈ 67.25 kg/ha. One pound per acre is 0.45359237 kg over 4,046.8564224 m², about 1.1209 kg/ha.

Formula

lb/ac = bu/ac × 60

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 bushel per acre equals 60 pounds per acre, and the reverse factor is 0.01666666667.

Simple example

1 bu/ac × 60 = 60 lb/ac

1 bushel per acre = 60 pounds per acre.

Real-world example

10 bu/ac × 60 = 600 lb/ac

10 bushels per acre = 600 pounds per acre.

Convention used on this page

Understanding the Bushel per acre

Bushel per acre (bu/ac) measures mass application rate. A bushel per acre is a yield rate. At the wheat and soybean standard of 60 pounds per bushel, 1 bu/ac = 27.2155422 kg ÷ 4,046.8564224 m² ≈ 67.25 kg/ha.

Convention: 60 lb per bushel (wheat and soybeans), exactly 27.2155422 kg. Shelled corn is 56 lb per bushel, barley 48 lb and oats 32 lb, so those crops give different mass yields for the same bushels per acre.

Standard and definition

The bushel per acre is covered by USDA Agricultural Handbook No. 697 — Weights, Measures, and Conversion Factors for Agricultural Commodities and Their Products.

Uses of the bushel per acre today

  • US grain and oilseed yield statistics (USDA)
  • Farm-planning and yield-comparison tools
