About Converting Seeds per Acre to Seeds per Hectare

Seed per acre and seed per hectare are both units of seed rate. One seed per acre is a planting density of one seed on each acre (4,046.8564224 m²). One seed per hectare is a planting density of one seed on each 10,000 m².

Formula

seeds/ha = seeds/ac × 2.471053815

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 seed per acre equals 2.471053815 seeds per hectare, and the reverse factor is 0.4046856422.

Simple example

1 seeds/ac × 2.471053815 = 2.471053815 seeds/ha

1 seed per acre = 2.471053815 seeds per hectare.

Real-world example

10 seeds/ac × 2.471053815 = 24.71053815 seeds/ha

10 seeds per acre = 24.71053815 seeds per hectare.

Understanding the Seed per acre

Seed per acre (seeds/ac) measures seed rate. One seed per acre is a planting density of one seed on each acre (4,046.8564224 m²).

Standard and definition

The seed per acre is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the seed per acre today

  • US seeding rates and plant populations (for example corn at 32,000 seeds per acre)
  • Seed-planning calculations

Understanding the Seed per hectare

Seed per hectare (seeds/ha) measures seed rate. One seed per hectare is a planting density of one seed on each 10,000 m².

Standard and definition

The seed per hectare is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the seed per hectare today

  • Metric seeding rates and plant populations
  • Agronomy and forestry planting
