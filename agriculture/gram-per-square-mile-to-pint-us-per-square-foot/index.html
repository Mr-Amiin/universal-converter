Is the grams per square mile to pints per square foot conversion exact?

A direct physical conversion between the two requires them to share the same measurement family; use this figure as a scale reference rather than a like-for-like agronomic equivalence.

Where is the grams per square mile unit commonly used?

What is grams per square mile used for in farming?

Why does this conversion involve square mile?
