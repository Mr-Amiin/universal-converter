About Converting Metric Tons to Bushels (Wheat/Soy std)

Metric ton and bushel (wheat/soy) are both units of mass. A metric ton is 1,000 kilograms. A bushel of wheat or soybeans is the standard 60 pounds, exactly 27.2155422 kilograms.

Formula

bu = t × 36.74371036

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 metric ton equals 36.74371036 bushels (wheat/soy), and the reverse factor is 0.0272155422.

Understanding the Bushel (wheat/soy)

Bushel (wheat/soy) (bu) measures mass. A bushel of wheat or soybeans is the standard 60 pounds, exactly 27.2155422 kilograms.

Convention: 60 lb per bushel (wheat and soybeans). Shelled corn is 56 lb (25.4012 kg) per bushel, barley 48 lb and oats 32 lb, so bushel-to-tonne factors differ by commodity.

Standard and definition

The bushel (wheat/soy) is covered by USDA Agricultural Handbook No. 697 — Weights, Measures, and Conversion Factors for Agricultural Commodities and Their Products.

Uses of the bushel (wheat/soy) today

  • US grain-market quotes (CBOT wheat and soybeans)
  • Converting bushel quotes to metric tons
