About Converting Metric Tons to Bushels (Corn 56 lb vs. Wheat/Soy 60 lb)

A metric ton is 1,000 kilograms. A bushel is a unit of volume that grain is traded by weight: shelled corn is 56 lb (25.40117272 kg) per bushel, wheat and soybeans 60 lb (27.2155422 kg). Other crops differ again (for example barley 48 lb, oats 32 lb).

Which bushel? Corn is 56 lb, wheat and soybeans are 60 lb

A bushel is a volume, but grain is traded by weight and the standard weight depends on the crop. This converter has a crop-standard selector above the result: shelled corn uses 56 lb per bushel (25.40117272 kg) and wheat and soybeans use 60 lb per bushel (27.2155422 kg). The page opens on wheat and soybeans (60 lb). Switch the selector to recalculate instantly in either direction.

Formula (both standards)

bushels = metric tons × 1,000 ÷ (lb per bushel × 0.45359237)

Shelled corn, 56 lb/bu: bu = t × 39.3682611

Wheat and soybeans, 60 lb/bu: bu = t × 36.74371036

Convention used on this page

Mass per bushel is the standard test weight in pounds × 0.45359237 kg per international pound: corn 56 lb = 25.40117272 kg, wheat and soybeans 60 lb = 27.2155422 kg. Yields use the international acre (0.40468564224 ha). A metric ton is 1,000 kg. Both factors are exact for these definitions; which one applies depends on the crop.

Frequently asked questions

How many bushels are in 1 metric ton?

It depends on the crop. For shelled corn (56 lb per bushel) 1 metric ton = 39.3682611 bushels. For wheat and soybeans (60 lb per bushel) 1 metric ton = 36.74371036 bushels.

How do I convert metric tons to bushels?

Pick the crop standard, then use bushels = metric tons × 1,000 ÷ (lb per bushel × 0.45359237). That is a multiplier of 39.3682611 for corn (56 lb/bu) or 36.74371036 for wheat and soybeans (60 lb/bu).

How do I convert bushels back to metric tons?

Use metric tons = bushels × (lb per bushel × 0.45359237) ÷ 1,000: 1 bushel = 0.02540117272 metric tons for corn (56 lb/bu) or 0.0272155422 metric tons for wheat and soybeans (60 lb/bu). The swap button reverses the direction and keeps the selected crop standard.

Why does a bushel weigh different amounts for different crops?

A bushel is a unit of volume. Grain is sold by weight using standard test weights per bushel, and each crop packs differently: shelled corn is 56 lb (25.40117272 kg), while wheat and soybeans are 60 lb (27.2155422 kg).

Which bushel standard should I choose?

Choose the crop you are converting: 56 lb per bushel for shelled corn, 60 lb per bushel for wheat or soybeans. The page defaults to wheat and soybeans. Other crops such as barley (48 lb) or oats (32 lb) use different standards that this selector does not cover.

Is the conversion exact?

Yes for each standard: the factors follow exactly from 56 or 60 pounds per bushel, the international pound (0.45359237 kg) and the international acre (0.40468564224 ha). Which standard is right depends on the crop, not on rounding.

Does swapping units keep my crop standard?

Yes. The selector is independent of the From and To units, so the chosen standard stays active when you swap the units.
