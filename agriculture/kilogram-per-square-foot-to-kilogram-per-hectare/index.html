Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kilograms per square foot to kilograms per hectare?

Multiply the kilograms per square foot value by 107639.1042 to get kilograms per hectare. For example, 1 kg/ft2 = 107639.1042 kg/ha.

What is the formula for kilograms per square foot to kilograms per hectare?

Use the formula: value in kg/ha = value in kg/ft2 × 107639.1042. To convert back, value in kg/ft2 = value in kg/ha ÷ 107639.1042.

Is the kilograms per square foot to kilograms per hectare conversion exact?

Yes. Both units belong to the same mass application rate family, so the factor 107639.1042 gives an exact, direct conversion.

Where is the kilograms per square foot unit commonly used?

It is commonly used in agriculture and agronomy for mass application rate calculations such as field records, fertilizer and seed planning, or irrigation and yield tracking.

What is kilograms per square foot used for in farming?

It's most often seen in fertilizer and seed application rates, for example granular fertilizer spreader settings. Converting it to kilograms per hectare makes it possible to compare that figure against records, labels, or equipment calibrated in a different unit system.

Why does this conversion involve square foot?

Square foot is a small-plot or garden-scale area unit, so it shows up whenever a rate needs to be reported per unit of land area rather than as a total quantity.
