About Converting Millimeters (depth) to Acre-inches

Millimeter (depth) and acre-inch are both units of volume. A millimeter of depth over one acre is 4.0468564224 cubic meters of water. An acre-inch is the volume of water covering one acre to a depth of one inch: 102.790153128 cubic meters.

ac in = mm (depth) × 0.03937007874

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 millimeter (depth) equals 0.03937007874 acre-inches, and the reverse factor is 25.4.
