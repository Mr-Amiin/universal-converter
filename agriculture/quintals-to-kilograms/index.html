About Converting Quintals to Kilograms

Quintal and kilogram are both units of mass. A metric quintal is 100 kilograms. The kilogram is the SI base unit of mass.

Formula

kg = q × 100

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 quintal equals 100 kilograms, and the reverse factor is 0.01.

Simple example

1 q × 100 = 100 kg

1 quintal = 100 kilograms.

Real-world example

10 q × 100 = 1000 kg

10 quintals = 1000 kilograms.
