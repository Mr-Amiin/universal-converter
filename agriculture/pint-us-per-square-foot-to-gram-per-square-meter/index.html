Frequently asked questions

How do I convert pints per square foot to grams per square meter?

Multiply the pints per square foot value by 5.093229167 to get grams per square meter. For example, 1 pt/ft2 = 5.093229167 g/m2.

What is the formula for pints per square foot to grams per square meter?

Use the formula: value in g/m2 = value in pt/ft2 × 5.093229167. To convert back, value in pt/ft2 = value in g/m2 ÷ 5.093229167.

Is the pints per square foot to grams per square meter conversion exact?

The numeric factor shown (5.093229167) is calculated from each unit's defined scale, but Pints per square foot and Grams per square meter belong to different agriculture measurement families (liquid application rate vs mass application rate). A direct physical conversion between the two requires them to share the same measurement family; use this figure as a scale reference rather than a like-for-like agronomic equivalence.

Where is the pints per square foot unit commonly used?

It is commonly used in agriculture and agronomy for liquid application rate calculations such as field records, fertilizer and seed planning, or irrigation and yield tracking.

About this conversion

Pints per square foot and Grams per square meter are units used in the Universal Converter agriculture category. Pint per Square foot (pt/ft2): Liquid application rate. Gram per Square Meter (g/m2): Mass application rate.

Pints per square foot belongs to the liquid application rate family, while Grams per square meter belongs to the mass application rate family.
