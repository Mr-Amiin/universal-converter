Frequently asked questions

How do I convert pounds per hectare to gallons per hectare?

Multiply the pounds per hectare value by 119.8264273 to get gallons per hectare. For example, 1 lb/ha = 119.8264273 gal/ha.

What is the formula for pounds per hectare to gallons per hectare?

Use the formula: value in gal/ha = value in lb/ha × 119.8264273. To convert back, value in lb/ha = value in gal/ha ÷ 119.8264273.

Is the pounds per hectare to gallons per hectare conversion exact?

The numeric factor shown (119.8264273) is calculated from each unit's defined scale, but Pounds per hectare and Gallons per hectare belong to different agriculture measurement families (mass application rate vs liquid application rate). A direct physical conversion between the two requires them to share the same measurement family; use this figure as a scale reference rather than a like-for-like agronomic equivalence.

Where is the pounds per hectare unit commonly used?

It is commonly used in agriculture and agronomy for mass application rate calculations such as field records, fertilizer and seed planning, or irrigation and yield tracking.

What is pounds per hectare used for in farming?

It's most often seen in US customary fertilizer and seed rates, for example seed drills and spreader calibration. Converting it to gallons per hectare makes it possible to compare that figure against records, labels, or equipment calibrated in a different unit system.

Why does this conversion involve hectare?

Hectare is the metric land-area unit used across most of the world outside the US, so it shows up whenever a rate needs to be reported per unit of land area rather than as a total quantity.
