About Converting Pounds per Acre to Bushels per Acre (Corn 56 lb vs. Wheat/Soy 60 lb)

One pound per acre is 0.45359237 kg over one international acre (4,046.8564224 m²), about 1.1209 kg/ha. A bushel per acre is a yield rate. Its mass depends on the crop: shelled corn is 56 lb per bushel (25.40117272 kg) and wheat and soybeans are 60 lb per bushel (27.2155422 kg). Acreage uses the international acre, 0.40468564224 hectares.

Which bushel? Corn is 56 lb, wheat and soybeans are 60 lb

A bushel is a volume, but grain is traded by weight and the standard weight depends on the crop. Convention used on this page

Frequently asked questions

How many bushels per acre are in 1 pound per acre?

It depends on the crop. For shelled corn (56 lb per bushel) 1 pound per acre = 0.01785714286 bushels per acre. For wheat and soybeans (60 lb per bushel) 1 pound per acre = 0.01666666667 bushels per acre.

How do I convert pounds per acre to bushels per acre?

Pick the crop standard, then use bu/ac = lb/ac ÷ lb per bushel. That is a multiplier of 0.01785714286 for corn (56 lb/bu) or 0.01666666667 for wheat and soybeans (60 lb/bu).

How do I convert bushels per acre back to pounds per acre?

Use lb/ac = bu/ac × lb per bushel: 1 bushel per acre = 56 pounds per acre for corn (56 lb/bu) or 60 pounds per acre for wheat and soybeans (60 lb/bu). The swap button reverses the direction and keeps the selected crop standard.

Why does a bushel weigh different amounts for different crops?

A bushel is a unit of volume. Grain is sold by weight using standard test weights per bushel, and each crop packs differently: shelled corn is 56 lb (25.40117272 kg), while wheat and soybeans are 60 lb (27.2155422 kg).

Which bushel standard should I choose?

Choose the crop you are converting: 56 lb per bushel for shelled corn, 60 lb per bushel for wheat or soybeans. The page defaults to wheat and soybeans. Other crops such as barley (48 lb) or oats (32 lb) use different standards that this selector does not cover.

Is the conversion exact?

Yes for each standard: the factors follow exactly from 56 or 60 pounds per bushel, the international pound (0.45359237 kg) and the international acre (0.40468564224 ha). Which standard is right depends on the crop, not on rounding.

Does swapping units keep my crop standard?

Yes. The selector is independent of the From and To units, so the chosen standard stays active when you swap the units.
