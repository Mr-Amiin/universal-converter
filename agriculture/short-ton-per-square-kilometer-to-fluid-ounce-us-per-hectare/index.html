Is the short tons per square kilometer to fluid ounces per hectare conversion exact?

The numeric factor shown (306755.6539) is calculated from each unit's defined scale, but Short tons per square kilometer and Fluid ounces per hectare belong to different agriculture measurement families (mass application rate vs liquid application rate). A direct physical conversion between the two requires them to share the same measurement family; use this figure as a scale reference rather than a like-for-like agronomic equivalence.

Where is the short tons per square kilometer unit commonly used?

It is commonly used in agriculture and agronomy for mass application rate calculations such as field records, fertilizer and seed planning, or irrigation and yield tracking.

What is short tons per square kilometer used for in farming?

Why does this conversion involve square kilometer?
