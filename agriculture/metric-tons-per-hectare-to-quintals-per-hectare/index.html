About Converting Metric Tons per Hectare to Quintals per Hectare

Metric ton per hectare and quintal per hectare are both units of mass application rate. One metric ton per hectare is 1,000 kg over 10,000 m² (0.1 kg/m²). One quintal per hectare is 100 kilograms per hectare (10⁻² kg/m²).

Formula

q/ha = t/ha × 10

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 metric ton per hectare equals 10 quintals per hectare, and the reverse factor is 0.1.

Simple example

1 t/ha × 10 = 10 q/ha

1 metric ton per hectare = 10 quintals per hectare.

Real-world example

10 t/ha × 10 = 100 q/ha

10 metric tons per hectare = 100 quintals per hectare.

Uses of the metric ton per hectare today

Understanding the Quintal per hectare

Quintal per hectare (q/ha) measures mass application rate. One quintal per hectare is 100 kilograms per hectare (10⁻² kg/m²).

Convention: metric quintal of exactly 100 kg.

Standard and definition
