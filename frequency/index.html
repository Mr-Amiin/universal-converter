What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the frequency converter category.

Is the hertz to kilohertz conversion exact?

Yes. The kilo prefix represents exactly 1,000, so 1 kilohertz equals exactly 1,000 hertz with no rounding involved.

Can I convert kilohertz back to hertz?

Yes. Use the reverse converter to multiply kilohertz by 1,000 and get hertz.
