About Converting Radians per Second to Degrees per Second

Radian per second and degree per second are both units of frequency. One radian per second is 1/(2π) hertz. One degree per second is 1/360 of a revolution per second, or 1/360 hertz.

Formula

°/s = rad/s × 57.29577951

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 radian per second equals 57.29577951 degrees per second, and the reverse factor is 0.01745329252.

Simple example

1 rad/s × 57.29577951 = 57.29577951 °/s

1 radian per second = 57.29577951 degrees per second.

Real-world example

10 rad/s × 57.29577951 = 572.9577951 °/s

10 radians per second = 572.9577951 degrees per second.
