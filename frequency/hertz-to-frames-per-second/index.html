About Converting Hertz to Frames per Second

Hertz and frame per second are both units of frequency. The hertz is the SI unit of frequency: one cycle per second. One frame per second is one frame (one cycle) each second, equivalent to one hertz.

Formula

fps = Hz × 1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 hertz equals 1 frames per second, and the reverse factor is 1.

Simple example

1 Hz × 1 = 1 fps

1 hertz = 1 frames per second.

Real-world example

10 Hz × 1 = 10 fps

10 hertzs = 10 frames per second.
