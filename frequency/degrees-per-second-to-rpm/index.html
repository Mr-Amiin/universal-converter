About Converting Degrees per Second to RPM

Degree per second and revolution per minute are both units of frequency. One degree per second is 1/360 of a revolution per second, or 1/360 hertz. One revolution per minute is 1/60 hertz.

Formula

rpm = °/s × 0.1666666667

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 degree per second equals 0.1666666667 revolutions per minute, and the reverse factor is 6.

Simple example

1 °/s × 0.1666666667 = 0.1666666667 rpm

1 degree per second = 0.1666666667 revolutions per minute.

Real-world example

10 °/s × 0.1666666667 = 1.666666667 rpm

10 degrees per second = 1.666666667 revolutions per minute.

Frequently asked questions

How many revolutions per minute are in 1 degree per second?

1 degree per second equals 0.1666666667 revolutions per minute.

How do I convert degree per second to revolution per minute?

Multiply the degree per second value by 0.1666666667. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert revolution per minute back to degree per second?

Use the reverse factor: 1 revolution per minute equals 6 degrees per second. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

Is the degree per second to revolution per minute conversion exact?
