About Converting RPM to Revolutions per Hour

Revolution per minute and revolution per hour are both units of frequency. One revolution per minute is 1/60 hertz. One revolution per hour is 1/3,600 hertz.

Formula

rev/h = rpm × 60

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 revolution per minute equals 60 revolutions per hour, and the reverse factor is 0.01666666667.

Simple example

1 rpm × 60 = 60 rev/h

1 revolution per minute = 60 revolutions per hour.

Real-world example

10 rpm × 60 = 600 rev/h

10 revolutions per minute = 600 revolutions per hour.
