Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the hectohertz (hHz).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the revolutions per minute (rpm).

How do I convert hectohertz to revolutions per minute?

Multiply the hectohertz value by 6000. For example, 5 hHz × 6000 = 30000 rpm.

Why do hectohertz and revolutions per minute need converting at all?

Hecto (h) represents 10^2 and is rarely used outside the hectare and hectopascal. RPM (revolutions per minute) is the standard unit for engine, motor, and turbine rotational speed.

Is the hectohertz to revolutions per minute conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between hectohertz and revolutions per minute is a fixed, defined ratio (× 6000), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert revolutions per minute back to hectohertz?

Yes. Multiply the revolutions per minute value by 0.0001666667 (or divide by 6000) to get hectohertz.

About this conversion

This page converts hectohertz (hHz) into revolutions per minute (rpm) within the frequency converter, using the fixed relationship 1 hHz = 6000 rpm.

Worked example: to convert 10 hHz to rpm, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 6000 = 60000 rpm.

A signal at 60 hHz corresponds to about 360000 rpm.

Reverse conversion: to go from revolutions per minute back to hectohertz, divide by 6000 (equivalently, multiply by 0.0001666667).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
