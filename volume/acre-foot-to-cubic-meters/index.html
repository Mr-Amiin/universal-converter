About Converting Acre-feet to Cubic Meters

Acre-foot and cubic meter are both units of volume. An acre-foot is the volume of one acre covered to a depth of one international foot: 43,560 cubic feet, about 1,233.48 cubic meters. The cubic meter is the SI unit of volume: the volume of a cube with 1-meter edges (1,000 liters).

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 acre-foot equals 1233.481838 cubic meters, and the reverse factor is 0.0008107131938.

Understanding the Acre-foot

Acre-foot (ac ft) measures volume. An acre-foot is the volume of one acre covered to a depth of one international foot: 43,560 cubic feet, about 1,233.48 cubic meters.

Convention: international foot (0.3048 m) and international acre. The older US survey acre-foot differs by about 2 parts per million.

Standard and definition

The acre-foot is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the acre-foot today

  • Reservoir storage and water-rights accounting in the western United States
  • Irrigation district allocations and municipal water supply planning

Understanding the Cubic meter

Cubic meter (m³) measures volume. The cubic meter is the SI unit of volume: the volume of a cube with 1-meter edges (1,000 liters).

Standard and definition

The cubic meter is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the cubic meter today

  • Bulk liquids, gases, concrete and water volumes
  • Engineering and shipping volumes
