Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the decaliters (daL).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the imperial quarts (qt (UK)).

How do I convert decaliters to imperial quarts?

Multiply the decaliters value by 8.79877. For example, 2 daL × 8.79877 = 17.59754 qt (UK).

Why do decaliters and imperial quarts need converting at all?

Deca (da) represents 10^1 and is one of the least commonly used SI prefixes. Imperial Quarts is a standard unit used in this category's conversions.

Is the decaliters to imperial quarts conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between decaliters and imperial quarts is a fixed, defined ratio (× 8.79877), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert imperial quarts back to decaliters?

Yes. Multiply the imperial quarts value by 0.1136522491 (or divide by 8.79877) to get decaliters.

About this conversion

This page converts decaliters (daL) into imperial quarts (qt (UK)) within the volume converter, using the fixed relationship 1 daL = 8.79877 qt (UK).

Worked example: to convert 5 daL to qt (UK), multiply by the conversion factor: 5 × 8.79877 = 43.99385 qt (UK).

A 2-daL bottle holds approximately 17.6 qt (UK).

Reverse conversion: to go from imperial quarts back to decaliters, divide by 8.79877 (equivalently, multiply by 0.1136522491).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
