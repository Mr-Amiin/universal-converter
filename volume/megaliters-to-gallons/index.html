Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the megaliters (ML).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the gallons (gal).

How do I convert megaliters to gallons?

Multiply the megaliters value by 264172. For example, 2 ML × 264172 = 528344 gal.

Why do megaliters and gallons need converting at all?

Mega (M) represents 10^6, one of the most commonly used SI prefixes. The US gallon (231 cubic inches) is used for fuel, milk, and other liquid measures in the United States.

Is the megaliters to gallons conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between megaliters and gallons is a fixed, defined ratio (× 264172), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert gallons back to megaliters?

Yes. Multiply the gallons value by 3.785413e-06 (or divide by 264172) to get megaliters.
