About this conversion

This page converts cubic inches (in³) into teaspoons (tsp) within the volume converter, using the fixed relationship 1 in³ = 3.32468 tsp.

Worked example: to convert 5 in³ to tsp, multiply by the conversion factor: 5 × 3.32468 = 16.6234 tsp.

A 2-in³ bottle holds approximately 6.65 tsp.

Reverse conversion: to go from teaspoons back to cubic inches, divide by 3.32468 (equivalently, multiply by 0.300780827).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
