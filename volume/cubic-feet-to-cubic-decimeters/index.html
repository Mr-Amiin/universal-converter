About this conversion

The Cubic Feet and the Cubic Decimeter are both units of volume. The cubic foot is an imperial volume unit equal to a cube one foot on each side, commonly used for shipping, gas, and construction volumes. The cubic decimeter is a metric volume unit equal to a cube one decimeter on each edge, used in everyday and industrial metric measurements. To convert, multiply the Cubic Feet value by 28.316846592 (formula: dm³ = ft³ × 28.316846592). It is commonly used for fuel, beverages, cooking, water storage, agriculture, laboratories, and everyday industrial liquid measurements.
