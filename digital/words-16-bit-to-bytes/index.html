About Converting Words (16-bit) to Bytes

This page converts 16-bit words (word) to bytes (B). A 16-bit word is 16 bits, or 2 bytes. A byte is 8 bits.

Formula

B = word × 2

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 16-bit word equals 2 bytes, and the reverse factor is 0.5.

Simple example

1 word × 2 = 2 B

1 16-bit word = 2 bytes.

Real-world example

10 word × 2 = 20 B

10 16-bit words = 20 bytes.

Understanding the 16-bit word

16-bit word (word) measures digital information. A 16-bit word is 16 bits, or 2 bytes.

Standard and definition

The 16-bit word is covered by IEC 80000-13 — Information science and technology: quantities and units.

Uses of the 16-bit word today

  • x86 and Windows programming data types
  • Embedded and legacy computing

Understanding the Byte

Byte (B) measures storage. A byte is 8 bits.

Standard and definition

The byte is covered by IEC 80000-13 — Information science and technology: quantities and units.

Uses of the byte today

  • File sizes and memory
  • Data-transfer and storage specifications

Frequently asked questions

How many bytes are in 1 16-bit word?

1 16-bit word equals 2 bytes.

How do I convert 16-bit word to byte?

Multiply the 16-bit word value by 2. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert byte back to 16-bit word?

Use the reverse factor: 1 byte equals 0.5 16-bit words. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a 16-bit word?

16-bit word (word) measures digital information. A 16-bit word is 16 bits, or 2 bytes.

What is a byte?

Byte (B) measures storage. A byte is 8 bits.

Is the 16-bit word to byte conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are fixed by definition, so the factor is exact and any rounding you see is a display choice.
