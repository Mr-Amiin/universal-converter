About Converting Quad Words (64-bit) to Bytes

This page converts 64-bit quad words (qword) to bytes (B). A quad word (QWORD) is 64 bits, or 8 bytes. A byte is 8 bits.

Formula

B = qword × 8

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 64-bit quad word equals 8 bytes, and the reverse factor is 0.125.

Simple example

1 qword × 8 = 8 B

1 64-bit quad word = 8 bytes.

Real-world example

10 qword × 8 = 80 B

10 64-bit quad words = 80 bytes.

Understanding the 64-bit quad word

64-bit quad word (qword) measures digital information. A quad word (QWORD) is 64 bits, or 8 bytes.

Convention: the x86/Windows meaning of a word as 16 bits, so a quad word is 64 bits. Word size differs on other architectures.

Standard and definition

The 64-bit quad word is covered by IEC 80000-13 — Information science and technology: quantities and units.

Uses of the 64-bit quad word today

  • 64-bit integer and pointer sizes
  • Windows and x86-64 programming data types

Understanding the Byte

Byte (B) measures storage. A byte is 8 bits.

Standard and definition

The byte is covered by IEC 80000-13 — Information science and technology: quantities and units.

Uses of the byte today

  • File sizes and memory
  • Data-transfer and storage specifications
