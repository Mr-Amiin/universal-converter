About Converting Kilobytes to Bytes

Kilobyte and byte are both units of storage. A kilobyte is 1,000 bytes (decimal, SI prefix). A byte is 8 bits.

Formula

B = kB × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 kilobyte equals 1000 bytes, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 kB × 1000 = 1000 B

1 kilobyte = 1000 bytes.

Real-world example

10 kB × 1000 = 10000 B

10 kilobytes = 10000 bytes.

Understanding the Kilobyte

Kilobyte (kB) measures storage. A kilobyte is 1,000 bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

Standard and definition

The kilobyte is covered by IEC 80000-13 — Information science and technology: quantities and units.

Uses of the kilobyte today

  • Small file and message sizes
  • Network and storage specifications

Frequently asked questions

How many bytes are in 1 kilobyte?

1 kilobyte equals 1000 bytes.

How do I convert kilobyte to byte?

Multiply the kilobyte value by 1000. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert byte back to kilobyte?

Use the reverse factor: 1 byte equals 0.001 kilobytes. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a kilobyte?

Kilobyte (kB) measures storage. A kilobyte is 1,000 bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

What is a byte?

Byte (B) measures storage. A byte is 8 bits.

Is the kilobyte to byte conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are fixed by definition, so the factor is exact and any rounding you see is a display choice.
