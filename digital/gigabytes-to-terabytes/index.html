About Converting Gigabytes to Terabytes

Gigabyte and terabyte are both units of storage. A gigabyte is 10⁹ bytes (decimal, SI prefix). A terabyte is 10¹² bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

Formula

TB = GB × 0.001

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 gigabyte equals 0.001 terabytes, and the reverse factor is 1000.

Simple example

1 GB × 0.001 = 0.001 TB

1 gigabyte = 0.001 terabytes.

Real-world example

10 GB × 0.001 = 0.01 TB

10 gigabytes = 0.01 terabytes.
