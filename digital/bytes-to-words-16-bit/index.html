About Converting Bytes to Words (16-bit)

This page converts bytes (B) to 16-bit words (word). A byte is 8 bits. A 16-bit word is 16 bits, or 2 bytes.

Formula

word = B × 0.5

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 byte equals 0.5 16-bit words, and the reverse factor is 2.

Simple example

1 B × 0.5 = 0.5 word

1 byte = 0.5 16-bit words.

Real-world example

10 B × 0.5 = 5 word

10 bytes = 5 16-bit words.

Understanding the 16-bit word

16-bit word (word) measures digital information. A 16-bit word is 16 bits, or 2 bytes.

Standard and definition

The 16-bit word is covered by IEC 80000-13 — Information science and technology: quantities and units.

Uses of the 16-bit word today

  • x86 and Windows programming data types
  • Embedded and legacy computing

Frequently asked questions

How many 16-bit words are in 1 byte?

1 byte equals 0.5 16-bit words.

How do I convert byte to 16-bit word?

Multiply the byte value by 0.5. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert 16-bit word back to byte?

Use the reverse factor: 1 16-bit word equals 2 bytes. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a byte?

Byte (B) measures storage. A byte is 8 bits.

What is a 16-bit word?

16-bit word (word) measures digital information. A 16-bit word is 16 bits, or 2 bytes.

Is the byte to 16-bit word conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are fixed by definition, so the factor is exact and any rounding you see is a display choice.
