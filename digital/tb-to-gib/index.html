Frequently asked questions

How do I convert terabytes to gibibytes?

Multiply the terabyte value by 931.322574615478515625. For example, 4 TB × 931.322574615478515625 = 3,725.2902984619140625 GiB.

How do I convert gibibytes back to terabytes?

Divide the gibibyte value by 931.322574615478515625, which is the same as multiplying by 0.001073741824.

Is the terabyte to gibibyte conversion exact?

Yes. Both units use fixed, defined prefixes (decimal and/or binary), so the conversion factor is exact rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation, though remember decimal and binary prefixes diverge further apart as the scale grows.

Where is this conversion used in practice?

It comes up when comparing external hard drives, small server storage, surveillance camera archives.

When would I use gibibytes instead of terabytes?

Use whichever unit keeps the number easiest to read for the context: an external hard drive, or a small business's shared file server is usually described in terabytes, while a two-hour HD movie download, or a phone's entire photo library reads more naturally in gibibytes.

About this conversion

This page converts terabytes (TB), a decimal (SI) unit of digital storage equal to 1012 bytes, into gibibytes (GiB), a binary (IEC) unit of digital storage equal to 230 bytes.

This conversion is used when comparing external hard drives and HD video files.

The formula is GiB = TB × 931.322574615478515625, based on the fixed relationship between these two digital storage units.

The “tera” prefix was formalized alongside giga at the 11th CGPM, standardized in 1960. The “gibi” binary prefix was introduced by the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC 60027-2) to remove the ambiguity of reusing decimal SI prefixes for binary, power-of-two computer quantities, standardized in 1998.

Digital storage prefixes follow the International System of Units (SI) for decimal units and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) recommendations for binary units, keeping decimal and binary quantities unambiguous.
