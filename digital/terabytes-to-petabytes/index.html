About Converting Terabytes to Petabytes

Terabyte and petabyte are both units of storage. A terabyte is 10¹² bytes (decimal, SI prefix). A petabyte is 10¹⁵ bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

Formula

PB = TB × 0.001

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 terabyte equals 0.001 petabytes, and the reverse factor is 1000.

Simple example

1 TB × 0.001 = 0.001 PB

1 terabyte = 0.001 petabytes.

Real-world example

10 TB × 0.001 = 0.01 PB

10 terabytes = 0.01 petabytes.

Understanding the Terabyte

Standard and definition

The terabyte is covered by IEC 80000-13 — Information science and technology: quantities and units.

Uses of the terabyte today

  • Hard-drive and SSD capacities
  • Cloud storage plans
