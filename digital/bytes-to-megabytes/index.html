About Converting Bytes to Megabytes

Byte and megabyte are both units of storage. A byte is 8 bits. A megabyte is 10⁶ bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

Formula

MB = B × 0.000001

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 byte equals 0.000001 megabytes, and the reverse factor is 1000000.

Simple example

1 B × 0.000001 = 0.000001 MB

1 byte = 0.000001 megabytes.

Real-world example

10 B × 0.000001 = 0.00001 MB

10 bytes = 0.00001 megabytes.

Frequently asked questions

How many megabytes are in 1 byte?

1 byte equals 0.000001 megabytes.

How do I convert byte to megabyte?

Multiply the byte value by 0.000001. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert megabyte back to byte?

Use the reverse factor: 1 megabyte equals 1000000 bytes. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a byte?

Byte (B) measures storage. A byte is 8 bits.

What is a megabyte?

Megabyte (MB) measures storage. A megabyte is 10⁶ bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

Is the byte to megabyte conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are fixed by definition, so the factor is exact and any rounding you see is a display choice.
