About this conversion

This page converts zettabytes (ZB), a decimal (SI) unit of digital storage equal to 1021 bytes, into tebibytes (TiB), a binary (IEC) unit of digital storage equal to 240 bytes.

This conversion is used when comparing global annual data creation estimates and external hard drives.

The formula is TiB = ZB × 909,494,701.7729282379150390625, based on the fixed relationship between these two digital storage units.

The “zetta” prefix was added at the 19th CGPM to describe emerging global data volumes, standardized in 1991. The “tebi” binary prefix was introduced by the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC 60027-2) to remove the ambiguity of reusing decimal SI prefixes for binary, power-of-two computer quantities, standardized in 1998.

Digital storage prefixes follow the International System of Units (SI) for decimal units and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) recommendations for binary units, keeping decimal and binary quantities unambiguous.
