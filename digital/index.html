What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the digital storage converter category.

Is the gigabyte to megabyte conversion decimal or binary?

This category uses decimal (SI) storage definitions, where 1 gigabyte equals 1,000 megabytes. Binary variants (GiB, MiB) are also available in the full converter.

Can I convert megabytes back to gigabytes?

Yes. Use the reverse converter to divide megabytes by 1,000 to get gigabytes.
