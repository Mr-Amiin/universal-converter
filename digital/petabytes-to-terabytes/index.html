About Converting Petabytes to Terabytes

Petabyte and terabyte are both units of storage. A petabyte is 10¹⁵ bytes (decimal, SI prefix). A terabyte is 10¹² bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

Formula

TB = PB × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 petabyte equals 1000 terabytes, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 PB × 1000 = 1000 TB

1 petabyte = 1000 terabytes.

Real-world example

10 PB × 1000 = 10000 TB

10 petabytes = 10000 terabytes.

  • Large-scale analytics
