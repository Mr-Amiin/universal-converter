About Converting Exabytes to Petabytes

Exabyte and petabyte are both units of storage. An exabyte is 10¹⁸ bytes (decimal, SI prefix). A petabyte is 10¹⁵ bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

Formula

PB = EB × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 exabyte equals 1000 petabytes, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 EB × 1000 = 1000 PB

1 exabyte = 1000 petabytes.

Real-world example

10 EB × 1000 = 10000 PB

10 exabytes = 10000 petabytes.

Understanding the Exabyte

Exabyte (EB) measures storage. An exabyte is 10¹⁸ bytes (decimal, SI prefix).

Standard and definition

The exabyte is covered by IEC 80000-13 — Information science and technology: quantities and units.

Uses of the exabyte today

  • Global data-center and network traffic totals
  • Large archive sizes
