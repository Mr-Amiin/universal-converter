Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the gibibyte (GiB), a binary (IEC) unit of digital storage equal to 1,024 mebibytes, or 2^30 bytes.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the kibibyte (KiB), a binary (IEC) unit of digital storage equal to 1,024 bytes, or 2^10 bytes.

How do I convert gibibytes to kibibytes?

Multiply the gibibyte value by 1,048,576 to get kibibytes. For example, 4 GiB × 1,048,576 = 4,194,304 KiB.

Is the gibibyte to kibibyte conversion exact?

It's exact under the binary (IEC) definitions used here, where each binary storage prefix is a fixed factor of 1,024 from the next.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

It's used for describing file sizes, storage drive capacity, RAM, network bandwidth, and internet data plans or downloads.

Can I convert kibibytes back to gibibytes?

Yes. Divide the kibibyte value by 1,048,576 to get gibibytes.

About this conversion

This page converts gibibytes (GiB), a binary (IEC) unit of digital storage equal to 1,024 mebibytes, or 2^30 bytes, into kibibytes (KiB), a binary (IEC) unit of digital storage equal to 1,024 bytes, or 2^10 bytes.

This conversion is used constantly when checking file sizes, storage drive and memory capacity, network bandwidth, and mobile or broadband data plan allowances.

The formula is KiB = GiB × 1,048,576, based on the fixed relationship between these two digital storage units.

It's exact under the binary (IEC) definitions used here, where each binary storage prefix is a fixed factor of 1,024 from the next.

Digital storage prefixes follow the International System of Units (SI) for decimal units and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) recommendations for binary units, keeping decimal and binary quantities unambiguous.
