Frequently asked questions

How do you convert us gallon to liter?

Use the formula L = gal × 3.785411784. One us gallon equals 3.785411784 liters.

What is US gallon?

The US gallon is a customary unit of liquid volume.

What is Liter?

The liter is a metric unit of volume used for liquids and gases.

Is the us gallon to liter conversion exact?

Yes. US gallon and Liter are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

How do I convert liter back to us gallon?

Use the reverse formula gal = L ÷ 3.785411784. This site's reverse converter does the same calculation automatically.

About this conversion

This volume converter turns us gallon into liter using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. A 15-gallon fuel tank holds about 56.78 liters. The US gallon derives from an old English wine gallon measure, distinct from the larger imperial gallon used in the UK. The liter was introduced with the metric system in 1795 and is defined as exactly one cubic decimeter. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same us gallon-to-liter factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
