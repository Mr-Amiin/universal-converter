How the formula works

Both millisiemens and megasiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in millisiemens by 1.000000e-09 to get megasiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a megasiemens value by 1.000000e+09 to get back to millisiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert millisiemens to megasiemens?

Multiply the millisiemens value by 1 × 10-9. For example, 4 mS × 1 × 10-9 = 4 × 10-9 MS.

How do I convert megasiemens back to millisiemens?

Divide the megasiemens value by 1 × 10-9, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000,000,000.

Is the millisiemens to megasiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a millisiemens used for?

The millisiemens (mS) is commonly encountered in the conductance of resistors in the kΩ-MΩ range.

About this conversion

This page converts millisiemens (mS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-3 siemens (millisiemens = milli + siemens), into megasiemens (MS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 106 siemens (megasiemens = mega + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of resistors in the kΩ-MΩ range.

The formula is MS = mS × 1 × 10-9, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “milli” prefix (m-, 10-3) was standardized in 1795.
