How the formula works

Both yoctosiemens and zeptosiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in yoctosiemens by 0.001 to get zeptosiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a zeptosiemens value by 1000 to get back to yoctosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert yoctosiemens to zeptosiemens?

Multiply the yoctosiemens value by 0.001. For example, 4 yS × 0.001 = 0.004 zS.

How do I convert zeptosiemens back to yoctosiemens?

Divide the zeptosiemens value by 0.001, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000.

Is the yoctosiemens to zeptosiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as large as the zeptosiemens?

The zeptosiemens (zS) mostly shows up in the conductance of near-perfect insulators, where using the base siemens would mean writing out unwieldy strings of digits.

What is a yoctosiemens used for?

The yoctosiemens (yS) is commonly encountered in the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

About this conversion

This page converts yoctosiemens (yS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-24 siemens (yoctosiemens = yocto + siemens), into zeptosiemens (zS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-21 siemens (zeptosiemens = zepto + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

The formula is zS = yS × 0.001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “yocto” prefix (y-, 10-24) was standardized in 1991.
