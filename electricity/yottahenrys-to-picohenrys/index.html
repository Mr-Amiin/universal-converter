How the formula works

Both yottahenry and picohenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in yottahenrys by 1.000000e+36 to get picohenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a picohenry value by 1.000000e-36 to get back to yottahenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert yottahenrys to picohenrys?

Multiply the yottahenry value by 1 × 1036. For example, 4 YH × 1 × 1036 = 4 × 1036 pH.

How do I convert picohenrys back to yottahenrys?

Is the yottahenry to picohenry conversion exact?

About this conversion

This page converts yottahenrys (YH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 1024 henrys (yottahenry = yotta + henry), into picohenrys (pH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 10-12 henrys (picohenry = pico + henry).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical inductance scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is pH = YH × 1 × 1036, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The henry is named after Joseph Henry and was formally adopted at the 1893 Chicago International Electrical Congress. The “yotta” prefix (Y-, 1024) was standardized in 1991.
