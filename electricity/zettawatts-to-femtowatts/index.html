How the formula works

Both zettawatt and femtowatt measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in zettawatts by 1.000000e+36 to get femtowatts. To reverse the conversion, multiply a femtowatt value by 1.000000e-36 to get back to zettawatts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert zettawatts to femtowatts?

Multiply the zettawatt value by 1 × 1036. For example, 4 ZW × 1 × 1036 = 4 × 1036 fW.

How do I convert femtowatts back to zettawatts?

Divide the femtowatt value by 1 × 1036, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-36.

Is the zettawatt to femtowatt conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of power, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

About this conversion

This page converts zettawatts (ZW), an SI-prefixed unit of power equal to 1021 watts (zettawatt = zetta + watt), into femtowatts (fW), an SI-prefixed unit of power equal to 10-15 watts (femtowatt = femto + watt).

This conversion is used when working with planetary or stellar energy-output scales, far beyond engineered systems.

The formula is fW = ZW × 1 × 1036, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The watt is named after James Watt and was proposed in 1882 and formally adopted by the 2nd International Electrical Congress in 1889. The “zetta” prefix (Z-, 1021) was standardized in 1991.
