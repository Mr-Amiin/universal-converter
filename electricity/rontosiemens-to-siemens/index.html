How the formula works

Both rontosiemens and siemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in rontosiemens by 1.000000e-27 to get siemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a siemens value by 1.000000e+27 to get back to rontosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert rontosiemens to siemens?

Multiply the rontosiemens value by 1 × 10-27. For example, 4 rS × 1 × 10-27 = 4 × 10-27 S.

How do I convert siemens back to rontosiemens?

Divide the siemens value by 1 × 10-27, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 1027.

Is the rontosiemens to siemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as small as the rontosiemens?

The rontosiemens (rS) shows up in the conductance of near-perfect insulators, where the base siemens would be an inconveniently large unit for the quantities involved.

What is a rontosiemens used for?

The rontosiemens (rS) is commonly encountered in the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

About this conversion

This page converts rontosiemens (rS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-27 siemens (rontosiemens = ronto + siemens), into siemens (S), the SI base unit of conductance, equal to the conductance of a component with one ohm of resistance.

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

The formula is S = rS × 1 × 10-27, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “ronto” prefix (r-, 10-27) was standardized in 2022.
