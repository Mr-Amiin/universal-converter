About Converting Tesla to Gauss

Tesla and gauss are both units of magnetic flux density. The tesla is the SI unit of magnetic flux density: one weber per square meter. The gauss is the CGS unit of magnetic flux density: 10⁻⁴ tesla.

Formula

G = T × 10000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 tesla equals 10000 gauss, and the reverse factor is 0.0001.

Simple example

1 T × 10000 = 10000 G

1 tesla = 10000 gauss.

Real-world example

10 T × 10000 = 100000 G

10 teslas = 100000 gauss.

Understanding the Tesla

Tesla (T) measures magnetic flux density. The tesla is the SI unit of magnetic flux density: one weber per square meter.

Standard and definition

The tesla is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the tesla today

  • MRI scanners and laboratory magnets
  • Magnetic sensors and motor design

Understanding the Gauss
