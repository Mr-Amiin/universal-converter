About Converting Amp-hours to Coulombs

Ampere-hour and coulomb are both units of charge. An ampere-hour is the charge carried by one ampere for one hour: 3,600 coulombs. The coulomb is the SI unit of electric charge: one ampere-second.

Formula

C = Ah × 3600

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 ampere-hour equals 3600 coulombs, and the reverse factor is 0.0002777777778.

Simple example

1 Ah × 3600 = 3600 C

1 ampere-hour = 3600 coulombs.

Real-world example

10 Ah × 3600 = 36000 C

10 ampere-hours = 36000 coulombs.

1 ampere-hour equals 3600 coulombs.

How do I convert ampere-hour to coulomb?

Multiply the ampere-hour value by 3600. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert coulomb back to ampere-hour?

Use the reverse factor: 1 coulomb equals 0.0002777777778 ampere-hours. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.
