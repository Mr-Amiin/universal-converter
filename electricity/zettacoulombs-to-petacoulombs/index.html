How the formula works

Both zettacoulomb and petacoulomb measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in zettacoulombs by 1.000000e+06 to get petacoulombs. To reverse the conversion, multiply a petacoulomb value by 0.000001 to get back to zettacoulombs.

How do I convert zettacoulombs to petacoulombs?

Multiply the zettacoulomb value by 1,000,000. For example, 4 ZC × 1,000,000 = 4,000,000 PC.

How do I convert petacoulombs back to zettacoulombs?

Is the zettacoulomb to petacoulomb conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of charge, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

About this conversion

This page converts zettacoulombs (ZC), an SI-prefixed unit of charge equal to 1021 coulombs (zettacoulomb = zetta + coulomb), into petacoulombs (PC), an SI-prefixed unit of charge equal to 1015 coulombs (petacoulomb = peta + coulomb).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical charge scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is PC = ZC × 1,000,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The coulomb is named after Charles-Augustin de Coulomb and was formally adopted at the 1881 International Electrical Congress in Paris. The “zetta” prefix (Z-, 1021) was standardized in 1991.
