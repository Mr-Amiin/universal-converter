How the formula works

Both quectosiemens and centisiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in quectosiemens by 1.000000e-28 to get centisiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a centisiemens value by 1.000000e+28 to get back to quectosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert quectosiemens to centisiemens?

Multiply the quectosiemens value by 1 × 10-28. For example, 4 qS × 1 × 10-28 = 4 × 10-28 cS.

How do I convert centisiemens back to quectosiemens?

Divide the centisiemens value by 1 × 10-28, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 1028.

Is the quectosiemens to centisiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as small as the quectosiemens?

The quectosiemens (qS) shows up in the conductance of near-perfect insulators, where the base siemens would be an inconveniently large unit for the quantities involved.

About this conversion

This page converts quectosiemens (qS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-30 siemens (quectosiemens = quecto + siemens), into centisiemens (cS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-2 siemens (centisiemens = centi + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

The formula is cS = qS × 1 × 10-28, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “quecto” prefix (q-, 10-30) was standardized in 2022.
