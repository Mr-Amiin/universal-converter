How the formula works

Both terafarad and hectofarad measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in terafarads by 1.000000e+10 to get hectofarads. To reverse the conversion, multiply a hectofarad value by 1.000000e-10 to get back to terafarads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert terafarads to hectofarads?

Multiply the terafarad value by 10,000,000,000. For example, 4 TF × 10,000,000,000 = 40,000,000,000 hF.

How do I convert hectofarads back to terafarads?

Divide the hectofarad value by 10,000,000,000, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-10.

Is the terafarad to hectofarad conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of capacitance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a terafarad used for?

The terafarad (TF) is commonly encountered in large energy-storage supercapacitor banks.

About this conversion

This page converts terafarads (TF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 1012 farads (terafarad = tera + farad), into hectofarads (hF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 102 farads (hectofarad = hecto + farad).

This conversion is used when working with large energy-storage supercapacitor banks.

The formula is hF = TF × 10,000,000,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The farad is named after Michael Faraday and was proposed in 1861 and formally adopted in 1881. The “tera” prefix (T-, 1012) was standardized in 1960.
