How the formula works

Both kilofarad and megafarad measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in kilofarads by 0.001 to get megafarads. To reverse the conversion, multiply a megafarad value by 1000 to get back to kilofarads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kilofarads to megafarads?

Multiply the kilofarad value by 0.001. For example, 4 kF × 0.001 = 0.004 MF.

How do I convert megafarads back to kilofarads?

Divide the megafarad value by 0.001, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000.

Is the kilofarad to megafarad conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of capacitance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a kilofarad used for?

The kilofarad (kF) is commonly encountered in supercapacitors, which can reach single farads.

About this conversion

This page converts kilofarads (kF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 103 farads (kilofarad = kilo + farad), into megafarads (MF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 106 farads (megafarad = mega + farad).

This conversion is used when working with supercapacitors, which can reach single farads.

The formula is MF = kF × 0.001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The farad is named after Michael Faraday and was proposed in 1861 and formally adopted in 1881. The “kilo” prefix (k-, 103) was standardized in 1795.
