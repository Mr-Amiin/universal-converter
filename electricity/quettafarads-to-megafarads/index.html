How the formula works

Both quettafarad and megafarad measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in quettafarads by 1.000000e+24 to get megafarads. To reverse the conversion, multiply a megafarad value by 1.000000e-24 to get back to quettafarads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert quettafarads to megafarads?

Multiply the quettafarad value by 1 × 1024. For example, 4 QF × 1 × 1024 = 4 × 1024 MF.

How do I convert megafarads back to quettafarads?

Divide the megafarad value by 1 × 1024, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-24.

Is the quettafarad to megafarad conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of capacitance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as large as the quettafarad?

The quettafarad (QF) mostly shows up in theoretical capacitance scales with no real engineered counterpart, where using the base farad would mean writing out unwieldy strings of digits.

About this conversion

This page converts quettafarads (QF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 1030 farads (quettafarad = quetta + farad), into megafarads (MF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 106 farads (megafarad = mega + farad).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical capacitance scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is MF = QF × 1 × 1024, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.
