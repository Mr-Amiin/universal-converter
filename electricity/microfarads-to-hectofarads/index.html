How the formula works

Both microfarad and hectofarad measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in microfarads by 1.000000e-08 to get hectofarads. To reverse the conversion, multiply a hectofarad value by 1.000000e+08 to get back to microfarads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert microfarads to hectofarads?

Multiply the microfarad value by 1 × 10-8. For example, 4 uF × 1 × 10-8 = 4 × 10-8 hF.

How do I convert hectofarads back to microfarads?

Divide the hectofarad value by 1 × 10-8, which is the same as multiplying by 100,000,000.

Is the microfarad to hectofarad conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of capacitance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a microfarad used for?

The microfarad (uF) is commonly encountered in ceramic and film capacitors (nanofarads to microfarads).

About this conversion

This page converts microfarads (uF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 10-6 farads (microfarad = micro + farad), into hectofarads (hF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 102 farads (hectofarad = hecto + farad).

This conversion is used when working with ceramic and film capacitors (nanofarads to microfarads).

The formula is hF = uF × 1 × 10-8, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The farad is named after Michael Faraday and was proposed in 1861 and formally adopted in 1881. The “micro” prefix (u-, 10-6) was standardized in 1873.
