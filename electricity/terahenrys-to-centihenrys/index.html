How the formula works

Both terahenry and centihenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in terahenrys by 1.000000e+14 to get centihenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a centihenry value by 1.000000e-14 to get back to terahenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert terahenrys to centihenrys?

Multiply the terahenry value by 100,000,000,000,000. For example, 4 TH × 100,000,000,000,000 = 400,000,000,000,000 cH.

How do I convert centihenrys back to terahenrys?

Divide the centihenry value by 100,000,000,000,000, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-14.

Is the terahenry to centihenry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a terahenry used for?

The terahenry (TH) is commonly encountered in large industrial transformer and grid-inductor scales.

About this conversion

This page converts terahenrys (TH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 1012 henrys (terahenry = tera + henry), into centihenrys (cH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 10-2 henrys (centihenry = centi + henry).

This conversion is used when working with large industrial transformer and grid-inductor scales.

The formula is cH = TH × 100,000,000,000,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The henry is named after Joseph Henry and was formally adopted at the 1893 Chicago International Electrical Congress. The “tera” prefix (T-, 1012) was standardized in 1960.
