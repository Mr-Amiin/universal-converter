How the formula works

Both decacoulomb and megacoulomb measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in decacoulombs by 0.00001 to get megacoulombs. To reverse the conversion, multiply a megacoulomb value by 100000 to get back to decacoulombs.

About this conversion

This page converts decacoulombs (daC), an SI-prefixed unit of charge equal to 101 coulombs (decacoulomb = deca + coulomb), into megacoulombs (MC), an SI-prefixed unit of charge equal to 106 coulombs (megacoulomb = mega + coulomb).

This conversion is used when working with the charge moved by a phone battery over about an hour (a few thousand coulombs).

The formula is MC = daC × 0.00001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The coulomb is named after Charles-Augustin de Coulomb and was formally adopted at the 1881 International Electrical Congress in Paris. The “deca” prefix (da-, 101) was standardized in 1795.
