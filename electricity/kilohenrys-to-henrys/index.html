How the formula works

Both kilohenry and henry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in kilohenrys by 1000 to get henrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a henry value by 0.001 to get back to kilohenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kilohenrys to henrys?

Multiply the kilohenry value by 1,000. For example, 4 kH × 1,000 = 4,000 H.

How do I convert henrys back to kilohenrys?

Divide the henry value by 1,000, which is the same as multiplying by 0.001.

Is the kilohenry to henry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a kilohenry used for?

The kilohenry (kH) is commonly encountered in power-supply chokes and transformer windings.

About this conversion

This page converts kilohenrys (kH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 103 henrys (kilohenry = kilo + henry), into henrys (H), the SI base unit of inductance, equal to the inductance that induces one volt when current changes at one ampere per second.

This conversion is used when working with power-supply chokes and transformer windings.

The formula is H = kH × 1,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.
