How the formula works

Both femtosiemens and terasiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in femtosiemens by 1.000000e-27 to get terasiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a terasiemens value by 1.000000e+27 to get back to femtosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert femtosiemens to terasiemens?

Multiply the femtosiemens value by 1 × 10-27. For example, 4 fS × 1 × 10-27 = 4 × 10-27 TS.

How do I convert terasiemens back to femtosiemens?

Divide the terasiemens value by 1 × 10-27, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 1027.

Is the femtosiemens to terasiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as small as the femtosiemens?

The femtosiemens (fS) shows up in the conductance of near-perfect insulators, where the base siemens would be an inconveniently large unit for the quantities involved.

What is a femtosiemens used for?

The femtosiemens (fS) is commonly encountered in the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

About this conversion

This page converts femtosiemens (fS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-15 siemens (femtosiemens = femto + siemens), into terasiemens (TS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 1012 siemens (terasiemens = tera + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

The formula is TS = fS × 1 × 10-27, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “femto” prefix (f-, 10-15) was standardized in 1975.
