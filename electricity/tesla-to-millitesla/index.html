About Converting Tesla to Millitesla

Tesla and millitesla are both units of magnetic flux density. The tesla is the SI unit of magnetic flux density: one weber per square meter. A millitesla is one thousandth of a tesla.

Formula

mT = T × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 tesla equals 1000 milliteslas, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 T × 1000 = 1000 mT

1 tesla = 1000 milliteslas.

Real-world example

10 T × 1000 = 10000 mT

10 teslas = 10000 milliteslas.
