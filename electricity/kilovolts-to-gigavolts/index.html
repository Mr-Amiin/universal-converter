How the formula works

Both kilovolt and gigavolt measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in kilovolts by 0.000001 to get gigavolts. To reverse the conversion, multiply a gigavolt value by 1.000000e+06 to get back to kilovolts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kilovolts to gigavolts?

Multiply the kilovolt value by 0.000001. For example, 4 kV × 0.000001 = 0.000004 GV.

How do I convert gigavolts back to kilovolts?

Divide the gigavolt value by 0.000001, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000,000.

Is the kilovolt to gigavolt conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of voltage, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a kilovolt used for?

The kilovolt (kV) is commonly encountered in household outlets and small electronics (mains power runs 120-240 V).

About this conversion

This page converts kilovolts (kV), an SI-prefixed unit of voltage equal to 103 volts (kilovolt = kilo + volt), into gigavolts (GV), an SI-prefixed unit of voltage equal to 109 volts (gigavolt = giga + volt).

This conversion is used when working with household outlets and small electronics (mains power runs 120-240 V).

The formula is GV = kV × 0.000001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The volt is named after Alessandro Volta and was formally adopted at the 1881 International Electrical Congress in Paris. The “kilo” prefix (k-, 103) was standardized in 1795.
