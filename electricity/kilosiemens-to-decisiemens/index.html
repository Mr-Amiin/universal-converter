How the formula works

Both kilosiemens and decisiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in kilosiemens by 10000 to get decisiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a decisiemens value by 0.0001 to get back to kilosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kilosiemens to decisiemens?

Multiply the kilosiemens value by 10,000. For example, 4 kS × 10,000 = 40,000 dS.

How do I convert decisiemens back to kilosiemens?

Divide the decisiemens value by 10,000, which is the same as multiplying by 0.0001.

Is the kilosiemens to decisiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a kilosiemens used for?

The kilosiemens (kS) is commonly encountered in typical resistor conductances (a 1 kΩ resistor is 1 mS).

About this conversion

This page converts kilosiemens (kS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 103 siemens (kilosiemens = kilo + siemens), into decisiemens (dS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-1 siemens (decisiemens = deci + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with typical resistor conductances (a 1 kΩ resistor is 1 mS).

The formula is dS = kS × 10,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “kilo” prefix (k-, 103) was standardized in 1795.
