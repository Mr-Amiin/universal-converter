How the formula works

Both yoctohenry and centihenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in yoctohenrys by 1.000000e-22 to get centihenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a centihenry value by 1.000000e+22 to get back to yoctohenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert yoctohenrys to centihenrys?

Multiply the yoctohenry value by 1 × 10-22. For example, 4 yH × 1 × 10-22 = 4 × 10-22 cH.

How do I convert centihenrys back to yoctohenrys?

Divide the centihenry value by 1 × 10-22, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 1022.

Is the yoctohenry to centihenry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as small as the yoctohenry?

The yoctohenry (yH) shows up in the parasitic inductance of a short wire, where the base henry would be an inconveniently large unit for the quantities involved.

What is a yoctohenry used for?

About this conversion

This page converts yoctohenrys (yH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 10-24 henrys (yoctohenry = yocto + henry), into centihenrys (cH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 10-2 henrys (centihenry = centi + henry).

This conversion is used when working with the parasitic inductance of a short wire.

The formula is cH = yH × 1 × 10-22, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The henry is named after Joseph Henry and was formally adopted at the 1893 Chicago International Electrical Congress. The “yocto” prefix (y-, 10-24) was standardized in 1991.
