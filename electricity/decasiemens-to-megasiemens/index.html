How the formula works

Both decasiemens and megasiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in decasiemens by 0.00001 to get megasiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a megasiemens value by 100000 to get back to decasiemens.

How do I convert decasiemens to megasiemens?

About this conversion

This page converts decasiemens (daS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 101 siemens (decasiemens = deca + siemens), into megasiemens (MS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 106 siemens (megasiemens = mega + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with typical resistor conductances (a 1 kΩ resistor is 1 mS).

The formula is MS = daS × 0.00001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “deca” prefix (da-, 101) was standardized in 1795.
