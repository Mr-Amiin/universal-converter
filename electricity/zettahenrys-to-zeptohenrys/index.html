How the formula works

Both zettahenry and zeptohenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in zettahenrys by 1.000000e+42 to get zeptohenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a zeptohenry value by 1.000000e-42 to get back to zettahenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert zettahenrys to zeptohenrys?

Multiply the zettahenry value by 1 × 1042. For example, 4 ZH × 1 × 1042 = 4 × 1042 zH.

How do I convert zeptohenrys back to zettahenrys?

Divide the zeptohenry value by 1 × 1042, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-42.

Is the zettahenry to zeptohenry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

About this conversion

This page converts zettahenrys (ZH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 1021 henrys (zettahenry = zetta + henry), into zeptohenrys (zH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 10-21 henrys (zeptohenry = zepto + henry).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical inductance scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is zH = ZH × 1 × 1042, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The henry is named after Joseph Henry and was formally adopted at the 1893 Chicago International Electrical Congress. The “zetta” prefix (Z-, 1021) was standardized in 1991.
