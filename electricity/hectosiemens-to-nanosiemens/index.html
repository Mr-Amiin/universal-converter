How the formula works

Both hectosiemens and nanosiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in hectosiemens by 1.000000e+11 to get nanosiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a nanosiemens value by 1.000000e-11 to get back to hectosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert hectosiemens to nanosiemens?

Multiply the hectosiemens value by 100,000,000,000. For example, 4 hS × 100,000,000,000 = 400,000,000,000 nS.

How do I convert nanosiemens back to hectosiemens?

Divide the nanosiemens value by 100,000,000,000, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-11.

Is the hectosiemens to nanosiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a hectosiemens used for?

The hectosiemens (hS) is commonly encountered in typical resistor conductances (a 1 kΩ resistor is 1 mS).

About this conversion

This page converts hectosiemens (hS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 102 siemens (hectosiemens = hecto + siemens), into nanosiemens (nS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-9 siemens (nanosiemens = nano + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with typical resistor conductances (a 1 kΩ resistor is 1 mS).

The formula is nS = hS × 100,000,000,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “hecto” prefix (h-, 102) was standardized in 1795.
