How the formula works

Both nanofarad and microfarad measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in nanofarads by 0.001 to get microfarads. To reverse the conversion, multiply a microfarad value by 1000 to get back to nanofarads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert nanofarads to microfarads?

Multiply the nanofarad value by 0.001. For example, 4 nF × 0.001 = 0.004 uF.

How do I convert microfarads back to nanofarads?

Divide the microfarad value by 0.001, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000.

Is the nanofarad to microfarad conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of capacitance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a nanofarad used for?

The nanofarad (nF) is commonly encountered in ceramic and film capacitors (nanofarads to microfarads).

About this conversion

This page converts nanofarads (nF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 10-9 farads (nanofarad = nano + farad), into microfarads (uF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 10-6 farads (microfarad = micro + farad).

This conversion is used when working with ceramic and film capacitors (nanofarads to microfarads).

The formula is uF = nF × 0.001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The farad is named after Michael Faraday and was proposed in 1861 and formally adopted in 1881. The “nano” prefix (n-, 10-9) was standardized in 1960.
