How the formula works

Both nanowatt and microwatt measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in nanowatts by 0.001 to get microwatts. To reverse the conversion, multiply a microwatt value by 1000 to get back to nanowatts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert nanowatts to microwatts?

Multiply the nanowatt value by 0.001. For example, 4 nW × 0.001 = 0.004 uW.

How do I convert microwatts back to nanowatts?

Divide the microwatt value by 0.001, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000.

Is the nanowatt to microwatt conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of power, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a nanowatt used for?

The nanowatt (nW) is commonly encountered in LED and phone-charger power draw.

About this conversion

This page converts nanowatts (nW), an SI-prefixed unit of power equal to 10-9 watts (nanowatt = nano + watt), into microwatts (uW), an SI-prefixed unit of power equal to 10-6 watts (microwatt = micro + watt).

This conversion is used when working with LED and phone-charger power draw.

The formula is uW = nW × 0.001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The watt is named after James Watt and was proposed in 1882 and formally adopted by the 2nd International Electrical Congress in 1889. The “nano” prefix (n-, 10-9) was standardized in 1960.
