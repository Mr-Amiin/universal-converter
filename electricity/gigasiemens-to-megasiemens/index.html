How the formula works

Both gigasiemens and megasiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in gigasiemens by 1000 to get megasiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a megasiemens value by 0.001 to get back to gigasiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert gigasiemens to megasiemens?

Multiply the gigasiemens value by 1,000. For example, 4 GS × 1,000 = 4,000 MS.

How do I convert megasiemens back to gigasiemens?

Divide the megasiemens value by 1,000, which is the same as multiplying by 0.001.

Is the gigasiemens to megasiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a gigasiemens used for?

The gigasiemens (GS) is commonly encountered in high-conductance busbars and superconducting contacts.

About this conversion

This page converts gigasiemens (GS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 109 siemens (gigasiemens = giga + siemens), into megasiemens (MS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 106 siemens (megasiemens = mega + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with high-conductance busbars and superconducting contacts.

The formula is MS = GS × 1,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “giga” prefix (G-, 109) was standardized in 1960.
