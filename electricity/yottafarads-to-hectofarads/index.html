How the formula works

Both yottafarad and hectofarad measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in yottafarads by 1.000000e+22 to get hectofarads. To reverse the conversion, multiply a hectofarad value by 1.000000e-22 to get back to yottafarads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert yottafarads to hectofarads?

Multiply the yottafarad value by 1 × 1022. For example, 4 YF × 1 × 1022 = 4 × 1022 hF.

How do I convert hectofarads back to yottafarads?

Divide the hectofarad value by 1 × 1022, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-22.

Is the yottafarad to hectofarad conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of capacitance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as large as the yottafarad?

The yottafarad (YF) mostly shows up in theoretical capacitance scales with no real engineered counterpart, where using the base farad would mean writing out unwieldy strings of digits.

About this conversion

This page converts yottafarads (YF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 1024 farads (yottafarad = yotta + farad), into hectofarads (hF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 102 farads (hectofarad = hecto + farad).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical capacitance scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is hF = YF × 1 × 1022, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The farad is named after Michael Faraday and was proposed in 1861 and formally adopted in 1881. The “yotta” prefix (Y-, 1024) was standardized in 1991.
