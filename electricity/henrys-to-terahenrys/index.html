How the formula works

Both henry and terahenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in henrys by 1.000000e-12 to get terahenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a terahenry value by 1.000000e+12 to get back to henrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert henrys to terahenrys?

Multiply the henry value by 1 × 10-12. For example, 4 H × 1 × 10-12 = 4 × 10-12 TH.

How do I convert terahenrys back to henrys?

Divide the terahenry value by 1 × 10-12, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000,000,000,000.

Is the henry to terahenry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a henry used for?

The henry (H) is commonly encountered in power-supply chokes and transformer windings.

About this conversion

This page converts henrys (H), the SI base unit of inductance, equal to the inductance that induces one volt when current changes at one ampere per second, into terahenrys (TH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 1012 henrys (terahenry = tera + henry).

This conversion is used when working with power-supply chokes and transformer windings.

The formula is TH = H × 1 × 10-12, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.
