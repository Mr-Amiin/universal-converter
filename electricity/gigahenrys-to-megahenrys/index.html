How the formula works

Both gigahenry and megahenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in gigahenrys by 1000 to get megahenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a megahenry value by 0.001 to get back to gigahenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert gigahenrys to megahenrys?

Multiply the gigahenry value by 1,000. For example, 4 GH × 1,000 = 4,000 MH.

How do I convert megahenrys back to gigahenrys?

Divide the megahenry value by 1,000, which is the same as multiplying by 0.001.

Is the gigahenry to megahenry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a gigahenry used for?

The gigahenry (GH) is commonly encountered in large industrial transformer and grid-inductor scales.

About this conversion

This page converts gigahenrys (GH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 109 henrys (gigahenry = giga + henry), into megahenrys (MH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 106 henrys (megahenry = mega + henry).

This conversion is used when working with large industrial transformer and grid-inductor scales.

The formula is MH = GH × 1,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.
