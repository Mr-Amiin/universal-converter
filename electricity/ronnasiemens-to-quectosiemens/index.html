How the formula works

Both ronnasiemens and quectosiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in ronnasiemens by 1.000000e+57 to get quectosiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a quectosiemens value by 1.000000e-57 to get back to ronnasiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert ronnasiemens to quectosiemens?

Multiply the ronnasiemens value by 1 × 1057. For example, 4 RS × 1 × 1057 = 4 × 1057 qS.

How do I convert quectosiemens back to ronnasiemens?

Divide the quectosiemens value by 1 × 1057, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-57.

Is the ronnasiemens to quectosiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

About this conversion

This page converts ronnasiemens (RS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 1027 siemens (ronnasiemens = ronna + siemens), into quectosiemens (qS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-30 siemens (quectosiemens = quecto + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical conductance scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is qS = RS × 1 × 1057, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “ronna” prefix (R-, 1027) was standardized in 2022.
