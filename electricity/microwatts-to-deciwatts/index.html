How the formula works

Both microwatt and deciwatt measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in microwatts by 0.00001 to get deciwatts. To reverse the conversion, multiply a deciwatt value by 100000 to get back to microwatts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert microwatts to deciwatts?

Multiply the microwatt value by 0.00001. For example, 4 uW × 0.00001 = 0.00004 dW.

How do I convert deciwatts back to microwatts?

Divide the deciwatt value by 0.00001, which is the same as multiplying by 100,000.

Is the microwatt to deciwatt conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of power, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a microwatt used for?

The microwatt (uW) is commonly encountered in LED and phone-charger power draw.

About this conversion

This page converts microwatts (uW), an SI-prefixed unit of power equal to 10-6 watts (microwatt = micro + watt), into deciwatts (dW), an SI-prefixed unit of power equal to 10-1 watts (deciwatt = deci + watt).

This conversion is used when working with LED and phone-charger power draw.

The formula is dW = uW × 0.00001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The watt is named after James Watt and was proposed in 1882 and formally adopted by the 2nd International Electrical Congress in 1889. The “micro” prefix (u-, 10-6) was standardized in 1873.
