How the formula works

Both nanosiemens and kilosiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in nanosiemens by 1.000000e-12 to get kilosiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a kilosiemens value by 1.000000e+12 to get back to nanosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert nanosiemens to kilosiemens?

Multiply the nanosiemens value by 1 × 10-12. For example, 4 nS × 1 × 10-12 = 4 × 10-12 kS.

How do I convert kilosiemens back to nanosiemens?

Divide the kilosiemens value by 1 × 10-12, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000,000,000,000.

Is the nanosiemens to kilosiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a nanosiemens used for?

The nanosiemens (nS) is commonly encountered in the conductance of resistors in the kΩ-MΩ range.

About this conversion

This page converts nanosiemens (nS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-9 siemens (nanosiemens = nano + siemens), into kilosiemens (kS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 103 siemens (kilosiemens = kilo + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of resistors in the kΩ-MΩ range.

The formula is kS = nS × 1 × 10-12, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “nano” prefix (n-, 10-9) was standardized in 1960.
