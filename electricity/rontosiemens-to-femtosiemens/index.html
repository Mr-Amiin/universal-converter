How the formula works

Both rontosiemens and femtosiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in rontosiemens by 1.000000e-12 to get femtosiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a femtosiemens value by 1.000000e+12 to get back to rontosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert rontosiemens to femtosiemens?

Multiply the rontosiemens value by 1 × 10-12. For example, 4 rS × 1 × 10-12 = 4 × 10-12 fS.

How do I convert femtosiemens back to rontosiemens?

Divide the femtosiemens value by 1 × 10-12, which is the same as multiplying by 1,000,000,000,000.

Is the rontosiemens to femtosiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as large as the femtosiemens?

The femtosiemens (fS) mostly shows up in the conductance of near-perfect insulators, where using the base siemens would mean writing out unwieldy strings of digits.

What is a rontosiemens used for?

The rontosiemens (rS) is commonly encountered in the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

About this conversion

This page converts rontosiemens (rS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-27 siemens (rontosiemens = ronto + siemens), into femtosiemens (fS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-15 siemens (femtosiemens = femto + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

The formula is fS = rS × 1 × 10-12, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “ronto” prefix (r-, 10-27) was standardized in 2022.
