How the formula works

Both zeptosiemens and quectosiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in zeptosiemens by 1.000000e+09 to get quectosiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a quectosiemens value by 1.000000e-09 to get back to zeptosiemens.

Is the zeptosiemens to quectosiemens conversion exact?

About this conversion

This page converts zeptosiemens (zS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-21 siemens (zeptosiemens = zepto + siemens), into quectosiemens (qS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-30 siemens (quectosiemens = quecto + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

The formula is qS = zS × 1,000,000,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “zepto” prefix (z-, 10-21) was standardized in 1991.
