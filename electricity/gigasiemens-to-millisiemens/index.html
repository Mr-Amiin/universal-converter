How the formula works

Both gigasiemens and millisiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in gigasiemens by 1.000000e+12 to get millisiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a millisiemens value by 1.000000e-12 to get back to gigasiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert gigasiemens to millisiemens?

Multiply the gigasiemens value by 1,000,000,000,000. For example, 4 GS × 1,000,000,000,000 = 4,000,000,000,000 mS.

How do I convert millisiemens back to gigasiemens?

Divide the millisiemens value by 1,000,000,000,000, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-12.

Is the gigasiemens to millisiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a gigasiemens used for?

The gigasiemens (GS) is commonly encountered in high-conductance busbars and superconducting contacts.

About this conversion

This page converts gigasiemens (GS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 109 siemens (gigasiemens = giga + siemens), into millisiemens (mS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-3 siemens (millisiemens = milli + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with high-conductance busbars and superconducting contacts.

The formula is mS = GS × 1,000,000,000,000, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “giga” prefix (G-, 109) was standardized in 1960.
