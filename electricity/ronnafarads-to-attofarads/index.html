How the formula works

Both ronnafarad and attofarad measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in ronnafarads by 1.000000e+45 to get attofarads. To reverse the conversion, multiply a attofarad value by 1.000000e-45 to get back to ronnafarads.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert ronnafarads to attofarads?

Multiply the ronnafarad value by 1 × 1045. For example, 4 RF × 1 × 1045 = 4 × 1045 aF.

How do I convert attofarads back to ronnafarads?

Divide the attofarad value by 1 × 1045, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-45.

Is the ronnafarad to attofarad conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of capacitance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

About this conversion

This page converts ronnafarads (RF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 1027 farads (ronnafarad = ronna + farad), into attofarads (aF), an SI-prefixed unit of capacitance equal to 10-18 farads (attofarad = atto + farad).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical capacitance scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is aF = RF × 1 × 1045, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.
