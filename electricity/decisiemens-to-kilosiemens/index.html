How the formula works

Both decisiemens and kilosiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in decisiemens by 0.0001 to get kilosiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a kilosiemens value by 10000 to get back to decisiemens.

How do I convert decisiemens to kilosiemens?

Is the decisiemens to kilosiemens conversion exact?

About this conversion

This page converts decisiemens (dS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-1 siemens (decisiemens = deci + siemens), into kilosiemens (kS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 103 siemens (kilosiemens = kilo + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with typical resistor conductances (a 1 kΩ resistor is 1 mS).

The formula is kS = dS × 0.0001, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “deci” prefix (d-, 10-1) was standardized in 1795.
