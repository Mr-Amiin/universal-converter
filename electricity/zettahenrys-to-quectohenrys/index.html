How the formula works

Both zettahenry and quectohenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in zettahenrys by 1.000000e+51 to get quectohenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a quectohenry value by 1.000000e-51 to get back to zettahenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert zettahenrys to quectohenrys?

Multiply the zettahenry value by 1 × 1051. For example, 4 ZH × 1 × 1051 = 4 × 1051 qH.

How do I convert quectohenrys back to zettahenrys?

Is the zettahenry to quectohenry conversion exact?

About this conversion

This page converts zettahenrys (ZH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 1021 henrys (zettahenry = zetta + henry), into quectohenrys (qH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 10-30 henrys (quectohenry = quecto + henry).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical inductance scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is qH = ZH × 1 × 1051, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The henry is named after Joseph Henry and was formally adopted at the 1893 Chicago International Electrical Congress. The “zetta” prefix (Z-, 1021) was standardized in 1991.
