How the formula works

Both yottasiemens and rontosiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in yottasiemens by 1.000000e+51 to get rontosiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a rontosiemens value by 1.000000e-51 to get back to yottasiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert yottasiemens to rontosiemens?

Multiply the yottasiemens value by 1 × 1051. For example, 4 YS × 1 × 1051 = 4 × 1051 rS.

How do I convert rontosiemens back to yottasiemens?

Divide the rontosiemens value by 1 × 1051, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-51.

Is the yottasiemens to rontosiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as large as the yottasiemens?

About this conversion

This page converts yottasiemens (YS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 1024 siemens (yottasiemens = yotta + siemens), into rontosiemens (rS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-27 siemens (rontosiemens = ronto + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with theoretical conductance scales with no real engineered counterpart.

The formula is rS = YS × 1 × 1051, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “yotta” prefix (Y-, 1024) was standardized in 1991.
