How the formula works

Both megahenry and rontohenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in megahenrys by 1.000000e+33 to get rontohenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a rontohenry value by 1.000000e-33 to get back to megahenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert megahenrys to rontohenrys?

Multiply the megahenry value by 1 × 1033. For example, 4 MH × 1 × 1033 = 4 × 1033 rH.

How do I convert rontohenrys back to megahenrys?

Divide the rontohenry value by 1 × 1033, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 10-33.

Is the megahenry to rontohenry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as small as the rontohenry?

The rontohenry (rH) shows up in the parasitic inductance of a short wire, where the base henry would be an inconveniently large unit for the quantities involved.

What is a megahenry used for?

The megahenry (MH) is commonly encountered in large industrial transformer and grid-inductor scales.

About this conversion

This conversion is used when working with large industrial transformer and grid-inductor scales.

The formula is rH = MH × 1 × 1033, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.
