About Converting Amp-hours to Milliamp-hours

Ampere-hour and milliampere-hour are both units of charge. An ampere-hour is the charge carried by one ampere for one hour: 3,600 coulombs. A milliampere-hour is one thousandth of an ampere-hour: 3.6 coulombs.

Formula

mAh = Ah × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 ampere-hour equals 1000 milliampere-hours, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 Ah × 1000 = 1000 mAh

1 ampere-hour = 1000 milliampere-hours.

Real-world example

10 Ah × 1000 = 10000 mAh

10 ampere-hours = 10000 milliampere-hours.
