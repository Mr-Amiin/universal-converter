Formula

No fixed factor — different physical quantities

Hectoohm measures electrical resistance and Picovolt measures electric potential (voltage). These are different physical quantities, so there is no fixed multiplication factor between them. Use Ohm’s law and energy over time: V = I × R, P = V × I, E = P × time.
