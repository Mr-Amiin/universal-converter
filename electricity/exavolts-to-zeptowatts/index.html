Relationship (no fixed conversion)

Exavolt (EV) measures voltage. Zeptowatt (zW) measures power. Because these are different physical dimensions, there is no single fixed factor that converts one directly into the other — a numeric answer here would be fabricated and misleading. They are related through the Power Law: given just these two values, you can calculate current (I = P / V) and resistance (R = V² / P).
