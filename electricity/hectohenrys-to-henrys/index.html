How the formula works

Both hectohenry and henry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in hectohenrys by 100 to get henrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a henry value by 0.01 to get back to hectohenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert hectohenrys to henrys?

Multiply the hectohenry value by 100. For example, 4 hH × 100 = 400 H.

How do I convert henrys back to hectohenrys?

Divide the henry value by 100, which is the same as multiplying by 0.01.

Is the hectohenry to henry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a hectohenry used for?

The hectohenry (hH) is commonly encountered in power-supply chokes and transformer windings.

About this conversion

This page converts hectohenrys (hH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 102 henrys (hectohenry = hecto + henry), into henrys (H), the SI base unit of inductance, equal to the inductance that induces one volt when current changes at one ampere per second.

This conversion is used when working with power-supply chokes and transformer windings.

The formula is H = hH × 100, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.
