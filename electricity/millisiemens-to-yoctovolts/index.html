From definition

A Millisiemens (mS) equals 1/1,000 siemens(s), a scaled unit of electrical conductance used for milli-scale electrical work.

To definition

A Yoctovolt (yV) equals 1e-24 volt(s), a scaled unit of electric potential used for yocto-scale electrical work.

Formula

No fixed factor — different physical quantities

Millisiemens measures electrical conductance and Yoctovolt measures electric potential (voltage). These are different physical quantities, so there is no fixed multiplication factor between them. Use Ohm’s law and energy over time: V = I × R, P = V × I, E = P × time.
