How the formula works

Both nanohenry and centihenry measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in nanohenrys by 1.000000e-07 to get centihenrys. To reverse the conversion, multiply a centihenry value by 1.000000e+07 to get back to nanohenrys.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert nanohenrys to centihenrys?

Multiply the nanohenry value by 1 × 10-7. For example, 4 nH × 1 × 10-7 = 4 × 10-7 cH.

How do I convert centihenrys back to nanohenrys?

Divide the centihenry value by 1 × 10-7, which is the same as multiplying by 10,000,000.

Is the nanohenry to centihenry conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of inductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

What is a nanohenry used for?

About this conversion

This page converts nanohenrys (nH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 10-9 henrys (nanohenry = nano + henry), into centihenrys (cH), an SI-prefixed unit of inductance equal to 10-2 henrys (centihenry = centi + henry).

This conversion is used when working with small signal inductors and coils.

The formula is cH = nH × 1 × 10-7, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The henry is named after Joseph Henry and was formally adopted at the 1893 Chicago International Electrical Congress. The “nano” prefix (n-, 10-9) was standardized in 1960.
