How the formula works

Both attosiemens and decasiemens measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in attosiemens by 1.000000e-19 to get decasiemens. To reverse the conversion, multiply a decasiemens value by 1.000000e+19 to get back to attosiemens.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert attosiemens to decasiemens?

Multiply the attosiemens value by 1 × 10-19. For example, 4 aS × 1 × 10-19 = 4 × 10-19 daS.

How do I convert decasiemens back to attosiemens?

Divide the decasiemens value by 1 × 10-19, which is the same as multiplying by 1 × 1019.

Is the attosiemens to decasiemens conversion exact?

Yes. Both are SI-prefixed units of conductance, so the conversion factor is an exact power of ten rather than a rounded, measurement-based approximation.

Why would anyone need a unit as small as the attosiemens?

About this conversion

This page converts attosiemens (aS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 10-18 siemens (attosiemens = atto + siemens), into decasiemens (daS), an SI-prefixed unit of conductance equal to 101 siemens (decasiemens = deca + siemens).

This conversion is used when working with the conductance of near-perfect insulators.

The formula is daS = aS × 1 × 10-19, a fixed decimal ratio between these two SI-prefixed units.

The siemens is named after Ernst Werner von Siemens and was adopted by the CGPM in 1971, replacing the older non-SI “mho”. The “atto” prefix (a-, 10-18) was standardized in 1975.
