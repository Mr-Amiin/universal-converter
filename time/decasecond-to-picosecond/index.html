Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the decasecond (das).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the picosecond (ps).

How do I convert decasecond to picosecond?

Multiply the decasecond value by 1.000000e+13. For example, 5 das × 1.000000e+13 = 5.000000e+13 ps.

Why do decasecond and picosecond need converting at all?

Deca (da) represents 10^1 and is one of the least commonly used SI prefixes. Pico (p) represents 10^-12, used for extremely small physical quantities.

Is the decasecond to picosecond conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between decasecond and picosecond is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e+13), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert picosecond back to decasecond?

Yes. Multiply the picosecond value by 1.000000e-13 (or divide by 1.000000e+13) to get decasecond.
