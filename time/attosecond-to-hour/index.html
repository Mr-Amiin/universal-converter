Frequently asked questions

How do I convert attosecond to hours?

Multiply the attosecond value by 2.777778e-22. For example, 10 as × 2.777778e-22 = 2.777778e-21 h.

Is the attosecond to hours conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between these units is a fixed mathematical or SI-defined ratio, so the conversion factor is exact rather than a measured approximation.

Can I convert hours back to attosecond?
