Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the minutes (min).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the months (mo).

How do I convert minutes to months?

Multiply the minutes value by 2.300000e-05. For example, 5 min × 2.300000e-05 = 0.000115 mo.

Why do minutes and months need converting at all?

The minute (60 seconds) is a non-SI unit accepted for use with the SI system. Months is a standard unit used in this category's conversions.

Is the minutes to months conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between minutes and months is a fixed, defined ratio (× 2.300000e-05), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert months back to minutes?

Yes. Multiply the months value by 43478.2608695652 (or divide by 2.300000e-05) to get minutes.
