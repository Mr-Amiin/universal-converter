Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the decisecond (ds).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the decasecond (das).

How do I convert decisecond to decasecond?

Multiply the decisecond value by 0.01. For example, 5 ds × 0.01 = 0.05 das.

Why do decisecond and decasecond need converting at all?

Deci (d) represents 10^-1, familiar from the decibel and deciliter. Deca (da) represents 10^1 and is one of the least commonly used SI prefixes.

Is the decisecond to decasecond conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between decisecond and decasecond is a fixed, defined ratio (× 0.01), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert decasecond back to decisecond?

Yes. Multiply the decasecond value by 100 (or divide by 0.01) to get decisecond.
