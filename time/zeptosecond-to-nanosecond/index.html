Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the zeptosecond (zs).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the nanosecond (ns).

How do I convert zeptosecond to nanosecond?

Multiply the zeptosecond value by 1.000000e-12. For example, 5 zs × 1.000000e-12 = 5.000000e-12 ns.

Why do zeptosecond and nanosecond need converting at all?

Zepto (z) represents 10^-21, one of the smallest SI prefixes in practical use. Nano (n) represents 10^-9, familiar from nanotechnology and nanometers.

Is the zeptosecond to nanosecond conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between zeptosecond and nanosecond is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-12), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert nanosecond back to zeptosecond?

Yes. Multiply the nanosecond value by 1.000000e+12 (or divide by 1.000000e-12) to get zeptosecond.
