Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the yoctosecond (ys).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the terasecond (Ts).

How do I convert yoctosecond to terasecond?

Multiply the yoctosecond value by 1.000000e-36. For example, 5 ys × 1.000000e-36 = 5.000000e-36 Ts.

Why do yoctosecond and terasecond need converting at all?

Yocto (y) represents 10^-24, historically the smallest SI prefix before 2022. Tera (T) represents 10^12, familiar from terabyte-scale data storage.

Is the yoctosecond to terasecond conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between yoctosecond and terasecond is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-36), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert terasecond back to yoctosecond?

Yes. Multiply the terasecond value by 1.000000e+36 (or divide by 1.000000e-36) to get yoctosecond.
