Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the days (d).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the minutes (min).

How do I convert days to minutes?

Multiply the days value by 1440. For example, 5 d × 1440 = 7200 min.

Why do days and minutes need converting at all?

The day (86,400 seconds) is based on one rotation of the Earth relative to the Sun. The minute (60 seconds) is a non-SI unit accepted for use with the SI system.

Is the days to minutes conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between days and minutes is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1440), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert minutes back to days?

Yes. Multiply the minutes value by 0.0006944444 (or divide by 1440) to get days.

About this conversion

This page converts days (d) into minutes (min) within the time converter, using the fixed relationship 1 d = 1440 min.

Worked example: to convert 10 d to min, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1440 = 14400 min.

A 30-days meeting lasts about 43200 min.

Reverse conversion: to go from minutes back to days, divide by 1440 (equivalently, multiply by 0.0006944444).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
