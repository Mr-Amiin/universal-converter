Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the millisecond (ms).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the picosecond (ps).

How do I convert millisecond to picosecond?

Multiply the millisecond value by 1000000000. For example, 5 ms × 1000000000 = 5000000000 ps.

Why do millisecond and picosecond need converting at all?

Milli (m) represents 10^-3, one of the most common small-scale SI prefixes. Pico (p) represents 10^-12, used for extremely small physical quantities.

Is the millisecond to picosecond conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between millisecond and picosecond is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1000000000), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert picosecond back to millisecond?

Yes. Multiply the picosecond value by 1.000000e-09 (or divide by 1000000000) to get millisecond.

About this conversion

This page converts millisecond (ms) into picosecond (ps) within the time converter, using the fixed relationship 1 ms = 1000000000 ps.

Worked example: to convert 10 ms to ps, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1000000000 = 10000000000 ps.

A 30-millisecond meeting lasts about 30000000000 ps.

Reverse conversion: to go from picosecond back to millisecond, divide by 1000000000 (equivalently, multiply by 1.000000e-09).
