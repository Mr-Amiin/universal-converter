Frequently asked questions

How do you convert pound to kilogram?

Use the formula kg = lb × 0.45359237. One pound equals 0.45359237 kilograms.

What is Pound?

The pound (lb) is a unit of mass commonly used in the United States and other countries.

What is Kilogram?

The kilogram (kg) is the SI base unit of mass.

Is the pound to kilogram conversion exact?

Yes. Pound and Kilogram are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

How do I convert kilogram back to pound?

Use the reverse formula lb = kg ÷ 0.45359237. This site's reverse converter does the same calculation automatically.

About this conversion

This weight converter turns pound into kilogram using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. An adult who weighs 150 lbs weighs about 68.04 kg. The international avoirdupois pound was standardized in 1959 by agreement between the US and Commonwealth countries at exactly 0.45359237 kg. The kilogram was redefined in 2019 to be based on the Planck constant rather than a physical platinum-iridium cylinder kept in France since 1889. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same pound-to-kilogram factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
