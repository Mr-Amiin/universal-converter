Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the square foot (ft²), used to measure area.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the square meter (m²), used to measure area.

How do I convert square feet to square meters?

Multiply the number of square feet by 0.09290304 to get square meters. For example, 5 ft² × 0.09290304 = 0.464515 m².

Is the square foot to square meter conversion exact?

Yes. The foot is defined as exactly 0.3048 meters, so a square foot is exactly 0.09290304 square meters and the factor used here is exact, not rounded.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

It's commonly used in real-estate listings, flooring and construction material estimates, and converting imperial floor plans to the metric system.

Can I convert square meters back to square feet?

Yes. Multiply the square meter value by 10.76391042, or divide it by 0.09290304, to convert back to square feet.
