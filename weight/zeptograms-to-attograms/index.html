Frequently asked questions

How do I convert zeptogram to attogram?

Multiply the zeptogram value by 0.001. For example, 5 zg × 0.001 = 0.005 ag.

Why do zeptogram and attogram need converting at all?

Zepto (z) represents 10^-21, one of the smallest SI prefixes in practical use. Atto (a) represents 10^-18, used for atomic and subatomic-scale quantities.

Is the zeptogram to attogram conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between zeptogram and attogram is a fixed, defined ratio (× 0.001), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert attogram back to zeptogram?

Yes. Multiply the attogram value by 1000 (or divide by 0.001) to get zeptogram.
