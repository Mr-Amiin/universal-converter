Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the megagram (Mg). Megagram is 1000000 gram units.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the femtogram (fg). Femtogram is 1e-15 gram units.

How do I convert megagram to femtogram?

Multiply the megagram value by 1.000000e+21. For example, 5 Mg × 1.000000e+21 = 5.000000e+21 fg.

Why do megagram and femtogram need converting at all?

Mega (M) represents 10^6, one of the most commonly used SI prefixes. Femto (f) represents 10^-15, used in particle physics and ultrafast optics.

Is the megagram to femtogram conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between megagram and femtogram is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e+21), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert femtogram back to megagram?

Yes. Multiply the femtogram value by 1.000000e-21 (or divide by 1.000000e+21) to get megagram.
