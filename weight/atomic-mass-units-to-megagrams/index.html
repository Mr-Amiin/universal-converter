How do I convert atomic mass units to megagrams?

Multiply the number of atomic mass units by 1.6605390666e-30 to get the equivalent value in megagrams. For example, 5 u × 1.6605390666e-30 = 8.302695e-30 Mg.

Is the atomic mass unit to megagram conversion exact?

Can I convert megagrams back to atomic mass units?

Yes. Multiply the megagrams value by 6.02214076208e+29, or divide it by 1.6605390666e-30, to convert back to atomic mass units.
