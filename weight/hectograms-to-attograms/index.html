Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the hectogram (hg). Hectogram is 100 gram units.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the attogram (ag). Attogram is 1e-18 gram units.

How do I convert hectogram to attogram?

Multiply the hectogram value by 1.000000e+20. For example, 5 hg × 1.000000e+20 = 5.000000e+20 ag.

Why do hectogram and attogram need converting at all?

Hecto (h) represents 10^2 and is rarely used outside the hectare and hectopascal. Atto (a) represents 10^-18, used for atomic and subatomic-scale quantities.

Is the hectogram to attogram conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between hectogram and attogram is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e+20), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert attogram back to hectogram?

Yes. Multiply the attogram value by 1.000000e-20 (or divide by 1.000000e+20) to get hectogram.
