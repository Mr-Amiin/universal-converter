Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the nanogram (ng). Nanogram is 1e-9 gram units.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the milligram (mg). Milligram is 0.001 gram units.

How do I convert nanogram to milligram?

Multiply the nanogram value by 1.000000e-06. For example, 5 ng × 1.000000e-06 = 5.000000e-06 mg.

Why do nanogram and milligram need converting at all?

Nano (n) represents 10^-9, familiar from nanotechnology and nanometers. Milli (m) represents 10^-3, one of the most common small-scale SI prefixes.

Is the nanogram to milligram conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between nanogram and milligram is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-06), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert milligram back to nanogram?

Yes. Multiply the milligram value by 1000000 (or divide by 1.000000e-06) to get nanogram.

About this conversion
