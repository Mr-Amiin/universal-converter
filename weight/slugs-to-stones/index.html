Why do slug and stone need converting at all?

The slug is the imperial unit of mass consistent with pounds-force, pounds, and feet in engineering calculations. The stone (14 pounds) remains a common way to state body weight in the United Kingdom and Ireland.

Can I convert stone back to slug?
