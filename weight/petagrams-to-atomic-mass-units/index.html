Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the petagram (Pg). Petagram is 1000000000000000 gram units.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the atomic mass unit (u). The unified atomic mass unit is used in atomic physics.

How do I convert petagram to atomic mass unit?

Multiply the petagram value by 6.022141e+38. For example, 5 Pg × 6.022141e+38 = 3.011070e+39 u.

Why do petagram and atomic mass unit need converting at all?

Peta (P) represents 10^15, familiar from petabyte-scale data storage. The atomic mass unit (also called the dalton) is defined as 1/12 the mass of a carbon-12 atom, used for atomic and molecular masses.

Is the petagram to atomic mass unit conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between petagram and atomic mass unit is a fixed, defined ratio (× 6.022141e+38), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert atomic mass unit back to petagram?

Yes. Multiply the atomic mass unit value by 1.660539e-39 (or divide by 6.022141e+38) to get petagram.
