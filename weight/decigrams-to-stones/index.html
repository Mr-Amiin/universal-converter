Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the decigram (dg). Decigram is 0.1 gram units.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the stone (st). A stone is 14 pounds.

How do I convert decigram to stone?

Multiply the decigram value by 1.600000e-05. For example, 5 dg × 1.600000e-05 = 8.000000e-05 st.

Why do decigram and stone need converting at all?

Deci (d) represents 10^-1, familiar from the decibel and deciliter. The stone (14 pounds) remains a common way to state body weight in the United Kingdom and Ireland.

Is the decigram to stone conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between decigram and stone is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.600000e-05), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert stone back to decigram?

Yes. Multiply the stone value by 62500 (or divide by 1.600000e-05) to get decigram.
