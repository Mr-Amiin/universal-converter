Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the parsecs (pc).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the miles (mi).

How do I convert parsecs to miles?

Multiply the parsecs value by 1.917400e+13. For example, 5 pc × 1.917400e+13 = 9.587000e+13 mi.

Why do parsecs and miles need converting at all?

A parsec (about 3.26 light-years) is defined via stellar parallax and is common in professional astronomy. The international mile is defined as exactly 1,609.344 meters, based on 5,280 feet.

Is the parsecs to miles conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between parsecs and miles is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.917400e+13), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert miles back to parsecs?

Yes. Multiply the miles value by 5.215396e-14 (or divide by 1.917400e+13) to get parsecs.

About this conversion

This page converts parsecs (pc) into miles (mi) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 pc = 1.917400e+13 mi.

Worked example: to convert 10 pc to mi, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1.917400e+13 = 1.917400e+14 mi.

A 5-kilometer run measured in parsecs converts to about 9.587000e+13 mi.

Reverse conversion: to go from miles back to parsecs, divide by 1.917400e+13 (equivalently, multiply by 5.215396e-14).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
