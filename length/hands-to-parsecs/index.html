Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the hands (hh).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the parsecs (pc).

How do I convert hands to parsecs?

Multiply the hands value by 3.292600e-18. For example, 5 hh × 3.292600e-18 = 1.646300e-17 pc.

Why do hands and parsecs need converting at all?

Hands is a standard unit used in this category's conversions. A parsec (about 3.26 light-years) is defined via stellar parallax and is common in professional astronomy.

Is the hands to parsecs conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between hands and parsecs is a fixed, defined ratio (× 3.292600e-18), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert parsecs back to hands?

Yes. Multiply the parsecs value by 3.037114e+17 (or divide by 3.292600e-18) to get hands.

About this conversion

This page converts hands (hh) into parsecs (pc) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 hh = 3.292600e-18 pc.

Worked example: to convert 10 hh to pc, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 3.292600e-18 = 3.292600e-17 pc.

A 5-kilometer run measured in hands converts to about 1.646300e-17 pc.

Reverse conversion: to go from parsecs back to hands, divide by 3.292600e-18 (equivalently, multiply by 3.037114e+17).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
