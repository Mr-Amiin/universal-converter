Frequently asked questions

How many petameters are in one centimeter?

One Centimeter equals 1×10^-17 petameters.

How do I convert centimeters to petameters?

Multiply the number of centimeters by 1×10^-17 to get petameters. To go the other way, multiply petameters by 1×10^17 (or divide by 1×10^-17) to get centimeters.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between centimeters and petameters, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

When is centimeter to petameter conversion commonly used?

It is commonly used in everyday metric measurements, construction and design, as well as geography, cartography and large-scale scientific measurement.

About this conversion

Centimeters and petameters are both units used to measure length. A centimeter equals 0.01 meters. A petameter equals 1e+15 meters. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 centimeter = 1×10^-17 petameters (equivalently 1 petameter = 1×10^17 centimeters), so results stay accurate for everyday metric measurements, construction and design, as well as geography, cartography and large-scale scientific measurement.
