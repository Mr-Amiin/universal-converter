Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the gigameters (Gm).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the parsecs (pc).

How do I convert gigameters to parsecs?

Multiply the gigameters value by 3.240800e-08. For example, 5 Gm × 3.240800e-08 = 1.620400e-07 pc.

Why do gigameters and parsecs need converting at all?

Giga (G) represents 10^9, familiar from gigabyte and gigahertz measurements. A parsec (about 3.26 light-years) is defined via stellar parallax and is common in professional astronomy.

Is the gigameters to parsecs conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between gigameters and parsecs is a fixed, defined ratio (× 3.240800e-08), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert parsecs back to gigameters?

Yes. Multiply the parsecs value by 30856578.6225623302 (or divide by 3.240800e-08) to get gigameters.

About this conversion

This page converts gigameters (Gm) into parsecs (pc) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 Gm = 3.240800e-08 pc.

Worked example: to convert 10 Gm to pc, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 3.240800e-08 = 3.240800e-07 pc.

A 5-kilometer run measured in gigameters converts to about 1.620400e-07 pc.

Reverse conversion: to go from parsecs back to gigameters, divide by 3.240800e-08 (equivalently, multiply by 30856578.6225623302).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
