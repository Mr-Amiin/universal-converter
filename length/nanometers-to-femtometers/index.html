Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the nanometers (nm).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the femtometers (fm).

How do I convert nanometers to femtometers?

Multiply the nanometers value by 1000000. For example, 5 nm × 1000000 = 5000000 fm.

Why do nanometers and femtometers need converting at all?

Nano (n) represents 10^-9, familiar from nanotechnology and nanometers. Femto (f) represents 10^-15, used in particle physics and ultrafast optics.

Is the nanometers to femtometers conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between nanometers and femtometers is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1000000), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert femtometers back to nanometers?

Yes. Multiply the femtometers value by 1.000000e-06 (or divide by 1000000) to get nanometers.

About this conversion

This page converts nanometers (nm) into femtometers (fm) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 nm = 1000000 fm.

Worked example: to convert 10 nm to fm, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1000000 = 10000000 fm.

A 5-kilometer run measured in nanometers converts to about 5000000 fm.

Reverse conversion: to go from femtometers back to nanometers, divide by 1000000 (equivalently, multiply by 1.000000e-06).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
