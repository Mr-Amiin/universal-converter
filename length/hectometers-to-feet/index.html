Frequently asked questions

How many feet are in one hectometer?

One Hectometer equals 328.0839895 feet.

How do I convert hectometers to feet?

Multiply the number of hectometers by 328.0839895 to get feet. To go the other way, multiply feet by 0.003048 (or divide by 328.0839895) to get hectometers.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between hectometers and feet, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

When is hectometer to foot conversion commonly used?

About this conversion

Hectometers and feet are both units used to measure length. A hectometer equals 100 meters. A foot is exactly 0.3048 meters, divided into 12 inches. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 hectometer = 328.0839895 feet (equivalently 1 foot = 0.003048 hectometers), so results stay accurate for everyday metric measurements, construction and design, as well as construction, carpentry, real estate and everyday US and UK measurements.
