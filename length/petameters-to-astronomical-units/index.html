About this conversion

Petameters and astronomical units are both units used to measure length. A petameter equals 1e+15 meters. An astronomical unit is the mean Earth-Sun distance, about 149.6 million kilometers. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 petameter = 6684.587122 astronomical units (equivalently 1 astronomical unit = 0.0001495978707 petameters), so results stay accurate for geography, cartography and large-scale scientific measurement, as well as astronomy and measuring distances within the solar system.
