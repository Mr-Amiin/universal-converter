Frequently asked questions

How many hectometers are in one femtometer?

One Femtometer equals 1×10^-17 hectometers.

How do I convert femtometers to hectometers?

Multiply the number of femtometers by 1×10^-17 to get hectometers. To go the other way, multiply hectometers by 1×10^17 (or divide by 1×10^-17) to get femtometers.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between femtometers and hectometers, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

When is femtometer to hectometer conversion commonly used?

It is commonly used in science, engineering and precision measurement at small scales, as well as everyday metric measurements, construction and design.

About this conversion

Femtometers and hectometers are both units used to measure length. A femtometer equals 1e-15 meters. A hectometer equals 100 meters. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 femtometer = 1×10^-17 hectometers (equivalently 1 hectometer = 1×10^17 femtometers), so results stay accurate for science, engineering and precision measurement at small scales, as well as everyday metric measurements, construction and design.
