Frequently asked questions

How many light-years are in one mile?

One Mile equals 1.70107795×10^-13 light-years.

How do I convert miles to light-years?

Multiply the number of miles by 1.70107795×10^-13 to get light-years. To go the other way, multiply light-years by 5.878625373×10^12 (or divide by 1.70107795×10^-13) to get miles.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between miles and light-years, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

When is mile to light-year conversion commonly used?

It is commonly used in road distances, sports and everyday travel measurements, as well as astronomy and measuring distances between stars.

About this conversion

Miles and light-years are both units used to measure length. A statute mile is exactly 1,609.344 meters. A light-year is the distance light travels in a Julian year, roughly 9.46 trillion kilometers. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 mile = 1.70107795×10^-13 light-years (equivalently 1 light-year = 5.878625373×10^12 miles), so results stay accurate for road distances, sports and everyday travel measurements, as well as astronomy and measuring distances between stars.
