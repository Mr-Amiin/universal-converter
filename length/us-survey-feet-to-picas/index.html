About this conversion

US survey feet and picas are both units used to measure length. The US survey foot is 1200/3937 meters, retained for legacy land records. A pica is 12 points, a traditional typesetting measurement. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 US survey foot = 72.00014401 picas (equivalently 1 pica = 0.01388886111 US survey feet), so results stay accurate for legacy US land surveying and cadastral records, as well as typography, graphic design and desktop publishing.
