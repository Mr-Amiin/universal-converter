Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the fathoms (ftm).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the decimeters (dm).

How do I convert fathoms to decimeters?

Multiply the fathoms value by 18.288. For example, 5 ftm × 18.288 = 91.44 dm.

Why do fathoms and decimeters need converting at all?

Fathoms is a standard unit used in this category's conversions. Deci (d) represents 10^-1, familiar from the decibel and deciliter.

Is the fathoms to decimeters conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between fathoms and decimeters is a fixed, defined ratio (× 18.288), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert decimeters back to fathoms?

Yes. Multiply the decimeters value by 0.0546806649 (or divide by 18.288) to get fathoms.

About this conversion

This page converts fathoms (ftm) into decimeters (dm) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 ftm = 18.288 dm.

Worked example: to convert 10 ftm to dm, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 18.288 = 182.88 dm.

A 5-kilometer run measured in fathoms converts to about 91.44 dm.

Reverse conversion: to go from decimeters back to fathoms, divide by 18.288 (equivalently, multiply by 0.0546806649).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
