Frequently asked questions

How many parsecs are in one nautical mile?

One Nautical mile equals 6.001923244×10^-14 parsecs.

How do I convert nautical miles to parsecs?

Multiply the number of nautical miles by 6.001923244×10^-14 to get parsecs. To go the other way, multiply parsecs by 1.666132603×10^13 (or divide by 6.001923244×10^-14) to get nautical miles.

Is this conversion exact?

About this conversion

Nautical miles and parsecs are both units used to measure length. A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 meters, based on the Earth's circumference. A parsec is about 3.26156 light-years, based on stellar parallax. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 nautical mile = 6.001923244×10^-14 parsecs (equivalently 1 parsec = 1.666132603×10^13 nautical miles), so results stay accurate for marine navigation, aviation and maritime charts, as well as professional astronomy and measuring galactic distances.
