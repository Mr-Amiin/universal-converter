Frequently asked questions

How many yoctometers are in one petameter?

One Petameter equals 1×10^39 yoctometers.

How do I convert petameters to yoctometers?

Multiply the number of petameters by 1×10^39 to get yoctometers. To go the other way, multiply yoctometers by 1×10^-39 (or divide by 1×10^39) to get petameters.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between petameters and yoctometers, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

When is petameter to yoctometer conversion commonly used?

It is commonly used in geography, cartography and large-scale scientific measurement, as well as science, engineering and precision measurement at small scales.

About this conversion

Petameters and yoctometers are both units used to measure length. A petameter equals 1e+15 meters. A yoctometer equals 1e-24 meters. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 petameter = 1×10^39 yoctometers (equivalently 1 yoctometer = 1×10^-39 petameters), so results stay accurate for geography, cartography and large-scale scientific measurement, as well as science, engineering and precision measurement at small scales.
