Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the nautical miles (nmi).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the inches (in).

How do I convert nautical miles to inches?

Multiply the nautical miles value by 72913.385827. For example, 5 nmi × 72913.385827 = 364566.929135 in.

Why do nautical miles and inches need converting at all?

The nautical mile (1,852 meters) is based on one minute of latitude and is standard in marine and air navigation. The inch is defined as exactly 2.54 centimeters under the international yard-and-pound agreement.

Is the nautical miles to inches conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between nautical miles and inches is a fixed, defined ratio (× 72913.385827), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert inches back to nautical miles?

Yes. Multiply the inches value by 1.371490e-05 (or divide by 72913.385827) to get nautical miles.
