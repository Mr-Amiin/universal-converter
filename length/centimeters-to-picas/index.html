How do I convert centimeters to picas?

About this conversion

Centimeters and picas are both units used to measure length. A centimeter equals 0.01 meters. A pica is 12 points, a traditional typesetting measurement. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 centimeter = 2.362204725 picas (equivalently 1 pica = 0.4233333333 centimeters), so results stay accurate for everyday metric measurements, construction and design, as well as typography, graphic design and desktop publishing.
