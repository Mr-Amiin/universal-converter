Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the foot (ft). The foot is a customary unit of length equal to 12 inches.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the centimeter (cm). The centimeter is one hundredth of a meter.

How do I convert foot to centimeter?

Multiply the foot value by 30.48. For example, 5 ft × 30.48 = 152.4 cm.

Why do foot and centimeter need converting at all?

The international foot has been defined as exactly 0.3048 meters since 1959. Centi (c) represents 10^-2, familiar from the centimeter.

Is the foot to centimeter conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between foot and centimeter is a fixed, defined ratio (× 30.48), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert centimeter back to foot?

Yes. Multiply the centimeter value by 0.032808399 (or divide by 30.48) to get foot.

About this conversion

This page converts foot (ft) into centimeter (cm) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 ft = 30.48 cm.

Worked example: to convert 10 ft to cm, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 30.48 = 304.8 cm.

A 5-kilometer run measured in foot converts to about 152.4 cm.

Reverse conversion: to go from centimeter back to foot, divide by 30.48 (equivalently, multiply by 0.032808399).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.

Foot: The foot is a customary unit of length equal to 12 inches. Centimeter: The centimeter is one hundredth of a meter.

Background: The international foot has been defined as exactly 0.3048 meters since 1959.
