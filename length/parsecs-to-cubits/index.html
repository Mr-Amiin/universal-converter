About this conversion

Parsecs and cubits are both units used to measure length. A parsec is about 3.26156 light-years, based on stellar parallax. A common cubit is approximated as 18 inches, based on the length of a forearm. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 parsec = 6.749076075×10^16 cubits (equivalently 1 cubit = 1.481684291×10^-17 parsecs), so results stay accurate for professional astronomy and measuring galactic distances, as well as historical, biblical and archaeological measurements.
