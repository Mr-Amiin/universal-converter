About this conversion

This page converts leagues (lea) into furlongs (fur) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 lea = 24 fur.

Worked example: to convert 10 lea to fur, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 24 = 240 fur.

A 5-kilometer run measured in leagues converts to about 120 fur.

Reverse conversion: to go from furlongs back to leagues, divide by 24 (equivalently, multiply by 0.0416666667).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
