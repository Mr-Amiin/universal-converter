How do I convert us survey feet to light years?

Multiply the us survey feet value by 3.221700e-17. For example, 5 ftUS × 3.221700e-17 = 1.610850e-16 ly.

Why do us survey feet and light years need converting at all?

Us Survey Feet is a standard unit used in this category's conversions. A light-year is the distance light travels in a Julian year, used for interstellar distances.

Can I convert light years back to us survey feet?

About this conversion

Worked example: to convert 10 ftUS to ly, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 3.221700e-17 = 3.221700e-16 ly.

Reverse conversion: to go from light years back to us survey feet, divide by 3.221700e-17 (equivalently, multiply by 3.103951e+16).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
