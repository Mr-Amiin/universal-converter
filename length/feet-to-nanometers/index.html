Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the feet (ft).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the nanometers (nm).

How do I convert feet to nanometers?

Multiply the feet value by 304800000. For example, 5 ft × 304800000 = 1524000000 nm.

Why do feet and nanometers need converting at all?

The international foot has been defined as exactly 0.3048 meters since 1959. Nano (n) represents 10^-9, familiar from nanotechnology and nanometers.

Is the feet to nanometers conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between feet and nanometers is a fixed, defined ratio (× 304800000), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert nanometers back to feet?

Yes. Multiply the nanometers value by 3.280840e-09 (or divide by 304800000) to get feet.

About this conversion

This page converts feet (ft) into nanometers (nm) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 ft = 304800000 nm.

Worked example: to convert 10 ft to nm, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 304800000 = 3048000000 nm.

A 5-kilometer run measured in feet converts to about 1524000000 nm.

Reverse conversion: to go from nanometers back to feet, divide by 304800000 (equivalently, multiply by 3.280840e-09).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
