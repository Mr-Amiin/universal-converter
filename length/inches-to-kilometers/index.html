How do I convert inches to kilometers?

About this conversion

Inches and kilometers are both units used to measure length. An inch is exactly 0.0254 meters, traditionally the width of a thumb. A kilometer equals 1,000 meters. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 inch = 0.0000254 kilometers (equivalently 1 kilometer = 39370.07874 inches), so results stay accurate for construction, carpentry, electronics and everyday US and UK measurements, as well as geography, cartography and large-scale scientific measurement.
