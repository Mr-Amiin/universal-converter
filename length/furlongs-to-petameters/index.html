Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the furlongs (fur).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the petameters (Pm).

How do I convert furlongs to petameters?

Multiply the furlongs value by 2.011700e-13. For example, 5 fur × 2.011700e-13 = 1.005850e-12 Pm.

Why do furlongs and petameters need converting at all?

Furlongs is a standard unit used in this category's conversions. Peta (P) represents 10^15, familiar from petabyte-scale data storage.

Is the furlongs to petameters conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between furlongs and petameters is a fixed, defined ratio (× 2.011700e-13), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert petameters back to furlongs?

Yes. Multiply the petameters value by 4.970920e+12 (or divide by 2.011700e-13) to get furlongs.

About this conversion

This page converts furlongs (fur) into petameters (Pm) within the length converter, using the fixed relationship 1 fur = 2.011700e-13 Pm.

Worked example: to convert 10 fur to Pm, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 2.011700e-13 = 2.011700e-12 Pm.

A 5-kilometer run measured in furlongs converts to about 1.005850e-12 Pm.

Reverse conversion: to go from petameters back to furlongs, divide by 2.011700e-13 (equivalently, multiply by 4.970920e+12).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
