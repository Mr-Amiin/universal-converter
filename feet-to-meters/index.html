Frequently asked questions

How do you convert foot to meter?

Use the formula m = ft × 0.3048. One foot equals 0.3048 meters.

What is Foot?

The foot is a customary unit of length equal to 12 inches.

What is Meter?

The meter (m) is the SI base unit of length.

Is the foot to meter conversion exact?

Yes. Foot and Meter are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

How do I convert meter back to foot?

Use the reverse formula ft = m ÷ 0.3048. This site's reverse converter does the same calculation automatically.

About this conversion

This length converter turns foot into meter using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. A 10-foot ceiling is about 3.05 meters high. The foot dates to antiquity and was based on human foot length before being fixed at exactly 0.3048 meters in the 1959 international yard-and-pound agreement. The meter was originally defined in 1793 as one ten-millionth of the distance from the equator to the North Pole; today it's defined by the speed of light. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same foot-to-meter factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
