About Converting Calorie (thermochemical) to Joules

Calorie (thermochemical) and joule are both units of energy. The thermochemical calorie is exactly 4.184 joules. The joule is the SI unit of energy: one newton-meter.

Formula

J = cal th × 4.184

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 calorie (thermochemical) equals 4.184 joules, and the reverse factor is 0.2390057361.

Simple example

1 cal th × 4.184 = 4.184 J
