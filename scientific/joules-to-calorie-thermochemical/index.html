About Converting Joules to Calorie (thermochemical)

Joule and calorie (thermochemical) are both units of energy. The joule is the SI unit of energy: one newton-meter. The thermochemical calorie is exactly 4.184 joules.

Formula

cal th = J × 0.2390057361

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 joule equals 0.2390057361 calories (thermochemical), and the reverse factor is 4.184.

Simple example

1 J × 0.2390057361 = 0.2390057361 cal th

1 joule = 0.2390057361 calories (thermochemical).
