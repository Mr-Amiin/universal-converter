About Converting Pascal-seconds to Poise

Pascal-second and poise are both units of dynamic viscosity. The pascal-second is the SI unit of dynamic viscosity. The poise is the CGS unit of dynamic viscosity: 0.1 pascal-second.

Formula

P = Pa·s × 10

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 pascal-second equals 10 poises, and the reverse factor is 0.1.

Simple example

1 Pa·s × 10 = 10 P

1 pascal-second = 10 poises.

Real-world example

10 Pa·s × 10 = 100 P

10 pascal-seconds = 100 poises.
