About Converting Candelas per Square Meter to Nits

Candela per square meter and nit are both units of luminance. The candela per square meter is the SI unit of luminance, also called the nit. One nit is one candela per square meter.

Formula

nit = cd/m² × 1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 candela per square meter equals 1 nits, and the reverse factor is 1.

Simple example

1 cd/m² × 1 = 1 nit

1 candela per square meter = 1 nits.

Real-world example

10 cd/m² × 1 = 10 nit

10 candelas per square meter = 10 nits.
