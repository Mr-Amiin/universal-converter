About Converting Gauss to Tesla

Gauss and tesla are both units of magnetic flux density. The gauss is the CGS unit of magnetic flux density: 10⁻⁴ tesla. The tesla is the SI unit of magnetic flux density.

Formula

T = G × 0.0001

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 gauss equals 0.0001 teslas, and the reverse factor is 10000.

Simple example

1 G × 0.0001 = 0.0001 T

1 gauss = 0.0001 teslas.

Real-world example

10 G × 0.0001 = 0.001 T

10 gauss = 0.001 teslas.
