About Converting Svedberg to Seconds

Svedberg and second are both units of time. The svedberg is 10⁻¹³ seconds, the unit of sedimentation coefficient. The second is the SI base unit of time.

Formula

s = S × 1 × 10⁻¹³

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 svedberg equals 1 × 10⁻¹³ seconds, and the reverse factor is 1 × 10¹³.

Simple example

1 S × 1 × 10⁻¹³ = 1 × 10⁻¹³ s

1 svedberg = 1 × 10⁻¹³ seconds.

Real-world example

10 S × 1 × 10⁻¹³ = 1 × 10⁻¹² s

10 svedbergs = 1 × 10⁻¹² seconds.
