About Converting Dynes to Newtons

Dyne and newton are both units of force. The dyne is the CGS unit of force: 10⁻⁵ newton. The newton is the SI unit of force.

Formula

N = dyn × 0.00001

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 dyne equals 0.00001 newtons, and the reverse factor is 100000.

Simple example

1 dyn × 0.00001 = 0.00001 N

1 dyne = 0.00001 newtons.

Real-world example

10 dyn × 0.00001 = 0.0001 N

10 dynes = 0.0001 newtons.
