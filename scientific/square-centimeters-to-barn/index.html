About Converting Square Centimeters to Barn

Square centimeter and barn are both units of area. One square centimeter is 10⁻⁴ square meters. The barn is 10⁻²⁸ square meters, used for nuclear cross-sections.

Formula

b = cm² × 1 × 10²⁴

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 square centimeter equals 1 × 10²⁴ barns, and the reverse factor is 1 × 10⁻²⁴.

Simple example

1 cm² × 1 × 10²⁴ = 1 × 10²⁴ b

1 square centimeter = 1 × 10²⁴ barns.

Real-world example

10 cm² × 1 × 10²⁴ = 1 × 10²⁵ b

10 square centimeters = 1 × 10²⁵ barns.
