About Converting Debye to Coulomb-meters

Debye and coulomb-meter are both units of dipole moment. The debye is the CGS-based unit of electric dipole moment: 10⁻²¹/c coulomb-meters ≈ 3.33564 × 10⁻³⁰ C·m. The coulomb-meter is the SI unit of electric dipole moment.

Formula

C·m = D × 3.335640952 × 10⁻³⁰

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 debye equals 3.335640952 × 10⁻³⁰ coulomb-meters, and the reverse factor is 2.99792458 × 10²⁹.

Simple example

1 D × 3.335640952 × 10⁻³⁰ = 3.335640952 × 10⁻³⁰ C·m

1 debye = 3.335640952 × 10⁻³⁰ coulomb-meters.

Real-world example

10 D × 3.335640952 × 10⁻³⁰ = 3.335640952 × 10⁻²⁹ C·m

10 debyes = 3.335640952 × 10⁻²⁹ coulomb-meters.
