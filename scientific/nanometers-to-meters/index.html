Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the nanometer (nm).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the meter (m).

How do I convert nanometer to meter?

Multiply the nanometer value by 1.000000e-09. For example, 5 nm × 1.000000e-09 = 5.000000e-09 m.

Why do nanometer and meter need converting at all?

Nano (n) represents 10^-9, familiar from nanotechnology and nanometers. The meter is the SI base unit of length, defined since 1983 by the distance light travels in 1/299,792,458 of a second.

Is the nanometer to meter conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between nanometer and meter is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-09), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert meter back to nanometer?

Yes. Multiply the meter value by 999999999.9999998808 (or divide by 1.000000e-09) to get nanometer.
