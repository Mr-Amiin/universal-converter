About Converting Reciprocal Centimeters (cm^-1) to Nanometers

This page converts reciprocal centimeters (cm⁻¹) to nanometers (nm). The reciprocal centimeter (wavenumber, cm⁻¹) counts waves per centimeter. As an energy equivalent, E = h·c·ν̃, so 1 cm⁻¹ ≙ 1.98644586 × 10⁻²³ J. A nanometer is 10⁻⁹ meters.

Formula

wavelength = 1 ÷ wavenumber

Wavelength is the reciprocal of wavenumber: divide 1 by the value (in cm⁻¹) to get centimeters, then scale to the target unit.

Convention: Energy conversions use E = h·c·ν̃ with the exact SI values of h and c. Wavenumber to wavelength uses λ = 1/ν̃ (a reciprocal relation, so zero wavenumber has no finite wavelength).

Standard and definition

The reciprocal centimeter is covered by NIST — CODATA Recommended Values of the Fundamental Physical Constants.

Uses of the reciprocal centimeter today

  • Infrared and Raman spectroscopy
  • Molecular vibrational and rotational energies

Understanding the Nanometer

Nanometer (nm) measures length. A nanometer is 10⁻⁹ meters.

Standard and definition

The nanometer is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the nanometer today

  • Wavelengths of light and semiconductor feature sizes
  • Nanotechnology and spectroscopy
