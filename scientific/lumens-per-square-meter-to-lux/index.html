About Converting Lumens per Square Meter to Lux

Lumen per square meter and lux are both units of illuminance. One lumen per square meter is exactly one lux. The lux is the SI unit of illuminance: one lumen per square meter.

Formula

lx = lm/m² × 1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 lumen per square meter equals 1 lux, and the reverse factor is 1.

Simple example

1 lm/m² × 1 = 1 lx

1 lumen per square meter = 1 lux.

Real-world example

10 lm/m² × 1 = 10 lx

10 lumens per square meter = 10 lux.
