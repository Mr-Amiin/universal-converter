What is an angstrom used for?

An angstrom (0.1 nanometers) is commonly used to describe atomic radii, bond lengths, and wavelengths of visible light.

What is an atomic mass unit?

The atomic mass unit (also called the dalton) is used to express the mass of atoms and molecules on an atomic scale.
