About Converting Lux to Foot-candles

Lux and foot-candle are both units of illuminance. The lux is the SI unit of illuminance: one lumen per square meter. One foot-candle is one lumen per square foot: 10.7639104 lux.

Formula

fc = lx × 0.09290304

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 lux equals 0.09290304 foot-candles, and the reverse factor is 10.76391042.

Simple example

1 lx × 0.09290304 = 0.09290304 fc

1 lux = 0.09290304 foot-candles.

Real-world example

10 lx × 0.09290304 = 0.9290304 fc

10 lux = 0.9290304 foot-candles.
