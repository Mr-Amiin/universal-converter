About Converting Nits to Foot-lamberts

Nit and foot-lambert are both units of luminance. One nit is one candela per square meter. One foot-lambert is (1/π) candela per square foot: 3.4262591 cd/m².

Formula

fL = nit × 0.291863508

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 nit equals 0.291863508 foot-lamberts, and the reverse factor is 3.4262591.
