About Converting Barn to Square Centimeters

Barn and square centimeter are both units of area. The barn is 10⁻²⁸ square meters, used for nuclear cross-sections. One square centimeter is 10⁻⁴ square meters.

Formula

cm² = b × 1 × 10⁻²⁴

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 barn equals 1 × 10⁻²⁴ square centimeters, and the reverse factor is 1 × 10²⁴.

Simple example

1 b × 1 × 10⁻²⁴ = 1 × 10⁻²⁴ cm²

1 barn = 1 × 10⁻²⁴ square centimeters.

Real-world example

10 b × 1 × 10⁻²⁴ = 1 × 10⁻²³ cm²

10 barns = 1 × 10⁻²³ square centimeters.
