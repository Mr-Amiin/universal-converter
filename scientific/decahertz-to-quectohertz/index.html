Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the decahertz (daHz).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the quectohertz (qHz).

How do I convert decahertz to quectohertz?

Multiply the decahertz value by 1.000000e+31. For example, 5 daHz × 1.000000e+31 = 5.000000e+31 qHz.

Why do decahertz and quectohertz need converting at all?

Deca (da) represents 10^1 and is one of the least commonly used SI prefixes. Quecto (q) represents 10^-30, the smallest SI prefix currently defined.

Is the decahertz to quectohertz conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between decahertz and quectohertz is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e+31), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert quectohertz back to decahertz?

Yes. Multiply the quectohertz value by 1.000000e-31 (or divide by 1.000000e+31) to get decahertz.
