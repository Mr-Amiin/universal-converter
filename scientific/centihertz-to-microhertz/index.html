Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the centihertz (cHz).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the microhertz (uHz).

How do I convert centihertz to microhertz?

Multiply the centihertz value by 10000. For example, 5 cHz × 10000 = 50000 uHz.

Why do centihertz and microhertz need converting at all?

Centi (c) represents 10^-2, familiar from the centimeter. Micro (µ) represents 10^-6, common in biology, chemistry and electronics.

Is the centihertz to microhertz conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between centihertz and microhertz is a fixed, defined ratio (× 10000), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert microhertz back to centihertz?

Yes. Multiply the microhertz value by 0.0001 (or divide by 10000) to get centihertz.

About this conversion

This page converts centihertz (cHz) into microhertz (uHz) within the scientific converter, using the fixed relationship 1 cHz = 10000 uHz.

Worked example: to convert 10 cHz to uHz, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 10000 = 100000 uHz.

Reverse conversion: to go from microhertz back to centihertz, divide by 10000 (equivalently, multiply by 0.0001).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
