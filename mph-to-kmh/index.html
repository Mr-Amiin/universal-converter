Frequently asked questions

How do you convert mile per hour to kilometer per hour?

Is the mile per hour to kilometer per hour conversion exact?

Yes. Mile per hour and Kilometer per hour are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

About this conversion

This speed converter turns mile per hour into kilometer per hour using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. A 60 mph highway speed limit is about 96.56 km/h. Miles per hour became the standard US speed measure alongside the growth of the automobile and highway system in the 20th century. Kilometers per hour is the standard speed unit in most countries that use the metric system for road signage. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same mile per hour-to-kilometer per hour factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
