Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is miles per hour (mph), a speed unit used mainly for road travel in the United States and the United Kingdom.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is kilometers per hour (km/h), the standard speed unit used in most of the world's road, rail, and weather systems.

How do I convert mph to km/h?

Multiply the mph value by 1.609344 to get km/h. For example, 60 mph × 1.609344 = 96.560640 km/h.

Is the mph to km/h conversion exact?

Yes. Since one mile is defined as exactly 1,609.344 meters, the factor 1.609344 is exact rather than rounded.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

It's used for road speed limits, vehicle speedometers, sports timing (running, cycling, motorsport), and weather reports involving wind speed.

Can I convert km/h back to mph?

Yes. Divide the km/h value by 1.609344, or use the km/h to mph converter directly.

About this conversion

This page converts miles per hour (mph), the speed unit used for road travel in the US and UK, into kilometers per hour (km/h), the metric speed unit used in most other countries.

This conversion is essential for drivers crossing between countries with different speedometer units, for international motorsport and cycling, and for interpreting weather and wind-speed reports.

The formula is km/h = mph × 1.609344, derived from the exact definition of the international mile as 1,609.344 meters.

The conversion is exact because both the mile and the hour are defined by fixed international standards, leaving no room for measurement error.

The kilometer per hour is the standard speed unit under the metric system used worldwide, while the mile per hour remains the everyday standard in the United States and United Kingdom.
