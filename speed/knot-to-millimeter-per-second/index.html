How do I convert knots to millimeters per second?

Multiply the knots value by 514.444444. For example, 5 kn × 514.444444 = 2572.22222 mm/s.

Why do knots and millimeters per second need converting at all?

The knot (one nautical mile per hour) is standard in maritime and aviation navigation. Milli (m) represents 10^-3, one of the most common small-scale SI prefixes.

Is the knots to millimeters per second conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between knots and millimeters per second is a fixed, defined ratio (× 514.444444), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert millimeters per second back to knots?

Yes. Multiply the millimeters per second value by 0.0019438445 (or divide by 514.444444) to get knots.

About this conversion

This page converts knots (kn) into millimeters per second (mm/s) within the speed converter, using the fixed relationship 1 kn = 514.444444 mm/s.

Worked example: to convert 10 kn to mm/s, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 514.444444 = 5144.44444 mm/s.

A car traveling at 100 kn is moving at about 51444.44 mm/s.

Reverse conversion: to go from millimeters per second back to knots, divide by 514.444444 (equivalently, multiply by 0.0019438445).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
