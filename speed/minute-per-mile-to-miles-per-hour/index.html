About Converting Minutes per Mile to Miles per Hour

Minute per mile and mile per hour are both units of speed. Minutes per mile is a pace: the time needed to cover one statute mile. It is the reciprocal of speed. One mile per hour is exactly 0.44704 meters per second (1 international mile per hour).

Formula

pace (min per distance) = 60 ÷ speed (distance per hour)

Pace and speed are reciprocals: divide 60 by the value, in matching distance units.

Simple example

3 min/mi = 20 mph

3 minutes per mile = 20 miles per hour.

Real-world example

5 min/mi = 12 mph

5 minutes per mile = 12 miles per hour.
