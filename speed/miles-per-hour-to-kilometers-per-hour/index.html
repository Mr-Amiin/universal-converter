About Converting Miles per Hour to Kilometers per Hour

Mile per hour and kilometer per hour are both units of speed. One mile per hour is exactly 0.44704 meters per second (1 international mile per hour). One kilometer per hour is exactly 1/3.6 meters per second.

Formula

km/h = mph × 1.609344

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 mile per hour equals 1.609344 kilometers per hour, and the reverse factor is 0.6213711922.

Simple example

1 mph × 1.609344 = 1.609344 km/h

1 mile per hour = 1.609344 kilometers per hour.

Real-world example

10 mph × 1.609344 = 16.09344 km/h

10 miles per hour = 16.09344 kilometers per hour.
