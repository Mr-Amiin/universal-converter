About this conversion

This page converts miles per minute (mi/min) into millimeters per day (mm/d) within the speed converter, using the fixed relationship 1 mi/min = 2317455000 mm/d.

Worked example: to convert 10 mi/min to mm/d, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 2317455000 = 23174550000 mm/d.

A car traveling at 100 mi/min is moving at about 231745500000 mm/d.

Reverse conversion: to go from millimeters per day back to miles per minute, divide by 2317455000 (equivalently, multiply by 4.315078e-10).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
