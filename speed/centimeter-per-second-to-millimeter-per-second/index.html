Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the centimeters per second (cm/s).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the millimeters per second (mm/s).

How do I convert centimeters per second to millimeters per second?

Multiply the centimeters per second value by 10. For example, 5 cm/s × 10 = 50 mm/s.

Why do centimeters per second and millimeters per second need converting at all?

Centi (c) represents 10^-2, familiar from the centimeter. Milli (m) represents 10^-3, one of the most common small-scale SI prefixes.

Is the centimeters per second to millimeters per second conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between centimeters per second and millimeters per second is a fixed, defined ratio (× 10), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert millimeters per second back to centimeters per second?

Yes. Multiply the millimeters per second value by 0.1 (or divide by 10) to get centimeters per second.

About this conversion

This page converts centimeters per second (cm/s) into millimeters per second (mm/s) within the speed converter, using the fixed relationship 1 cm/s = 10 mm/s.

Worked example: to convert 10 cm/s to mm/s, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 10 = 100 mm/s.

A car traveling at 100 cm/s is moving at about 1000 mm/s.

Reverse conversion: to go from millimeters per second back to centimeters per second, divide by 10 (equivalently, multiply by 0.1).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
