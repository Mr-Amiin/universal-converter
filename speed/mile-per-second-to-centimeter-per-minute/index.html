About this conversion

This page converts miles per second (mi/s) into centimeters per minute (cm/min) within the speed converter, using the fixed relationship 1 mi/s = 9656064 cm/min.

Worked example: to convert 10 mi/s to cm/min, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 9656064 = 96560640 cm/min.

A car traveling at 100 mi/s is moving at about 965606400 cm/min.

Reverse conversion: to go from centimeters per minute back to miles per second, divide by 9656064 (equivalently, multiply by 1.035619e-07).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
