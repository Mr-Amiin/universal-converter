About this conversion

Worked example: to convert 10 ft/d to yd/s, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 4.000000e-06 = 4.000000e-05 yd/s.

A car traveling at 100 ft/d is moving at about 0 yd/s.

Reverse conversion: to go from yards per second back to feet per day, divide by 4.000000e-06 (equivalently, multiply by 250000).
