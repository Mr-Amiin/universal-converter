About this conversion

This page converts yards per hour (yd/h) into feet per minute (ft/min) within the speed converter, using the fixed relationship 1 yd/h = 0.05 ft/min.

Worked example: to convert 10 yd/h to ft/min, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 0.05 = 0.5 ft/min.

A car traveling at 100 yd/h is moving at about 5 ft/min.

Reverse conversion: to go from feet per minute back to yards per hour, divide by 0.05 (equivalently, multiply by 20).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
