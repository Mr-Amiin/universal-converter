Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the foot (ft), used to measure length.

How do I convert meters to feet?

Multiply the number of meters by 3.280839895 to get feet. For example, 5 m × 3.280839895 = 16.404199 ft.

Is the meter to foot conversion exact?

Yes. The foot is defined as exactly 0.3048 meters, so the meter-to-foot factor 3.280839895013123 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

It's commonly used in construction and architecture, height measurements, and converting metric building plans to imperial units.

Can I convert feet back to meters?

Yes. Multiply the foot value by 0.3048, or divide it by 3.280839895, to convert back to meters.
