Frequently asked questions

How do you convert fahrenheit to celsius?

Use the formula °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Apply °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9: for example, 0°F converts to a very different number in celsius because the two scales don't share a zero point.

What is Fahrenheit?

Fahrenheit is a temperature scale used mainly in the United States.

What is Celsius?

Celsius is a temperature scale based on the freezing and boiling points of water.

Is the fahrenheit to celsius conversion exact?

Yes. Fahrenheit and Celsius are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

How do I convert celsius back to fahrenheit?

Use the reverse formula °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. This site's reverse converter does the same calculation automatically.

About this conversion

This temperature converter turns fahrenheit into celsius using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. Normal body temperature, 98.6°F, is about 37°C. Daniel Gabriel Fahrenheit devised the scale around 1724, anchoring it to a brine freezing mixture and, approximately, human body temperature. Anders Celsius proposed the scale in 1742, originally inverted (0 for boiling, 100 for freezing) before it was flipped to today's convention. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same fahrenheit-to-celsius factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
