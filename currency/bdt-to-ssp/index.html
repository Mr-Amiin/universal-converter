How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many South Sudanese Pounds (SSP) one Bangladeshi Taka (BDT) is worth. Multiply a BDT amount by 21.7616580311 to get the equivalent in SSP. To go the other way, multiply a SSP amount by 0.045952381. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
