How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Hungarian Forints (HUF) one Indian Rupee (INR) is worth. Multiply a INR amount by 4.3321299639 to get the equivalent in HUF. To go the other way, multiply a HUF amount by 0.2308333333. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
