How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many New Zealand Dollars (NZD) one CFP Franc (XPF) is worth. Multiply a XPF amount by 0.0151833333 to get the equivalent in NZD. To go the other way, multiply a NZD amount by 65.8616904501. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
