How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Congolese Francs (CDF) one Hong Kong Dollar (HKD) is worth. Multiply a HKD amount by 360.5633802817 to get the equivalent in CDF. To go the other way, multiply a CDF amount by 0.0027734375. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
