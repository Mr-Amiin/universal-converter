How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Samoan Talas (WST) one Seychellois Rupee (SCR) is worth. Multiply a SCR amount by 0.1956164384 to get the equivalent in WST. To go the other way, multiply a WST amount by 5.1120448179. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
