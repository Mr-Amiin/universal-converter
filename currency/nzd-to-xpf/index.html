How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many CFP Francs (XPF) one New Zealand Dollar (NZD) is worth. Multiply a NZD amount by 65.8616904501 to get the equivalent in XPF. To go the other way, multiply a XPF amount by 0.0151833333. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
