How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) one Canadian Dollar (CAD) is worth. Multiply a CAD amount by 221.2121212121 to get the equivalent in LKR. To go the other way, multiply a LKR amount by 0.0045205479. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
