How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Angolan Kwanzas (AOA) one Argentine Peso (ARS) is worth. Multiply a ARS amount by 0.9541284404 to get the equivalent in AOA. To go the other way, multiply a AOA amount by 1.0480769231. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
