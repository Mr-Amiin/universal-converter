How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Gibraltar Pounds (GIP) one Singapore Dollar (SGD) is worth. Multiply a SGD amount by 0.5866141732 to get the equivalent in GIP. To go the other way, multiply a GIP amount by 1.7046979866. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
