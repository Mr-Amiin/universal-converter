How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Kuwaiti Dinars (KWD) one Iraqi Dinar (IQD) is worth. Multiply a IQD amount by 0.0002345527 to get the equivalent in KWD. To go the other way, multiply a KWD amount by 4263.4338138925. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
