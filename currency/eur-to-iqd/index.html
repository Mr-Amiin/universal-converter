How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Iraqi Dinars (IQD) one Euro (EUR) is worth. Multiply a EUR amount by 1424.6395806029 to get the equivalent in IQD. To go the other way, multiply a IQD amount by 0.0007019319. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
