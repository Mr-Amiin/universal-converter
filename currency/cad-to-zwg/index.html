How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Zimbabwe Golds (ZWG) one Canadian Dollar (CAD) is worth. Multiply a CAD amount by 19.7297297297 to get the equivalent in ZWG. To go the other way, multiply a ZWG amount by 0.0506849315. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
