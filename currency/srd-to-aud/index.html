How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Australian Dollars (AUD) one Surinamese Dollar (SRD) is worth. Multiply a SRD amount by 0.0427272727 to get the equivalent in AUD. To go the other way, multiply a AUD amount by 23.4042553191. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
