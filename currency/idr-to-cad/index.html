How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Canadian Dollars (CAD) one Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) is worth. Multiply a IDR amount by 0.0000849315 to get the equivalent in CAD. To go the other way, multiply a CAD amount by 11774.1935483871. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
