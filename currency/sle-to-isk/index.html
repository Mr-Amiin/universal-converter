How the formula works

This reference rate expresses how many Icelandic Kronas (ISK) one Sierra Leonean Leone (SLE) is worth. Multiply a SLE amount by 5.9834938102 to get the equivalent in ISK. To go the other way, multiply a ISK amount by 0.1671264368. Live market rates move constantly, so treat this figure as an offline reference point rather than a real-time quote.
