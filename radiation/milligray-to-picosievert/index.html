How many Picosieverts are in one Milligray?

One Milligray equals 1e+9 Picosieverts.

How do I convert Milligray to Picosievert?

Multiply the number of Milligrays by 1e+9 to get Picosieverts. To go the other way, multiply Picosieverts by 1e-9 (or divide by 1e+9) to get Milligrays.

About this conversion

Milligray and Picosievert are units used in the radiation family of measurements. Milligray measures absorbed radiation dose (energy deposited per unit mass), while Picosievert measures equivalent radiation dose (biological risk-weighted dose). This page provides the direct numeric ratio between the two units. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 Milligray = 1e+9 Picosieverts (equivalently 1 Picosievert = 1e-9 Milligrays), so results stay accurate for nuclear medicine, radiation safety, health physics, and scientific research.
