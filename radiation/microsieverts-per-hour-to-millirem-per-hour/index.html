About Converting Microsieverts per Hour to Millirem per Hour

Microsievert per hour and millirem per hour are both units of equivalent dose rate. A microsievert per hour is an equivalent dose rate of 10⁻⁶ sievert in one hour. A millirem per hour is an equivalent dose rate of 10⁻⁵ sievert in one hour.

Formula

mrem/h = µSv/h × 0.1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 microsievert per hour equals 0.1 millirems per hour, and the reverse factor is 10.

Simple example

1 µSv/h × 0.1 = 0.1 mrem/h

1 microsievert per hour = 0.1 millirems per hour.

Real-world example

10 µSv/h × 0.1 = 1 mrem/h

10 microsieverts per hour = 1 millirems per hour.

Understanding the Microsievert per hour

Microsievert per hour (µSv/h) measures equivalent dose rate. A microsievert per hour is an equivalent dose rate of 10⁻⁶ sievert in one hour.

Standard and definition

The microsievert per hour is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the microsievert per hour today

  • Environmental and ambient dose-rate monitors
  • Radiation alarm thresholds

Understanding the Millirem per hour

Millirem per hour (mrem/h) measures equivalent dose rate. A millirem per hour is an equivalent dose rate of 10⁻⁵ sievert in one hour.

Standard and definition

The millirem per hour is covered by ICRU Report 33 — Radiation Quantities and Units.

Uses of the millirem per hour today

  • US radiation-area posting and survey readings
  • Occupational dose-rate records
