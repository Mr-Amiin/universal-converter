About Converting Microcuries to Kilobecquerels

Microcurie and kilobecquerel are both units of activity. A microcurie is one millionth of a curie: 3.7 × 10⁴ becquerels. A kilobecquerel is one thousand becquerels.

Formula

kBq = µCi × 37

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 microcurie equals 37 kilobecquerels, and the reverse factor is 0.02702702703.

Simple example

1 µCi × 37 = 37 kBq

1 microcurie = 37 kilobecquerels.

Real-world example

10 µCi × 37 = 370 kBq

10 microcuries = 370 kilobecquerels.

Understanding the Microcurie

Microcurie (µCi) measures activity. A microcurie is one millionth of a curie: 3.7 × 10⁴ becquerels.

Standard and definition

The microcurie is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the microcurie today

  • Laboratory tracers and smoke-detector source activity
  • Environmental sample activity

Understanding the Kilobecquerel

Kilobecquerel (kBq) measures activity. A kilobecquerel is one thousand becquerels.

Standard and definition

The kilobecquerel is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the kilobecquerel today

  • Low-activity samples and environmental measurements
  • Radon and food radioactivity reporting
