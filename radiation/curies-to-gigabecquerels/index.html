About Converting Curies to Gigabecquerels

Curie and gigabecquerel are both units of activity. The curie is exactly 3.7 × 10¹⁰ becquerels (the activity of about one gram of radium-226). A gigabecquerel is one billion becquerels (10⁹ decays per second).

Formula

GBq = Ci × 37

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 curie equals 37 gigabecquerels, and the reverse factor is 0.02702702703.

Simple example

1 Ci × 37 = 37 GBq

1 curie = 37 gigabecquerels.

Real-world example

10 Ci × 37 = 370 GBq

10 curies = 370 gigabecquerels.

Understanding the Curie

Curie (Ci) measures activity. The curie is exactly 3.7 × 10¹⁰ becquerels (the activity of about one gram of radium-226).

Standard and definition

The curie is covered by ICRU Report 33 — Radiation Quantities and Units.

Uses of the curie today

  • Radioactive source ratings in the United States
  • Older and clinical radioactivity references

Understanding the Gigabecquerel

Gigabecquerel (GBq) measures activity. A gigabecquerel is one billion becquerels (10⁹ decays per second).

Standard and definition

The gigabecquerel is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the gigabecquerel today

  • Industrial and sealed-source activity ratings
  • Reactor and waste inventories
