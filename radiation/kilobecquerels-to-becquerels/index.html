About Converting Kilobecquerels to Becquerels

Kilobecquerel and becquerel are both units of activity. A kilobecquerel is one thousand becquerels. The becquerel is the SI unit of activity: one nuclear decay per second.

Formula

Bq = kBq × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 kilobecquerel equals 1000 becquerels, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 kBq × 1000 = 1000 Bq

1 kilobecquerel = 1000 becquerels.

Real-world example

10 kBq × 1000 = 10000 Bq

10 kilobecquerels = 10000 becquerels.

Understanding the Kilobecquerel

Kilobecquerel (kBq) measures activity. A kilobecquerel is one thousand becquerels.

Standard and definition

The kilobecquerel is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the kilobecquerel today

  • Low-activity samples and environmental measurements
  • Radon and food radioactivity reporting

Understanding the Becquerel

Becquerel (Bq) measures activity. The becquerel is the SI unit of activity: one nuclear decay per second.

Standard and definition

The becquerel is covered by ICRU Report 33 — Radiation Quantities and Units.

Uses of the becquerel today

  • Radioactivity of sources, food and environmental samples
  • Nuclear medicine and safety
