Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the femtogray (fGy). Femtogray is 1e-15 gray units.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the hectogray (hGy). Hectogray is 100 gray units.

How do I convert femtogray to hectogray?

Multiply the femtogray value by 1.000000e-17. For example, 5 fGy × 1.000000e-17 = 5.000000e-17 hGy.

Why do femtogray and hectogray need converting at all?

Femto (f) represents 10^-15, used in particle physics and ultrafast optics. Hecto (h) represents 10^2 and is rarely used outside the hectare and hectopascal.

Is the femtogray to hectogray conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between femtogray and hectogray is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-17), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert hectogray back to femtogray?

Yes. Multiply the hectogray value by 1.000000e+17 (or divide by 1.000000e-17) to get femtogray.

About this conversion

Worked example: to convert 10 fGy to hGy, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1.000000e-17 = 1.000000e-16 hGy.
