How many Microsieverts are in one Yoctogray?

How do I convert Yoctogray to Microsievert?

Multiply the number of Yoctograys by 1e-18 to get Microsieverts. To go the other way, multiply Microsieverts by 1e+18 (or divide by 1e-18) to get Yoctograys.

About this conversion

Yoctogray and Microsievert are units used in the radiation family of measurements. Yoctogray measures absorbed radiation dose (energy deposited per unit mass), while Microsievert measures equivalent radiation dose (biological risk-weighted dose). This page provides the direct numeric ratio between the two units. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 Yoctogray = 1e-18 Microsieverts (equivalently 1 Microsievert = 1e+18 Yoctograys), so results stay accurate for nuclear medicine, radiation safety, health physics, and scientific research.
