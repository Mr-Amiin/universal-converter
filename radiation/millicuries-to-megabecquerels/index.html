About Converting Millicuries to Megabecquerels

Millicurie and megabecquerel are both units of activity. A millicurie is one thousandth of a curie: 3.7 × 10⁷ becquerels. A megabecquerel is one million becquerels (10⁶ decays per second).

Formula

MBq = mCi × 37

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 millicurie equals 37 megabecquerels, and the reverse factor is 0.02702702703.

Simple example

1 mCi × 37 = 37 MBq

1 millicurie = 37 megabecquerels.

Real-world example

10 mCi × 37 = 370 MBq

10 millicuries = 370 megabecquerels.

Understanding the Millicurie

Millicurie (mCi) measures activity. A millicurie is one thousandth of a curie: 3.7 × 10⁷ becquerels.

Standard and definition

The millicurie is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the millicurie today

  • Nuclear-medicine dosing in the United States
  • Laboratory radioisotope handling

Understanding the Megabecquerel

Megabecquerel (MBq) measures activity. A megabecquerel is one million becquerels (10⁶ decays per second).

Standard and definition

The megabecquerel is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the megabecquerel today

  • Radiopharmaceutical doses in nuclear medicine
  • Source activity labels
