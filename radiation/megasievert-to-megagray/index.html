About this conversion

This page converts megasievert (MSv) into megagray (MGy) within the radiation converter, using the fixed relationship 1 MSv = 1 MGy.

Worked example: to convert 10 MSv to MGy, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1 = 10 MGy.

A dose of 10 MSv corresponds to about 10 MGy.

Reverse conversion: to go from megagray back to megasievert, divide by 1 (equivalently, multiply by 1).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.

Megasievert: Megasievert is 1000000 sievert units. Megagray: Megagray is 1000000 gray units.

Background: Mega (M) represents 10^6, one of the most commonly used SI prefixes.

Note: this conversion treats absorbed dose and equivalent dose as numerically equal, which assumes a radiation weighting factor of 1 (true for X-rays, gamma rays, and beta particles, but not for alpha particles or neutrons, which carry higher weighting factors).
