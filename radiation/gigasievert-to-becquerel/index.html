Why there is no table here

Radioactivity (becquerel/curie) counts nuclear decays per second. Absorbed or equivalent dose (gray, sievert, rad, rem) measures energy deposited in tissue. Converting between them requires knowing the specific radionuclide, the type and energy of radiation emitted, and the exposure geometry -- there is no single fixed factor.
