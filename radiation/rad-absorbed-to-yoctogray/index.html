Frequently asked questions

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

How do I convert rad to yoctogray?

Multiply the rad value by 1.000000e+22. For example, 5 rad × 1.000000e+22 = 5.000000e+22 yGy.

Why do rad and yoctogray need converting at all?

The rad, from the older CGS system, equals 0.01 gray and is still used in some US medical contexts. Yocto (y) represents 10^-24, historically the smallest SI prefix before 2022.

Is the rad to yoctogray conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between rad and yoctogray is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e+22), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert yoctogray back to rad?

Yes. Multiply the yoctogray value by 1.000000e-22 (or divide by 1.000000e+22) to get rad.
