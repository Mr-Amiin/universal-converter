Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the picogray (pGy). Picogray is 1e-12 gray units.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the femtogray (fGy). Femtogray is 1e-15 gray units.

How do I convert picogray to femtogray?

Multiply the picogray value by 1000. For example, 5 pGy × 1000 = 5000 fGy.

Why do picogray and femtogray need converting at all?

Pico (p) represents 10^-12, used for extremely small physical quantities. Femto (f) represents 10^-15, used in particle physics and ultrafast optics.

Is the picogray to femtogray conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between picogray and femtogray is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1000), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert femtogray back to picogray?

Yes. Multiply the femtogray value by 0.001 (or divide by 1000) to get picogray.

About this conversion

This page converts picogray (pGy) into femtogray (fGy) within the radiation converter, using the fixed relationship 1 pGy = 1000 fGy.

Worked example: to convert 10 pGy to fGy, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1000 = 10000 fGy.

A dose of 10 pGy corresponds to about 10000 fGy.

Reverse conversion: to go from femtogray back to picogray, divide by 1000 (equivalently, multiply by 0.001).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.

Picogray: Picogray is 1e-12 gray units. Femtogray: Femtogray is 1e-15 gray units.
