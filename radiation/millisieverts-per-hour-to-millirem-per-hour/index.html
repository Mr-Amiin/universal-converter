About Converting Millisieverts per Hour to Millirem per Hour

Millisievert per hour and millirem per hour are both units of equivalent dose rate. A millisievert per hour is an equivalent dose rate of 10⁻³ sievert in one hour. A millirem per hour is an equivalent dose rate of 10⁻⁵ sievert in one hour.

Formula

mrem/h = mSv/h × 100

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 millisievert per hour equals 100 millirems per hour, and the reverse factor is 0.01.

Simple example

1 mSv/h × 100 = 100 mrem/h

1 millisievert per hour = 100 millirems per hour.

Real-world example

10 mSv/h × 100 = 1000 mrem/h

10 millisieverts per hour = 1000 millirems per hour.

Understanding the Millisievert per hour

Millisievert per hour (mSv/h) measures equivalent dose rate. A millisievert per hour is an equivalent dose rate of 10⁻³ sievert in one hour.

Standard and definition

The millisievert per hour is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the millisievert per hour today

  • Radiological protection and emergency work limits
  • Nuclear facility dose-rate surveys

Understanding the Millirem per hour

Millirem per hour (mrem/h) measures equivalent dose rate. A millirem per hour is an equivalent dose rate of 10⁻⁵ sievert in one hour.

Standard and definition

The millirem per hour is covered by ICRU Report 33 — Radiation Quantities and Units.

Uses of the millirem per hour today

  • US radiation-area posting and survey readings
  • Occupational dose-rate records
