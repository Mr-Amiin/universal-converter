How do I convert nanogray to zeptogray?

Multiply the nanogray value by 1.000000e+12. For example, 5 nGy × 1.000000e+12 = 5.000000e+12 zGy.

Why do nanogray and zeptogray need converting at all?

Nano (n) represents 10^-9, familiar from nanotechnology and nanometers. Zepto (z) represents 10^-21, one of the smallest SI prefixes in practical use.

Is the nanogray to zeptogray conversion exact?

Can I convert zeptogray back to nanogray?
