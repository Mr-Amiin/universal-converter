About Converting Roentgens to Milliroentgens

Roentgen and milliroentgen are both units of exposure. The roentgen is the unit of ionizing-radiation exposure in air: exactly 2.58 × 10⁻⁴ coulombs per kilogram. A milliroentgen is one thousandth of a roentgen: 2.58 × 10⁻⁷ C/kg.

Formula

mR = R × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 roentgen equals 1000 milliroentgens, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 R × 1000 = 1000 mR

1 roentgen = 1000 milliroentgens.

Real-world example

10 R × 1000 = 10000 mR

10 roentgens = 10000 milliroentgens.
