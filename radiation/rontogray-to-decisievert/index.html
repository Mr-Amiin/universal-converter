Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the rontogray (rGy). Rontogray is 1e-27 gray units.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the decisievert (dSv). Decisievert is 0.1 sievert units.

How do I convert rontogray to decisievert?

Multiply the rontogray value by 1.000000e-26. For example, 5 rGy × 1.000000e-26 = 5.000000e-26 dSv.

Why do rontogray and decisievert need converting at all?

Ronto (r) represents 10^-27, one of the newest and smallest SI prefixes, adopted in 2022. Deci (d) represents 10^-1, familiar from the decibel and deciliter.

Is the rontogray to decisievert conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between rontogray and decisievert is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-26), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert decisievert back to rontogray?

Yes. Multiply the decisievert value by 1.000000e+26 (or divide by 1.000000e-26) to get rontogray.

Does this conversion always hold exactly?

Only when the radiation weighting factor is 1, as it is for X-rays, gamma rays, and beta radiation. For alpha particles or neutrons, the equivalent dose in sieverts is higher than the absorbed dose in grays by that factor.

About this conversion

This page converts rontogray (rGy) into decisievert (dSv) within the radiation converter, using the fixed relationship 1 rGy = 1.000000e-26 dSv.

Worked example: to convert 10 rGy to dSv, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1.000000e-26 = 1.000000e-25 dSv.

A dose of 10 rGy corresponds to about 1.000000e-25 dSv.

Reverse conversion: to go from decisievert back to rontogray, divide by 1.000000e-26 (equivalently, multiply by 1.000000e+26).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.

Rontogray: Rontogray is 1e-27 gray units. Decisievert: Decisievert is 0.1 sievert units.

Background: Ronto (r) represents 10^-27, one of the newest and smallest SI prefixes, adopted in 2022.

Note: this conversion treats absorbed dose and equivalent dose as numerically equal, which assumes a radiation weighting factor of 1 (true for X-rays, gamma rays, and beta particles, but not for alpha particles or neutrons, which carry higher weighting factors).
