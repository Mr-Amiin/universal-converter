About Converting Microsieverts to Millirem

Microsievert and millirem are both units of equivalent dose. A microsievert is 10⁻⁶ sievert. A millirem is one thousandth of a rem, or 10⁻⁵ sievert (10 microsieverts).

Formula

mrem = µSv × 0.1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 microsievert equals 0.1 millirems, and the reverse factor is 10.

Simple example

1 µSv × 0.1 = 0.1 mrem

1 microsievert = 0.1 millirems.

Real-world example

10 µSv × 0.1 = 1 mrem

10 microsieverts = 1 millirems.

Understanding the Microsievert

Microsievert (µSv) measures equivalent dose. A microsievert is 10⁻⁶ sievert.

Standard and definition

The microsievert is covered by ICRU Report 33 — Radiation Quantities and Units.

Uses of the microsievert today

  • Typical radiation doses such as X-rays and flights
  • Dose-limit comparisons

Understanding the Millirem

Millirem (mrem) measures equivalent dose. A millirem is one thousandth of a rem, or 10⁻⁵ sievert (10 microsieverts).

Standard and definition

The millirem is covered by ICRU Report 33 — Radiation Quantities and Units.

Uses of the millirem today

  • US dose limits and occupational radiation records
  • Medical imaging and background-radiation comparisons
