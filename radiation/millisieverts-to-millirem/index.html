About Converting Millisieverts to Millirem

Millisievert and millirem are both units of equivalent dose. A millisievert is 10⁻³ sievert. A millirem is one thousandth of a rem, or 10⁻⁵ sievert (10 microsieverts).

Formula

mrem = mSv × 100

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 millisievert equals 100 millirems, and the reverse factor is 0.01.

Simple example

1 mSv × 100 = 100 mrem

1 millisievert = 100 millirems.

Real-world example

10 mSv × 100 = 1000 mrem

10 millisieverts = 1000 millirems.

Understanding the Millisievert

Millisievert (mSv) measures equivalent dose. A millisievert is 10⁻³ sievert.

Standard and definition

The millisievert is covered by ICRU Report 33 — Radiation Quantities and Units.

Uses of the millisievert today

  • Annual dose limits and CT-scan doses
  • Occupational dose records

Understanding the Millirem

Millirem (mrem) measures equivalent dose. A millirem is one thousandth of a rem, or 10⁻⁵ sievert (10 microsieverts).

Standard and definition

The millirem is covered by ICRU Report 33 — Radiation Quantities and Units.

Uses of the millirem today

  • US dose limits and occupational radiation records
  • Medical imaging and background-radiation comparisons
