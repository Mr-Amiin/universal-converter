About Converting Rutherfords to Megabecquerels

Rutherford and megabecquerel are both units of activity. A rutherford is one million disintegrations per second: 10⁶ becquerels. A megabecquerel is one million becquerels (10⁶ decays per second).

Formula

MBq = Rd × 1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 rutherford equals 1 megabecquerels, and the reverse factor is 1.

Simple example

1 Rd × 1 = 1 MBq

1 rutherford = 1 megabecquerels.

Real-world example

10 Rd × 1 = 10 MBq

10 rutherfords = 10 megabecquerels.
