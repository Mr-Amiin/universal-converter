About this conversion

This page compares Giganewton meter (GN m) and Megajoule (MJ) within the engineering converter.

Giganewton meter is 1000000000 newton meter units. Megajoule is 1000000 joule units.

Because Giganewton meter measures torque (rotational force) and Megajoule measures energy, the two units are not directly convertible: there is no fixed ratio that turns one into the other without additional information.

The calculator above will report that these units are not compatible. For a working result, choose two units from the same measurement family — see the related conversions below.

For scale, a bicycle pedal stroke applies roughly 40-80 newton-metres of torque, while a car engine can produce several hundred newton-metres. On the energy side, a typical phone battery stores on the order of 40,000 joules, and a household kettle uses roughly 1,000,000 joules to boil a litre of water. Seeing both scales side by side is often more useful than a single converted number would be.
