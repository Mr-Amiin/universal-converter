About Converting BTU per Hour-Foot-F to Watts per Meter-Kelvin

BTU per hour-foot-°F and watt per meter-kelvin are both units of thermal conductivity. One BTU per hour-foot-°F is 1.730734666 W/(m·K), using the International Table BTU. The watt per meter-kelvin is the SI unit of thermal conductivity.

Formula

W/(m·K) = BTU/(h·ft·°F) × 1.730734666

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 BTU per hour-foot-°F equals 1.730734666 watts per meter-kelvin, and the reverse factor is 0.5777893165.
