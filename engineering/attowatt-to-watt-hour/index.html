Formula

Attowatt (aW) measures power, while Watt-hour (Wh) measures energy. These are different physical quantities, so there is no single multiplication factor that converts one into the other.

aW relates to its own power base unit by a factor of 0. Wh relates to its own energy base unit by a factor of 3600.

Frequently asked questions

Can Attowatt be converted to Watt-hour?

Not directly. Attowatt measures power and Watt-hour measures energy. Converting between different physical quantities needs an extra physical relationship (such as area, distance, or time), not just a unit ratio.

What is Attowatt used for?

The Attowatt (aW) is a unit of power. Attowatt is 1e-18 watt units.

What is Watt-hour used for?

The Watt-hour (Wh) is a unit of energy. A watt-hour is 3,600 joules.

Why does the converter show an incompatible units message?

The Universal Converter only calculates a numeric result when both units share the same physical dimension. Since Attowatt and Watt-hour don't, the tool flags them as not directly compatible rather than returning a meaningless number.

What should I convert Attowatt to instead?

Use another power unit, such as one of the related Attowatt conversions linked below.

What should I convert Watt-hour to instead?

Use another energy unit, such as one of the related Watt-hour conversions linked below.
