About Converting Centipoise to Pascal-seconds

Centipoise and pascal-second are both units of dynamic viscosity. A centipoise is one hundredth of a poise: 0.001 pascal-second (1 mPa·s). The pascal-second is the SI unit of dynamic viscosity: one newton-second per square meter.

Formula

Pa·s = cP × 0.001

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 centipoise equals 0.001 pascal-seconds, and the reverse factor is 1000.

Simple example

1 cP × 0.001 = 0.001 Pa·s

1 centipoise = 0.001 pascal-seconds.

Real-world example

10 cP × 0.001 = 0.01 Pa·s

10 centipoises = 0.01 pascal-seconds.

Understanding the Centipoise

Centipoise (cP) measures dynamic viscosity. A centipoise is one hundredth of a poise: 0.001 pascal-second (1 mPa·s).

Standard and definition

The centipoise is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the centipoise today

  • Quoting lubricant and fluid viscosity (water at 20 °C is about 1 cP)
  • Paint, oil and food-process viscosity specifications

Understanding the Pascal-second

Pascal-second (Pa·s) measures dynamic viscosity. The pascal-second is the SI unit of dynamic viscosity: one newton-second per square meter.

Standard and definition

The pascal-second is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the pascal-second today

  • SI viscosity reporting
  • Rheology and fluid-flow calculations
