About Converting Pounds per Cubic Foot to Kilonewtons per Cubic Meter

Pound-force per cubic foot and kilonewton per cubic meter are both units of unit weight. One pound-force per cubic foot is 157.0874606 N/m³ (0.1570874606 kN/m³). One kilonewton per cubic meter is a unit weight (weight per volume) of 1,000 N/m³.

Formula

kN/m³ = lbf/ft³ × 0.1570874638

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 pound-force per cubic foot equals 0.1570874638 kilonewtons per cubic meter, and the reverse factor is 6.365880354.
