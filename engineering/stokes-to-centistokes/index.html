About Converting Stokes to Centistokes

Stokes and centistokes are both units of kinematic viscosity. The stokes is the CGS unit of kinematic viscosity: 10⁻⁴ square meters per second. A centistokes is one hundredth of a stokes: 10⁻⁶ square meters per second (1 mm²/s).

Formula

cSt = St × 100

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 stokes equals 100 centistokes, and the reverse factor is 0.01.

Simple example

1 St × 100 = 100 cSt

1 stokes = 100 centistokes.

Real-world example

10 St × 100 = 1000 cSt

10 stokes = 1000 centistokes.
