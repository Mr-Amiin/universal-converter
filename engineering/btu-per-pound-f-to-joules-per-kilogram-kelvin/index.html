About Converting BTU per Pound-F to Joules per Kilogram-Kelvin

BTU per pound-°F and joule per kilogram-kelvin are both units of specific heat. One BTU per pound-°F is exactly 4,186.8 J/(kg·K), the International Table definition of specific heat of water. The joule per kilogram-kelvin is the SI unit of specific heat capacity.

Formula

J/(kg·K) = BTU/(lb·°F) × 4186.8

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 BTU per pound-°F equals 4186.8 joules per kilogram-kelvin, and the reverse factor is 0.0002388458966.

Simple example

1 BTU/(lb·°F) × 4186.8 = 4186.8 J/(kg·K)

1 BTU per pound-°F = 4186.8 joules per kilogram-kelvin.

Real-world example

10 BTU/(lb·°F) × 4186.8 = 41868 J/(kg·K)

10 BTU per pound-°F = 41868 joules per kilogram-kelvin.
