Formula

Millinewton meter (mN m) measures torque (rotational force), while Ronnapascal (RPa) measures pressure and mechanical stress. These are different physical quantities, so there is no single multiplication factor that converts one into the other.

mN m relates to its own torque (rotational force) base unit by a factor of 0.001. RPa relates to its own pressure and mechanical stress base unit by a factor of 1e+27.

About this conversion

This page compares Millinewton meter (mN m) and Ronnapascal (RPa) within the engineering converter.

Millinewton meter is 0.001 newton meter units. Ronnapascal is 1e+27 pascal units.

Because Millinewton meter measures torque (rotational force) and Ronnapascal measures pressure and mechanical stress, the two units are not directly convertible: there is no fixed ratio that turns one into the other without additional information.

The calculator above will report that these units are not compatible. For a working result, choose two units from the same measurement family — see the related conversions below.

For scale, a bicycle pedal stroke applies roughly 40-80 newton-metres of torque, while a car engine can produce several hundred newton-metres. On the pressure side, normal atmospheric pressure at sea level is about 101,325 pascals, and a typical car tyre is inflated to roughly 220,000 pascals. Seeing both scales side by side is often more useful than a single converted number would be.
