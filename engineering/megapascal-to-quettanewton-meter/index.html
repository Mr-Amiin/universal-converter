Formula

Megapascal (MPa) measures pressure and mechanical stress, while Quettanewton meter (QN m) measures torque (rotational force). These are different physical quantities, so there is no single multiplication factor that converts one into the other.

MPa relates to its own pressure and mechanical stress base unit by a factor of 1000000. QN m relates to its own torque (rotational force) base unit by a factor of 1e+30.

What is Quettanewton meter used for?

How can I relate Megapascal to Quettanewton meter in practice?

About this conversion

This page compares Megapascal (MPa) and Quettanewton meter (QN m) within the engineering converter.

Megapascal is 1000000 pascal units. Quettanewton meter is 1e+30 newton meter units.

Because Megapascal measures pressure and mechanical stress and Quettanewton meter measures torque (rotational force), the two units are not directly convertible: there is no fixed ratio that turns one into the other without additional information.

The calculator above will report that these units are not compatible. For a working result, choose two units from the same measurement family — see the related conversions below.

For scale, normal atmospheric pressure at sea level is about 101,325 pascals, and a typical car tyre is inflated to roughly 220,000 pascals. On the torque (rotational force) side, a bicycle pedal stroke applies roughly 40-80 newton-metres of torque, while a car engine can produce several hundred newton-metres. Seeing both scales side by side is often more useful than a single converted number would be.
