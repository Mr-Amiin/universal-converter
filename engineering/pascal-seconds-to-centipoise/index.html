About Converting Pascal-seconds to Centipoise

Pascal-second and centipoise are both units of dynamic viscosity. The pascal-second is the SI unit of dynamic viscosity: one newton-second per square meter. A centipoise is one hundredth of a poise: 0.001 pascal-second (1 mPa·s).

Formula

cP = Pa·s × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 pascal-second equals 1000 centipoises, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 Pa·s × 1000 = 1000 cP

1 pascal-second = 1000 centipoises.

Real-world example

10 Pa·s × 1000 = 10000 cP

10 pascal-seconds = 10000 centipoises.

Understanding the Pascal-second

Standard and definition

Uses of the pascal-second today

  • SI viscosity reporting
  • Rheology and fluid-flow calculations
