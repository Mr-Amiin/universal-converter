About Converting BTU per Hour-sq ft to Watts per Square Meter

BTU per hour-sq ft and watt per square meter are both units of heat flux. One BTU per hour-square-foot is 3.154591 W/m², using the International Table BTU. The watt per square meter is the SI unit of heat flux and irradiance.

Formula

W/m² = BTU/(h·ft²) × 3.154590745

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 BTU per hour-sq ft equals 3.154590745 watts per square meter, and the reverse factor is 0.3169983306.
