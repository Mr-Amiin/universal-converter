Formula

Exapascal (EPa) measures pressure and mechanical stress, while Foot-pound per second (ft-lb/s) measures power. These are different physical quantities, so there is no single multiplication factor that converts one into the other.

EPa relates to its own pressure and mechanical stress base unit by a factor of 1e+18. ft-lb/s relates to its own power base unit by a factor of 1.35582.
