Frequently asked questions

What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the engineering converter category.

Why are engineering units grouped by dimension?

Stress, force, torque, power, and energy are different physical quantities, so this category groups compatible units together to avoid mismatched conversions.

Is PSI the same everywhere in engineering?

PSI (pounds per square inch) is a common pressure and stress unit in US engineering practice, while pascal-based units are standard internationally.
