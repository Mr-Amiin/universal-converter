What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is Rankine (°R). Rankine is an absolute temperature scale that uses Fahrenheit-sized degrees.

About this conversion

This page converts Kelvin (K) into Rankine (°R), two scales used to measure temperature.

Kelvin to Rankine conversions come up in science, engineering, weather reporting, and historical references, depending on which scale is in use.

The formula is °R = K × 9/5, a linear equation that accounts for both the scale factor and the zero-point offset between the two scales.

The conversion is exact: it uses fixed constants defined by the relationship between the two scales, not a measured or approximate factor.

Kelvin is the SI absolute temperature scale, where 0 K is absolute zero. Rankine is an absolute temperature scale that uses Fahrenheit-sized degrees.
