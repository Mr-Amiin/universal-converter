Frequently asked questions

What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the fuel economy converter category.

Why do some fuel economy units go up while others go down for better efficiency?

Efficiency-style units like miles per gallon increase with better efficiency, while consumption-style units like liters per 100 km decrease with better efficiency. The converter handles both directions.

Can I convert between MPG and L/100 km directly?

Yes. Fuel economy conversions in this category translate between distance-per-volume and volume-per-distance formats automatically.
