About Converting Miles per Liter to Miles per Gallon (UK)

Miles per liter and mile per imperial gallon are both units of fuel economy. Miles per liter is the distance in statute miles travelled per liter of fuel. Miles per imperial gallon is the distance in miles per 4.54609 liters of fuel.

Formula

mpg (imp) = mi/L × 4.54609

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 miles per liter equals 4.54609 miles per imperial gallon, and the reverse factor is 0.2199692483.

Simple example

1 mi/L × 4.54609 = 4.54609 mpg (imp)

1 miles per liter = 4.54609 miles per imperial gallon.

Real-world example

10 mi/L × 4.54609 = 45.4609 mpg (imp)

10 miles per liter = 45.4609 miles per imperial gallon.
