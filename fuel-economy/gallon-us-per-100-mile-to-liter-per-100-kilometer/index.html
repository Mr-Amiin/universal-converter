About this conversion

US gallons per 100 miles and Liters per 100 km are both units used to measure fuel economy. US gallons per 100 miles (gal/100 mi) measures how many US gallons of fuel a vehicle consumes over 100 miles; a lower number means better efficiency. It is occasionally used in the United States for consumption-style comparisons. Liters per 100 kilometers (L/100 km) measures how many liters of fuel a vehicle consumes over 100 kilometers; a lower number means better efficiency. It is the standard fuel consumption unit used across most of Europe and many other regions. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 gal/100 mi = 2.352145833 L/100 km (equivalently 1 L/100 km = 0.4251437074 gal/100 mi), so results stay accurate for automotive fuel efficiency ratings, vehicle comparisons and shopping, as well as EPA, WLTP and other international fuel economy standards.
