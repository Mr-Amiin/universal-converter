About this conversion

Meters per liter and Miles per US gallon are both units used to measure fuel economy. Meters per liter (m/L) measures how many meters a vehicle travels using one liter of fuel; a higher number means better efficiency. It is a finer-grained metric fuel economy unit useful for precise engineering calculations. Miles per US gallon (mpg) measures how many miles a vehicle travels using one US gallon of fuel; a higher number means better efficiency. It is the standard fuel economy unit used in the United States. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 m/L = 0.002352145833 mpg (equivalently 1 mpg = 425.1437074 m/L), so results stay accurate for automotive fuel efficiency ratings, vehicle comparisons and shopping, as well as EPA, WLTP and other international fuel economy standards.
