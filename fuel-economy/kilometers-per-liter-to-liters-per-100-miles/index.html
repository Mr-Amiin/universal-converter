About Converting Kilometers per Liter to Liters per 100 Miles

Kilometer per liter and liters per 100 miles are both units of fuel economy. Kilometers per liter is the distance travelled per liter of fuel. Liters per 100 miles is the fuel volume in liters used to travel 100 statute miles.

Formula

L/100 mi = 1 ÷ (km/L × 0.006213711922)

Kilometer per liter is an efficiency unit and liters per 100 miles is a consumption unit, so they are reciprocals: divide 1 by the value multiplied by 0.006213711922.

Simple example

1 km/L = 160.9344 L/100 mi

1 kilometers per liter = 160.9344 liters per 100 miles.

Real-world example

5 km/L = 32.18688 L/100 mi

5 kilometers per liter = 32.18688 liters per 100 miles.
