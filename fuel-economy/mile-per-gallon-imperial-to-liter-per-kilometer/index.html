Frequently asked questions

How many liters per km are in one miles per imperial gallon?

One mpg imp equals 2.824809363 L/km (this pair inverts, since one measures distance per volume and the other volume per distance).

How do I convert miles per imperial gallon to liters per km?

Divide 2.824809363 by the miles per imperial gallon value to get liters per km, and divide 2.824809363 by the liters per km value to get back miles per imperial gallon. This "invert the constant" rule works both ways because one unit is distance-per-volume and the other is volume-per-distance.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between miles per imperial gallon and liters per km, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

When is miles per imperial gallon to liters per km conversion commonly used?

It is commonly used in automotive fuel efficiency ratings, vehicle comparisons and shopping, as well as EPA, WLTP and other international fuel economy standards.

About this conversion

Miles per imperial gallon and Liters per km are both units used to measure fuel economy. Miles per imperial gallon (mpg imp) measures how many miles a vehicle travels using one imperial (UK) gallon of fuel; a higher number means better efficiency. It is used mainly in the United Kingdom and other Commonwealth countries. Liters per kilometer (L/km) measures how many liters of fuel a vehicle consumes over one kilometer; a lower number means better efficiency. It is used for precise consumption tracking. Miles per imperial gallon is a distance-per-volume efficiency unit while liters per km is a volume-per-distance consumption unit, so converting between them means inverting the value rather than multiplying by a flat factor: 1 mpg imp = 2.824809363 L/km. This relationship stays accurate for automotive fuel efficiency ratings, vehicle comparisons and shopping, as well as EPA, WLTP and other international fuel economy standards.
