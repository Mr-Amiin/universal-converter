About this conversion

This page converts liters per 100 km (L/100 km) into liters per km (L/km) within the fuel economy converter, using the fixed relationship 1 L/100 km = 0.01 L/km.

Worked example: to convert 10 L/100 km to L/km, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 0.01 = 0.1 L/km.

A car rated at 30 L/100 km achieves about 0.3 L/km.

Reverse conversion: to go from liters per km back to liters per 100 km, divide by 0.01 (equivalently, multiply by 100).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
