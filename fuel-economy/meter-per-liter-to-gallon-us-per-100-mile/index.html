Frequently asked questions

How many us gallons per 100 miles are in one meters per liter?

One m/L equals 42514.37074 gal/100 mi (this pair inverts, since one measures distance per volume and the other volume per distance).

How do I convert meters per liter to us gallons per 100 miles?

Divide 42514.37074 by the meters per liter value to get us gallons per 100 miles, and divide 42514.37074 by the us gallons per 100 miles value to get back meters per liter. This "invert the constant" rule works both ways because one unit is distance-per-volume and the other is volume-per-distance.

About this conversion

Meters per liter and US gallons per 100 miles are both units used to measure fuel economy. Meters per liter (m/L) measures how many meters a vehicle travels using one liter of fuel; a higher number means better efficiency. It is a finer-grained metric fuel economy unit useful for precise engineering calculations. US gallons per 100 miles (gal/100 mi) measures how many US gallons of fuel a vehicle consumes over 100 miles; a lower number means better efficiency. It is occasionally used in the United States for consumption-style comparisons. Meters per liter is a distance-per-volume efficiency unit while us gallons per 100 miles is a volume-per-distance consumption unit, so converting between them means inverting the value rather than multiplying by a flat factor: 1 m/L = 42514.37074 gal/100 mi. This relationship stays accurate for automotive fuel efficiency ratings, vehicle comparisons and shopping, as well as EPA, WLTP and other international fuel economy standards.
