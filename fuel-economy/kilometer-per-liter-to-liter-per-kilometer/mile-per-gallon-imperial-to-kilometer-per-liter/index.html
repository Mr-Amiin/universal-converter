About this conversion

This page converts miles per imperial gallon (mpg imp) into kilometers per liter (km/L) within the fuel economy converter, using the fixed relationship 1 mpg imp = 0.354006 km/L.

Worked example: to convert 10 mpg imp to km/L, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 0.354006 = 3.54006 km/L.

A car rated at 30 mpg imp achieves about 10.62 km/L.

Reverse conversion: to go from kilometers per liter back to miles per imperial gallon, divide by 0.354006 (equivalently, multiply by 2.8248108789).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
