About Converting Miles per Liter to Gallons (US) per 100 Miles

Miles per liter and US gallon per 100 miles are both units of fuel economy. Miles per liter is the distance in statute miles travelled per liter of fuel. US gallons per 100 miles is the fuel consumed to travel 100 statute miles.

Formula

gal/100 mi = 1 ÷ (mi/L × 0.03785411784)

Miles per liter is an efficiency unit and US gallon per 100 miles is a consumption unit, so they are reciprocals: divide 1 by the value multiplied by 0.03785411784.

Simple example

1 mi/L = 26.41720524 gal/100 mi

1 miles per liter = 26.41720524 US gallons per 100 miles.

Real-world example

5 mi/L = 5.283441047 gal/100 mi

5 miles per liter = 5.283441047 US gallons per 100 miles.
