About Converting Liters per 100 km to Miles per Liter

Liter per 100 kilometers and miles per liter are both units of fuel economy. Liters per 100 kilometers is the fuel volume used to travel 100 km. Miles per liter is the distance in statute miles travelled per liter of fuel.

Formula

mi/L = 1 ÷ (L/100 km × 0.01609344)

Liter per 100 kilometers is an consumption unit and miles per liter is an efficiency unit, so they are reciprocals: divide 1 by the value multiplied by 0.01609344.

Simple example

1 L/100 km = 62.13711922 mi/L

1 liters per 100 kilometers = 62.13711922 miles per liter.

Real-world example

5 L/100 km = 12.42742384 mi/L

5 liters per 100 kilometers = 12.42742384 miles per liter.
