About Converting Liters per 100 Miles to Gallons (US) per 100 Miles

Liters per 100 miles and US gallon per 100 miles are both units of fuel economy. Liters per 100 miles is the fuel volume in liters used to travel 100 statute miles. US gallons per 100 miles is the fuel consumed to travel 100 statute miles.

Formula

gal/100 mi = L/100 mi × 0.2641720524

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 liters per 100 miles equals 0.2641720524 US gallons per 100 miles, and the reverse factor is 3.785411784.

Simple example

1 L/100 mi × 0.2641720524 = 0.2641720524 gal/100 mi

1 liters per 100 miles = 0.2641720524 US gallons per 100 miles.

Real-world example

10 L/100 mi × 0.2641720524 = 2.641720524 gal/100 mi

10 liters per 100 miles = 2.641720524 US gallons per 100 miles.
