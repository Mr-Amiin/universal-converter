Frequently asked questions

How many parts per million are in one milligram per microliter?

One milligram per microliter equals 1×10^6 parts per million.

Why is milligram per microliter used in chemistry?

Milligram per microliter is used specifically when used in analytical and biochemical work with very small sample volumes, expressed via a metric mass unit (milligram).

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both milligrams per microliter and parts per million measure the same kind of quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor.

Can I use this conversion for any chemical?

Yes. Since milligram per microliter and part per million both measure the same kind of quantity, this factor applies regardless of which chemical is involved.

When would you use milligrams per microliter instead of parts per million?

milligram per microliter is typically used in reporting concentrations in used in analytical and biochemical work with very small sample volumes using a metric mass unit (milligram), while part per million is typically used in reporting trace contaminant levels in drinking water, air quality, and environmental testing.
