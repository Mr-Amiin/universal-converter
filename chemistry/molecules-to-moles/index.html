About Converting Molecules to Moles

Molecule and mole are both units of amount. One molecule, counted as an amount of substance, is 1/N_A mole, where the Avogadro constant N_A = 6.02214076 × 10²³ per mole (exact). The mole is the SI unit of amount of substance: exactly 6.02214076 × 10²³ elementary entities.

Formula

mol = molecule × 1.660539067 × 10⁻²⁴

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 molecule equals 1.660539067 × 10⁻²⁴ moles, and the reverse factor is 6.02214076 × 10²³.

Simple example

1 molecule × 1.660539067 × 10⁻²⁴ = 1.660539067 × 10⁻²⁴ mol

1 molecule = 1.660539067 × 10⁻²⁴ moles.
