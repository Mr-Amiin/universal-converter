About Converting Moles per Kilogram to Millimoles per Kilogram

Mole per kilogram and millimole per kilogram are both units of molality. One mole per kilogram is a molality of one mole of solute per kilogram of solvent. A millimole per kilogram is 10⁻³ mol of solute per kilogram of solvent.

Formula

mmol/kg = mol/kg × 1000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 mole per kilogram equals 1000 millimoles per kilogram, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 mol/kg × 1000 = 1000 mmol/kg

1 mole per kilogram = 1000 millimoles per kilogram.

Real-world example

10 mol/kg × 1000 = 10000 mmol/kg

10 moles per kilogram = 10000 millimoles per kilogram.
