Frequently asked questions

What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the chemistry converter category.

What is the difference between mass concentration and molarity?

Mass concentration expresses solute mass per volume (such as grams per liter), while molarity expresses the amount of substance per volume (moles per liter).

Are parts-per-million values exact?

Parts-per-million and parts-per-billion values here are approximated for dilute aqueous solutions, treating 1 ppm as roughly 1 milligram per liter of water.
