Frequently asked questions

How many ounces per us gallon are in one nanomole?

One Nanomole equals 1.3353×10^-10 ounces per us gallon on this converter's fixed reference assumption.

Why is nanomole used in chemistry?

The nanomole is used in molecular biology and pharmacology for very small reagent or drug quantities.

Is this conversion exact?

Not for a specific real substance. Nanomole measures amount of substance while ounce per us gallon measures mass-based concentration, and converting between them genuinely requires the molar mass of the exact chemical involved. This page uses a fixed reference assumption, so treat the result as illustrative rather than substance-specific.

Can I use this conversion for any chemical?

Not directly -- because it crosses between amount-of-substance and mass-based concentration, the real-world factor depends on the molar mass of whatever chemical you're working with. Use this page as a reference, then substitute the actual molar mass for precise lab work.
