About Converting Percent to Parts per Million

Percent and parts per million are both units of fraction. One percent is one part in a hundred: a dimensionless ratio of 0.01. One part per million is a dimensionless ratio of 10⁻⁶.

Formula

ppm = % × 10000

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 percent equals 10000 parts per million, and the reverse factor is 0.0001.

Simple example

1 % × 10000 = 10000 ppm

1 percent = 10000 parts per million.

Real-world example

10 % × 10000 = 100000 ppm

10 percents = 100000 parts per million.

Convention used on this page

Dimensionless fraction conversion: 1 ppm = 0.0001 %. It does not assume a solution density; the site's water-based ppm pages use 1 ppm ≈ 1 mg/L in water.
