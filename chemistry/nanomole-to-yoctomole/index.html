Frequently asked questions

How many yoctomoles are in one nanomole?

One Nanomole equals 1×10^15 yoctomoles.

Why is nanomole used in chemistry?

The nanomole is used in molecular biology and pharmacology for very small reagent or drug quantities.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both nanomoles and yoctomoles measure the same kind of quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor.

Can I use this conversion for any chemical?

When would you use nanomoles instead of yoctomoles?

Nanomole is typically used in used in molecular biology and pharmacology for very small reagent or drug quantities, while yoctomole is typically used in an SI prefix approaching the scale of individual molecules, used only in theoretical or specialized single-particle physics contexts.
