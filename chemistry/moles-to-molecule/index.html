About Converting Moles to Molecules (Avogadro)

Mole and molecule are both units of amount. The mole is the SI unit of amount of substance: exactly 6.02214076 × 10²³ elementary entities. One molecule, counted as an amount of substance, is 1/N_A mole, where the Avogadro constant N_A = 6.02214076 × 10²³ per mole (exact).

Formula

molecule = mol × 6.02214076 × 10²³

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 mole equals 6.02214076 × 10²³ molecules, and the reverse factor is 1.660539067 × 10⁻²⁴.

Simple example

1 mol × 6.02214076 × 10²³ = 6.02214076 × 10²³ molecule

1 mole = 6.02214076 × 10²³ molecules.

Real-world example

10 mol × 6.02214076 × 10²³ = 6.02214076 × 10²⁴ molecule

10 moles = 6.02214076 × 10²⁴ molecules.
