How many ounces per liter are in one pound per microliter?

One Pound per microliter equals 1.6×10^7 ounces per liter.

Why is pound per microliter used in chemistry?

Pound per microliter is used specifically when used in analytical and biochemical work with very small sample volumes, expressed via a US customary mass unit used in bulk industrial concentration reporting in the United States.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both pounds per microliter and ounces per liter measure the same kind of quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor.

Can I use this conversion for any chemical?

Yes. Since pound per microliter and ounce per liter both measure the same kind of quantity, this factor applies regardless of which chemical is involved.
