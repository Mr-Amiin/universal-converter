Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram to ounce?

Use the formula oz = g × 0.0352739619496. One gram equals 0.0352739619496 ounces.

What is Gram?

The gram is a metric unit of mass equal to one-thousandth of a kilogram.

What is Ounce?

The ounce is a customary unit of mass used for food, postal items, and small packages.

Is the gram to ounce conversion exact?

Yes. Gram and Ounce are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

How do I convert ounce back to gram?

Use the reverse formula g = oz ÷ 0.0352739619496. This site's reverse converter does the same calculation automatically.

About this conversion

This weight converter turns gram into ounce using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. A 250 g chocolate bar weighs about 8.82 ounces. The gram traces back to the late 18th century French metric reform, originally defined as the mass of one cubic centimeter of water. The avoirdupois ounce descends from Roman and medieval English trade weights and was fixed relative to the pound in the 1959 international agreement. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same gram-to-ounce factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
