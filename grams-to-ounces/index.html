Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the gram (g), a metric unit of mass equal to one-thousandth of a kilogram.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the ounce (oz), a customary unit of mass commonly used for food portions, postal items, and small packages.

How do I convert grams to ounces?

Multiply the number of grams by 0.0352739619 to get ounces. For example, 100 g × 0.0352739619 = 3.527396 oz.

Is the gram to ounce conversion exact?

Yes. Since the ounce is defined as exactly 28.349523125 grams, the factor used here is exact, not rounded.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

It's widely used in cooking and baking, food nutrition labels, jewelry and precious-metal weighing, and postal or courier shipments.

Can I convert ounces back to grams?

Yes. Multiply the ounce value by 28.349523125 to get the equivalent value in grams.

About this conversion

This page converts grams (g), a metric unit of mass, into ounces (oz), a customary unit of mass used mainly in the United States.

Gram-to-ounce conversions are essential in cooking and baking, food packaging labels, jewelry weighing, and small-parcel postage.

The formula is oz = g × 0.0352739619, derived from the exact definition of the avoirdupois ounce as 28.349523125 grams.

Because the ounce is defined precisely in terms of the gram through the international avoirdupois system, this conversion is exact.

The gram is part of the International System of Units (SI), while the avoirdupois ounce is standardized under the same 1959 international agreement that defines the pound.
