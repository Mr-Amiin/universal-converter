Frequently asked questions

What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the angle converter category.

Is the degree to radian conversion exact?

Yes. One full turn is exactly 2π radians, or 360 degrees, so the conversion factor is a fixed mathematical constant rather than an approximation.

Can I convert radians back to degrees?

Yes. Use the reverse converter to multiply radians by 180/π and get degrees.
