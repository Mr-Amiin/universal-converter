About Converting Quadrants to Radians

Quadrant and radian are both units of plane angle. A quadrant is a quarter of a full circle: 90 degrees or π/2 radians. The radian is the SI unit of plane angle: the angle subtended by an arc equal to the radius.

Formula

rad = quadrant × 1.570796327

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 quadrant equals 1.570796327 radians, and the reverse factor is 0.6366197724.

Simple example

1 quadrant × 1.570796327 = 1.570796327 rad
