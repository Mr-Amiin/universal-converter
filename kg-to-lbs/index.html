About this conversion

This weight converter turns kilogram into pound using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. An adult who weighs 70 kg weighs about 154.32 lbs. The kilogram was redefined in 2019 to be based on the Planck constant rather than a physical platinum-iridium cylinder kept in France since 1889. The international avoirdupois pound was standardized in 1959 by agreement between the US and Commonwealth countries at exactly 0.45359237 kg. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same kilogram-to-pound factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
