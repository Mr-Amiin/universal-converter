Frequently asked questions

What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the force converter category.

What is the SI unit of force?

The newton (N) is the SI derived unit of force, defined as the force needed to accelerate one kilogram at one meter per second squared.

How is pound-force different from pound mass?

Pound-force is a unit of force under standard gravity, while the pound most people use day to day is a unit of mass. The converter keeps these distinct.
