About Converting Tons-force (US) to Kilonewtons

Ton-force (US) and kilonewton are both units of force. A US (short) ton-force is 2,000 pound-force, about 8,896.44 newtons. A kilonewton is 1,000 newtons.

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 ton-force (US) equals 8.896443231 kilonewtons, and the reverse factor is 0.1124044715.

Understanding the Ton-force (US)

Ton-force (US) (tonf) measures force. A US (short) ton-force is 2,000 pound-force, about 8,896.44 newtons.

Standard and definition

The ton-force (US) is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the ton-force (US) today

  • US structural and mechanical load ratings
  • Press and testing-machine capacities in US specifications

Understanding the Kilonewton

Kilonewton (kN) measures force. A kilonewton is 1,000 newtons.

Standard and definition

The kilonewton is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the kilonewton today

  • Structural loads, anchors and rope ratings
  • Civil and mechanical design
