About Converting Newtons to Poundals

Newton and poundal are both units of force. The newton is the SI unit of force: one kilogram-meter per second squared. A poundal is the force that accelerates one pound by one foot per second squared: 0.138254954376 newtons.

Formula

pdl = N × 7.233013851

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 newton equals 7.233013851 poundals, and the reverse factor is 0.1382549544.

Simple example

1 N × 7.233013851 = 7.233013851 pdl

1 newton = 7.233013851 poundals.

Real-world example

10 N × 7.233013851 = 72.33013851 pdl

10 newtons = 72.33013851 poundals.
