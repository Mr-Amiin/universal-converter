Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the zeptonewton (zN). A zeptonewton is a metric submultiple of the newton equal to 10^-21 newtons.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the kilogram-force (kgf). The kilogram-force (kgf) is the force exerted by standard gravity on a mass of one kilogram.

How do I convert zeptonewton to kilogram-force?

Multiply the zeptonewton value by 1.019716e-22. For example, 5 zN × 1.019716e-22 = 5.098580e-22 kgf.

Why do zeptonewton and kilogram-force need converting at all?

Zepto (z) represents 10^-21, one of the smallest SI prefixes in practical use. Kilo (k) represents 10^3 and is the most familiar SI prefix in everyday use. The kilogram-force is the force exerted by standard gravity on a one-kilogram mass, still used in some engineering fields.

Is the zeptonewton to kilogram-force conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between zeptonewton and kilogram-force is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.019716e-22), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert kilogram-force back to zeptonewton?

Yes. Multiply the kilogram-force value by 9.806652e+21 (or divide by 1.019716e-22) to get zeptonewton.
