About Converting Grams-force to Millinewtons

Gram-force and millinewton are both units of force. A gram-force is the force exerted by one gram of mass under standard gravity: 0.00980665 newtons. A millinewton is one thousandth of a newton.

Formula

mN = gf × 9.80665

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 gram-force equals 9.80665 millinewtons, and the reverse factor is 0.1019716213.

Simple example

1 gf × 9.80665 = 9.80665 mN

1 gram-force = 9.80665 millinewtons.

Real-world example

10 gf × 9.80665 = 98.0665 mN

10 gram-forces = 98.0665 millinewtons.

Understanding the Gram-force

Gram-force (gf) measures force. A gram-force is the force exerted by one gram of mass under standard gravity: 0.00980665 newtons.

Standard and definition

The gram-force is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the gram-force today

  • Small-force measurement such as load cells, springs and sensor specifications
  • Stylus tracking force and tension gauges

Understanding the Millinewton

Millinewton (mN) measures force. A millinewton is one thousandth of a newton.

Standard and definition

The millinewton is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the millinewton today

  • Very small forces such as sensors, surface tension and micro-actuators
  • Laboratory force measurement
