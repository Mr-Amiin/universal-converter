How do I convert giganewton to attonewton?

Why do giganewton and attonewton need converting at all?

Giga (G) represents 10^9, familiar from gigabyte and gigahertz measurements. Atto (a) represents 10^-18, used for atomic and subatomic-scale quantities.

Is the giganewton to attonewton conversion exact?

Can I convert attonewton back to giganewton?
