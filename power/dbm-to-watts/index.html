About Converting dBm to Watts

dBm and watt are both units of power. dBm is a logarithmic power level referenced to one milliwatt: P(dBm) = 10 · log₁₀(P / 1 mW). The watt is the SI unit of power: one joule per second.

Formula

P(W) = 10^(P(dBm) ÷ 10) ÷ 1000

Raise 10 to the power of dBm ÷ 10 to get milliwatts, then divide by 1,000 for watts (and scale to the target unit).

Simple example

-30 dBm = 0.000001 W

-30 dBm = 0.000001 watts.

Real-world example

Understanding the dBm

dBm (dBm) measures power. dBm is a logarithmic power level referenced to one milliwatt: P(dBm) = 10 · log₁₀(P / 1 mW).

Convention: Logarithmic conversion with 1 mW = 0 dBm. Watts and milliwatts must be positive to have a finite dBm value.

Standard and definition

The dBm is covered by IEC 60027-3 — Logarithmic and related quantities, and their units.

Uses of the dBm today

  • Radio-frequency, fiber-optic and Wi-Fi signal levels
  • Antenna, amplifier and link-budget calculations

Understanding the Watt

Watt (W) measures power. The watt is the SI unit of power: one joule per second.

Standard and definition

The watt is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the watt today

  • Electrical and mechanical power
  • Lighting and appliances
