About Converting Watts to Joules per Second

Watt and joule per second are both units of power. The watt is the SI unit of power: one joule per second. One joule per second is one watt.

Formula

J/s = W × 1

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 watt equals 1 joules per second, and the reverse factor is 1.

Simple example

1 W × 1 = 1 J/s

1 watt = 1 joules per second.

Real-world example

10 W × 1 = 10 J/s

10 watts = 10 joules per second.
