About Converting Kilowatts to Boiler Horsepower

Kilowatt and boiler horsepower are both units of power. A kilowatt is 1,000 watts. A boiler horsepower is 33,475 BTU per hour, about 9.81 kilowatts.

Formula

bhp = kW × 0.1019310421

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 kilowatt equals 0.1019310421 boiler horsepowers, and the reverse factor is 9.810554074.

Simple example

1 kW × 0.1019310421 = 0.1019310421 bhp

1 kilowatt = 0.1019310421 boiler horsepowers.

Real-world example

10 kW × 0.1019310421 = 1.019310421 bhp

10 kilowatts = 1.019310421 boiler horsepowers.

Convention used on this page

33,475 BTU (International Table) per hour, the traditional boiler horsepower (≈ 34.5 lb of water evaporated per hour from and at 212 °F). It is not the mechanical horsepower (745.7 W).

Understanding the Boiler horsepower

Boiler horsepower (bhp) measures power. A boiler horsepower is 33,475 BTU per hour, about 9.81 kilowatts.

Convention: 33,475 BTU (International Table) per hour, the traditional boiler horsepower (≈ 34.5 lb of water evaporated per hour from and at 212 °F). It is not the mechanical horsepower (745.7 W).

Standard and definition

The boiler horsepower is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the boiler horsepower today

  • Rating steam and hot-water boilers
  • Boiler efficiency and fuel-use calculations
