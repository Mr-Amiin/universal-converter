About Converting Kilocalories per Hour to Watts

Kilocalorie per hour and watt are both units of power. One International Table kilocalorie per hour is 1.163 watts. The watt is the SI unit of power: one joule per second.

Formula

W = kcal/h × 1.163

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 kilocalorie per hour equals 1.163 watts, and the reverse factor is 0.8598452279.

Simple example

1 kcal/h × 1.163 = 1.163 W

1 kilocalorie per hour = 1.163 watts.

Real-world example

10 kcal/h × 1.163 = 11.63 W

10 kilocalories per hour = 11.63 watts.

Understanding the Kilocalorie per hour

Kilocalorie per hour (kcal/h) measures power. One International Table kilocalorie per hour is 1.163 watts.

Convention: International Table kilocalorie (4,186.8 J), so 1 kcal/h = 1.163 W. The thermochemical kilocalorie would give 1.1622 W.

Standard and definition

The kilocalorie per hour is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the kilocalorie per hour today

  • HVAC and heating capacity ratings in Europe, Asia and Latin America
  • Heat-load calculations for boilers and chillers

Understanding the Watt

Watt (W) measures power. The watt is the SI unit of power: one joule per second.

Standard and definition

The watt is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the watt today
