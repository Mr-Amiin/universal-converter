About Converting Watts to Kilocalories per Hour

Watt and kilocalorie per hour are both units of power. The watt is the SI unit of power: one joule per second. One International Table kilocalorie per hour is 1.163 watts.

Formula

kcal/h = W × 0.8598452279

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 watt equals 0.8598452279 kilocalories per hour, and the reverse factor is 1.163.

Simple example

1 W × 0.8598452279 = 0.8598452279 kcal/h

1 watt = 0.8598452279 kilocalories per hour.

Real-world example

10 W × 0.8598452279 = 8.598452279 kcal/h

10 watts = 8.598452279 kilocalories per hour.

Understanding the Kilocalorie per hour

Standard and definition

The kilocalorie per hour is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the kilocalorie per hour today

  • HVAC and heating capacity ratings in Europe, Asia and Latin America
  • Heat-load calculations for boilers and chillers
