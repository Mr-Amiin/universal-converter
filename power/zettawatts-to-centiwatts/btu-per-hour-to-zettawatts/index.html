Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the btu per hour (BTU/h).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the zettawatt (ZW).

How do I convert btu per hour to zettawatt?

Multiply the btu per hour value by 2.930710e-22. For example, 5 BTU/h × 2.930710e-22 = 1.465355e-21 ZW.

Why do btu per hour and zettawatt need converting at all?

BTU per hour is a standard unit used in this category's conversions. Zetta (Z) represents 10^21 and is mainly used for extremely large scientific quantities.

Is the btu per hour to zettawatt conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between btu per hour and zettawatt is a fixed, defined ratio (× 2.930710e-22), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert zettawatt back to btu per hour?

Yes. Multiply the zettawatt value by 3.412142e+21 (or divide by 2.930710e-22) to get btu per hour.

About this conversion

This page converts btu per hour (BTU/h) into zettawatt (ZW) within the power converter, using the fixed relationship 1 BTU/h = 2.930710e-22 ZW.

Worked example: to convert 10 BTU/h to ZW, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 2.930710e-22 = 2.930710e-21 ZW.

A 100-BTU/h appliance uses power equivalent to about 2.930710e-20 ZW.

Reverse conversion: to go from zettawatt back to btu per hour, divide by 2.930710e-22 (equivalently, multiply by 3.412142e+21).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
