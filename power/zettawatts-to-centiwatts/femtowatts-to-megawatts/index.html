Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the femtowatt (fW).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the megawatt (MW).

How do I convert femtowatt to megawatt?

Multiply the femtowatt value by 1.000000e-21. For example, 5 fW × 1.000000e-21 = 5.000000e-21 MW.

Why do femtowatt and megawatt need converting at all?

Femto (f) represents 10^-15, used in particle physics and ultrafast optics. Mega (M) represents 10^6, one of the most commonly used SI prefixes.

Is the femtowatt to megawatt conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between femtowatt and megawatt is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-21), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert megawatt back to femtowatt?

Yes. Multiply the megawatt value by 1.000000e+21 (or divide by 1.000000e-21) to get femtowatt.

About this conversion

This page converts femtowatt (fW) into megawatt (MW) within the power converter, using the fixed relationship 1 fW = 1.000000e-21 MW.

Worked example: to convert 10 fW to MW, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1.000000e-21 = 1.000000e-20 MW.

A 100-fW appliance uses power equivalent to about 1.000000e-19 MW.

Reverse conversion: to go from megawatt back to femtowatt, divide by 1.000000e-21 (equivalently, multiply by 1.000000e+21).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
