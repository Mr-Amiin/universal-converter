About Converting Milliwatts to dBm

Milliwatt and dBm are both units of power. A milliwatt is one thousandth of a watt. dBm is a logarithmic power level referenced to one milliwatt: P(dBm) = 10 · log₁₀(P / 1 mW).

Formula

P(dBm) = 10 · log₁₀(P in mW)

Convert the power to milliwatts, take the base-10 logarithm and multiply by 10.

Simple example

0.001 mW = -30 dBm

0.001 milliwatts = -30 dBm.

Real-world example

0.1 mW = -10 dBm

0.1 milliwatts = -10 dBm.
