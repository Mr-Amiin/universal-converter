About Converting Boiler Horsepower to Kilowatts

Boiler horsepower and kilowatt are both units of power. A boiler horsepower is 33,475 BTU per hour, about 9.81 kilowatts. A kilowatt is 1,000 watts.

Formula

kW = bhp × 9.810554074

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 boiler horsepower equals 9.810554074 kilowatts, and the reverse factor is 0.1019310421.

Simple example

1 bhp × 9.810554074 = 9.810554074 kW

1 boiler horsepower = 9.810554074 kilowatts.

Real-world example

Understanding the Boiler horsepower

Boiler horsepower (bhp) measures power. A boiler horsepower is 33,475 BTU per hour, about 9.81 kilowatts.

Convention: 33,475 BTU (International Table) per hour, the traditional boiler horsepower (≈ 34.5 lb of water evaporated per hour from and at 212 °F). It is not the mechanical horsepower (745.7 W).

Standard and definition

The boiler horsepower is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the boiler horsepower today

  • Rating steam and hot-water boilers
  • Boiler efficiency and fuel-use calculations

Understanding the Kilowatt

Kilowatt (kW) measures power. A kilowatt is 1,000 watts.

Standard and definition

The kilowatt is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the kilowatt today

  • Motor, engine and appliance power ratings
  • Electricity generation capacity
