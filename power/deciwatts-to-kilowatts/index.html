Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the deciwatt (dW).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the kilowatt (kW).

How do I convert deciwatt to kilowatt?

Multiply the deciwatt value by 0.0001. For example, 5 dW × 0.0001 = 0.0005 kW.

Why do deciwatt and kilowatt need converting at all?

Deci (d) represents 10^-1, familiar from the decibel and deciliter. Kilo (k) represents 10^3 and is the most familiar SI prefix in everyday use.

Is the deciwatt to kilowatt conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between deciwatt and kilowatt is a fixed, defined ratio (× 0.0001), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert kilowatt back to deciwatt?

Yes. Multiply the kilowatt value by 10000 (or divide by 0.0001) to get deciwatt.

About this conversion

This page converts deciwatt (dW) into kilowatt (kW) within the power converter, using the fixed relationship 1 dW = 0.0001 kW.

Worked example: to convert 10 dW to kW, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 0.0001 = 0.001 kW.

A 100-dW appliance uses power equivalent to about 0.01 kW.

Reverse conversion: to go from kilowatt back to deciwatt, divide by 0.0001 (equivalently, multiply by 10000).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
