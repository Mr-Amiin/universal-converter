Formula

1 square yard = 0.0083612736 ares

a = yd² × 0.0083612736

yd² = a ÷ 0.0083612736

Worked example

One square yard (0.8361 m² in absolute terms) is roughly the size of a sheet of A4 paper, while one are (100 m²) is about the size of a one-bedroom apartment -- so converting square yard to are means moving 0.008361x across that scale.

How many ares are in one square yard?

One Square yard equals 0.008361 ares.

Why is the square yard used for this kind of measurement?

Carpet, turf, and fabric are traditionally sold by the square yard in the US and UK, so the unit persists in those retail and installation trades even where other measurements have gone metric.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between square yards and ares, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

How does a square yard compare in size to an are?

By definition, one square yard is 119.599005 times smaller than one are (Square yard = 0.836127 m², Are = 100 m²).

When is square yards to ares conversion commonly used?

It commonly comes up in carpet and flooring sales, landscaping, and some construction material estimates in the US and UK, as well as small garden plots and allotments in parts of Europe, and as the base multiple from which the hectare is derived.

About this conversion

Square yards and Ares are both units used to measure area. A square yard is the area of a square with sides one yard long, defined exactly as 0.83612736 square meters. An are is a metric unit of area equal to 100 square meters, or a square 10 meters on each side. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 square yard = 0.0083612736 ares (equivalently 1 are = 119.5990046 square yards), so results stay accurate for flooring, landscaping, carpeting and construction, as well as small land plots and gardening in metric countries.
