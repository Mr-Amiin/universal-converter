About this conversion

Square feet and Townships are both units used to measure area. A square foot is the area of a square with sides one foot long, defined exactly as 0.09290304 square meters. A township is a US Public Land Survey System unit equal to 36 sections, or 36 square miles. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 square foot = 9.963906744×10^-10 townships (equivalently 1 township = 1.0036224×10^9 square feet), so results stay accurate for construction, real estate listings and interior design, as well as US land surveying, cadastral mapping and rural land administration.
