Formula

1 square millimeter = 0.0000107639 square feet

ft² = mm² × 0.0000107639

mm² = ft² ÷ 0.0000107639

Worked example

One square millimeter (1e-06 m² in absolute terms) is roughly the area of a pinhead, while one square foot (0.0929 m²) is about the size of a smartphone screen -- so converting square millimeter to square foot means moving 1.076391×10^-5x across that scale.

About this conversion

Square millimeters and Square feet are both units used to measure area. A square millimeter is a metric area unit equal to a square with sides one millimeter long (1×10^-6 times the area of a square meter). A square foot is the area of a square with sides one foot long, defined exactly as 0.09290304 square meters. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 square millimeter = 0.0000107639 square feet (equivalently 1 square foot = 92903.04 square millimeters), so results stay accurate for microscopy, nanotechnology, materials science and precision scientific research, as well as construction, real estate listings and interior design.
