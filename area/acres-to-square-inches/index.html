Frequently asked questions

How many square inches are in one acre?

One Acre equals 6.27264×10^6 square inches.

Why is the acre used for this kind of measurement?

Because the acre predates the metric system and is deeply embedded in US and UK property law, deeds, and zoning codes, it remains the standard land-area unit in those countries even though most of the world uses hectares.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between acres and square inches, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

How does an acre compare in size to a square inch?

By definition, one acre is 6.27264×10^6 times larger than one square inch (Acre = 4046.86 m², Square inch = 0.00064516 m²).

When is acres to square inches conversion commonly used?

It commonly comes up in real estate listings, farmland and ranch sizing, and land surveying in the United States, United Kingdom, and several Commonwealth countries, as well as engineering drawings, small manufactured parts, screen and material specifications, and tire contact-patch measurements.

About this conversion

Acres and Square inches are both units used to measure area. An acre is a traditional land area unit used in the US and UK, historically defined as one furlong by one chain. A square inch is the area of a square with sides one inch long. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 acre = 6272640 square inches (equivalently 1 square inch = 1.594225079×10^-7 acres), so results stay accurate for agriculture, land surveying, real estate and farmland measurement, as well as small parts, electronics, fabric and materials engineering.
