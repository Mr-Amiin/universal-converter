About this conversion

Townships and Hectares are both units used to measure area. A township is a US Public Land Survey System unit equal to 36 sections, or 36 square miles. A hectare is a metric unit of land area equal to 10,000 square meters, or a square 100 meters on each side. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 township = 9323.957197 hectares (equivalently 1 hectare = 0.0001072506 townships), so results stay accurate for US land surveying, cadastral mapping and rural land administration, as well as agriculture, forestry and international land measurement.
