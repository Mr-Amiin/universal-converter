How do I convert acres to square meters?

About this conversion

Acres and Square meters are both units used to measure area. An acre is a traditional land area unit used in the US and UK, historically defined as one furlong by one chain. A square meter is the SI derived unit of area, the area of a square with 1-meter sides. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 acre = 4046.856422 square meters (equivalently 1 square meter = 0.0002471054 acres), so results stay accurate for agriculture, land surveying, real estate and farmland measurement, as well as everyday construction, real estate, flooring and general area measurement.
