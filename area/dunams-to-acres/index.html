Understanding the Dunam

Dunam (dunam) measures area. A dunam is 1,000 square meters (0.1 hectare) in its standardized metric form.

Convention: the standardized metric dunam of exactly 1,000 m². Historical Ottoman dunams varied by region and are not used here.

Standard and definition

The dunam is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the dunam today

  • Land registration and agricultural land measurement in Türkiye, Israel, Jordan, Iraq and neighbouring countries
  • Property listings and farm-size statistics in the Eastern Mediterranean
