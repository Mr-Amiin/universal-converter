Worked example

About this conversion

Ares and Square kilometers are both units used to measure area. An are is a metric unit of area equal to 100 square meters, or a square 10 meters on each side. A square kilometer is a metric area unit equal to a square with sides one kilometer long (1×10^6 times the area of a square meter). This converter uses the precise relationship 1 are = 0.0001 square kilometers (equivalently 1 square kilometer = 10000 ares), so results stay accurate for small land plots and gardening in metric countries, as well as geography, astronomy, satellite mapping and large-scale scientific measurement.
