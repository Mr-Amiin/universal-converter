About this conversion

Square nanometers and Square yoctometers are both units used to measure area. A square nanometer is a metric area unit equal to a square with sides one nanometer long (1×10^-18 times the area of a square meter). A square yoctometer is a metric area unit equal to a square with sides one yoctometer long (1×10^-48 times the area of a square meter). This converter uses the precise relationship 1 square nanometer = 1×10^30 square yoctometers (equivalently 1 square yoctometer = 1×10^-30 square nanometers), so results stay accurate for microscopy, nanotechnology, materials science and precision scientific research.
