Frequently asked questions

How many acres are in one square meter?

One Square meter equals 0.000247 acres.

Why is the acre used for this kind of measurement?

Because the acre predates the metric system and is deeply embedded in US and UK property law, deeds, and zoning codes, it remains the standard land-area unit in those countries even though most of the world uses hectares.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses the exact defined relationship between square meters and acres, so the result shown is mathematically precise (rounded only for display).

How does a square meter compare in size to an acre?

By definition, one square meter is 4046.856422 times smaller than one acre (Square meter = 1 m², Acre = 4046.86 m²).

When is square meters to acres conversion commonly used?

It commonly comes up in the SI base unit for area, used in construction, real estate, and everyday science, as well as real estate listings, farmland and ranch sizing, and land surveying in the United States, United Kingdom, and several Commonwealth countries.

About this conversion

Square meters and Acres are both units used to measure area. A square meter is the SI derived unit of area, the area of a square with 1-meter sides. An acre is a traditional land area unit used in the US and UK, historically defined as one furlong by one chain. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 square meter = 0.0002471054 acres (equivalently 1 acre = 4046.856422 square meters), so results stay accurate for everyday construction, real estate, flooring and general area measurement, as well as agriculture, land surveying, real estate and farmland measurement.
