About this conversion

Square ronnameters and Square miles are both units used to measure area. A square ronnameter is a metric area unit equal to a square with sides one ronnameter long (1×10^54 times the area of a square meter). A square mile is the area of a square with sides one mile long, equal to 640 acres. This converter uses the precise relationship 1 square ronnameter = 3.861021585×10^47 square miles (equivalently 1 square mile = 2.58998811×10^-48 square ronnameters), so results stay accurate for geography, astronomy, satellite mapping and large-scale scientific measurement, as well as geography, city and regional planning, and large land or territory measurement.
