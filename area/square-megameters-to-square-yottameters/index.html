Formula

1 square megameter = 1×10^-36 square yottameters

Ym² = Mm² × 1×10^-36

Mm² = Ym² ÷ 1×10^-36

Worked example

One square megameter (1e+12 m² in absolute terms) is roughly the land area of a large country such as Egypt, while one square yottameter (1e+48 m²) is about the surface area covered by the Milky Way's galactic disk -- so converting square megameter to square yottameter means moving 1×10^-36x across that scale.

About this conversion

Square megameters and Square yottameters are both units used to measure area. A square megameter is a metric area unit equal to a square with sides one megameter long (1×10^12 times the area of a square meter). A square yottameter is a metric area unit equal to a square with sides one yottameter long (1×10^48 times the area of a square meter). This converter uses the precise relationship 1 square megameter = 1×10^-36 square yottameters (equivalently 1 square yottameter = 1×10^36 square megameters), so results stay accurate for geography, astronomy, satellite mapping and large-scale scientific measurement.
