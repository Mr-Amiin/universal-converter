About the Dram per Cup to Slug per Pint conversion

Dram per Cup (dr/cup) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. A US customary cup is 236.5882365 milliliters. Slug per Pint (slug/pt) is a density unit: A slug is an imperial mass unit. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. The slug is the coherent mass unit in the foot-pound-second system, defined so that 1 lbf accelerates 1 slug at 1 ft/s^2.
