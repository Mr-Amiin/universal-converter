Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per milliliter of volume.

Why is milliliter used to measure volume here?

Milli- (m), from the Latin 'mille' (thousand), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and among the most widely used today.

How do I convert long ton per cubic yard back to decigram per milliliter?
