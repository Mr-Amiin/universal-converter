Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per cubic meter to pound per cubic meter?

Multiply the grain per cubic meter value by 0.0001428571429, since 1 gr/m³ = 0.0001428571429 lb/m³. This works because grain per cubic meter and pound per cubic meter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Grain in kg / Cubic meter in liters) to (Pound in kg / Cubic meter in liters).

How do I convert pound per cubic meter back to grain per cubic meter?

Multiply by 7000, or equivalently divide by 0.0001428571429, since 1 lb/m³ = 7000 gr/m³.

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is cubic meter used as the volume unit?

A cubic meter is the SI derived unit of volume, equal to 1,000 liters. Cubic meter shows up most often in civil engineering, shipping, and international trade.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the grain per cubic meter to pound per cubic meter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on one side and US retail, shipping, construction, and everyday commerce on the other, or whenever a cubic meter-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic meter terms.
