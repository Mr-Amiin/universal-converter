Formula & worked example

Short ton per Liter and Troy ounce per Teaspoon both describe mass per unit volume, so converting between them is a single multiplication step.

oz t/tsp = ton/L × 143.76

ton/L = oz t/tsp × 0.00695603

Worked example

  1. Start with a sample density of 2 ton/L.
  2. Apply the formula: 2 × 143.76 = 287.52.
  3. Result: 2 ton/L is equal to 287.52 oz t/tsp.
