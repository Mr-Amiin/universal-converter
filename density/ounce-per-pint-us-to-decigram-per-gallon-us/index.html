Ounce per Pint vs Decigram per Gallon

Ounce per Pint is the larger density unit here: it takes 2267.962 dg/gal to equal one ounce per pint. Ounce per Pint is built from ounce (An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound.) divided by pint (A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.), while Decigram per Gallon is built from decigram (Decigram is 0.1 gram units.) divided by gallon (A US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters.). Both are imperial/US customary combinations, typically used in imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations.
