Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Liter: Defined in 1795 as part of the metric system, originally tied to the volume of a cubic decimeter of water.

Carat: Named for the carob seed once used by gem traders as a natural weight reference, the metric carat was fixed at 200 mg by international agreement in 1907.

Teaspoon: A traditional US kitchen-measurement unit standardized in the 20th century.
