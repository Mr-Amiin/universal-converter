Formula & worked example

Slug per Cubic Centimeter and Metric ton per Cup both describe mass per unit volume, so converting between them is a single multiplication step.

t/cup = slug/cm3 × 3.45275

slug/cm3 = t/cup × 0.289625

  3. Result: 60 slug/cm3 is equal to 207.165 t/cup.
