Formula

gr/tbsp = cg/tbsp x 0.1543235835

cg/tbsp = gr/tbsp / 0.1543235835

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 cg/tbsp. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 0.1543235835 = 1.929044794. So 12.5 cg/tbsp is equal to 1.929044794 gr/tbsp.

As a reference point, fresh water (about 1000 kg/m³) works out to roughly 1,479 cg/tbsp, which equals about 228.2 gr/tbsp using this same factor.

Conversion accuracy note: the 0.1543235835 factor is derived from exact SI mass and volume definitions (double-precision, ~15-17 significant digits); figures on this page are rounded to 6-10 significant figures for readability, which is far finer than any practical measurement uncertainty.
