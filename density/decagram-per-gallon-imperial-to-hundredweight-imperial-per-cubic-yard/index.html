Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per imperial gallon to imperial hundredweight per cubic yard?

Multiply the decagram per imperial gallon value by 0.0331044868 to get the equivalent in imperial hundredweight per cubic yard, since 1 dag/imp gal = 0.0331044868 long cwt/yd3.

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per imperial gallon of volume.

Why is imperial gallon used to measure volume here?

The imperial gallon was defined in 1824 as the volume of 10 pounds of water and remains standard in the UK.

How do I convert imperial hundredweight per cubic yard back to decagram per imperial gallon?

Divide by 0.0331044868, or multiply by 30.20738567, since 1 long cwt/yd3 = 30.20738567 dag/imp gal.

What has a density close to 1 dag/imp gal?
