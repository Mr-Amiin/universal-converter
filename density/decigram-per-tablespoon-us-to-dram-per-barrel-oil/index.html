Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per tablespoon of volume.

How do I convert dram per oil barrel back to decigram per tablespoon?

About the Decigram per Tablespoon to Dram per Oil barrel conversion

Decigram per Tablespoon (dg/tbsp) is a density unit: Decigram is 0.1 gram units. A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce. Dram per Oil barrel (dr/bbl) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. An oil barrel is 42 US gallons.

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. The 42-US-gallon oil barrel became the industry standard in Pennsylvania's early oil fields in the 1860s and is still used to quote crude oil prices.
