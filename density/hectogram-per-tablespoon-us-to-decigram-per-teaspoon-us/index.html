Hectogram vs Decigram

1 hectogram = 1000 decigram(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Decigram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Decigram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in deci (0.1x gram) amounts.

Tablespoon vs Teaspoon

1 tablespoon = 3 teaspoon(s). Tablespoon history: Standardized from the everyday dining tablespoon used for serving and measuring food. Teaspoon history: Standardized as one-third tablespoon for consistent US recipe measuring. Typical use: Tablespoon is common in recipes and food science, while Teaspoon is common in recipes, medicine dosing.
