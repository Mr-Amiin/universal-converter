Unit history

Milligram: A decimal-prefixed SI unit built on the gram, part of the metric system formalized in 18th-century France.

Cubic foot: Derived from the British imperial foot, the cubic foot has been used in English-speaking construction and engineering since before 19th-century standardization.

Carat: Named for the carob seed once used by gem traders as a natural weight reference, the metric carat was fixed at 200 mg by international agreement in 1907.

Milliliter: Defined in 1795 as part of the metric system, originally tied to the volume of a cubic centimeter of water.
