Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Long ton

1 us hundredweight = 0.04464285714 long ton(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Long ton history: The traditional British Imperial ton, still used in some shipping and commodity contexts. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Long ton is common in UK/Commonwealth shipping and bulk trade.

Imperial gallon vs Fluid ounce

1 imperial gallon = 153.7215905 fluid ounce(s). Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Fluid ounce history: Originally the volume of an avoirdupois ounce of water; still standard on US nutrition labels. Typical use: Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures, while Fluid ounce is common in US beverage and nutrition labeling.
