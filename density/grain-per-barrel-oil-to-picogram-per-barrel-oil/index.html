Picogram (pg): A picogram is one trillionth of a gram.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per barrel (oil) to picogram per barrel (oil)?

Multiply the grain per barrel (oil) value by 6.479891e+10, since 1 gr/bbl = 6.479891e+10 pg/bbl. This works because grain per barrel (oil) and picogram per barrel (oil) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Grain in kg / Barrel (oil) in liters) to (Picogram in kg / Barrel (oil) in liters).

How do I convert picogram per barrel (oil) back to grain per barrel (oil)?

Multiply by 0, or equivalently divide by 6.479891e+10, since 1 pg/bbl = 0 gr/bbl.

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is barrel (oil) used as the volume unit?

The oil barrel is fixed at 42 US gallons and is the standard unit for pricing crude oil and petroleum products. Barrel (oil) shows up most often in petroleum production, refining, and commodity trading.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the grain per barrel (oil) to picogram per barrel (oil) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on one side and DNA/RNA quantification and ultra-trace analytical chemistry on the other, or whenever a barrel (oil)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in barrel (oil) terms.
