Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per milliliter to carat per pint?

Multiply the decagram per milliliter value by 23658.82365 to get the equivalent in carat per pint, since 1 dag/mL = 23658.82365 ct/pt.

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per milliliter of volume.

Why is milliliter used to measure volume here?

Milli- (m), from the Latin 'mille' (thousand), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and among the most widely used today.

How do I convert carat per pint back to decagram per milliliter?

What has a density close to 1 dag/mL?

About the Decagram per Milliliter to Carat per Pint conversion

Decagram per Milliliter (dag/mL) is a density unit: Decagram is 10 gram units. Milliliter is 0.001 liter units. Carat per Pint (ct/pt) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. Milli- (m), from the Latin 'mille' (thousand), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and among the most widely used today. The carat is named after the carob seed, historically used by gem traders as a natural weight reference; it was fixed at 200 mg internationally in 1907.
