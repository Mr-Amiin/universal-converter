Ounce per Cubic Centimeter vs Short ton per Pint

Short ton per Pint is the larger density unit here: it takes 67.62805 units of ounce per cubic centimeter to equal one short ton per pint. Ounce per Cubic Centimeter is built from ounce (An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound.) divided by cubic centimeter (Cubic metric volume unit.), while Short ton per Pint is built from short ton (A US short ton is 2,000 pounds.) divided by pint (A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.). Both are imperial/US customary combinations, typically used in imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations.

Unit background

Ounce: An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound. Cubic Centimeter: Cubic metric volume unit.

Short ton: A US short ton is 2,000 pounds. Pint: A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

Density as a quantity (mass divided by volume) dates to Archimedes' work on buoyancy in the 3rd century BCE. Modern unit systems pair a mass unit with a volume unit to express it, which is why density has so many valid unit combinations — this page covers one specific pairing.
