Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Tablespoon (US) (tbsp): The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons.

Hectogram (hg): A hectogram equals one hundred grams.

Cubic meter (m³): A cubic meter is the SI derived unit of volume, equal to 1,000 liters.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (676.280454) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Gram = 0.001 kg, Tablespoon (US) = 0.01478676478 L, Hectogram = 0.1 kg, Cubic meter = 1000 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Unit comparison

Gram per Tablespoon (US) vs Hectogram per Cubic meter

  • Where used: Gram per Tablespoon (US) appears mainly in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging and cooking and recipe formulation, while hectogram per cubic meter is more common in European grocery retail and deli portioning and civil engineering, shipping, and international trade.
  • Scale: Gram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Tablespoon (US) is a small volume unit, versus a everyday-scale mass unit and large volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Gram history — Defined in 1795 as the mass of one cubic centimeter of water at its freezing point, the gram remains the everyday metric mass unit worldwide. Tablespoon (US) history — Standardized alongside the US cup by home-economics and cookbook authorities in the early 1900s.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per tablespoon (US) to hectogram per cubic meter?

Multiply the gram per tablespoon (US) value by 676.280454, since 1 g/tbsp = 676.280454 hg/m³. This works because gram per tablespoon (US) and hectogram per cubic meter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Tablespoon (US) in liters) to (Hectogram in kg / Cubic meter in liters).

How do I convert hectogram per cubic meter back to gram per tablespoon (US)?

Multiply by 0.001478676478, or equivalently divide by 676.280454, since 1 hg/m³ = 0.001478676478 g/tbsp.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is tablespoon (US) used as the volume unit?

The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons. Tablespoon (US) shows up most often in cooking and recipe formulation.

Where is the gram per tablespoon (US) to hectogram per cubic meter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging on one side and European grocery retail and deli portioning on the other, or whenever a tablespoon (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic meter terms.
