Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per liter to slug per cubic centimeter?

Multiply the decagram per liter value by 6.852177e-07 to get the equivalent in slug per cubic centimeter, since 1 dag/L = 6.852177e-07 slug/cm3.

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per liter of volume.

Why is liter used to measure volume here?

This is the unprefixed base unit with no metric multiplier applied.

How do I convert slug per cubic centimeter back to decagram per liter?

Divide by 6.852177e-07, or multiply by 1459390.294, since 1 slug/cm3 = 1459390.294 dag/L.

Is dag/L a practical everyday density unit?

1 dag/L equals about 10 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
