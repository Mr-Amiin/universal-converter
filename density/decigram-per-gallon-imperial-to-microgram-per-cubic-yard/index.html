Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decigram per imperial gallon to microgram per cubic yard?

Multiply the decigram per imperial gallon value by 16817855.74 to get the equivalent in microgram per cubic yard, since 1 dg/imp gal = 16817855.74 ug/yd3.

Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per imperial gallon of volume.

Why is imperial gallon used to measure volume here?

The imperial gallon was defined in 1824 as the volume of 10 pounds of water and remains standard in the UK.

How do I convert microgram per cubic yard back to decigram per imperial gallon?

Divide by 16817855.74, or multiply by 5.946061e-08, since 1 ug/yd3 = 5.946061e-08 dg/imp gal.

Is dg/imp gal a practical everyday density unit?

1 dg/imp gal equals about 0.02199692483 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
