Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Ounce

1 us hundredweight = 1600 ounce(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Ounce history: Derived from the Roman uncia ("twelfth part"); still standard in US retail packaging. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Ounce is common in food packaging, postal weight, US recipes.

Cubic millimeter vs Gallon

1 cubic millimeter = 0.0000002641720524 gallon(s). Cubic millimeter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Gallon history: Derived from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches. Typical use: Cubic millimeter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in cubic millimeter amounts, while Gallon is common in US fuel, milk, and liquid retail measures.
