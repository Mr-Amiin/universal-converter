Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Imperial hundredweight

1 us hundredweight = 0.8928571429 imperial hundredweight(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade.

Imperial gallon vs Quart

1 imperial gallon = 4.803799702 quart(s). Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Quart history: From the French "quart", meaning a fourth part of a gallon. Typical use: Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures, while Quart is common in US cooking and liquid retail.
