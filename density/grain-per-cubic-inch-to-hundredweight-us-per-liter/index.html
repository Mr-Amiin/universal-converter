Unit definitions

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Cubic inch (in³): A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides.

Hundredweight (short) (cwt (short)): The US (short) hundredweight equals 100 pounds and underlies the short ton (2,000 lb).

Liter (L): The liter is the standard metric unit of volume, equal to one cubic decimeter.

Unit comparison

Grain per Cubic inch vs Hundredweight (short) per Liter

  • Where used: Grain per Cubic inch appears mainly in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology and engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances, while hundredweight (short) per liter is more common in US grain, livestock, and steel pricing and virtually all metric-system science, industry, and retail.
  • Scale: Grain is a extremely small mass unit and Cubic inch is a small volume unit, versus a large mass unit and everyday-scale volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Grain history — Grain-based mass standards appear in nearly every historical weight system; it survives today mainly for bullet and arrow-point mass and pharmaceutical dosing. Cubic inch history — Widely used historically to rate engine displacement in US and UK automotive industries.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per cubic inch to hundredweight (short) per liter?

Multiply the grain per cubic inch value by 8.717677728e-05, since 1 gr/in³ = 8.717677728e-05 cwt (short)/L. This works because grain per cubic inch and hundredweight (short) per liter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Grain in kg / Cubic inch in liters) to (Hundredweight (short) in kg / Liter in liters).

How do I convert hundredweight (short) per liter back to grain per cubic inch?

Multiply by 11470.9448, or equivalently divide by 8.717677728e-05, since 1 cwt (short)/L = 11470.9448 gr/in³.

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is cubic inch used as the volume unit?

A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides. Cubic inch shows up most often in engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the grain per cubic inch to hundredweight (short) per liter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on one side and US grain, livestock, and steel pricing on the other, or whenever a cubic inch-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in liter terms.
