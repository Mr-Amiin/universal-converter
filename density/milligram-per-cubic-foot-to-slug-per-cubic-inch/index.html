Unit history

Milligram: A decimal-prefixed SI unit built on the gram, part of the metric system formalized in 18th-century France.

Cubic foot: Derived from the British imperial foot, the cubic foot has been used in English-speaking construction and engineering since before 19th-century standardization.

Slug: Defined in the early 20th century as the mass accelerated at 1 ft/s2 by 1 pound-force, the slug completes the foot-pound-second (FPS) system used in US engineering.

Cubic inch: Derived from the British imperial inch, the cubic inch has been used in English-speaking engineering and manufacturing since before 19th-century standardization.
