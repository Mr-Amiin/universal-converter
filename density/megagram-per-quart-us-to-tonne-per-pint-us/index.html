Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Quart: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon, standardized for cooking and retail trade.

Metric ton: The tonne (metric ton) came into common use in 19th-century industry as a convenient large metric mass unit equal to 1,000 kilograms.

Pint: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon, standardized for cooking, brewing, and retail trade.

Why is megagram per quart used to express density?

Megagram measures mass (Megagram is 1000000 gram units.) and quart measures volume (A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.), so megagram per quart expresses how much mass is packed into that volume — the definition of density.
