Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per milliliter to gram per cubic inch?

Multiply the decagram per milliliter value by 163.87064 to get the equivalent in gram per cubic inch, since 1 dag/mL = 163.87064 g/in3.

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per milliliter of volume.

Why is milliliter used to measure volume here?

Milli- (m), from the Latin 'mille' (thousand), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and among the most widely used today.

How do I convert gram per cubic inch back to decagram per milliliter?

Divide by 163.87064, or multiply by 0.006102374409, since 1 g/in3 = 0.006102374409 dag/mL.

What has a density close to 1 dag/mL?

1 dag/mL is about 10000 kg/m3, which is the same order of magnitude as silver (roughly 10490 kg/m3, or about 1.049 dag/mL).
