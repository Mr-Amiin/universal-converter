Milligram per Quart vs Stone per Cubic Centimeter

Stone per Cubic Centimeter is the larger density unit here: it takes 6.009619e+09 units of milligram per quart to equal one stone per cubic centimeter. Milligram per Quart is built from milligram (Milligram is 0.001 gram units.) divided by quart (A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.), while Stone per Cubic Centimeter is built from stone (A stone is 14 pounds.) divided by cubic centimeter (Cubic metric volume unit.). Both are imperial/US customary combinations, typically used in imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations.

Unit background

Milligram: Milligram is 0.001 gram units. Quart: A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

Stone: A stone is 14 pounds. Cubic Centimeter: Cubic metric volume unit.

Density as a quantity (mass divided by volume) dates to Archimedes' work on buoyancy in the 3rd century BCE. Modern unit systems pair a mass unit with a volume unit to express it, which is why density has so many valid unit combinations — this page covers one specific pairing.
