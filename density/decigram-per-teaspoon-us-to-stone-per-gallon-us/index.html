Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per teaspoon of volume.

How do I convert stone per gallon back to decigram per teaspoon?

About the Decigram per Teaspoon to Stone per Gallon conversion

Decigram per Teaspoon (dg/tsp) is a density unit: Decigram is 0.1 gram units. A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon. Stone per Gallon (st/gal) is a density unit: A stone is 14 pounds. A US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters.

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. The stone was standardized to 14 pounds in England in 1835 and remains in everyday use for body weight in the UK and Ireland. The US gallon derives from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches, still used for fuel and milk in the US.
