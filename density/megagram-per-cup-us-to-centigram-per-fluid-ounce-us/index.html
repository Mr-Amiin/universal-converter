Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Cup: Standardized in the 20th century as a US kitchen-measurement unit equal to half a US pint.

Centigram: A decimal-prefixed SI unit built on the gram, part of the metric system formalized in 18th-century France.

Fluid ounce: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon used for small liquid quantities in cooking and retail.
