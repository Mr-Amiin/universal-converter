Unit definitions

Hectogram (hg): A hectogram equals one hundred grams.

Cubic inch (in³): A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides.

Carat (ct): A carat is the standard mass unit for gemstones and pearls, fixed at exactly 200 milligrams.

Cubic yard (yd³): A cubic yard is the volume of a cube with 1-yard sides, commonly used for bulk materials like concrete and soil.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (23328000) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Hectogram = 0.1 kg, Cubic inch = 0.016387064 L, Carat = 0.0002 kg, Cubic yard = 764.554858 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Unit comparison

Hectogram per Cubic inch vs Carat per Cubic yard

  • Where used: Hectogram per Cubic inch appears mainly in European grocery retail and deli portioning and engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances, while carat per cubic yard is more common in gemology, jewelry appraisal, and diamond grading and concrete, landscaping, and bulk earthwork estimating.
  • Scale: Hectogram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Cubic inch is a small volume unit, versus a small mass unit and large volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Hectogram history — The hectogram uses the metric 'hecto-' (x100) prefix and is a common everyday unit for food portions in several European countries. Cubic inch history — Widely used historically to rate engine displacement in US and UK automotive industries.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert hectogram per cubic inch to carat per cubic yard?

Multiply the hectogram per cubic inch value by 23328000, since 1 hg/in³ = 23328000 ct/yd³. This works because hectogram per cubic inch and carat per cubic yard both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Hectogram in kg / Cubic inch in liters) to (Carat in kg / Cubic yard in liters).

How do I convert carat per cubic yard back to hectogram per cubic inch?

Multiply by 4.286694102e-08, or equivalently divide by 23328000, since 1 ct/yd³ = 4.286694102e-08 hg/in³.

Why is hectogram used to measure mass here?

A hectogram equals one hundred grams. It is common in European grocery retail and deli portioning, which is why densities are often reported using hectogram.

Why is cubic inch used as the volume unit?

A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides. Cubic inch shows up most often in engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances.

Where is the hectogram per cubic inch to carat per cubic yard conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between European grocery retail and deli portioning on one side and gemology, jewelry appraisal, and diamond grading on the other, or whenever a cubic inch-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic yard terms.
