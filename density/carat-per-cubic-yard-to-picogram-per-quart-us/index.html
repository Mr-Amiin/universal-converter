From definition

Carat per Cubic yard (ct/yd3) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. A cubic yard is exactly 0.764554857984 cubic meters.

To definition

Picogram per Quart (pg/qt) is a density unit: Picogram is 1e-12 gram units. A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

pg/qt = ct/yd3 x 2.475566e+08
