From definition

Centigram per Quart (cg/qt) is a density unit: Centigram is 0.01 gram units. A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

To definition

Long ton per Pint (long ton/pt) is a density unit: A British long ton is 2,240 pounds. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

long ton/pt = cg/qt x 4.921033e-09
