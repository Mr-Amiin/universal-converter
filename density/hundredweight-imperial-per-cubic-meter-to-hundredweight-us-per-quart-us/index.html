Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs US hundredweight

1 imperial hundredweight = 1.12 us hundredweight(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock).

Cubic meter vs Quart

1 cubic meter = 1056.688209 quart(s). Cubic meter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Quart history: From the French "quart", meaning a fourth part of a gallon. Typical use: Cubic meter is common in everyday and industrial cubic-meter volume measurement, while Quart is common in US cooking and liquid retail.
