Formula

kg/fl oz = cg/ft3 x 1.044379e-08

cg/ft3 = kg/fl oz / 1.044379e-08

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 cg/ft3. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 1.044379e-08 = 1.305474e-07. So 12.5 cg/ft3 is equal to 1.305474e-07 kg/fl oz.

As a reference point, fresh water (about 1000 kg/m³) works out to roughly 2,831,685 cg/ft3, which equals about 0.02957 kg/fl oz using this same factor.

Conversion accuracy note: the 1.04437934e-08 factor is derived from exact SI mass and volume definitions (double-precision, ~15-17 significant digits); figures on this page are rounded to 6-10 significant figures for readability, which is far finer than any practical measurement uncertainty.
