About the Dram per Pint to Hectogram per Tablespoon conversion

Dram per Pint (dr/pt) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon. Hectogram per Tablespoon (hg/tbsp) is a density unit: Hectogram is 100 gram units. A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. Hecto- (h), from the Greek 'hekaton' (hundred), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, though rarely used outside the hectare.
