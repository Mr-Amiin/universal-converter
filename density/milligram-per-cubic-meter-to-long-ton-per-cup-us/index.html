Unit history

Milligram: A decimal-prefixed SI unit built on the gram, part of the metric system formalized in 18th-century France.

Cubic Meter: A coherent SI volume unit derived directly from the meter, the base unit of length.

Long ton: The 2,240-pound 'long ton' (or imperial ton) was the traditional British ton, based on 20 hundredweight of 112 pounds each.

Cup: Standardized in the 20th century as a US kitchen-measurement unit equal to half a US pint.
