Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Grain

1 us hundredweight = 700000 grain(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Grain history: Historically based on the mass of a single grain of barley; the oldest unit still in common use today. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Grain is common in ammunition/gunpowder charges, archery, pharmacy.

Imperial gallon vs Tablespoon

1 imperial gallon = 307.4431809 tablespoon(s). Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Tablespoon history: Standardized from the everyday dining tablespoon used for serving and measuring food. Typical use: Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures, while Tablespoon is common in recipes and food science.
