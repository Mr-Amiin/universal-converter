Ounce per Cubic yard vs US hundredweight per Quart

US hundredweight per Quart is the larger density unit here: it takes 1292634 units of ounce per cubic yard to equal one us hundredweight per quart. Ounce per Cubic yard is built from ounce (An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound.) divided by cubic yard (A cubic yard is exactly 0.764554857984 cubic meters.), while US hundredweight per Quart is built from us hundredweight (A US short hundredweight is 100 pounds.) divided by quart (A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.). Both are imperial/US customary combinations, typically used in imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations.

Unit background

Ounce: An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound. Cubic yard: A cubic yard is exactly 0.764554857984 cubic meters.

US hundredweight: A US short hundredweight is 100 pounds. Quart: A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

Density as a quantity (mass divided by volume) dates to Archimedes' work on buoyancy in the 3rd century BCE. Modern unit systems pair a mass unit with a volume unit to express it, which is why density has so many valid unit combinations — this page covers one specific pairing.
