Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Dram

1 us hundredweight = 25600 dram(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Dram history: From the Greek drachma, once both a coin and a small weight unit. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Dram is common in pharmacy dosing, small-parts weighing.

Fluid ounce vs Gallon

1 fluid ounce = 0.0078125 gallon(s). Fluid ounce history: Originally the volume of an avoirdupois ounce of water; still standard on US nutrition labels. Gallon history: Derived from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches. Typical use: Fluid ounce is common in US beverage and nutrition labeling, while Gallon is common in US fuel, milk, and liquid retail measures.
