Formula

ng/mL = ct/ft3 x 7062.933344

ct/ft3 = ng/mL / 7062.933344

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 ct/ft3. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 7062.933344 = 88286.6668. So 12.5 ct/ft3 is equal to 88286.6668 ng/mL.

Conversion accuracy note: the 7062.933344 factor is derived from exact SI mass and volume definitions (double-precision, ~15-17 significant digits); figures on this page are rounded to 6-10 significant figures for readability, which is far finer than any practical measurement uncertainty.
