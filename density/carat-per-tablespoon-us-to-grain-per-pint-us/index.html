From definition

Carat per Tablespoon (ct/tbsp) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

To definition

Grain per Pint (gr/pt) is a density unit: A grain is exactly 64.79891 milligrams. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

Formula

gr/pt = ct/tbsp x 98.76709346
