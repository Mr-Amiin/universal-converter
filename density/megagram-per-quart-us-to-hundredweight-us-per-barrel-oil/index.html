Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Quart: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon, standardized for cooking and retail trade.

US hundredweight: The US short hundredweight of 100 pounds was adopted so that 20 hundredweight equal one short ton.

Oil barrel: Standardized at 42 US gallons in 1866 by early Pennsylvania oil producers, the oil barrel remains the global unit for crude oil volume.
