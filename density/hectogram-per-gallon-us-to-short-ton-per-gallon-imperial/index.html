Unit comparison

Hectogram vs Short ton

1 hectogram = 0.0001102311311 short ton(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Short ton history: Standardized in the United States as the customary "ton", distinct from the UK long ton. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Short ton is common in US freight, construction aggregate, scrap metal.

Gallon vs Imperial gallon

1 gallon = 0.8326741846 imperial gallon(s). Gallon history: Derived from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Gallon is common in US fuel, milk, and liquid retail measures, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
