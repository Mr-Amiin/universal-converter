How the formula works

Both kilogram per teaspoon and pound per cup measure the same physical quantity (mass per unit volume), so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in kilogram per teaspoon by 105.821886 to get pound per cup. To reverse the conversion, multiply a pound per cup value by 0.00945 to get back to kilogram per teaspoon.
