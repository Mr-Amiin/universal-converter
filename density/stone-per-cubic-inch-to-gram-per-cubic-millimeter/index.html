How do you convert stone per cubic inch to gram per cubic millimeter?

Why use st/in3 instead of g/mm3?

Stone per Cubic inch is the more familiar unit in some regions or industries, while gram per cubic millimeter is easier to compare against SI-based references — this page lets you move between them instantly.

What real-world material is close to this density?

How do I convert gram per cubic millimeter back to stone per cubic inch?

Is the st/in3 to g/mm3 conversion factor exact?

Yes. Both stone and gram are defined as exact multiples of the kilogram, and both cubic inch and cubic millimeter are defined as exact multiples of the cubic meter, so the 0.387519 factor is an exact ratio rather than an approximation.
