About the Dram per Pint to Long ton per Imperial gallon conversion

Dram per Pint (dr/pt) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon. Long ton per Imperial gallon (long ton/imp gal) is a density unit: A British long ton is 2,240 pounds. An imperial gallon is exactly 4.54609 liters.

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. The long ton of 2,240 lb (20 hundredweight) remained Britain's standard ton until metrication. The imperial gallon was defined in 1824 as the volume of 10 pounds of water and remains standard in the UK.
