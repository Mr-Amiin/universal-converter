Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs Metric ton

1 imperial hundredweight = 0.05080234544 metric ton(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Metric ton history: Adopted with the metric system in 18th-century France as a convenient large-mass unit for trade and industry. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Metric ton is common in shipping, bulk commodities, heavy industry.

Quart vs Cubic yard

1 quart = 0.001237782922 cubic yard(s). Quart history: From the French "quart", meaning a fourth part of a gallon. Cubic yard history: The imperial volume unit for a cube one yard on a side, standard in bulk earthworks. Typical use: Quart is common in US cooking and liquid retail, while Cubic yard is common in concrete, soil, and landscaping materials.
