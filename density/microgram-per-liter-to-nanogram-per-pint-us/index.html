From definition

To definition

Nanogram per Pint (ng/pt) is a density unit: Nanogram is 1e-9 gram units. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

Unit history

Microgram: An SI-prefixed fraction of the gram used since the metric system's 18th-century origins for very small quantities.

Liter: Defined in 1795 as part of the metric system, originally tied to the volume of a cubic decimeter of water.

Nanogram: An SI-prefixed fraction of the gram, part of the metric system formalized in 18th-century France, used for trace-level measurement.

Pint: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon, standardized for cooking, brewing, and retail trade.
