Unit definitions

Dram (dr): An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound.

Tablespoon (US) (tbsp): The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons.

Ounce (oz): The avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 of a pound, the everyday ounce used for food and retail goods.

Barrel (oil) (bbl): The oil barrel is fixed at 42 US gallons and is the standard unit for pricing crude oil and petroleum products.

Conversion accuracy

Unit comparison

Dram per Tablespoon (US) vs Ounce per Barrel (oil)

  • Where used: Dram per Tablespoon (US) appears mainly in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing and cooking and recipe formulation, while ounce per barrel (oil) is more common in US food packaging, retail, and postal weight limits and petroleum production, refining, and commodity trading.
  • Scale: Dram is a small mass unit and Tablespoon (US) is a small volume unit, versus a everyday-scale mass unit and large volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Dram history — The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was folded into the English avoirdupois system used for everyday trade goods. Tablespoon (US) history — Standardized alongside the US cup by home-economics and cookbook authorities in the early 1900s.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per tablespoon (US) to ounce per barrel (oil)?

Multiply the dram per tablespoon (US) value by 672, since 1 dr/tbsp = 672 oz/bbl. This works because dram per tablespoon (US) and ounce per barrel (oil) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Dram in kg / Tablespoon (US) in liters) to (Ounce in kg / Barrel (oil) in liters).

How do I convert ounce per barrel (oil) back to dram per tablespoon (US)?

Multiply by 0.001488095238, or equivalently divide by 672, since 1 oz/bbl = 0.001488095238 dr/tbsp.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound. It is common in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing, which is why densities are often reported using dram.

Why is tablespoon (US) used as the volume unit?

The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons. Tablespoon (US) shows up most often in cooking and recipe formulation.

Where is the dram per tablespoon (US) to ounce per barrel (oil) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing on one side and US food packaging, retail, and postal weight limits on the other, or whenever a tablespoon (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in barrel (oil) terms.
