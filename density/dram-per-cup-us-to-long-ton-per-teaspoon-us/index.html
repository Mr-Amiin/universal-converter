About the Dram per Cup to Long ton per Teaspoon conversion

Dram per Cup (dr/cup) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. A US customary cup is 236.5882365 milliliters. Long ton per Teaspoon (long ton/tsp) is a density unit: A British long ton is 2,240 pounds. A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. The long ton of 2,240 lb (20 hundredweight) remained Britain's standard ton until metrication.
