Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per tablespoon to megagram per cup?

Multiply the decagram per tablespoon value by 0.00016 to get the equivalent in megagram per cup, since 1 dag/tbsp = 0.00016 Mg/cup.

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per tablespoon of volume.

Why is tablespoon used to measure volume here?

A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

How do I convert megagram per cup back to decagram per tablespoon?

Divide by 0.00016, or multiply by 6250, since 1 Mg/cup = 6250 dag/tbsp.

What has a density close to 1 dag/tbsp?
