Formula

ton/gal = lb/cup × 0.008

lb/cup = ton/gal ÷ 0.008 (equivalently, lb/cup = ton/gal × 125)

Step-by-step worked example

  1. Start with 12.5 lb/cup.
  2. Apply the density conversion factor: multiply by 0.008 because 1 lb/cup = 0.008 ton/gal.
  3. 12.5 x 0.008 = 0.1, so 12.5 lb/cup equals 0.1 ton/gal.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert pound per cup to short ton per gallon?

How do I convert short ton per gallon back to pound per cup?

Why is pound per cup used as a density unit?

Pound per Cup pairs pound as the mass unit (An avoirdupois pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms.) with cup as the volume unit (A US customary cup is 236.5882365 milliliters.), which makes it a natural fit wherever those two units are already standard.

Why use short ton per gallon instead of another density unit?

Short ton per Gallon combines short ton (A US short ton is 2,000 pounds.) with gallon (A US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters.), which keeps values in a convenient range for its field rather than forcing very large or very small numbers.

Why are pound per cup and short ton per gallon a moderate factor apart?

The gap comes from the underlying mass and volume unit sizes: lb vs ton for mass, and cup vs gal for volume. Multiplying those ratios together gives the 0.008 conversion factor.

How accurate is this conversion?

The factor 0.008 is computed directly from exact SI-defined mass and volume constants, not from a rounded lookup table, so it is accurate to full floating-point precision.
