From definition

Troy ounce per Fluid ounce (oz t/fl oz) is a density unit: A troy ounce is exactly 31.1034768 grams. A US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon.

To definition

Microgram per Tablespoon (ug/tbsp) is a density unit: Microgram is 0.000001 gram units. A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.
