Unit definitions

Hectogram (hg): A hectogram equals one hundred grams.

Cubic centimeter (cm³): A cubic centimeter is the volume of a 1 cm cube and is numerically identical to a milliliter.

Tonne (t): The metric tonne equals 1,000 kilograms.

Teaspoon (US) (tsp): The US teaspoon is a small standard cooking-measurement volume unit, 1/3 of a US tablespoon.

Conversion accuracy

Unit comparison

Hectogram per Cubic centimeter vs Tonne per Teaspoon (US)

  • Where used: Hectogram per Cubic centimeter appears mainly in European grocery retail and deli portioning and medical dosing, engine displacement, and laboratory science, while tonne per teaspoon (US) is more common in international shipping, bulk commodities, and manufacturing and cooking, baking, and over-the-counter medicine dosing.
  • Scale: Hectogram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Cubic centimeter is a small volume unit, versus a very large mass unit and small volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Hectogram history — The hectogram uses the metric 'hecto-' (x100) prefix and is a common everyday unit for food portions in several European countries. Cubic centimeter history — A direct geometric derivative of the metric meter, adopted for small-volume science and medicine.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert hectogram per cubic centimeter to tonne per teaspoon (US)?

Multiply the hectogram per cubic centimeter value by 0.0004928921594, since 1 hg/cm³ = 0.0004928921594 t/tsp. This works because hectogram per cubic centimeter and tonne per teaspoon (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Hectogram in kg / Cubic centimeter in liters) to (Tonne in kg / Teaspoon (US) in liters).

How do I convert tonne per teaspoon (US) back to hectogram per cubic centimeter?

Multiply by 2028.841362, or equivalently divide by 0.0004928921594, since 1 t/tsp = 2028.841362 hg/cm³.

Why is hectogram used to measure mass here?

A hectogram equals one hundred grams. It is common in European grocery retail and deli portioning, which is why densities are often reported using hectogram.

Why is cubic centimeter used as the volume unit?

A cubic centimeter is the volume of a 1 cm cube and is numerically identical to a milliliter. Cubic centimeter shows up most often in medical dosing, engine displacement, and laboratory science.

Why are such very large mass units used for density?

Tonne is very large on its own, but density conversions often need it because international shipping, bulk commodities, and manufacturing routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the hectogram per cubic centimeter to tonne per teaspoon (US) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between European grocery retail and deli portioning on one side and international shipping, bulk commodities, and manufacturing on the other, or whenever a cubic centimeter-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in teaspoon (US) terms.
