Formula & worked example

Stone per Cup and Stone per Liter both describe mass per unit volume, so converting between them is a single multiplication step.

st/L = st/cup × 4.22675

st/cup = st/L × 0.236588

Worked example

  1. Start with a sample density of 7.5 st/cup.
  2. Apply the formula: 7.5 × 4.22675 = 31.7006.
  3. Result: 7.5 st/cup is equal to 31.7006 st/L.
