Megagram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Megagram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in mega (1e+06x gram) amounts.

Cubic inch vs Imperial gallon

1 cubic inch = 0.00360465015 imperial gallon(s). Cubic inch history: Used since imperial units were standardized; still common in US engine displacement and machining. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Cubic inch is common in US engineering, machining, engine displacement, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
