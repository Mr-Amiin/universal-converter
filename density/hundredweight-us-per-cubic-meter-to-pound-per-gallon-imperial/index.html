Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Pound

1 us hundredweight = 100 pound(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Pound history: Descended from the Roman libra; the modern avoirdupois pound was fixed by international agreement in 1959. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Pound is common in US/UK retail, cooking, body weight, construction materials.

Cubic meter vs Imperial gallon

1 cubic meter = 219.9692483 imperial gallon(s). Cubic meter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Cubic meter is common in everyday and industrial cubic-meter volume measurement, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
