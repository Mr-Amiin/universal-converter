Formula & worked example

To convert slug per milliliter to centigram per tablespoon, multiply the slug/mL value by the conversion factor.

cg/tbsp = slug/mL × 2.15797e+07

slug/mL = cg/tbsp × 4.633993e-08

  1. Start with a sample density of 60 slug/mL.
