Formula & worked example

ct/gal = ng/cup × 8e-08

ng/cup = ct/gal ÷ 8e-08 (or × 1.25e+07)

For example, granite has a density of about 2700 kg/m³, which is roughly 6.38788e+11 ng/cup. Multiplying by the conversion factor: 6.38788e+11 × 8e-08 = 51103.06. So 6.38788e+11 ng/cup of granite is equal to 51103.06 ct/gal.

How do I convert carat per gallon back to nanogram per cup?

Why is the nanogram per cup to carat per gallon conversion factor so small?
