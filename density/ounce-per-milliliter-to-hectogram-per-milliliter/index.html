Formula & worked example

hg/mL = oz/mL × 0.2834952

oz/mL = hg/mL ÷ 0.2834952 (or × 3.527396)

For example, oak wood has a density of about 750 kg/m³, which is roughly 0.026455 oz/mL. Multiplying by the conversion factor: 0.026455 × 0.2834952 = 0.007499866. So 0.026455 oz/mL of oak wood is equal to 0.007499866 hg/mL.
