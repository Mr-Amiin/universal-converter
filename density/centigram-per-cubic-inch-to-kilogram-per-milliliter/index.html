Formula

kg/mL = cg/in3 x 6.102374e-07

cg/in3 = kg/mL / 6.102374e-07

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 cg/in3. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 6.102374e-07 = 7.627968012e-06. So 12.5 cg/in3 is equal to 7.627968012e-06 kg/mL.

As a reference point, fresh water (about 1000 kg/m³) works out to roughly 1,639 cg/in3, which equals about 0.001 kg/mL using this same factor.

Conversion accuracy note: the 6.102374409e-07 factor is derived from exact SI mass and volume definitions (double-precision, ~15-17 significant digits); figures on this page are rounded to 6-10 significant figures for readability, which is far finer than any practical measurement uncertainty.
