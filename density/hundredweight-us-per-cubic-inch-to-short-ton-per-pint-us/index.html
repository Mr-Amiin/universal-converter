Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Short ton

1 us hundredweight = 0.05 short ton(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Short ton history: Standardized in the United States as the customary "ton", distinct from the UK long ton. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Short ton is common in US freight, construction aggregate, scrap metal.

Cubic inch vs Pint

1 cubic inch = 0.03463203463 pint(s). Cubic inch history: Used since imperial units were standardized; still common in US engine displacement and machining. Pint history: Historically tied to the volume of a pound of water; still the standard US beer/milk measure. Typical use: Cubic inch is common in US engineering, machining, engine displacement, while Pint is common in US beverages, cooking.
