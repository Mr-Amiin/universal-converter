Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per cup to decagram per liter?

Multiply the decagram per cup value by 4.226752838 to get the equivalent in decagram per liter, since 1 dag/cup = 4.226752838 dag/L.

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per cup of volume.

Why is cup used to measure volume here?

A US customary cup is 236.5882365 milliliters.

How do I convert decagram per liter back to decagram per cup?

Divide by 4.226752838, or multiply by 0.2365882365, since 1 dag/L = 0.2365882365 dag/cup.
