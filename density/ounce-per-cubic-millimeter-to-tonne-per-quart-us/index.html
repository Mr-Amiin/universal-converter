About the Ounce per Cubic Millimeter to Metric ton per Quart conversion

Ounce per Cubic Millimeter (oz/mm3) is a density unit: Ounce is defined as an avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound. Cubic Millimeter is defined as cubic metric volume unit. Metric ton per Quart (t/qt) is a density unit: Metric ton is defined as a metric ton is 1,000 kilograms. Quart is defined as a us liquid quart is one quarter of a us gallon. Use this calculator for imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations, or any workflow that needs an accurate ounce per cubic millimeter-to-metric ton per quart density figure.

Ounce per Cubic Millimeter vs Metric ton per Quart

Ounce per Cubic Millimeter is the larger density unit here: it takes 26.82865 t/qt to equal one ounce per cubic millimeter. Ounce per Cubic Millimeter is built from ounce (An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound.) divided by cubic millimeter (Cubic metric volume unit.), while Metric ton per Quart is built from metric ton (A metric ton is 1,000 kilograms.) divided by quart (A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.). Both are imperial/US customary combinations, typically used in imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations.

Unit background

Ounce: An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound. Cubic Millimeter: Cubic metric volume unit.

Metric ton: A metric ton is 1,000 kilograms. Quart: A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

Density as a quantity (mass divided by volume) dates to Archimedes' work on buoyancy in the 3rd century BCE. Modern unit systems pair a mass unit with a volume unit to express it, which is why density has so many valid unit combinations — this page covers one specific pairing.
