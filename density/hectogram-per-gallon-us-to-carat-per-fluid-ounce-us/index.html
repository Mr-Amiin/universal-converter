Unit comparison

Hectogram vs Carat

1 hectogram = 500 carat(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Carat history: Named after the carob seed, once used by gem traders as a natural mass reference. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Carat is common in gemstones and diamonds.

Gallon vs Fluid ounce

1 gallon = 128 fluid ounce(s). Gallon history: Derived from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches. Fluid ounce history: Originally the volume of an avoirdupois ounce of water; still standard on US nutrition labels. Typical use: Gallon is common in US fuel, milk, and liquid retail measures, while Fluid ounce is common in US beverage and nutrition labeling.
