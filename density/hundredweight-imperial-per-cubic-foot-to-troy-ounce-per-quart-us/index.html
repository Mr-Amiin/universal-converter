Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs Troy ounce

1 imperial hundredweight = 1633.333333 troy ounce(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Troy ounce history: Named after Troyes, France, a medieval trading city; it became the standard for precious metals. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Troy ounce is common in gold, silver, and other precious-metal markets.

Cubic foot vs Quart

1 cubic foot = 29.92207792 quart(s). Cubic foot history: A standard imperial volume unit long used in construction and utilities. Quart history: From the French "quart", meaning a fourth part of a gallon. Typical use: Cubic foot is common in construction, HVAC, natural gas metering, while Quart is common in US cooking and liquid retail.
