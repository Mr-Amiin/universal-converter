Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Imperial gallon: Defined in 1824 as part of British imperial standardization, the imperial gallon was originally based on the volume of 10 pounds of water.

Troy ounce: Named for the French city of Troyes, the troy ounce has been the standard for weighing precious metals since medieval European trade fairs.

Cup: Standardized in the 20th century as a US kitchen-measurement unit equal to half a US pint.
