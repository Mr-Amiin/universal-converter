Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Centigram

1 us hundredweight = 4535923.7 centigram(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Centigram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Centigram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in centi (0.01x gram) amounts.

Imperial gallon vs Cubic meter

1 imperial gallon = 0.00454609 cubic meter(s). Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Cubic meter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Typical use: Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures, while Cubic meter is common in everyday and industrial cubic-meter volume measurement.
