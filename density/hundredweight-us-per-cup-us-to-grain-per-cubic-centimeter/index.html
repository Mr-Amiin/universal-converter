Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Grain

1 us hundredweight = 700000 grain(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Grain history: Historically based on the mass of a single grain of barley; the oldest unit still in common use today. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Grain is common in ammunition/gunpowder charges, archery, pharmacy.

Cup vs Cubic centimeter

1 cup = 236.5882365 cubic centimeter(s). Cup history: A kitchen-measure unit formalized in the US in the early 20th century. Cubic centimeter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Typical use: Cup is common in US home cooking and baking, while Cubic centimeter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in cubic centimeter amounts.
