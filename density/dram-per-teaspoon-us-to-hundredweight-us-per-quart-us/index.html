Formula

cwt (short)/qt = dr/tsp × 0.0075

dr/tsp = cwt (short)/qt ÷ 0.0075

Step-by-step worked example: Say a material sample measures 12.5 dr/tsp. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 × 0.0075 = 0.09375. So 12.5 dr/tsp is equal to 0.09375 cwt (short)/qt.

In context: For scale, gold has a density of about 53.74440467 dr/tsp (roughly 0.403083035 cwt (short)/qt). Materials denser than water push both numbers up; gases like air push them far below 1.

Unit definitions

Dram (dr): An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound.

Teaspoon (US) (tsp): The US teaspoon is a small standard cooking-measurement volume unit, 1/3 of a US tablespoon.

Hundredweight (short) (cwt (short)): The US (short) hundredweight equals 100 pounds and underlies the short ton (2,000 lb).

Quart (US) (qt): The US liquid quart is 1/4 of a US gallon, or two US pints.

Unit comparison

Dram per Teaspoon (US) vs Hundredweight (short) per Quart (US)

  • Where used: Dram per Teaspoon (US) appears mainly in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing and cooking, baking, and over-the-counter medicine dosing, while hundredweight (short) per quart (US) is more common in US grain, livestock, and steel pricing and US dairy, cooking, and automotive fluid packaging.
  • Scale: Dram is a small mass unit and Teaspoon (US) is a small volume unit, versus a large mass unit and everyday-scale volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Dram history — The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was folded into the English avoirdupois system used for everyday trade goods. Teaspoon (US) history — Standardized for home cooking measurement in the US alongside the tablespoon and cup.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per teaspoon (US) to hundredweight (short) per quart (US)?

Multiply the dram per teaspoon (US) value by 0.0075, since 1 dr/tsp = 0.0075 cwt (short)/qt. This works because dram per teaspoon (US) and hundredweight (short) per quart (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Dram in kg / Teaspoon (US) in liters) to (Hundredweight (short) in kg / Quart (US) in liters).

How do I convert hundredweight (short) per quart (US) back to dram per teaspoon (US)?

Multiply by 133.3333333, or equivalently divide by 0.0075, since 1 cwt (short)/qt = 133.3333333 dr/tsp.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound. It is common in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing, which is why densities are often reported using dram.

Why is teaspoon (US) used as the volume unit?

The US teaspoon is a small standard cooking-measurement volume unit, 1/3 of a US tablespoon. Teaspoon (US) shows up most often in cooking, baking, and over-the-counter medicine dosing.

Where is the dram per teaspoon (US) to hundredweight (short) per quart (US) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing on one side and US grain, livestock, and steel pricing on the other, or whenever a teaspoon (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in quart (US) terms.
