About Converting Ounces per US Gallon to Grams per Liter

Ounce per US gallon and gram per liter are both units of density. One ounce per US gallon is 0.028349523125 kg ÷ 0.003785411784 m³ ≈ 7.489152 kg/m³. One gram per liter is 1 kilogram per cubic meter.

Formula

g/L = oz/gal × 7.489151707

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 ounce per US gallon equals 7.489151707 grams per liter, and the reverse factor is 0.1335264712.
