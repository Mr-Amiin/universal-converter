st/fl oz = dr/gal × 2.179827e-06

Frequently asked questions

Why use st/fl oz instead of dr/gal?

Stone per Fluid ounce is the more familiar unit in some regions or industries, while dram per gallon is easier to compare against SI-based references — this page lets you move between them instantly.

How do you convert stone per fluid ounce to dram per gallon?

Multiply the st/fl oz value by 458752 to get dr/gal, since 1 st/fl oz = 458752 dr/gal.

How do I convert dram per gallon back to stone per fluid ounce?

Divide by 458752, or multiply by 2.179827e-06, since 1 dr/gal = 2.179827e-06 st/fl oz.

What is 1 Stone per Fluid ounce in Dram per Gallon?

1 st/fl oz equals 458752 dr/gal.

Is the st/fl oz to dr/gal conversion factor exact?

Yes. Both stone and dram are defined as exact multiples of the kilogram, and both fluid ounce and gallon are defined as exact multiples of the cubic meter, so the 458752 factor is an exact ratio rather than an approximation.
