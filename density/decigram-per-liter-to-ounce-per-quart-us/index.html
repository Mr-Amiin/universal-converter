Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decigram per liter to ounce per quart?

Multiply the decigram per liter value by 0.003338161781 to get the equivalent in ounce per quart, since 1 dg/L = 0.003338161781 oz/qt.

Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per liter of volume.

Why is liter used to measure volume here?

This is the unprefixed base unit with no metric multiplier applied.

How do I convert ounce per quart back to decigram per liter?

Divide by 0.003338161781, or multiply by 299.5660683, since 1 oz/qt = 299.5660683 dg/L.

What has a density close to 1 dg/L?

1 dg/L is about 0.1 kg/m3, which is the same order of magnitude as hydrogen gas at room temperature (roughly 0.0899 kg/m3, or about 0.899 dg/L).
