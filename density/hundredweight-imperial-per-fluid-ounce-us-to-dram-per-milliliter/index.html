Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs Dram

1 imperial hundredweight = 28672 dram(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Dram history: From the Greek drachma, once both a coin and a small weight unit. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Dram is common in pharmacy dosing, small-parts weighing.

Fluid ounce vs Milliliter

1 fluid ounce = 29.57352956 milliliter(s). Fluid ounce history: Originally the volume of an avoirdupois ounce of water; still standard on US nutrition labels. Milliliter history: Part of the metric/SI system of volume prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Fluid ounce is common in US beverage and nutrition labeling, while Milliliter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in milli (0.001x liter) amounts.
