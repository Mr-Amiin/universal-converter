Hectogram vs Picogram

1 hectogram = 100000000000000 picogram(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Picogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Picogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in pico (1e-12x gram) amounts.

Imperial gallon vs Quart

1 imperial gallon = 4.803799702 quart(s). Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Quart history: From the French "quart", meaning a fourth part of a gallon. Typical use: Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures, while Quart is common in US cooking and liquid retail.
