Formula

dag/m3 = cg/cm3 x 1000

cg/cm3 = dag/m3 / 1000

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 cg/cm3. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 1000 = 12500. So 12.5 cg/cm3 is equal to 12500 dag/m3.

As a reference point, fresh water (about 1000 kg/m³) works out to roughly 100 cg/cm3, which equals about 100,000 dag/m3 using this same factor.

Conversion accuracy note: the 1000 factor is derived from exact SI mass and volume definitions (double-precision, ~15-17 significant digits); figures on this page are rounded to 6-10 significant figures for readability, which is far finer than any practical measurement uncertainty.
