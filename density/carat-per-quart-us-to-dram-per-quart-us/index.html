From definition

Carat per Quart (ct/qt) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

To definition

Dram per Quart (dr/qt) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

dr/qt = ct/qt x 0.1128766782

Formula

dr/qt = ct/qt x 0.1128766782

ct/qt = dr/qt / 0.1128766782

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 ct/qt. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 0.1128766782 = 1.410958478. So 12.5 ct/qt is equal to 1.410958478 dr/qt.

As a reference point, fresh water (about 1000 kg/m³) works out to roughly 4,732 ct/qt, which equals about 534.1 dr/qt using this same factor.
