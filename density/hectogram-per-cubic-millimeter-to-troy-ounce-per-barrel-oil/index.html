Unit comparison

Hectogram vs Troy ounce

1 hectogram = 3.215074657 troy ounce(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Troy ounce history: Named after Troyes, France, a medieval trading city; it became the standard for precious metals. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Troy ounce is common in gold, silver, and other precious-metal markets.

Cubic millimeter vs Oil barrel

1 cubic millimeter = 0.00000000628981077 oil barrel(s). Cubic millimeter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Oil barrel history: Standardized in the 19th-century US oil industry from the volume of a typical wooden barrel. Typical use: Cubic millimeter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in cubic millimeter amounts, while Oil barrel is common in petroleum production and trading.
