Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per pint to nanogram per fluid ounce?

Multiply the decagram per pint value by 625000000 to get the equivalent in nanogram per fluid ounce, since 1 dag/pt = 625000000 ng/fl oz.

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per pint of volume.

Why is pint used to measure volume here?

A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

How do I convert nanogram per fluid ounce back to decagram per pint?

Divide by 625000000, or multiply by 1.6e-09, since 1 ng/fl oz = 1.6e-09 dag/pt.

Is dag/pt a practical everyday density unit?

1 dag/pt equals about 21.13376419 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
