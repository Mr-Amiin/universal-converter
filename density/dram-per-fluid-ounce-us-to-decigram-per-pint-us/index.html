From definition

Dram per Fluid ounce (dr/fl oz) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. A US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon.

To definition

Decigram per Pint (dg/pt) is a density unit: Decigram is 0.1 gram units. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

Formula

dg/pt = dr/fl oz x 283.4952312

About the Dram per Fluid ounce to Decigram per Pint conversion

Dram per Fluid ounce (dr/fl oz) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. A US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon. Decigram per Pint (dg/pt) is a density unit: Decigram is 0.1 gram units. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter.
