Unit definitions

Hectogram (hg): A hectogram equals one hundred grams.

Cubic inch (in³): A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides.

Hundredweight (long) (cwt (long)): The imperial (long) hundredweight equals 112 pounds, used historically in the UK and Commonwealth.

Barrel (oil) (bbl): The oil barrel is fixed at 42 US gallons and is the standard unit for pricing crude oil and petroleum products.

How do you convert hectogram per cubic inch to hundredweight (long) per barrel (oil)?

This works because hectogram per cubic inch and hundredweight (long) per barrel (oil) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Hectogram in kg / Cubic inch in liters) to (Hundredweight (long) in kg / Barrel (oil) in liters).

How do I convert hundredweight (long) per barrel (oil) back to hectogram per cubic inch?

Multiply by 0.05236275556, or equivalently divide by 19.09754346, since 1 cwt (long)/bbl = 0.05236275556 hg/in³.

Why is hectogram used to measure mass here?

Why is cubic inch used as the volume unit?

Where is the hectogram per cubic inch to hundredweight (long) per barrel (oil) conversion used?
