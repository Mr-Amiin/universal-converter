Unit definitions

Dram (dr): An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound.

Tablespoon (US) (tbsp): The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons.

Milligram (mg): A milligram is one thousandth of a gram.

Gallon (US) (gal): The US liquid gallon is the standard US volume unit for fuel, beverages, and household liquids.

Conversion accuracy

Unit comparison

Dram per Tablespoon (US) vs Milligram per Gallon (US)

  • Where used: Dram per Tablespoon (US) appears mainly in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing and cooking and recipe formulation, while milligram per gallon (US) is more common in medicine dosing, nutrition labeling, and chemistry and US fuel economy, dairy, and beverage industries.
  • Scale: Dram is a small mass unit and Tablespoon (US) is a small volume unit, versus a extremely small mass unit and everyday-scale volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Dram history — The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was folded into the English avoirdupois system used for everyday trade goods. Tablespoon (US) history — Standardized alongside the US cup by home-economics and cookbook authorities in the early 1900s.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per tablespoon (US) to milligram per gallon (US)?

Multiply the dram per tablespoon (US) value by 453592.37, since 1 dr/tbsp = 453592.37 mg/gal. This works because dram per tablespoon (US) and milligram per gallon (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Dram in kg / Tablespoon (US) in liters) to (Milligram in kg / Gallon (US) in liters).

How do I convert milligram per gallon (US) back to dram per tablespoon (US)?

Multiply by 2.204622622e-06, or equivalently divide by 453592.37, since 1 mg/gal = 2.204622622e-06 dr/tbsp.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound. It is common in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing, which is why densities are often reported using dram.

Why is tablespoon (US) used as the volume unit?

The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons. Tablespoon (US) shows up most often in cooking and recipe formulation.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Milligram is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because medicine dosing, nutrition labeling, and chemistry routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the dram per tablespoon (US) to milligram per gallon (US) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing on one side and medicine dosing, nutrition labeling, and chemistry on the other, or whenever a tablespoon (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in gallon (US) terms.
