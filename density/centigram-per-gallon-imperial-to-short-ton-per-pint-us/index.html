From definition

Centigram per Imperial gallon (cg/imp gal) is a density unit: Centigram is 0.01 gram units. An imperial gallon is exactly 4.54609 liters.

To definition

Short ton per Pint (ton/pt) is a density unit: A US short ton is 2,000 pounds. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

ton/pt = cg/imp gal x 1.147333e-09
