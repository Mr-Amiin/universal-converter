Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per cubic yard to megagram per fluid ounce?

Multiply the dram per cubic yard value by 6.853624e-11 to get the equivalent in megagram per fluid ounce, since 1 dr/yd3 = 6.853624e-11 Mg/fl oz.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. In this pairing it measures mass per cubic yard of volume.

Why is cubic yard used to measure volume here?

A cubic yard is exactly 0.764554857984 cubic meters.

How do I convert megagram per fluid ounce back to dram per cubic yard?

Divide by 6.853624e-11, or multiply by 1.459082e+10, since 1 Mg/fl oz = 1.459082e+10 dr/yd3.

Is dr/yd3 a practical everyday density unit?

1 dr/yd3 equals about 0.002317486021 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
