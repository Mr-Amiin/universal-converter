Unit comparison

Hectogram vs Milligram

1 hectogram = 100000 milligram(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Milligram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Milligram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in milli (0.001x gram) amounts.

Fluid ounce vs Imperial gallon

1 fluid ounce = 0.006505267067 imperial gallon(s). Fluid ounce history: Originally the volume of an avoirdupois ounce of water; still standard on US nutrition labels. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Fluid ounce is common in US beverage and nutrition labeling, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
