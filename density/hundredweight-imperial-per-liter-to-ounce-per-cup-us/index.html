Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs Ounce

1 imperial hundredweight = 1792 ounce(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Ounce history: Derived from the Roman uncia ("twelfth part"); still standard in US retail packaging. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Ounce is common in food packaging, postal weight, US recipes.

Liter vs Cup

1 liter = 4.226752838 cup(s). Liter history: Part of the metric/SI system of volume prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Cup history: A kitchen-measure unit formalized in the US in the early 20th century. Typical use: Liter is common in everyday liquid measurement, while Cup is common in US home cooking and baking.
