How the formula works

Both decigram per cup and carat per liter measure the same physical quantity (mass per unit volume), so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in decigram per cup by 2.113376 to get carat per liter. To reverse the conversion, multiply a carat per liter value by 0.473176 to get back to decigram per cup.
