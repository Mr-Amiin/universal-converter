Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per fluid ounce of volume.

A US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon.

About the Decigram per Fluid ounce to Centigram per Imperial gallon conversion

Decigram per Fluid ounce (dg/fl oz) is a density unit: Decigram is 0.1 gram units. A US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon. Centigram per Imperial gallon (cg/imp gal) is a density unit: Centigram is 0.01 gram units. An imperial gallon is exactly 4.54609 liters.

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. Centi- (c), from the Latin 'centum' (hundred), has been used since 1795 and is best known from the centimeter. The imperial gallon was defined in 1824 as the volume of 10 pounds of water and remains standard in the UK.
