Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Pint: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon, standardized for cooking, brewing, and retail trade.

Short ton: The 2,000-pound 'short ton' became the US standard in the 19th century, distinct from the British long ton.

Liter: Defined in 1795 as part of the metric system, originally tied to the volume of a cubic decimeter of water.
