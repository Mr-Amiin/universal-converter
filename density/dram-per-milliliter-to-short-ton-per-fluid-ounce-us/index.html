Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per milliliter to short ton per fluid ounce?

Multiply the dram per milliliter value by 5.77608e-05 to get the equivalent in short ton per fluid ounce, since 1 dr/mL = 5.77608e-05 ton/fl oz.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. In this pairing it measures mass per milliliter of volume.

Why is milliliter used to measure volume here?

Milli- (m), from the Latin 'mille' (thousand), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and among the most widely used today.

How do I convert short ton per fluid ounce back to dram per milliliter?

Divide by 5.77608e-05, or multiply by 17312.77962, since 1 ton/fl oz = 17312.77962 dr/mL.

What has a density close to 1 dr/mL?
