Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Milliliter: Defined in 1795 as part of the metric system, originally tied to the volume of a cubic centimeter of water.

Imperial hundredweight: The British long hundredweight of 112 pounds reflects the older practice of weighing goods in stones of 14 pounds (8 x 14 = 112).

Cubic Centimeter: A coherent SI volume unit derived directly from the meter, the base unit of length.
