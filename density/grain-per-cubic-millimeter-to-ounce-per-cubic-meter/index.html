Unit definitions

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Cubic millimeter (mm³): A cubic millimeter is an extremely small metric volume unit, a thousandth of a cubic centimeter.

Ounce (oz): The avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 of a pound, the everyday ounce used for food and retail goods.

Cubic meter (m³): A cubic meter is the SI derived unit of volume, equal to 1,000 liters.

Conversion accuracy

Unit comparison

Grain per Cubic millimeter vs Ounce per Cubic meter

  • Where used: Grain per Cubic millimeter appears mainly in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology and microfluidics, precision manufacturing, and materials science, while ounce per cubic meter is more common in US food packaging, retail, and postal weight limits and civil engineering, shipping, and international trade.
  • Scale: Grain is a extremely small mass unit and Cubic millimeter is a extremely small volume unit, versus a everyday-scale mass unit and large volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Grain history — Grain-based mass standards appear in nearly every historical weight system; it survives today mainly for bullet and arrow-point mass and pharmaceutical dosing. Cubic millimeter history — Used wherever the metric system needs to describe volumes far smaller than a milliliter, such as in microfabrication.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per cubic millimeter to ounce per cubic meter?

Multiply the grain per cubic millimeter value by 2285714.286, since 1 gr/mm³ = 2285714.286 oz/m³. This works because grain per cubic millimeter and ounce per cubic meter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Grain in kg / Cubic millimeter in liters) to (Ounce in kg / Cubic meter in liters).

How do I convert ounce per cubic meter back to grain per cubic millimeter?

Multiply by 4.375e-07, or equivalently divide by 2285714.286, since 1 oz/m³ = 4.375e-07 gr/mm³.

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is cubic millimeter used as the volume unit?

A cubic millimeter is an extremely small metric volume unit, a thousandth of a cubic centimeter. Cubic millimeter shows up most often in microfluidics, precision manufacturing, and materials science.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the grain per cubic millimeter to ounce per cubic meter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on one side and US food packaging, retail, and postal weight limits on the other, or whenever a cubic millimeter-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic meter terms.
