From definition

Kilogram per Liter (kg/L) is a density unit: Kilogram is 1000 gram units. A liter is one cubic decimeter.

To definition

Centigram per Tablespoon (cg/tbsp) is a density unit: Centigram is 0.01 gram units. A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

cg/tbsp = kg/L x 1478.676478

Real-world density comparison

1 Centigram per Tablespoon (cg/tbsp) equals about 0.67628 kg/m³, which is close to the density of air at sea level (about 1.225 kg/m³).
