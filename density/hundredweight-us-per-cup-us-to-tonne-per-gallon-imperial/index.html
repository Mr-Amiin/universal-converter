Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Metric ton

1 us hundredweight = 0.045359237 metric ton(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Metric ton history: Adopted with the metric system in 18th-century France as a convenient large-mass unit for trade and industry. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Metric ton is common in shipping, bulk commodities, heavy industry.

Cup vs Imperial gallon

1 cup = 0.05204213654 imperial gallon(s). Cup history: A kitchen-measure unit formalized in the US in the early 20th century. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Cup is common in US home cooking and baking, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
