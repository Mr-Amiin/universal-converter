From definition

Carat per Teaspoon (ct/tsp) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.

To definition

Troy ounce per Quart (oz t/qt) is a density unit: A troy ounce is exactly 31.1034768 grams. A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.
