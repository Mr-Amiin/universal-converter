Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decigram per teaspoon to kilogram per cubic meter?

Multiply the decigram per teaspoon value by 20.28841362 to get the equivalent in kilogram per cubic meter, since 1 dg/tsp = 20.28841362 kg/m3.

Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per teaspoon of volume.

Why is teaspoon used to measure volume here?

A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.

How do I convert kilogram per cubic meter back to decigram per teaspoon?

Divide by 20.28841362, or multiply by 0.04928921594, since 1 kg/m3 = 0.04928921594 dg/tsp.

Is dg/tsp a practical everyday density unit?
