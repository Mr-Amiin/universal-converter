Unit history

Microgram: An SI-prefixed fraction of the gram used since the metric system's 18th-century origins for very small quantities.

Cubic Millimeter: A coherent SI volume unit derived directly from the meter, used where fine spatial precision is required.

Slug: Defined in the early 20th century as the mass accelerated at 1 ft/s2 by 1 pound-force, the slug completes the foot-pound-second (FPS) system used in US engineering.

Pint: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon, standardized for cooking, brewing, and retail trade.
