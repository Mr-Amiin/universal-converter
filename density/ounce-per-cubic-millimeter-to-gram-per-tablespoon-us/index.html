Formula & worked example

g/tbsp = oz/mm3 × 419197.7

oz/mm3 = g/tbsp ÷ 419197.7 (or × 2.385509e-06)

For example, lead has a density of about 11340 kg/m³, which is roughly 0.0004 oz/mm3. Multiplying by the conversion factor: 0.0004 × 419197.7 = 167.6791. So 0.0004 oz/mm3 of lead is equal to 167.6791 g/tbsp.

About the Ounce per Cubic Millimeter to Gram per Tablespoon conversion

Ounce per Cubic Millimeter (oz/mm3) is a density unit: Ounce is defined as an avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound. Cubic Millimeter is defined as cubic metric volume unit. Gram per Tablespoon (g/tbsp) is a density unit: Gram is defined as a gram is one thousandth of a kilogram. Tablespoon is defined as a us tablespoon is half a us fluid ounce. Use this calculator for imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations, or any workflow that needs an accurate ounce per cubic millimeter-to-gram per tablespoon density figure.

Ounce per Cubic Millimeter vs Gram per Tablespoon

Ounce per Cubic Millimeter is the larger density unit here: it takes 419197.7 g/tbsp to equal one ounce per cubic millimeter. Ounce per Cubic Millimeter is built from ounce (An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound.) divided by cubic millimeter (Cubic metric volume unit.), while Gram per Tablespoon is built from gram (A gram is one thousandth of a kilogram.) divided by tablespoon (A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.). Both are imperial/US customary combinations, typically used in imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations.

Unit background

Ounce: An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound. Cubic Millimeter: Cubic metric volume unit.

Gram: A gram is one thousandth of a kilogram. Tablespoon: A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

Density as a quantity (mass divided by volume) dates to Archimedes' work on buoyancy in the 3rd century BCE. Modern unit systems pair a mass unit with a volume unit to express it, which is why density has so many valid unit combinations — this page covers one specific pairing.
