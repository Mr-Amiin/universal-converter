Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decigram per teaspoon to milligram per cubic inch?

Multiply the decigram per teaspoon value by 332.4675325 to get the equivalent in milligram per cubic inch, since 1 dg/tsp = 332.4675325 mg/in3.

Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per teaspoon of volume.

Why is teaspoon used to measure volume here?

A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.

How do I convert milligram per cubic inch back to decigram per teaspoon?

Divide by 332.4675325, or multiply by 0.0030078125, since 1 mg/in3 = 0.0030078125 dg/tsp.

Is dg/tsp a practical everyday density unit?

1 dg/tsp equals about 20.28841362 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
