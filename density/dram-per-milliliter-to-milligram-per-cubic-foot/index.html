Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per milliliter to milligram per cubic foot?

Multiply the dram per milliliter value by 50173068.58 to get the equivalent in milligram per cubic foot, since 1 dr/mL = 50173068.58 mg/ft3.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. In this pairing it measures mass per milliliter of volume.

Why is milliliter used to measure volume here?

Milli- (m), from the Latin 'mille' (thousand), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and among the most widely used today.

How do I convert milligram per cubic foot back to dram per milliliter?

Divide by 50173068.58, or multiply by 1.993101e-08, since 1 mg/ft3 = 1.993101e-08 dr/mL.

What has a density close to 1 dr/mL?

1 dr/mL is about 1771.845195 kg/m3, which is the same order of magnitude as honey (roughly 1420 kg/m3, or about 0.8014244155 dr/mL).
