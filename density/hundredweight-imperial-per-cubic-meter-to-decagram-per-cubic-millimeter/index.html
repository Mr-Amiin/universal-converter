Cubic meter vs Cubic millimeter

1 cubic meter = 1000000000 cubic millimeter(s). Cubic meter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Cubic millimeter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Typical use: Cubic meter is common in everyday and industrial cubic-meter volume measurement, while Cubic millimeter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in cubic millimeter amounts.
