From definition

Carat per Teaspoon (ct/tsp) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.

To definition

Short ton per Tablespoon (ton/tbsp) is a density unit: A US short ton is 2,000 pounds. A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

Formula

ton/tbsp = ct/tsp x 6.613868e-07

Formula

ton/tbsp = ct/tsp x 6.613868e-07

ct/tsp = ton/tbsp / 6.613868e-07

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 ct/tsp. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 6.613868e-07 = 8.267334832e-06. So 12.5 ct/tsp is equal to 8.267334832e-06 ton/tbsp.

As a reference point, fresh water (about 1000 kg/m³) works out to roughly 24.64 ct/tsp, which equals about 0.0000163 ton/tbsp using this same factor.

Conversion accuracy note: the 6.613867885e-07 factor is derived from exact SI mass and volume definitions (double-precision, ~15-17 significant digits); figures on this page are rounded to 6-10 significant figures for readability, which is far finer than any practical measurement uncertainty.
