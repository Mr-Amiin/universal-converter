Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Centigram

1 us hundredweight = 4535923.7 centigram(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Centigram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Centigram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in centi (0.01x gram) amounts.

Gallon vs Teaspoon

1 gallon = 768 teaspoon(s). Gallon history: Derived from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches. Teaspoon history: Standardized as one-third tablespoon for consistent US recipe measuring. Typical use: Gallon is common in US fuel, milk, and liquid retail measures, while Teaspoon is common in recipes, medicine dosing.
