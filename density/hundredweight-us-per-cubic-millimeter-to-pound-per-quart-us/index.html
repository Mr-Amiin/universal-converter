Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Pound

1 us hundredweight = 100 pound(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Pound history: Descended from the Roman libra; the modern avoirdupois pound was fixed by international agreement in 1959. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Pound is common in US/UK retail, cooking, body weight, construction materials.

Cubic millimeter vs Quart

1 cubic millimeter = 0.000001056688209 quart(s). Cubic millimeter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Quart history: From the French "quart", meaning a fourth part of a gallon. Typical use: Cubic millimeter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in cubic millimeter amounts, while Quart is common in US cooking and liquid retail.
