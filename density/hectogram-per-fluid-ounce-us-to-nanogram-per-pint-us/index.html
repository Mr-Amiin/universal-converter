Hectogram vs Nanogram

Nanogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Nanogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in nano (1e-09x gram) amounts.

Fluid ounce vs Pint

1 fluid ounce = 0.0625 pint(s). Fluid ounce history: Originally the volume of an avoirdupois ounce of water; still standard on US nutrition labels. Pint history: Historically tied to the volume of a pound of water; still the standard US beer/milk measure. Typical use: Fluid ounce is common in US beverage and nutrition labeling, while Pint is common in US beverages, cooking.
