Hectogram vs Imperial hundredweight

1 hectogram = 0.001968413055 imperial hundredweight(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade.

Cubic yard vs Liter

1 cubic yard = 764.554858 liter(s). Cubic yard history: The imperial volume unit for a cube one yard on a side, standard in bulk earthworks. Liter history: Part of the metric/SI system of volume prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Cubic yard is common in concrete, soil, and landscaping materials, while Liter is common in everyday liquid measurement.
