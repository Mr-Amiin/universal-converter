Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs Stone

1 imperial hundredweight = 8 stone(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Stone history: A traditional British unit standardized in the 19th century, originally based on physical stone weights used at markets. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Stone is common in body weight in the UK and Ireland.

Cubic centimeter vs Imperial gallon

1 cubic centimeter = 0.0002199692483 imperial gallon(s). Cubic centimeter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Cubic centimeter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in cubic centimeter amounts, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
