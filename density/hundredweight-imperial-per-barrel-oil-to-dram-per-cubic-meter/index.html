Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs Dram

1 imperial hundredweight = 28672 dram(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Dram history: From the Greek drachma, once both a coin and a small weight unit. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Dram is common in pharmacy dosing, small-parts weighing.

Oil barrel vs Cubic meter

1 oil barrel = 0.1589872949 cubic meter(s). Oil barrel history: Standardized in the 19th-century US oil industry from the volume of a typical wooden barrel. Cubic meter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Typical use: Oil barrel is common in petroleum production and trading, while Cubic meter is common in everyday and industrial cubic-meter volume measurement.
