Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Carat

1 us hundredweight = 226796.185 carat(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Carat history: Named after the carob seed, once used by gem traders as a natural mass reference. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Carat is common in gemstones and diamonds.

Cubic yard vs Imperial gallon

1 cubic yard = 168.1785574 imperial gallon(s). Cubic yard history: The imperial volume unit for a cube one yard on a side, standard in bulk earthworks. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Cubic yard is common in concrete, soil, and landscaping materials, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
