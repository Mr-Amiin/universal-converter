Unit comparison

Hectogram vs Centigram

1 hectogram = 10000 centigram(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Centigram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Centigram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in centi (0.01x gram) amounts.

Quart vs Gallon

1 quart = 0.25 gallon(s). Quart history: From the French "quart", meaning a fourth part of a gallon. Gallon history: Derived from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches. Typical use: Quart is common in US cooking and liquid retail, while Gallon is common in US fuel, milk, and liquid retail measures.
