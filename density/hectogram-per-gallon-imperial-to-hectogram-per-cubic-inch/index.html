Imperial gallon vs Cubic inch

1 imperial gallon = 277.4194328 cubic inch(s). Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Cubic inch history: Used since imperial units were standardized; still common in US engine displacement and machining. Typical use: Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures, while Cubic inch is common in US engineering, machining, engine displacement.
