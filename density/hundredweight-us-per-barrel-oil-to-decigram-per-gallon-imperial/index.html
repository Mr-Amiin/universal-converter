Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Decigram

1 us hundredweight = 453592.37 decigram(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Decigram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Decigram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in deci (0.1x gram) amounts.

Oil barrel vs Imperial gallon

1 oil barrel = 34.97231575 imperial gallon(s). Oil barrel history: Standardized in the 19th-century US oil industry from the volume of a typical wooden barrel. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Oil barrel is common in petroleum production and trading, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
