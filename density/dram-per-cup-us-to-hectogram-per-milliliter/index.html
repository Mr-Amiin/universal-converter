How the formula works

Both dram per cup and hectogram per milliliter measure the same physical quantity (mass per unit volume), so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in dram per cup by 7.489152e-5 to get hectogram per milliliter. To reverse the conversion, multiply a hectogram per milliliter value by 13352.647123 to get back to dram per cup.
