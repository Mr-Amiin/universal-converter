Unit definitions

Hectogram (hg): A hectogram equals one hundred grams.

Cubic foot (ft³): A cubic foot is the volume of a cube with 1-foot sides.

Centigram (cg): A centigram is one hundredth of a gram in the metric system.

Teaspoon (US) (tsp): The US teaspoon is a small standard cooking-measurement volume unit, 1/3 of a US tablespoon.
