Unit history

Microgram: An SI-prefixed fraction of the gram used since the metric system's 18th-century origins for very small quantities.

Quart: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon, standardized for cooking and retail trade.

Carat: Named for the carob seed once used by gem traders as a natural weight reference, the metric carat was fixed at 200 mg by international agreement in 1907.

Fluid ounce: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon used for small liquid quantities in cooking and retail.
