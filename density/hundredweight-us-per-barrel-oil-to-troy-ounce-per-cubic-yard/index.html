Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Troy ounce

1 us hundredweight = 1458.333333 troy ounce(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Troy ounce history: Named after Troyes, France, a medieval trading city; it became the standard for precious metals. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Troy ounce is common in gold, silver, and other precious-metal markets.

Oil barrel vs Cubic yard

1 oil barrel = 0.2079475309 cubic yard(s). Oil barrel history: Standardized in the 19th-century US oil industry from the volume of a typical wooden barrel. Cubic yard history: The imperial volume unit for a cube one yard on a side, standard in bulk earthworks. Typical use: Oil barrel is common in petroleum production and trading, while Cubic yard is common in concrete, soil, and landscaping materials.
