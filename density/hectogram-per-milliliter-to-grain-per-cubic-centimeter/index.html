Unit comparison

Hectogram vs Grain

1 hectogram = 1543.235835 grain(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Grain history: Historically based on the mass of a single grain of barley; the oldest unit still in common use today. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Grain is common in ammunition/gunpowder charges, archery, pharmacy.

Milliliter vs Cubic centimeter

1 milliliter = 1 cubic centimeter(s). Milliliter history: Part of the metric/SI system of volume prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Cubic centimeter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Typical use: Milliliter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in milli (0.001x liter) amounts, while Cubic centimeter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in cubic centimeter amounts.
