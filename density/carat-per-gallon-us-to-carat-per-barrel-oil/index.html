Formula

ct/bbl = ct/gal x 42

ct/gal = ct/bbl / 42

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 ct/gal. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 42 = 525. So 12.5 ct/gal is equal to 525 ct/bbl.

As a reference point, fresh water (about 1000 kg/m³) works out to roughly 18,927 ct/gal, which equals about 794,936 ct/bbl using this same factor.

Conversion accuracy note: the 42 factor is derived from exact SI mass and volume definitions (double-precision, ~15-17 significant digits); figures on this page are rounded to 6-10 significant figures for readability, which is far finer than any practical measurement uncertainty.
