Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs Kilogram

1 imperial hundredweight = 50.80234544 kilogram(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Kilogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Kilogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in kilo (1000x gram) amounts.

Oil barrel vs Cubic inch

1 oil barrel = 9702 cubic inch(s). Oil barrel history: Standardized in the 19th-century US oil industry from the volume of a typical wooden barrel. Cubic inch history: Used since imperial units were standardized; still common in US engine displacement and machining. Typical use: Oil barrel is common in petroleum production and trading, while Cubic inch is common in US engineering, machining, engine displacement.
