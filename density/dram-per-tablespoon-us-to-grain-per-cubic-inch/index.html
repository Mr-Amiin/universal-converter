Unit definitions

Dram (dr): An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound.

Tablespoon (US) (tbsp): The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons.

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Cubic inch (in³): A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (30.3030303) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Dram = 0.001771845195 kg, Tablespoon (US) = 0.01478676478 L, Grain = 6.479891e-05 kg, Cubic inch = 0.016387064 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Unit comparison

Dram per Tablespoon (US) vs Grain per Cubic inch

  • Where used: Dram per Tablespoon (US) appears mainly in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing and cooking and recipe formulation, while grain per cubic inch is more common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology and engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances.
  • Scale: Dram is a small mass unit and Tablespoon (US) is a small volume unit, versus a extremely small mass unit and small volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Dram history — The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was folded into the English avoirdupois system used for everyday trade goods. Tablespoon (US) history — Standardized alongside the US cup by home-economics and cookbook authorities in the early 1900s.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per tablespoon (US) to grain per cubic inch?

Multiply the dram per tablespoon (US) value by 30.3030303, since 1 dr/tbsp = 30.3030303 gr/in³. This works because dram per tablespoon (US) and grain per cubic inch both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Dram in kg / Tablespoon (US) in liters) to (Grain in kg / Cubic inch in liters).

How do I convert grain per cubic inch back to dram per tablespoon (US)?

Multiply by 0.033, or equivalently divide by 30.3030303, since 1 gr/in³ = 0.033 dr/tbsp.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound. It is common in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing, which is why densities are often reported using dram.

Why is tablespoon (US) used as the volume unit?

The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons. Tablespoon (US) shows up most often in cooking and recipe formulation.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the dram per tablespoon (US) to grain per cubic inch conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing on one side and ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on the other, or whenever a tablespoon (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic inch terms.
