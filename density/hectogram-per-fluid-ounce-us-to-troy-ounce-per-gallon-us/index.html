Unit comparison

Hectogram vs Troy ounce

1 hectogram = 3.215074657 troy ounce(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Troy ounce history: Named after Troyes, France, a medieval trading city; it became the standard for precious metals. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Troy ounce is common in gold, silver, and other precious-metal markets.

Fluid ounce vs Gallon

1 fluid ounce = 0.0078125 gallon(s). Fluid ounce history: Originally the volume of an avoirdupois ounce of water; still standard on US nutrition labels. Gallon history: Derived from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches. Typical use: Fluid ounce is common in US beverage and nutrition labeling, while Gallon is common in US fuel, milk, and liquid retail measures.
