Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Grain

1 us hundredweight = 700000 grain(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Grain history: Historically based on the mass of a single grain of barley; the oldest unit still in common use today. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Grain is common in ammunition/gunpowder charges, archery, pharmacy.

Cubic meter vs Pint

1 cubic meter = 2113.376419 pint(s). Cubic meter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Pint history: Historically tied to the volume of a pound of water; still the standard US beer/milk measure. Typical use: Cubic meter is common in everyday and industrial cubic-meter volume measurement, while Pint is common in US beverages, cooking.
