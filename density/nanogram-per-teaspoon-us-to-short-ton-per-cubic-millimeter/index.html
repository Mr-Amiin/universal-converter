About the Nanogram per Teaspoon to Short ton per Cubic Millimeter conversion

Short ton per Cubic Millimeter (ton/mm3) is a density unit: Short ton is defined as a us short ton is 2,000 pounds. Cubic Millimeter is defined as cubic metric volume unit. Use this calculator for imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations, or any workflow that needs an accurate nanogram per teaspoon-to-short ton per cubic millimeter density figure.

Nanogram per Teaspoon vs Short ton per Cubic Millimeter

Short ton per Cubic Millimeter is the larger density unit here: it takes 4.471442e+18 units of nanogram per teaspoon to equal one short ton per cubic millimeter. Nanogram per Teaspoon is built from nanogram (Nanogram is 1e-9 gram units.) divided by teaspoon (A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.), while Short ton per Cubic Millimeter is built from short ton (A US short ton is 2,000 pounds.) divided by cubic millimeter (Cubic metric volume unit.). Both are imperial/US customary combinations, typically used in imperial and US customary engineering, construction, and shipping calculations.

Unit background

Nanogram: Nanogram is 1e-9 gram units. Teaspoon: A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.

Short ton: A US short ton is 2,000 pounds. Cubic Millimeter: Cubic metric volume unit.

Density as a quantity (mass divided by volume) dates to Archimedes' work on buoyancy in the 3rd century BCE. Modern unit systems pair a mass unit with a volume unit to express it, which is why density has so many valid unit combinations — this page covers one specific pairing.
