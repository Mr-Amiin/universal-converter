Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per cubic inch to long ton per cubic inch?

Multiply the gram per cubic inch value by 9.842065276e-07, since 1 g/in³ = 9.842065276e-07 long tn/in³. This works because gram per cubic inch and long ton per cubic inch both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Cubic inch in liters) to (Long ton in kg / Cubic inch in liters).

How do I convert long ton per cubic inch back to gram per cubic inch?

Multiply by 1016046.909, or equivalently divide by 9.842065276e-07, since 1 long tn/in³ = 1016046.909 g/in³.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is cubic inch used as the volume unit?

A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides. Cubic inch shows up most often in engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances.

Why are such very large mass units used for density?

Long ton is very large on its own, but density conversions often need it because maritime shipping and UK heavy industry routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the gram per cubic inch to long ton per cubic inch conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging on one side and maritime shipping and UK heavy industry on the other, or whenever a cubic inch-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic inch terms.
