Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Pint (US) (pt): The US liquid pint is 1/8 of a US gallon.

Dram (dr): An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound.

Cubic meter (m³): A cubic meter is the SI derived unit of volume, equal to 1,000 liters.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (1192.75455) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Gram = 0.001 kg, Pint (US) = 0.473176473 L, Dram = 0.001771845195 kg, Cubic meter = 1000 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Unit comparison

Gram per Pint (US) vs Dram per Cubic meter

  • Where used: Gram per Pint (US) appears mainly in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging and US beverage retail (beer, milk) and cooking, while dram per cubic meter is more common in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing and civil engineering, shipping, and international trade.
  • Scale: Gram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Pint (US) is a everyday-scale volume unit, versus a small mass unit and large volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Gram history — Defined in 1795 as the mass of one cubic centimeter of water at its freezing point, the gram remains the everyday metric mass unit worldwide. Pint (US) history — Inherited from English liquid-measure tradition, the US pint diverged slightly from the UK pint after independence.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per pint (US) to dram per cubic meter?

Multiply the gram per pint (US) value by 1192.75455, since 1 g/pt = 1192.75455 dr/m³. This works because gram per pint (US) and dram per cubic meter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Pint (US) in liters) to (Dram in kg / Cubic meter in liters).

How do I convert dram per cubic meter back to gram per pint (US)?

Multiply by 0.0008383954602, or equivalently divide by 1192.75455, since 1 dr/m³ = 0.0008383954602 g/pt.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is pint (US) used as the volume unit?

The US liquid pint is 1/8 of a US gallon. Pint (US) shows up most often in US beverage retail (beer, milk) and cooking.

Where is the gram per pint (US) to dram per cubic meter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging on one side and pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing on the other, or whenever a pint (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic meter terms.
