Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Cubic millimeter (mm³): A cubic millimeter is an extremely small metric volume unit, a thousandth of a cubic centimeter.

Long ton (long tn): The long (imperial) ton equals 2,240 pounds, still used in UK shipping and some commodity markets.

Cubic centimeter (cm³): A cubic centimeter is the volume of a 1 cm cube and is numerically identical to a milliliter.

Unit comparison

Gram per Cubic millimeter vs Long ton per Cubic centimeter

  • Where used: Gram per Cubic millimeter appears mainly in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging and microfluidics, precision manufacturing, and materials science, while long ton per cubic centimeter is more common in maritime shipping and UK heavy industry and medical dosing, engine displacement, and laboratory science.
  • Scale: Gram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Cubic millimeter is a extremely small volume unit, versus a very large mass unit and small volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Gram history — Defined in 1795 as the mass of one cubic centimeter of water at its freezing point, the gram remains the everyday metric mass unit worldwide. Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per cubic millimeter to long ton per cubic centimeter?

Multiply the gram per cubic millimeter value by 0.0009842065276, since 1 g/mm³ = 0.0009842065276 long tn/cm³. This works because gram per cubic millimeter and long ton per cubic centimeter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Cubic millimeter in liters) to (Long ton in kg / Cubic centimeter in liters).

How do I convert long ton per cubic centimeter back to gram per cubic millimeter?

Multiply by 1016.046909, or equivalently divide by 0.0009842065276, since 1 long tn/cm³ = 1016.046909 g/mm³.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is cubic millimeter used as the volume unit?

A cubic millimeter is an extremely small metric volume unit, a thousandth of a cubic centimeter. Cubic millimeter shows up most often in microfluidics, precision manufacturing, and materials science.

Why are such very large mass units used for density?

Long ton is very large on its own, but density conversions often need it because maritime shipping and UK heavy industry routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the gram per cubic millimeter to long ton per cubic centimeter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging on one side and maritime shipping and UK heavy industry on the other, or whenever a cubic millimeter-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic centimeter terms.
