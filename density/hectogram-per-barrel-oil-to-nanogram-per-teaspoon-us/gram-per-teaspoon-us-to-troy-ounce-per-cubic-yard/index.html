Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Teaspoon (US) (tsp): The US teaspoon is a small standard cooking-measurement volume unit, 1/3 of a US tablespoon.

Troy ounce (oz t): The troy ounce is a heavier ounce (about 10% more than the avoirdupois ounce) used exclusively for precious metals and gemstones.

Cubic yard (yd³): A cubic yard is the volume of a cube with 1-yard sides, commonly used for bulk materials like concrete and soil.
