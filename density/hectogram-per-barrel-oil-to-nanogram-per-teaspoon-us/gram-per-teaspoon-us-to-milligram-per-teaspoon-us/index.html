Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per teaspoon (US) to milligram per teaspoon (US)?

Multiply the gram per teaspoon (US) value by 1000, since 1 g/tsp = 1000 mg/tsp. How do I convert milligram per teaspoon (US) back to gram per teaspoon (US)?

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is teaspoon (US) used as the volume unit?

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Where is the gram per teaspoon (US) to milligram per teaspoon (US) conversion used?
