Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Cubic inch (in³): A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides.

Hundredweight (short) (cwt (short)): The US (short) hundredweight equals 100 pounds and underlies the short ton (2,000 lb).

Cubic meter (m³): A cubic meter is the SI derived unit of volume, equal to 1,000 liters.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (1.345343267) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Gram = 0.001 kg, Cubic inch = 0.016387064 L, Hundredweight (short) = 45.359237 kg, Cubic meter = 1000 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Unit comparison

Gram per Cubic inch vs Hundredweight (short) per Cubic meter

  • Where used: Gram per Cubic inch appears mainly in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging and engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances, while hundredweight (short) per cubic meter is more common in US grain, livestock, and steel pricing and civil engineering, shipping, and international trade.
  • Scale: Gram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Cubic inch is a small volume unit, versus a large mass unit and large volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Gram history — Defined in 1795 as the mass of one cubic centimeter of water at its freezing point, the gram remains the everyday metric mass unit worldwide. Cubic inch history — Widely used historically to rate engine displacement in US and UK automotive industries.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per cubic inch to hundredweight (short) per cubic meter?

Multiply the gram per cubic inch value by 1.345343267, since 1 g/in³ = 1.345343267 cwt (short)/m³. This works because gram per cubic inch and hundredweight (short) per cubic meter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Cubic inch in liters) to (Hundredweight (short) in kg / Cubic meter in liters).

How do I convert hundredweight (short) per cubic meter back to gram per cubic inch?

Multiply by 0.7433047197, or equivalently divide by 1.345343267, since 1 cwt (short)/m³ = 0.7433047197 g/in³.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is cubic inch used as the volume unit?

Where is the gram per cubic inch to hundredweight (short) per cubic meter conversion used?
