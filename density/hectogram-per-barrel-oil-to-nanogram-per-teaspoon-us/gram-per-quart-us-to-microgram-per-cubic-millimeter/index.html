Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Quart (US) (qt): The US liquid quart is 1/4 of a US gallon, or two US pints.

Microgram (µg): A microgram is one millionth of a gram.

Cubic millimeter (mm³): A cubic millimeter is an extremely small metric volume unit, a thousandth of a cubic centimeter.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per quart (US) to microgram per cubic millimeter?

Multiply the gram per quart (US) value by 1.056688209, since 1 g/qt = 1.056688209 µg/mm³. This works because gram per quart (US) and microgram per cubic millimeter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Quart (US) in liters) to (Microgram in kg / Cubic millimeter in liters).

How do I convert microgram per cubic millimeter back to gram per quart (US)?

Multiply by 0.946352946, or equivalently divide by 1.056688209, since 1 µg/mm³ = 0.946352946 g/qt.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is quart (US) used as the volume unit?

The US liquid quart is 1/4 of a US gallon, or two US pints. Quart (US) shows up most often in US dairy, cooking, and automotive fluid packaging.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Microgram is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because pharmacology, toxicology, and trace-contaminant testing routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the gram per quart (US) to microgram per cubic millimeter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging on one side and pharmacology, toxicology, and trace-contaminant testing on the other, or whenever a quart (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic millimeter terms.
