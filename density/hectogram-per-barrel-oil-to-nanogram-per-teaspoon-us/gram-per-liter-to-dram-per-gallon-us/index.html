Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Liter (L): The liter is the standard metric unit of volume, equal to one cubic decimeter.

Dram (dr): An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound.

Gallon (US) (gal): The US liquid gallon is the standard US volume unit for fuel, beverages, and household liquids.

Unit comparison

Gram per Liter vs Dram per Gallon (US)

  • Where used: Gram per Liter appears mainly in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging and virtually all metric-system science, industry, and retail, while dram per gallon (US) is more common in pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing and US fuel economy, dairy, and beverage industries.
  • Scale: Gram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Liter is a everyday-scale volume unit, versus a small mass unit and everyday-scale volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Gram history — Defined in 1795 as the mass of one cubic centimeter of water at its freezing point, the gram remains the everyday metric mass unit worldwide. Liter history — Introduced with the metric system in 1795 and redefined in terms of the cubic decimeter in 1964 for exactness.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per liter to dram per gallon (US)?

Multiply the gram per liter value by 2.13642354, since 1 g/L = 2.13642354 dr/gal. This works because gram per liter and dram per gallon (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Liter in liters) to (Dram in kg / Gallon (US) in liters).

How do I convert dram per gallon (US) back to gram per liter?

Multiply by 0.4680719817, or equivalently divide by 2.13642354, since 1 dr/gal = 0.4680719817 g/L.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is liter used as the volume unit?

The liter is the standard metric unit of volume, equal to one cubic decimeter. Liter shows up most often in virtually all metric-system science, industry, and retail.

Where is the gram per liter to dram per gallon (US) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging on one side and pharmacy compounding and small-parts manufacturing on the other, or whenever a liter-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in gallon (US) terms.
