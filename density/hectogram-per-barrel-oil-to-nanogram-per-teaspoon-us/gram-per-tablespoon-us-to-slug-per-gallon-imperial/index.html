Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Tablespoon (US) (tbsp): The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons.

Slug (slug): The slug is the coherent unit of mass in the imperial (foot-pound-second) engineering system, equal to 1 lbf·s²/ft.

Gallon (imperial) (gal (imp)): The imperial gallon is the UK standard liquid volume unit, larger than the US gallon.
