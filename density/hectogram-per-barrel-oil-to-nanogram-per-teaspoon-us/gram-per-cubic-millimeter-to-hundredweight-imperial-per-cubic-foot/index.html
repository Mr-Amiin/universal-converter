Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Cubic millimeter (mm³): A cubic millimeter is an extremely small metric volume unit, a thousandth of a cubic centimeter.

Hundredweight (long) (cwt (long)): The imperial (long) hundredweight equals 112 pounds, used historically in the UK and Commonwealth.

Cubic foot (ft³): A cubic foot is the volume of a cube with 1-foot sides.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per cubic millimeter to hundredweight (long) per cubic foot?

Multiply the gram per cubic millimeter value by 557.3925051, since 1 g/mm³ = 557.3925051 cwt (long)/ft³. This works because gram per cubic millimeter and hundredweight (long) per cubic foot both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Cubic millimeter in liters) to (Hundredweight (long) in kg / Cubic foot in liters).

How do I convert hundredweight (long) per cubic foot back to gram per cubic millimeter?

Multiply by 0.001794067898, or equivalently divide by 557.3925051, since 1 cwt (long)/ft³ = 0.001794067898 g/mm³.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is cubic millimeter used as the volume unit?

A cubic millimeter is an extremely small metric volume unit, a thousandth of a cubic centimeter. Cubic millimeter shows up most often in microfluidics, precision manufacturing, and materials science.

Where is the gram per cubic millimeter to hundredweight (long) per cubic foot conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging on one side and UK agricultural and bulk commodity trading on the other, or whenever a cubic millimeter-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic foot terms.
