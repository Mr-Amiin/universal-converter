Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per quart (US) to decigram per quart (US)?

Multiply the gram per quart (US) value by 10, since 1 g/qt = 10 dg/qt. This works because gram per quart (US) and decigram per quart (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Quart (US) in liters) to (Decigram in kg / Quart (US) in liters).

How do I convert decigram per quart (US) back to gram per quart (US)?

Multiply by 0.1, or equivalently divide by 10, since 1 dg/qt = 0.1 g/qt.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

Why is quart (US) used as the volume unit?

Where is the gram per quart (US) to decigram per quart (US) conversion used?
