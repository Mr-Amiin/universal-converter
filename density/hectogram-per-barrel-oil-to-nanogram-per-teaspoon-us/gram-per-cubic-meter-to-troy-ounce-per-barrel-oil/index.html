Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Cubic meter (m³): A cubic meter is the SI derived unit of volume, equal to 1,000 liters.

Troy ounce (oz t): The troy ounce is a heavier ounce (about 10% more than the avoirdupois ounce) used exclusively for precious metals and gemstones.

Barrel (oil) (bbl): The oil barrel is fixed at 42 US gallons and is the standard unit for pricing crude oil and petroleum products.
