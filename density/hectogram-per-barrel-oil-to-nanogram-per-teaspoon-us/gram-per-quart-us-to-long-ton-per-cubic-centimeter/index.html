Unit definitions

Gram (g): The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram.

Quart (US) (qt): The US liquid quart is 1/4 of a US gallon, or two US pints.

Long ton (long tn): The long (imperial) ton equals 2,240 pounds, still used in UK shipping and some commodity markets.

Cubic centimeter (cm³): A cubic centimeter is the volume of a 1 cm cube and is numerically identical to a milliliter.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (1.039999433e-09) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Gram = 0.001 kg, Quart (US) = 0.946352946 L, Long ton = 1016.046909 kg, Cubic centimeter = 0.001 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Unit comparison

Gram per Quart (US) vs Long ton per Cubic centimeter

  • Where used: Gram per Quart (US) appears mainly in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging and US dairy, cooking, and automotive fluid packaging, while long ton per cubic centimeter is more common in maritime shipping and UK heavy industry and medical dosing, engine displacement, and laboratory science.
  • Scale: Gram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Quart (US) is a everyday-scale volume unit, versus a very large mass unit and small volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Gram history — Defined in 1795 as the mass of one cubic centimeter of water at its freezing point, the gram remains the everyday metric mass unit worldwide. Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gram per quart (US) to long ton per cubic centimeter?

Multiply the gram per quart (US) value by 1.039999433e-09, since 1 g/qt = 1.039999433e-09 long tn/cm³. This works because gram per quart (US) and long ton per cubic centimeter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Gram in kg / Quart (US) in liters) to (Long ton in kg / Cubic centimeter in liters).

How do I convert long ton per cubic centimeter back to gram per quart (US)?

Multiply by 961538985.4, or equivalently divide by 1.039999433e-09, since 1 long tn/cm³ = 961538985.4 g/qt.

Why is gram used to measure mass here?

The gram is the base metric unit of mass, defined as one thousandth of a kilogram. It is common in cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging, which is why densities are often reported using gram.

Why is quart (US) used as the volume unit?

The US liquid quart is 1/4 of a US gallon, or two US pints. Quart (US) shows up most often in US dairy, cooking, and automotive fluid packaging.

Why are such very large mass units used for density?

Long ton is very large on its own, but density conversions often need it because maritime shipping and UK heavy industry routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the gram per quart (US) to long ton per cubic centimeter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between cooking, chemistry, and retail food packaging on one side and maritime shipping and UK heavy industry on the other, or whenever a quart (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic centimeter terms.
