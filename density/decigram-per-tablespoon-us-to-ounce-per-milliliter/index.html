Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decigram per tablespoon to ounce per milliliter?

Multiply the decigram per tablespoon value by 0.00023855091 to get the equivalent in ounce per milliliter, since 1 dg/tbsp = 0.00023855091 oz/mL.

Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per tablespoon of volume.

Why is tablespoon used to measure volume here?

A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

How do I convert ounce per milliliter back to decigram per tablespoon?

Divide by 0.00023855091, or multiply by 4191.977301, since 1 oz/mL = 4191.977301 dg/tbsp.

Is dg/tbsp a practical everyday density unit?
