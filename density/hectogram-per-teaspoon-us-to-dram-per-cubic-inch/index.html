Dram history: From the Greek drachma, once both a coin and a small weight unit. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Dram is common in pharmacy dosing, small-parts weighing.

Teaspoon vs Cubic inch
