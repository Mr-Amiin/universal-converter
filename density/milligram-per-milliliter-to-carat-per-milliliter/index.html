Formula & worked example

ct/mL = mg/mL × 0.005

mg/mL = ct/mL ÷ 0.005 (or × 200)

For example, cork has a density of about 240 kg/m³, which is roughly 240 mg/mL. Multiplying by the conversion factor: 240 × 0.005 = 1.2. So 240 mg/mL of cork is equal to 1.2 ct/mL.
