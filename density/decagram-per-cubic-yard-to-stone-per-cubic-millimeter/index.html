Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per cubic yard to stone per cubic millimeter?

Multiply the decagram per cubic yard value by 2.05967e-12 to get the equivalent in stone per cubic millimeter, since 1 dag/yd3 = 2.05967e-12 st/mm3.

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per cubic yard of volume.

Why is cubic yard used to measure volume here?

A cubic yard is exactly 0.764554857984 cubic meters.

How do I convert stone per cubic millimeter back to decagram per cubic yard?

Divide by 2.05967e-12, or multiply by 4.855148e+11, since 1 st/mm3 = 4.855148e+11 dag/yd3.

Is dag/yd3 a practical everyday density unit?

1 dag/yd3 equals about 0.01307950619 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
