From definition

Carat per Oil barrel (ct/bbl) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. An oil barrel is 42 US gallons.

To definition

Hectogram per Pint (hg/pt) is a density unit: Hectogram is 100 gram units. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

ct/bbl = hg/pt / 5.952380952e-06

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 ct/bbl. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 5.952380952e-06 = 7.44047619e-05. So 12.5 ct/bbl is equal to 7.44047619e-05 hg/pt.
