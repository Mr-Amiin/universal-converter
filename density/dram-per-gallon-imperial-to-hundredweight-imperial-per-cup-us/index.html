Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per imperial gallon to imperial hundredweight per cup?

Multiply the dram per imperial gallon value by 1.815086e-06 to get the equivalent in imperial hundredweight per cup, since 1 dr/imp gal = 1.815086e-06 long cwt/cup.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. In this pairing it measures mass per imperial gallon of volume.

Why is imperial gallon used to measure volume here?

The imperial gallon was defined in 1824 as the volume of 10 pounds of water and remains standard in the UK.

How do I convert imperial hundredweight per cup back to dram per imperial gallon?

Divide by 1.815086e-06, or multiply by 550938.1802, since 1 long cwt/cup = 550938.1802 dr/imp gal.

What has a density close to 1 dr/imp gal?
