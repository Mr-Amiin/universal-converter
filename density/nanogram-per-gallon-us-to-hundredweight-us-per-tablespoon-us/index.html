Nanogram per Gallon vs US hundredweight per Tablespoon

Nanogram per Gallon is built from nanogram (Nanogram is 1e-9 gram units.) divided by gallon (A US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters.), while US hundredweight per Tablespoon is built from us hundredweight (A US short hundredweight is 100 pounds.) divided by tablespoon (A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.). Unit background

Nanogram: Nanogram is 1e-9 gram units. Gallon: A US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters.

US hundredweight: A US short hundredweight is 100 pounds. Tablespoon: A US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

Density as a quantity (mass divided by volume) dates to Archimedes' work on buoyancy in the 3rd century BCE. Modern unit systems pair a mass unit with a volume unit to express it, which is why density has so many valid unit combinations — this page covers one specific pairing.
