Unit definitions

Hectogram (hg): A hectogram equals one hundred grams.

Cubic inch (in³): A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides.

Hundredweight (short) (cwt (short)): The US (short) hundredweight equals 100 pounds and underlies the short ton (2,000 lb).

Gallon (US) (gal): The US liquid gallon is the standard US volume unit for fuel, beverages, and household liquids.

How do I convert hundredweight (short) per gallon (US) back to hectogram per cubic inch?
