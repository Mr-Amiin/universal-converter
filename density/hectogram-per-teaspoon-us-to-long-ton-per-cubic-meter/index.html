Long ton history: The traditional British Imperial ton, still used in some shipping and commodity contexts. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Long ton is common in UK/Commonwealth shipping and bulk trade.

Teaspoon vs Cubic meter

1 teaspoon = 0.000004928921594 cubic meter(s). Teaspoon history: Standardized as one-third tablespoon for consistent US recipe measuring. Cubic meter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Typical use: Teaspoon is common in recipes, medicine dosing, while Cubic meter is common in everyday and industrial cubic-meter volume measurement.
