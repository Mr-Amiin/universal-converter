Microgram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Microgram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in micro (1e-06x gram) amounts.

Teaspoon vs Imperial gallon

1 teaspoon = 0.001084211178 imperial gallon(s). Teaspoon history: Standardized as one-third tablespoon for consistent US recipe measuring. Imperial gallon history: Defined in the British Weights and Measures Act of 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water. Typical use: Teaspoon is common in recipes, medicine dosing, while Imperial gallon is common in UK/Commonwealth fuel and liquid measures.
