Unit definitions

Dram (dr): An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 of an ounce, or 1/256 of a pound.

Tablespoon (US) (tbsp): The US tablespoon is a standard cooking-measurement volume unit equal to 3 US teaspoons.

Centigram (cg): A centigram is one hundredth of a gram in the metric system.

Quart (US) (qt): The US liquid quart is 1/4 of a US gallon, or two US pints.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per tablespoon (US) to centigram per quart (US)?

This works because dram per tablespoon (US) and centigram per quart (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Dram in kg / Tablespoon (US) in liters) to (Centigram in kg / Quart (US) in liters).

How do I convert centigram per quart (US) back to dram per tablespoon (US)?

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

Why is tablespoon (US) used as the volume unit?
