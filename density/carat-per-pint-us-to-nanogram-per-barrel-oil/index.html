From definition

Carat per Pint (ct/pt) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

To definition

Nanogram per Oil barrel (ng/bbl) is a density unit: Nanogram is 1e-9 gram units. An oil barrel is 42 US gallons.
