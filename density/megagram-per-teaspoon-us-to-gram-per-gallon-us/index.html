Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Teaspoon: A traditional US kitchen-measurement unit standardized in the 20th century.

Gram: Introduced during the French Revolution in 1795 as the base mass unit of the original metric system, originally tied to the mass of water.

Gallon: The US liquid gallon derives from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches, still used in the United States today.
