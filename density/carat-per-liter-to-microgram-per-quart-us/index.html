From definition

Carat per Liter (ct/L) is a density unit: A metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams. A liter is one cubic decimeter.

To definition

Microgram per Quart (ug/qt) is a density unit: Microgram is 0.000001 gram units. A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

Formula

ug/qt = ct/L x 189270.5892
