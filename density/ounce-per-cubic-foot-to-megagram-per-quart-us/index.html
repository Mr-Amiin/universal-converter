Ounce per Cubic foot vs Megagram per Quart

Ounce per Cubic foot is built from ounce (An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound.) divided by cubic foot (A cubic foot is exactly 0.028316846592 cubic meters.), while Megagram per Quart is built from megagram (Megagram is 1000000 gram units.) divided by quart (A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.).
