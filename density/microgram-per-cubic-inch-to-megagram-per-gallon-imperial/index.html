Unit history

Microgram: An SI-prefixed fraction of the gram used since the metric system's 18th-century origins for very small quantities.

Cubic inch: Derived from the British imperial inch, the cubic inch has been used in English-speaking engineering and manufacturing since before 19th-century standardization.

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Imperial gallon: Defined in 1824 as part of British imperial standardization, the imperial gallon was originally based on the volume of 10 pounds of water.
