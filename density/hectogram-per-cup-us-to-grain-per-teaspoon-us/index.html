Grain history: Historically based on the mass of a single grain of barley; the oldest unit still in common use today. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Grain is common in ammunition/gunpowder charges, archery, pharmacy.

Cup vs Teaspoon

1 cup = 48 teaspoon(s). Cup history: A kitchen-measure unit formalized in the US in the early 20th century. Teaspoon history: Standardized as one-third tablespoon for consistent US recipe measuring. Typical use: Cup is common in US home cooking and baking, while Teaspoon is common in recipes, medicine dosing.
