About Converting Grams per Liter to Ounces per US Gallon

Gram per liter and ounce per US gallon are both units of density. One gram per liter is 1 kilogram per cubic meter. One ounce per US gallon is 0.028349523125 kg ÷ 0.003785411784 m³ ≈ 7.489152 kg/m³.

Formula

oz/gal = g/L × 0.1335264712

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 gram per liter equals 0.1335264712 ounces per US gallon, and the reverse factor is 7.489151707.
