From definition

Centigram per Imperial gallon (cg/imp gal) is a density unit: Centigram is 0.01 gram units. An imperial gallon is exactly 4.54609 liters.

To definition

Nanogram per Quart (ng/qt) is a density unit: Nanogram is 1e-9 gram units. A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.

ng/qt = cg/imp gal x 2.081685e+06
