Frequently asked questions

How do you convert picogram per gallon to gram per pint?

Multiply the picogram per gallon value by 1.25e-13 to get the equivalent in gram per pint, since 1 pg/gal = 1.25e-13 g/pt.

What is 1 Picogram per Gallon in Gram per Pint?

1 pg/gal equals 1.25e-13 g/pt.

How do I convert gram per pint back to picogram per gallon?

Divide by 1.25e-13, or multiply by 8e+12, since 1 g/pt = 8e+12 pg/gal.

Why is picogram per gallon used as a density unit?

Picogram per Gallon pairs picogram as the mass unit (Picogram is 1e-12 gram units.) with gallon as the volume unit (A US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters.), which makes it a natural fit wherever those two units are already standard.

Why use gram per pint instead of another density unit?

Gram per Pint combines gram (A gram is one thousandth of a kilogram.) with pint (A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.), which keeps values in a convenient range for its field rather than forcing very large or very small numbers.

Why are picogram per gallon and gram per pint many orders of magnitude apart?

The gap comes from the underlying mass and volume unit sizes: pg vs g for mass, and gal vs pt for volume. Multiplying those ratios together gives the 1.25e-13 conversion factor.

How accurate is this conversion?

The factor 1.25e-13 is computed directly from exact SI-defined mass and volume constants, not from a rounded lookup table, so it is accurate to full floating-point precision.
