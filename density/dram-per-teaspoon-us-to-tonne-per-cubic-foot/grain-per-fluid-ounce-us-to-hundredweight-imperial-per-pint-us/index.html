Unit definitions

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Fluid ounce (US) (fl oz): The US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon, used for liquid measurement in cooking and retail beverages.

Hundredweight (long) (cwt (long)): The imperial (long) hundredweight equals 112 pounds, used historically in the UK and Commonwealth.

Pint (US) (pt): The US liquid pint is 1/8 of a US gallon.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (2.040816327e-05) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Grain = 6.479891e-05 kg, Fluid ounce (US) = 0.02957352956 L, Hundredweight (long) = 50.80234544 kg, Pint (US) = 0.473176473 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Unit comparison

Grain per Fluid ounce (US) vs Hundredweight (long) per Pint (US)

  • Where used: Grain per Fluid ounce (US) appears mainly in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology and beverage packaging, pharmacy, and recipe measurement, while hundredweight (long) per pint (US) is more common in UK agricultural and bulk commodity trading and US beverage retail (beer, milk) and cooking.
  • Scale: Grain is a extremely small mass unit and Fluid ounce (US) is a small volume unit, versus a large mass unit and everyday-scale volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Grain history — Grain-based mass standards appear in nearly every historical weight system; it survives today mainly for bullet and arrow-point mass and pharmaceutical dosing. Fluid ounce (US) history — Derived from the US gallon rather than the imperial gallon, giving it a slightly different value than the UK fluid ounce.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per fluid ounce (US) to hundredweight (long) per pint (US)?

Multiply the grain per fluid ounce (US) value by 2.040816327e-05, since 1 gr/fl oz = 2.040816327e-05 cwt (long)/pt. This works because grain per fluid ounce (US) and hundredweight (long) per pint (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Grain in kg / Fluid ounce (US) in liters) to (Hundredweight (long) in kg / Pint (US) in liters).

How do I convert hundredweight (long) per pint (US) back to grain per fluid ounce (US)?

Multiply by 49000, or equivalently divide by 2.040816327e-05, since 1 cwt (long)/pt = 49000 gr/fl oz.

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is fluid ounce (US) used as the volume unit?

The US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon, used for liquid measurement in cooking and retail beverages. Fluid ounce (US) shows up most often in beverage packaging, pharmacy, and recipe measurement.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the grain per fluid ounce (US) to hundredweight (long) per pint (US) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on one side and UK agricultural and bulk commodity trading on the other, or whenever a fluid ounce (US)-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in pint (US) terms.
