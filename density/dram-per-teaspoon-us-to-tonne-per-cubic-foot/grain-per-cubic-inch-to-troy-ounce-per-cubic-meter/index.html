Unit definitions

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Cubic inch (in³): A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides.

Troy ounce (oz t): The troy ounce is a heavier ounce (about 10% more than the avoirdupois ounce) used exclusively for precious metals and gemstones.

Cubic meter (m³): A cubic meter is the SI derived unit of volume, equal to 1,000 liters.

Conversion accuracy

Unit comparison

Grain per Cubic inch vs Troy ounce per Cubic meter

  • Where used: Grain per Cubic inch appears mainly in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology and engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances, while troy ounce per cubic meter is more common in precious-metal bullion trading (gold, silver, platinum) and civil engineering, shipping, and international trade.
  • Scale: Grain is a extremely small mass unit and Cubic inch is a small volume unit, versus a everyday-scale mass unit and large volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Grain history — Grain-based mass standards appear in nearly every historical weight system; it survives today mainly for bullet and arrow-point mass and pharmaceutical dosing. Cubic inch history — Widely used historically to rate engine displacement in US and UK automotive industries.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per cubic inch to troy ounce per cubic meter?

Multiply the grain per cubic inch value by 127.1328002, since 1 gr/in³ = 127.1328002 oz t/m³. This works because grain per cubic inch and troy ounce per cubic meter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Grain in kg / Cubic inch in liters) to (Troy ounce in kg / Cubic meter in liters).

How do I convert troy ounce per cubic meter back to grain per cubic inch?

Multiply by 0.00786579072, or equivalently divide by 127.1328002, since 1 oz t/m³ = 0.00786579072 gr/in³.

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is cubic inch used as the volume unit?

A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides. Cubic inch shows up most often in engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the grain per cubic inch to troy ounce per cubic meter conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on one side and precious-metal bullion trading (gold, silver, platinum) on the other, or whenever a cubic inch-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic meter terms.
