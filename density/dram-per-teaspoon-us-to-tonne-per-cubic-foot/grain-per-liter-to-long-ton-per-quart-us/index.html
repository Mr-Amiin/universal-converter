Unit definitions

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Liter (L): The liter is the standard metric unit of volume, equal to one cubic decimeter.

Long ton (long tn): The long (imperial) ton equals 2,240 pounds, still used in UK shipping and some commodity markets.

Quart (US) (qt): The US liquid quart is 1/4 of a US gallon, or two US pints.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (6.035414196e-08) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Grain = 6.479891e-05 kg, Liter = 1 L, Long ton = 1016.046909 kg, Quart (US) = 0.946352946 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Unit comparison

Grain per Liter vs Long ton per Quart (US)

  • Where used: Grain per Liter appears mainly in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology and virtually all metric-system science, industry, and retail, while long ton per quart (US) is more common in maritime shipping and UK heavy industry and US dairy, cooking, and automotive fluid packaging.
  • Scale: Grain is a extremely small mass unit and Liter is a everyday-scale volume unit, versus a very large mass unit and everyday-scale volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Grain history — Grain-based mass standards appear in nearly every historical weight system; it survives today mainly for bullet and arrow-point mass and pharmaceutical dosing. Liter history — Introduced with the metric system in 1795 and redefined in terms of the cubic decimeter in 1964 for exactness.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per liter to long ton per quart (US)?

Multiply the grain per liter value by 6.035414196e-08, since 1 gr/L = 6.035414196e-08 long tn/qt. This works because grain per liter and long ton per quart (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Grain in kg / Liter in liters) to (Long ton in kg / Quart (US) in liters).

How do I convert long ton per quart (US) back to grain per liter?

Multiply by 16568871.12, or equivalently divide by 6.035414196e-08, since 1 long tn/qt = 16568871.12 gr/L.

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is liter used as the volume unit?

The liter is the standard metric unit of volume, equal to one cubic decimeter. Liter shows up most often in virtually all metric-system science, industry, and retail.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the grain per liter to long ton per quart (US) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on one side and maritime shipping and UK heavy industry on the other, or whenever a liter-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in quart (US) terms.
