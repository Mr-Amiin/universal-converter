Unit definitions

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Teaspoon (US) (tsp): The US teaspoon is a small standard cooking-measurement volume unit, 1/3 of a US tablespoon.

Decagram (dag): A decagram equals ten grams, using the metric 'deca-' (x10) prefix.

Milliliter (mL): A milliliter is one thousandth of a liter, equal to one cubic centimeter.

How do you convert grain per teaspoon (US) to decagram per milliliter?

How do I convert decagram per milliliter back to grain per teaspoon (US)?

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

Where is the grain per teaspoon (US) to decagram per milliliter conversion used?
