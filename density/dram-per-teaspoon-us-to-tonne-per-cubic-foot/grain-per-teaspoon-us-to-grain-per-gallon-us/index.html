Formula

gr/gal = gr/tsp × 768

gr/tsp = gr/gal ÷ 768

Step-by-step worked example: Say a material sample measures 12.5 gr/tsp. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 × 768 = 9600. So 12.5 gr/tsp is equal to 9600 gr/gal.

In context: For scale, gasoline has a density of about 56.2880144 gr/tsp (roughly 43229.19506 gr/gal). Materials denser than water push both numbers up; gases like air push them far below 1.

How do I convert grain per gallon (US) back to grain per teaspoon (US)?
