Unit definitions

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Milliliter (mL): A milliliter is one thousandth of a liter, equal to one cubic centimeter.

Long ton (long tn): The long (imperial) ton equals 2,240 pounds, still used in UK shipping and some commodity markets.

Cubic yard (yd³): A cubic yard is the volume of a cube with 1-yard sides, commonly used for bulk materials like concrete and soil.

Conversion accuracy

Unit comparison

Grain per Milliliter vs Long ton per Cubic yard

  • Where used: Grain per Milliliter appears mainly in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology and medical dosing, cosmetics, and laboratory chemistry, while long ton per cubic yard is more common in maritime shipping and UK heavy industry and concrete, landscaping, and bulk earthwork estimating.
  • Scale: Grain is a extremely small mass unit and Milliliter is a small volume unit, versus a very large mass unit and large volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Grain history — Grain-based mass standards appear in nearly every historical weight system; it survives today mainly for bullet and arrow-point mass and pharmaceutical dosing. Milliliter history — Used wherever the liter is too large a unit, especially in medicine, chemistry, and small-batch food and drink packaging.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per milliliter to long ton per cubic yard?

Multiply the grain per milliliter value by 0.04875987615, since 1 gr/mL = 0.04875987615 long tn/yd³. This works because grain per milliliter and long ton per cubic yard both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Grain in kg / Milliliter in liters) to (Long ton in kg / Cubic yard in liters).

How do I convert long ton per cubic yard back to grain per milliliter?

Multiply by 20.50866571, or equivalently divide by 0.04875987615, since 1 long tn/yd³ = 20.50866571 gr/mL.

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is milliliter used as the volume unit?

A milliliter is one thousandth of a liter, equal to one cubic centimeter. Milliliter shows up most often in medical dosing, cosmetics, and laboratory chemistry.

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Grain is extremely small on its own, but density conversions often need it because ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the grain per milliliter to long ton per cubic yard conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology on one side and maritime shipping and UK heavy industry on the other, or whenever a milliliter-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in cubic yard terms.
