Gram per cubic centimeter and pound per US gallon are both units of density. One gram per cubic centimeter is 1,000 kilograms per cubic meter (1 kg/L), the density of water. One pound per US gallon is 0.45359237 kg ÷ 0.003785411784 m³ ≈ 119.826 kg/m³.
