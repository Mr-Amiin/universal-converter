Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decigram per cubic yard to stone per cubic yard?

Multiply the decigram per cubic yard value by 1.57473e-05 to get the equivalent in stone per cubic yard, since 1 dg/yd3 = 1.57473e-05 st/yd3.

Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per cubic yard of volume.

Why is cubic yard used to measure volume here?

A cubic yard is exactly 0.764554857984 cubic meters.

How do I convert stone per cubic yard back to decigram per cubic yard?

Divide by 1.57473e-05, or multiply by 63502.9318, since 1 st/yd3 = 63502.9318 dg/yd3.

Is dg/yd3 a practical everyday density unit?
