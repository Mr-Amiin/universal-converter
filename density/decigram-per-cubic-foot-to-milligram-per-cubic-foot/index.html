Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decigram per cubic foot to milligram per cubic foot?

Multiply the decigram per cubic foot value by 100 to get the equivalent in milligram per cubic foot, since 1 dg/ft3 = 100 mg/ft3.

Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per cubic foot of volume.

Why is cubic foot used to measure volume here?

A cubic foot is exactly 0.028316846592 cubic meters.

How do I convert milligram per cubic foot back to decigram per cubic foot?

Divide by 100, or multiply by 0.01, since 1 mg/ft3 = 0.01 dg/ft3.

Is dg/ft3 a practical everyday density unit?

1 dg/ft3 equals about 0.003531466672 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
