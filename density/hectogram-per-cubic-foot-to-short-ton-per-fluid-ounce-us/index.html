Unit definitions

Hectogram (hg): A hectogram equals one hundred grams.

Cubic foot (ft³): A cubic foot is the volume of a cube with 1-foot sides.

Short ton (tn): The US short ton equals 2,000 pounds, the standard 'ton' used in the United States.

Fluid ounce (US) (fl oz): The US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon, used for liquid measurement in cooking and retail beverages.

Conversion accuracy

Unit comparison

Hectogram per Cubic foot vs Short ton per Fluid ounce (US)

  • Where used: Hectogram per Cubic foot appears mainly in European grocery retail and deli portioning and natural gas metering, HVAC, and construction material volume, while short ton per fluid ounce (US) is more common in US bulk material trade: gravel, grain, scrap metal, and freight and beverage packaging, pharmacy, and recipe measurement.
  • Scale: Hectogram is a everyday-scale mass unit and Cubic foot is a everyday-scale volume unit, versus a very large mass unit and small volume unit on the other side, which is why the numeric values can look very different for the same physical density.
  • Precision: Both units convert exactly through their defined SI equivalents, so no rounding is introduced by the unit choice itself — only by how many digits you choose to keep in the result.
  • History: Hectogram history — The hectogram uses the metric 'hecto-' (x100) prefix and is a common everyday unit for food portions in several European countries. Cubic foot history — A natural extension of the imperial foot used throughout US and UK construction and utility metering.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert hectogram per cubic foot to short ton per fluid ounce (US)?

Multiply the hectogram per cubic foot value by 1.15123116e-07, since 1 hg/ft³ = 1.15123116e-07 tn/fl oz. This works because hectogram per cubic foot and short ton per fluid ounce (US) both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Hectogram in kg / Cubic foot in liters) to (Short ton in kg / Fluid ounce (US) in liters).

How do I convert short ton per fluid ounce (US) back to hectogram per cubic foot?

Multiply by 8686352.794, or equivalently divide by 1.15123116e-07, since 1 tn/fl oz = 8686352.794 hg/ft³.

Why is hectogram used to measure mass here?

A hectogram equals one hundred grams. It is common in European grocery retail and deli portioning, which is why densities are often reported using hectogram.

Why is cubic foot used as the volume unit?

A cubic foot is the volume of a cube with 1-foot sides. Cubic foot shows up most often in natural gas metering, HVAC, and construction material volume.

Why are such very large mass units used for density?

Short ton is very large on its own, but density conversions often need it because US bulk material trade: gravel, grain, scrap metal, and freight routinely deal with concentrations or masses at that scale, and using a matched unit avoids writing out long strings of zeros.

Where is the hectogram per cubic foot to short ton per fluid ounce (US) conversion used?

It comes up most often when moving between European grocery retail and deli portioning on one side and US bulk material trade: gravel, grain, scrap metal, and freight on the other, or whenever a cubic foot-based recipe or spec needs to be re-expressed in fluid ounce (US) terms.
