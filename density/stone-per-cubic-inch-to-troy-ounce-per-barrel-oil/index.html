Formula & worked example

Stone per Cubic inch and Troy ounce per Oil barrel both describe mass per unit volume, so converting between them is a single multiplication step.

oz t/bbl = st/in3 × 1.98083e+06

st/in3 = oz t/bbl × 5.048402e-07
