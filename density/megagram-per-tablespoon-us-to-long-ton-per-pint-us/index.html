Unit history

Megagram: The SI-prefixed name for 1,000 kilograms; industry generally calls the same quantity a 'tonne'.

Tablespoon: A traditional US kitchen-measurement unit standardized in the 20th century.

Long ton: The 2,240-pound 'long ton' (or imperial ton) was the traditional British ton, based on 20 hundredweight of 112 pounds each.

Pint: A traditional subdivision of the US gallon, standardized for cooking, brewing, and retail trade.
