Unit definitions

Grain (gr): A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat.

Teaspoon (US) (tsp): The US teaspoon is a small standard cooking-measurement volume unit, 1/3 of a US tablespoon.

Megagram (Mg): A megagram equals one million grams, numerically identical to a metric tonne.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert grain per teaspoon (US) to megagram per teaspoon (US)?

How do I convert megagram per teaspoon (US) back to grain per teaspoon (US)?

Why is grain used to measure mass here?

A grain is the smallest common avoirdupois mass unit, originally based on the mass of a single grain of barley or wheat. It is common in ammunition reloading, archery, and pharmacology, which is why densities are often reported using grain.

Why is teaspoon (US) used as the volume unit?

Why are such extremely small mass units used for density?

Where is the grain per teaspoon (US) to megagram per teaspoon (US) conversion used?
