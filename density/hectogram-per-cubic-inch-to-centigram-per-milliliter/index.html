Unit definitions

Hectogram (hg): A hectogram equals one hundred grams.

Cubic inch (in³): A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides.

Centigram (cg): A centigram is one hundredth of a gram in the metric system.

Milliliter (mL): A milliliter is one thousandth of a liter, equal to one cubic centimeter.

Conversion accuracy

This factor (610.2374409) is computed directly from each unit's exact SI definition (Hectogram = 0.1 kg, Cubic inch = 0.016387064 L, Centigram = 1e-05 kg, Milliliter = 0.001 L), so results stay consistent to the precision shown regardless of which direction you convert.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert hectogram per cubic inch to centigram per milliliter?

Multiply the hectogram per cubic inch value by 610.2374409, since 1 hg/in³ = 610.2374409 cg/mL. This works because hectogram per cubic inch and centigram per milliliter both describe mass per volume, so the conversion factor is simply the ratio of (Hectogram in kg / Cubic inch in liters) to (Centigram in kg / Milliliter in liters).

How do I convert centigram per milliliter back to hectogram per cubic inch?

Multiply by 0.0016387064, or equivalently divide by 610.2374409, since 1 cg/mL = 0.0016387064 hg/in³.

Why is hectogram used to measure mass here?

A hectogram equals one hundred grams. It is common in European grocery retail and deli portioning, which is why densities are often reported using hectogram.

Why is cubic inch used as the volume unit?

A cubic inch is the volume of a cube with 1-inch sides. Cubic inch shows up most often in engine displacement and small mechanical part tolerances.

Where is the hectogram per cubic inch to centigram per milliliter conversion used?
