Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per oil barrel to gram per tablespoon?

Multiply the dram per oil barrel value by 0.0001647921499 to get the equivalent in gram per tablespoon, since 1 dr/bbl = 0.0001647921499 g/tbsp.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. In this pairing it measures mass per oil barrel of volume.

Why is oil barrel used to measure volume here?

The 42-US-gallon oil barrel became the industry standard in Pennsylvania's early oil fields in the 1860s and is still used to quote crude oil prices.

How do I convert gram per tablespoon back to dram per oil barrel?

Divide by 0.0001647921499, or multiply by 6068.250222, since 1 g/tbsp = 6068.250222 dr/bbl.

Is dr/bbl a practical everyday density unit?

1 dr/bbl equals about 0.01114457099 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
