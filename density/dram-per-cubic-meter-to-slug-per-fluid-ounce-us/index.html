Frequently asked questions

How do you convert dram per cubic meter to slug per fluid ounce?

Multiply the dram per cubic meter value by 3.590521e-09 to get the equivalent in slug per fluid ounce, since 1 dr/m3 = 3.590521e-09 slug/fl oz.

Why is dram used to measure mass here?

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. In this pairing it measures mass per cubic meter of volume.

Why is cubic meter used to measure volume here?

This is the unprefixed base unit with no metric multiplier applied.

How do I convert slug per fluid ounce back to dram per cubic meter?

Divide by 3.590521e-09, or multiply by 278511106.1, since 1 slug/fl oz = 278511106.1 dr/m3.

About the Dram per Cubic Meter to Slug per Fluid ounce conversion

Dram per Cubic Meter (dr/m3) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. Cubic metric volume unit. Slug per Fluid ounce (slug/fl oz) is a density unit: A slug is an imperial mass unit. A US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon.

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. This is the unprefixed base unit with no metric multiplier applied. The slug is the coherent mass unit in the foot-pound-second system, defined so that 1 lbf accelerates 1 slug at 1 ft/s^2.
