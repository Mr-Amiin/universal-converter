Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decagram per cup to ounce per cubic foot?

Why is decagram used to measure mass here?

Deca- (da), from the Greek 'deka' (ten), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes and is uncommon in everyday use. In this pairing it measures mass per cup of volume.

Why is cup used to measure volume here?

A US customary cup is 236.5882365 milliliters.

How do I convert ounce per cubic foot back to decagram per cup?
