Formula

oz/mL = ct/m3 x 7.054792e-09

ct/m3 = oz/mL / 7.054792e-09

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 ct/m3. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 7.054792e-09 = 8.818490e-08. So 12.5 ct/m3 is equal to 8.818490e-08 oz/mL.
