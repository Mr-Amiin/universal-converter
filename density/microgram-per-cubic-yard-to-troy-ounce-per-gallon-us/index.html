Unit history

Microgram: An SI-prefixed fraction of the gram used since the metric system's 18th-century origins for very small quantities.

Cubic yard: Derived from the British imperial yard, the cubic yard has long been used in US construction for bulk materials like concrete and soil.

Troy ounce: Named for the French city of Troyes, the troy ounce has been the standard for weighing precious metals since medieval European trade fairs.

Gallon: The US liquid gallon derives from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches, still used in the United States today.
