Unit comparison

Hectogram vs Metric ton

1 hectogram = 0.0001 metric ton(s). Hectogram history: Part of the metric/SI system of mass prefixes established in 1795 and refined since. Metric ton history: Adopted with the metric system in 18th-century France as a convenient large-mass unit for trade and industry. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Metric ton is common in shipping, bulk commodities, heavy industry.

Teaspoon vs Cup

1 teaspoon = 0.02083333333 cup(s). Teaspoon history: Standardized as one-third tablespoon for consistent US recipe measuring. Cup history: A kitchen-measure unit formalized in the US in the early 20th century. Typical use: Teaspoon is common in recipes, medicine dosing, while Cup is common in US home cooking and baking.
