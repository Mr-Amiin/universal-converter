About the Dram per Fluid ounce to Troy ounce per Cubic Meter conversion

Dram per Fluid ounce (dr/fl oz) is a density unit: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce. A US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon. Troy ounce per Cubic Meter (oz t/m3) is a density unit: A troy ounce is exactly 31.1034768 grams. Cubic metric volume unit.

The dram descends from the ancient Greek drachma and was long used by apothecaries for small weights. The troy ounce takes its name from Troyes, France, a medieval trading city, and is still the standard unit for precious metals. This is the unprefixed base unit with no metric multiplier applied.
