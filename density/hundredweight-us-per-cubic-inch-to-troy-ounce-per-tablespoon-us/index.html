Unit comparison

US hundredweight vs Troy ounce

1 us hundredweight = 1458.333333 troy ounce(s). US hundredweight history: A US customary subdivision of the short ton (20 cwt = 1 ton). Troy ounce history: Named after Troyes, France, a medieval trading city; it became the standard for precious metals. Typical use: US hundredweight is common in US agricultural commodities (grain, livestock), while Troy ounce is common in gold, silver, and other precious-metal markets.

Cubic inch vs Tablespoon

1 cubic inch = 1.108225108 tablespoon(s). Cubic inch history: Used since imperial units were standardized; still common in US engine displacement and machining. Tablespoon history: Standardized from the everyday dining tablespoon used for serving and measuring food. Typical use: Cubic inch is common in US engineering, machining, engine displacement, while Tablespoon is common in recipes and food science.
