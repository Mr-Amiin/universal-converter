Long ton history: The traditional British Imperial ton, still used in some shipping and commodity contexts. Typical use: Hectogram is common in contexts where quantities are naturally measured in hecto (100x gram) amounts, while Long ton is common in UK/Commonwealth shipping and bulk trade.

Cup vs Pint

1 cup = 0.5 pint(s). Cup history: A kitchen-measure unit formalized in the US in the early 20th century. Pint history: Historically tied to the volume of a pound of water; still the standard US beer/milk measure. Typical use: Cup is common in US home cooking and baking, while Pint is common in US beverages, cooking.
