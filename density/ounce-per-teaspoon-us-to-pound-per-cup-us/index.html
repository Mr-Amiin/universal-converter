Formula

lb/cup = oz/tsp × 3

oz/tsp = lb/cup ÷ 3 (equivalently, oz/tsp = lb/cup × 0.3333333333)

Step-by-step worked example

  1. Start with 12.5 oz/tsp.
  2. Apply the density conversion factor: multiply by 3 because 1 oz/tsp = 3 lb/cup.
  3. 12.5 x 3 = 37.5, so 12.5 oz/tsp equals 37.5 lb/cup.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert ounce per teaspoon to pound per cup?

Multiply the ounce per teaspoon value by 3 to get the equivalent in pound per cup, since 1 oz/tsp = 3 lb/cup.

What is 1 Ounce per Teaspoon in Pound per Cup?

1 oz/tsp equals 3 lb/cup.

How do I convert pound per cup back to ounce per teaspoon?

Divide by 3, or multiply by 0.3333333333, since 1 lb/cup = 0.3333333333 oz/tsp.

Why is ounce per teaspoon used as a density unit?

Ounce per Teaspoon pairs ounce as the mass unit (An avoirdupois ounce is 1/16 pound.) with teaspoon as the volume unit (A US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.), which makes it a natural fit wherever those two units are already standard.

Why use pound per cup instead of another density unit?

Pound per Cup combines pound (An avoirdupois pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms.) with cup (A US customary cup is 236.5882365 milliliters.), which keeps values in a convenient range for its field rather than forcing very large or very small numbers.

Why are ounce per teaspoon and pound per cup a moderate factor apart?

The gap comes from the underlying mass and volume unit sizes: oz vs lb for mass, and tsp vs cup for volume. Multiplying those ratios together gives the 3 conversion factor.

How accurate is this conversion?

The factor 3 is computed directly from exact SI-defined mass and volume constants, not from a rounded lookup table, so it is accurate to full floating-point precision.
