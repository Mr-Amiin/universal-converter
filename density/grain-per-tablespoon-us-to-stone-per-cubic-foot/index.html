How the formula works

Both grain per tablespoon and stone per cubic foot measure the same physical quantity (mass per unit volume), so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in grain per tablespoon by 0.019541 to get stone per cubic foot. To reverse the conversion, multiply a stone per cubic foot value by 51.174588 to get back to grain per tablespoon.
