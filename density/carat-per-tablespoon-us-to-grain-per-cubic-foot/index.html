ct/tbsp = gr/ft3 / 5910.633333

Suppose a lab sample or material has a density of 12.5 ct/tbsp. Multiply by the conversion factor: 12.5 x 5910.633333 = 73882.91667. So 12.5 ct/tbsp is equal to 73882.91667 gr/ft3.
