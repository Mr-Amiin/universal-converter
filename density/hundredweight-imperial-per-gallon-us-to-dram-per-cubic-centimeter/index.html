Unit comparison

Imperial hundredweight vs Dram

1 imperial hundredweight = 28672 dram(s). Imperial hundredweight history: The British subdivision of the long ton (20 long cwt = 1 long ton). Dram history: From the Greek drachma, once both a coin and a small weight unit. Typical use: Imperial hundredweight is common in UK agricultural and industrial trade, while Dram is common in pharmacy dosing, small-parts weighing.

Gallon vs Cubic centimeter

1 gallon = 3785.411784 cubic centimeter(s). Gallon history: Derived from the old English wine gallon of 231 cubic inches. Cubic centimeter history: A derived SI volume unit equal to a cube with edges of the prefixed meter length. Typical use: Gallon is common in US fuel, milk, and liquid retail measures, while Cubic centimeter is common in contexts where volumes are naturally measured in cubic centimeter amounts.
