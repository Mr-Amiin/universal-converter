Frequently asked questions

How do you convert decigram per oil barrel to troy ounce per cubic yard?

Multiply the decigram per oil barrel value by 0.01546098982 to get the equivalent in troy ounce per cubic yard, since 1 dg/bbl = 0.01546098982 oz t/yd3.

Why is decigram used to measure mass here?

Deci- (d), from the Latin 'decimus' (tenth), is one of the original 1795 metric prefixes, familiar from the deciliter. In this pairing it measures mass per oil barrel of volume.

Why is oil barrel used to measure volume here?

The 42-US-gallon oil barrel became the industry standard in Pennsylvania's early oil fields in the 1860s and is still used to quote crude oil prices.

How do I convert troy ounce per cubic yard back to decigram per oil barrel?

Divide by 0.01546098982, or multiply by 64.67891202, since 1 oz t/yd3 = 64.67891202 dg/bbl.

Is dg/bbl a practical everyday density unit?

1 dg/bbl equals about 0.000628981077 kg/m3, a magnitude far outside ordinary materials. Combinations like this exist mainly for complete unit-family coverage in scientific and mathematical contexts rather than everyday measurement.
