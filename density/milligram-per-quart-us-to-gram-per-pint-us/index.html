How do I convert gram per pint back to milligram per quart?

Why is the milligram per quart to gram per pint conversion factor so small?

The Milligram per Quart is a much smaller unit of density than the Gram per Pint, because Milligram and Gram (and/or the underlying volume units) differ by several orders of magnitude. That is why the conversion factor (0.0005) is far from 1.

Milligram per Quart vs Gram per Pint

Milligram per Quart is built from milligram (Milligram is 0.001 gram units.) divided by quart (A US liquid quart is one quarter of a US gallon.), while Gram per Pint is built from gram (A gram is one thousandth of a kilogram.) divided by pint (A US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.).
