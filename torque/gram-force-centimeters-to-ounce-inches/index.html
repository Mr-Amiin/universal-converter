About Converting Gram-force centimeters to Ounce-inches

Gram-force centimeter and ounce-inch are both units of torque. One gram-force centimeter is 0.0000980665 newton meters. One ounce-inch is the torque of one ounce-force at one inch: about 0.00706155 newton meters.

Formula

oz·in = gf cm × 0.01388738659

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 gram-force centimeter equals 0.01388738659 ounce-inches, and the reverse factor is 72.00778874.

Standard and definition

The gram-force centimeter is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the gram-force centimeter today

Understanding the Ounce-inch

Ounce-inch (oz·in) measures torque. One ounce-inch is the torque of one ounce-force at one inch: about 0.00706155 newton meters.
