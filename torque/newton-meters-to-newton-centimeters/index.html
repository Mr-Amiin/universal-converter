About Converting Newton-meters to Newton-centimeters

Newton meter and newton centimeter are both units of torque. The newton meter is the SI unit of torque: one newton acting at a perpendicular distance of one meter. One newton centimeter is 0.01 newton meters.

Formula

N cm = N·m × 100

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 newton meter equals 100 newton centimeters, and the reverse factor is 0.01.

Simple example

1 N·m × 100 = 100 N cm

1 newton meter = 100 newton centimeters.

Real-world example

10 N·m × 100 = 1000 N cm

10 newton meters = 1000 newton centimeters.
