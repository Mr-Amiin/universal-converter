About Converting Pound-inches to Newton-centimeters

Pound-inch and newton centimeter are both units of torque. One pound-inch is the torque of one pound-force at one inch: about 0.112984829 newton meters. One newton centimeter is 0.01 newton meters.

Formula

N cm = lbf·in × 11.2984829

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 pound-inch equals 11.2984829 newton centimeters, and the reverse factor is 0.08850745791.

Simple example

1 lbf·in × 11.2984829 = 11.2984829 N cm

1 pound-inch = 11.2984829 newton centimeters.

Real-world example

10 lbf·in × 11.2984829 = 112.984829 N cm

10 pound-inches = 112.984829 newton centimeters.
