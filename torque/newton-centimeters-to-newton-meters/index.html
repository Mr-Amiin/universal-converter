About Converting Newton-centimeters to Newton-meters

Newton centimeter and newton meter are both units of torque. One newton centimeter is 0.01 newton meters. The newton meter is the SI unit of torque: one newton acting at a perpendicular distance of one meter.

Formula

N·m = N cm × 0.01

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 newton centimeter equals 0.01 newton meters, and the reverse factor is 100.

Simple example

1 N cm × 0.01 = 0.01 N·m

1 newton centimeter = 0.01 newton meters.

Real-world example

10 N cm × 0.01 = 0.1 N·m

10 newton centimeters = 0.1 newton meters.
