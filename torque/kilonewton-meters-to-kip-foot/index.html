Understanding the Kilonewton meter

Kilonewton meter (kN·m) measures torque. A kilonewton meter is 1,000 newton meters of torque or moment.

Standard and definition

The kilonewton meter is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the kilonewton meter today

  • Structural bending moments and heavy machinery torque
  • Civil and mechanical design

Understanding the Kip-foot

Kip-foot (kip ft) measures torque. A kip-foot is the moment of 1,000 pound-force acting at one foot: 1,355.8179 newton meters.

Standard and definition

The kip-foot is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the kip-foot today

  • Structural beam and column bending-moment design in the United States
  • Steel and concrete design tables
