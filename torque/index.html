Frequently asked questions

What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the torque converter category.

What is the SI unit of torque?

The newton meter (N m) is the SI derived unit of torque, defined as the moment produced by a one newton force applied at a one meter perpendicular distance.

How is pound-foot different from pound-inch?

Both measure torque from a pound-force, but at different lever-arm lengths: a pound-foot uses a one foot distance while a pound-inch uses a one inch distance. The converter keeps these distinct.
