Frequently asked questions

What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the torque converter category.

What is torque measured in?

Torque is a rotational force measured in newton meters (N m) in SI, or pound-feet and pound-inches in US customary units.

Is torque the same as work or energy?

Torque and energy share the same base units dimensionally (force times distance), but torque is a vector describing rotational force, while energy is a scalar quantity.
