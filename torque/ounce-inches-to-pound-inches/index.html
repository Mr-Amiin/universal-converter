About this conversion

This page converts ounce-inch (oz in) into pound-inch (lb in) within the torque converter, using the fixed relationship 1 oz in = 0.0625 lb in.

Worked example: to convert 10 oz in to lb in, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 0.0625 = 0.625 lb in.

A bolt torqued to 25 oz in is set to about 1.56 lb in.

Reverse conversion: to go from pound-inch back to ounce-inch, divide by 0.0625 (equivalently, multiply by 16).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
