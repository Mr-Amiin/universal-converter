About Converting Light-seconds to Kilometers

Light-second and kilometer are both units of distance. A light-second is the distance light travels in vacuum in one second: exactly 299,792,458 meters. A kilometer is 1,000 meters.

Formula

km = ls × 299792.458

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 light-second equals 299792.458 kilometers, and the reverse factor is 0.000003335640952.

Simple example

1 ls × 299792.458 = 299792.458 km

1 light-second = 299792.458 kilometers.

Real-world example

10 ls × 299792.458 = 2997924.58 km

10 light-seconds = 2997924.58 kilometers.

Understanding the Light-second

Light-second (ls) measures distance. A light-second is the distance light travels in vacuum in one second: exactly 299,792,458 meters.

Standard and definition

The light-second is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the light-second today

  • Earth–Moon and spacecraft signal delays
  • Ranging and timing

Understanding the Kilometer

Kilometer (km) measures distance. A kilometer is 1,000 meters.

Standard and definition

The kilometer is covered by IAU 2015 Resolution B3 — Nominal values for selected solar and planetary quantities.

Uses of the kilometer today

  • Road distances and astronomy-scale references in metric countries
  • Geography and travel
