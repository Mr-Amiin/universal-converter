About Converting Sidereal Days to Solar Hours

Sidereal day and solar hour are both units of time. A mean sidereal day is the time Earth takes to rotate once relative to the stars: about 86,164.0905 seconds (23 h 56 min 4.0905 s). A mean solar hour is 3,600 seconds, one twenty-fourth of a mean solar day.

Formula

h = sd × 23.93446958

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 sidereal day equals 23.93446958 solar hours, and the reverse factor is 0.04178074624.

Simple example

1 sd × 23.93446958 = 23.93446958 h

1 sidereal day = 23.93446958 solar hours.

Real-world example

10 sd × 23.93446958 = 239.3446958 h

10 sidereal days = 239.3446958 solar hours.
