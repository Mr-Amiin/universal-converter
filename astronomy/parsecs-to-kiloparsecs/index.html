About Converting Parsecs to Kiloparsecs

Parsec and kiloparsec are both units of distance. A parsec is 648,000/π astronomical units, about 3.2616 light-years. A kiloparsec is 1,000 parsecs, about 3,261.56 light-years.

Formula

kpc = pc × 0.001

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 parsec equals 0.001 kiloparsecs, and the reverse factor is 1000.

Simple example

1 pc × 0.001 = 0.001 kpc

1 parsec = 0.001 kiloparsecs.

Real-world example

10 pc × 0.001 = 0.01 kpc

10 parsecs = 0.01 kiloparsecs.

Understanding the Parsec

Parsec (pc) measures distance. A parsec is 648,000/π astronomical units, about 3.2616 light-years.

Standard and definition

The parsec is covered by IAU 2015 Resolution B3 — Nominal values for selected solar and planetary quantities.

Uses of the parsec today

  • Distances to stars and galaxies
  • Professional astronomy

Understanding the Kiloparsec

Kiloparsec (kpc) measures distance. A kiloparsec is 1,000 parsecs, about 3,261.56 light-years.

Standard and definition

The kiloparsec is covered by IAU 2015 Resolution B3 — Nominal values for selected solar and planetary quantities.

Uses of the kiloparsec today

  • Distances within the Milky Way and nearby galaxies
  • Galactic structure studies
