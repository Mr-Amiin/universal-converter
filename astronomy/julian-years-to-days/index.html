About Converting Julian Years to Days

Julian year and day are both units of time. A Julian year is exactly 365.25 days of 86,400 seconds: 31,557,600 seconds. A day is 86,400 seconds.

Formula

d = a × 365.25

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 julian year equals 365.25 days, and the reverse factor is 0.002737850787.

Simple example

1 a × 365.25 = 365.25 d

1 julian year = 365.25 days.

Real-world example

10 a × 365.25 = 3652.5 d

10 julian years = 3652.5 days.

Understanding the Julian year

Julian year (a) measures time. A Julian year is exactly 365.25 days of 86,400 seconds: 31,557,600 seconds.

Convention: 365.25 days. This differs from the Gregorian average year (365.2425 days) used by the site's generic "year" unit.

Standard and definition

The julian year is covered by IAU 2015 Resolution B3 — Nominal values for selected solar and planetary quantities.

Uses of the julian year today

  • Defining the light-year and astronomical epochs
  • Stellar proper motion and orbital periods
