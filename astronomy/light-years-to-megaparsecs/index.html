About Converting Light-years to Megaparsecs

Light-year and megaparsec are both units of distance. A light-year is the distance light travels in a Julian year: 9,460,730,472,580,800 meters. A megaparsec is one million parsecs, about 3.26156 million light-years.

Formula

Mpc = ly × 3.066013938 × 10⁻⁷

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 light-year equals 3.066013938 × 10⁻⁷ megaparsecs, and the reverse factor is 3261563.777.

Simple example

1 ly × 3.066013938 × 10⁻⁷ = 3.066013938 × 10⁻⁷ Mpc

1 light-year = 3.066013938 × 10⁻⁷ megaparsecs.

Real-world example

10 ly × 3.066013938 × 10⁻⁷ = 0.000003066013938 Mpc

Standard and definition

The light-year is covered by IAU 2015 Resolution B3 — Nominal values for selected solar and planetary quantities.

Uses of the light-year today

Understanding the Megaparsec
