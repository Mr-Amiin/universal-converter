Frequently asked questions

What is meter?

The meter is the SI base unit of length, currently defined by the distance light travels in a fraction of a second.

What is tonne?

A tonne (metric ton) is a unit of mass equal to 1,000 kilograms.

Can I convert meters to tonnes?

Not directly. Meter measures distance and tonne measures mass, and the astronomy converter keeps these families separate so results stay physically meaningful.

Why doesn't the converter give a result for this pair?

Converting distance to mass would require an extra physical assumption (such as a specific object's speed, density, or orbital behavior), which isn't a fixed constant the way same-dimension factors are.

What should I use meter for?

Pair meter with other distance units, such as Kilometer, Mile, for an instant, exact conversion.

What should I use tonne for?

Pair tonne with other mass units, such as Kilogram, Earth mass, for an instant, exact conversion.
