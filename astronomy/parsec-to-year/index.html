Frequently asked questions

What is parsec?

A parsec is the distance at which one astronomical unit subtends an angle of one arcsecond, about 3.26 light-years.

What is year?

A year, in this converter, is the Julian year of exactly 365.25 days, the astronomical standard used to define the light-year.

Can I convert parsecs to years?

Not directly. Parsec measures distance and year measures time, and the astronomy converter keeps these families separate so results stay physically meaningful.

Why doesn't the converter give a result for this pair?

Converting distance to time would require an extra physical assumption (such as a specific object's speed, density, or orbital behavior), which isn't a fixed constant the way same-dimension factors are.

What should I use parsec for?

Pair parsec with other distance units, such as Meter, Kilometer, for an instant, exact conversion.

What should I use year for?

Pair year with other time units, such as Second, Day, for an instant, exact conversion.
