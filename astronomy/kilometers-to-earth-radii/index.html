About Converting Kilometers to Earth Radii

Kilometer and earth radius are both units of distance. A kilometer is 1,000 meters. The nominal equatorial Earth radius is exactly 6,378.1 kilometers (IAU 2015).

Formula

R⊕ = km × 0.0001567865038

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 kilometer equals 0.0001567865038 earth radius, and the reverse factor is 6378.1.

Simple example

1 km × 0.0001567865038 = 0.0001567865038 R⊕

1 kilometer = 0.0001567865038 earth radius.

Real-world example

10 km × 0.0001567865038 = 0.001567865038 R⊕

10 kilometers = 0.001567865038 earth radius.

Convention used on this page
