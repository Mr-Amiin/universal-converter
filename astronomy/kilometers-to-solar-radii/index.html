About Converting Kilometers to Solar Radii

Kilometer and solar radius are both units of distance. A kilometer is 1,000 meters. The nominal solar radius is exactly 695,700 kilometers (IAU 2015).

Formula

R☉ = km × 0.000001437401179

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 kilometer equals 0.000001437401179 solar radius, and the reverse factor is 695700.

Simple example

1 km × 0.000001437401179 = 0.000001437401179 R☉

1 kilometer = 0.000001437401179 solar radius.

Real-world example

10 km × 0.000001437401179 = 0.00001437401179 R☉

10 kilometers = 0.00001437401179 solar radius.
