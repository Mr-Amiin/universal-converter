About Converting Megaparsecs to Kiloparsecs

Megaparsec and kiloparsec are both units of distance. A megaparsec is one million parsecs, about 3.26156 million light-years. A kiloparsec is 1,000 parsecs, about 3,261.56 light-years.

Formula

kpc = Mpc × 1000

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 megaparsec equals 1000 kiloparsecs, and the reverse factor is 0.001.

Simple example

1 Mpc × 1000 = 1000 kpc

1 megaparsec = 1000 kiloparsecs.

Real-world example

10 Mpc × 1000 = 10000 kpc

10 megaparsecs = 10000 kiloparsecs.

Understanding the Megaparsec

Megaparsec (Mpc) measures distance. A megaparsec is one million parsecs, about 3.26156 million light-years.

Standard and definition

The megaparsec is covered by IAU 2015 Resolution B3 — Nominal values for selected solar and planetary quantities.

Uses of the megaparsec today

  • Distances to galaxies and clusters
  • Cosmological distance scales and the Hubble constant

Understanding the Kiloparsec

Kiloparsec (kpc) measures distance. A kiloparsec is 1,000 parsecs, about 3,261.56 light-years.

Standard and definition

The kiloparsec is covered by IAU 2015 Resolution B3 — Nominal values for selected solar and planetary quantities.

Uses of the kiloparsec today

  • Distances within the Milky Way and nearby galaxies
  • Galactic structure studies
