Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the jupiter mass (MJ). Jupiter mass is the mass of Jupiter, used as a reference unit for giant planets.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the solar mass (M☉). Solar mass is the mass of the Sun, used as a reference unit for stars, black holes, and galaxies.

How do I convert jupiter mass to solar mass?

Multiply the jupiter mass value by 0.000954568085. For example, 10 MJ × 0.000954568085 = 0.00954568085 M☉.

Is the jupiter mass to solar mass conversion exact?

Yes. Both units have a fixed, defined relationship to the astronomy converter's mass base unit, so the conversion factor is exact rather than a measured approximation.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

It's used in astronomy, physics, and space science whenever a mass value expressed in jupiter mass needs to be compared with, or reported in, solar mass.

Can I convert solar mass back to jupiter mass?

Yes. Multiply the solar mass value by 1047.594211 to get jupiter mass.
