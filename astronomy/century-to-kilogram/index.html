Frequently asked questions

What is century?

A century is 100 average Gregorian years.

What is kilogram?

The kilogram is the SI base unit of mass.

Can I convert century to kilogram?

Not directly. Century is a time unit and Kilogram is a mass unit, and the astronomy converter keeps these families separate so results stay physically meaningful.

Why doesn't the converter give a result for this pair?

Converting between time and mass would require an extra physical assumption (such as a specific object's density, composition, or motion), which isn't a fixed constant the way time-to-time or mass-to-mass factors are.

What should I use century for?

Use century together with other time units, such as the ones listed under related conversions below.

What should I use kilogram for?

Use kilogram together with other mass units to get an exact, dimension-consistent result.
