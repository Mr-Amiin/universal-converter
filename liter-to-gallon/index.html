Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the liter (L), used to measure volume.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the us gallon (gal), used to measure volume.

How do I convert liters to us gallons?

Multiply the number of liters by 0.2641720524 to get us gallons. For example, 5 L × 0.2641720524 = 1.32086 gal.

Is the liter to gallon conversion exact?

Yes. The US gallon is defined as exactly 3.785411784 liters, so the liter-to-gallon factor 0.2641720523581484 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

It's commonly used in fuel economy comparisons, cooking and beverage recipes, and converting metric containers to US gallon measurements.

Can I convert us gallons back to liters?

Yes. Multiply the us gallon value by 3.785411784, or divide it by 0.2641720524, to convert back to liters.
