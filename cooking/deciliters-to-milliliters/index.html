About Converting Deciliters to Milliliters

Deciliter and milliliter are both units of volume. A deciliter is one tenth of a liter: 100 milliliters. A milliliter is one thousandth of a liter (1 cm³).

Formula

mL = dL × 100

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 deciliter equals 100 milliliters, and the reverse factor is 0.01.

Simple example

1 dL × 100 = 100 mL

1 deciliter = 100 milliliters.

Real-world example

10 dL × 100 = 1000 mL

10 deciliters = 1000 milliliters.
