Frequently asked questions

What is this page for?

This page gives a structured overview of the cooking converter category.

Can I convert cups to grams directly?

Cups measure volume and grams measure mass, so a direct conversion needs an ingredient's density. This category keeps volume and mass conversions separate for accuracy.

Are US and metric cooking measurements the same?

No. A US cup, tablespoon, and teaspoon are slightly different from their metric counterparts, and both are included here.
