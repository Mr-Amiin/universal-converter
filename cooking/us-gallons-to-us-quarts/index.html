About Converting US Gallons to US Quarts

US gallon and US quart are both units of volume. The US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters (231 cubic inches). The US liquid quart is 946.352946 milliliters (1/4 gallon).

Formula

qt = gal × 4

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 US gallon equals 4 US quarts, and the reverse factor is 0.25.

Simple example

1 gal × 4 = 4 qt

1 US gallon = 4 US quarts.

Real-world example

10 gal × 4 = 40 qt

10 US gallons = 40 US quarts.
