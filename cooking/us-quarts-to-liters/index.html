About Converting US Quarts to Liters

US quart and liter are both units of volume. The US liquid quart is 946.352946 milliliters (1/4 gallon). The liter is exactly one cubic decimeter (0.001 m³).

Formula

L = qt × 0.946352946

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 US quart equals 0.946352946 liters, and the reverse factor is 1.056688209.

Simple example

1 qt × 0.946352946 = 0.946352946 L

1 US quart = 0.946352946 liters.

Real-world example

10 qt × 0.946352946 = 9.46352946 L

10 US quarts = 9.46352946 liters.
