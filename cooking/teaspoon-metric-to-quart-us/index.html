Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the metric teaspoon (metric tsp). A metric teaspoon is defined as exactly 5 milliliters.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the quart (qt). A US liquid quart equals 2 US pints, or about 946.35 milliliters.

How do I convert metric teaspoon to quart?

Multiply the metric teaspoon value by 0.005283441047. For example, 10 metric tsp × 0.005283441047 = 0.052834 qt.

Is the metric teaspoon to quart conversion exact?

Yes. This conversion uses a fixed factor defined for cooking measurement standards, so results are consistent every time.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

It's used when following recipes, adjusting ingredient quantities, or converting between US customary and metric cooking measurements.

Can I convert quart back to metric teaspoon?

Yes. Multiply the quart value by 189.2705892 to get metric teaspoon.
