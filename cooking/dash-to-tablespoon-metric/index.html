Frequently asked questions

How many metric tablespoons are in one dash?

One Dash equals 0.041074 metric tablespoons.

Why is the dash used in this kind of recipe?

Recipes use the dash when exact precision doesn't matter and a rough small quantity communicates the intent better than a fraction of a teaspoon.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both dashes and metric tablespoons measure the same physical quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor with no density assumption involved.

Can I use this conversion for any ingredient?

Yes. Since dash and metric tablespoon both measure the same kind of quantity, this conversion applies equally to any ingredient.

When would a recipe use dashes instead of metric tablespoons?

Dash is typically used in seasoning, hot sauce, bitters in cocktails, and other 'to taste' ingredient additions, while metric tablespoon shows up in recipes published in Australia, the UK, and much of Europe.
