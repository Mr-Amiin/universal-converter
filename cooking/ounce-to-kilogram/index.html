Frequently asked questions

How many kilograms are in one ounce?

One Ounce equals 0.02835 kilograms.

Why is the ounce used in this kind of recipe?

US grocery packaging and recipes weigh solid ingredients in ounces because it's the traditional small-mass unit in the American customary system.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both ounces and kilograms measure the same physical quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor with no density assumption involved.

Can I use this conversion for any ingredient?

Yes. Since ounce and kilogram both measure the same kind of quantity, this conversion applies equally to any ingredient.

When would a recipe use ounces instead of kilograms?

Ounce is typically used in measuring dry ingredients like meat, cheese, and packaged goods in US recipes and grocery labeling, while kilogram shows up in large-batch and commercial cooking, bulk ingredient purchasing, and recipe scaling.
