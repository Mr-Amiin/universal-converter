About Converting Dessertspoons to Teaspoons

Dessertspoon and metric teaspoon are both units of volume. The metric dessertspoon is 10 milliliters. The metric teaspoon is exactly 5 milliliters.

Formula

tsp = dstsp × 2

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 dessertspoon equals 2 metric teaspoons, and the reverse factor is 0.5.

Simple example

1 dstsp × 2 = 2 tsp

1 dessertspoon = 2 metric teaspoons.

Real-world example

10 dstsp × 2 = 20 tsp

10 dessertspoons = 20 metric teaspoons.
