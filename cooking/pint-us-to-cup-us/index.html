Frequently asked questions

How many cups are in one pint?

One Pint equals 2 cups.

Why is the pint used in this kind of recipe?

US beverage servings and mid-batch recipes are measured in pints because it sits conveniently between the cup and the quart in the American volume system.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both pints and cups measure the same physical quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor with no density assumption involved.

Can I use this conversion for any ingredient?

Yes. Since pint and cup both measure the same kind of quantity, this conversion applies equally to any ingredient.

When would a recipe use pints instead of cups?

Pint is typically used in measuring beer, cream, and mid-sized liquid quantities in US recipes and beverage service, while cup shows up in American home recipes, baking, and US packaged-food serving sizes.
