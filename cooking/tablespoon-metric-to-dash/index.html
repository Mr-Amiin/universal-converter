Frequently asked questions

How many dashes are in one metric tablespoon?

One Metric tablespoon equals 24.346096 dashes.

Why is the metric tablespoon used in this kind of recipe?

Metric-system countries round the tablespoon to 15 mL so it converts cleanly with milliliters and liters elsewhere in a recipe.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both metric tablespoons and dashes measure the same physical quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor with no density assumption involved.

Can I use this conversion for any ingredient?

Yes. Since metric tablespoon and dash both measure the same kind of quantity, this conversion applies equally to any ingredient.

When would a recipe use metric tablespoons instead of dashes?

Metric tablespoon is typically used in recipes published in Australia, the UK, and much of Europe, while dash shows up in seasoning, hot sauce, bitters in cocktails, and other 'to taste' ingredient additions.
