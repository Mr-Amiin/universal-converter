About Converting US Cups to Deciliters

US cup and deciliter are both units of volume. The US customary cup is 236.5882365 milliliters (1/16 gallon). A deciliter is one tenth of a liter: 100 milliliters.

Formula

dL = cup × 2.365882365

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 US cup equals 2.365882365 deciliters, and the reverse factor is 0.4226752838.

Simple example

1 cup × 2.365882365 = 2.365882365 dL

1 US cup = 2.365882365 deciliters.

Real-world example

10 cup × 2.365882365 = 23.65882365 dL
