Frequently asked questions

How many us gallons are in one quart?

One Quart equals 0.25 us gallons.

Why is the quart used in this kind of recipe?

US recipes for soups, stocks, and preserves are commonly measured in quarts because it matches standard US canning-jar and storage-container sizes.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both quarts and us gallons measure the same physical quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor with no density assumption involved.

Can I use this conversion for any ingredient?

Yes. Since quart and us gallon both measure the same kind of quantity, this conversion applies equally to any ingredient.

When would a recipe use quarts instead of us gallons?

Quart is typically used in measuring soups, stocks, and mid-to-large batch liquid recipes in the US, while us gallon shows up in large-batch cooking and brewing, fuel measurement, and bulk liquid ingredients in the US.
