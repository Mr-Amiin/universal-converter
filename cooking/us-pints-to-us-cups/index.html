About Converting US Pints to US Cups

US pint and US cup are both units of volume. The US liquid pint is 473.176473 milliliters (1/8 gallon, 2 cups). The US customary cup is 236.5882365 milliliters (1/16 gallon).

Formula

cup = pt × 2

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 US pint equals 2 US cups, and the reverse factor is 0.5.

Simple example

1 pt × 2 = 2 cup

1 US pint = 2 US cups.

Real-world example

10 pt × 2 = 20 cup

10 US pints = 20 US cups.
