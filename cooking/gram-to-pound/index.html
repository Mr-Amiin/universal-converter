Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in one gram?

One Gram equals 0.002205 pounds.

Why is the pound used in this kind of recipe?

US grocery stores price and package larger food quantities in pounds because it's the standard bulk-mass unit in American commerce and recipes.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both grams and pounds measure the same physical quantity, so this conversion uses a fixed, exact factor with no density assumption involved.

Can I use this conversion for any ingredient?

Yes. Since gram and pound both measure the same kind of quantity, this conversion applies equally to any ingredient.

When would a recipe use grams instead of pounds?

Gram is typically used in precise baking recipes worldwide, where weighing ingredients gives more consistent results than measuring by volume, while pound shows up in purchasing and measuring meat, flour, sugar, and other bulk ingredients in the US.
