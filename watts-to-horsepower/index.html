About this conversion

This power converter turns watt into mechanical horsepower using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. A 750-watt electric motor produces about 1.01 horsepower. The watt is named for James Watt and was adopted as the SI unit of power in 1960, defined as one joule per second. James Watt popularized horsepower in the 1780s to compare his steam engines to the working output of draft horses. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same watt-to-mechanical horsepower factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
