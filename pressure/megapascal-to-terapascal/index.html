Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the megapascal (MPa).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the terapascal (TPa).

How do I convert megapascal to terapascal?

Multiply the megapascal value by 1.000000e-06. For example, 5 MPa × 1.000000e-06 = 5.000000e-06 TPa.

Why do megapascal and terapascal need converting at all?

Mega (M) represents 10^6, one of the most commonly used SI prefixes. Tera (T) represents 10^12, familiar from terabyte-scale data storage.

Is the megapascal to terapascal conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between megapascal and terapascal is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-06), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert terapascal back to megapascal?

Yes. Multiply the terapascal value by 1000000 (or divide by 1.000000e-06) to get megapascal.

About this conversion

This page converts megapascal (MPa) into terapascal (TPa) within the pressure converter, using the fixed relationship 1 MPa = 1.000000e-06 TPa.

Worked example: to convert 10 MPa to TPa, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1.000000e-06 = 1.000000e-05 TPa.

A car tire inflated to 32 MPa is at about 3.200000e-05 TPa.

Reverse conversion: to go from terapascal back to megapascal, divide by 1.000000e-06 (equivalently, multiply by 1000000).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
