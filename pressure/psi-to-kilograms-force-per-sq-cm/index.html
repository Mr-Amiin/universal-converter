About Converting PSI to Kilograms-force per sq cm

Pound-force per square inch and kilogram-force per square centimeter are both units of pressure. One psi is exactly 6,894.757293168 pascals (one pound-force acting on one square inch). One kilogram-force per square centimeter is 98,066.5 pascals (the technical atmosphere).

Formula

kgf/cm² = psi × 0.07030695796

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 pound-force per square inch equals 0.07030695796 kilogram-forces per square centimeter, and the reverse factor is 14.22334331.

Uses of the pound-force per square inch today

Understanding the Kilogram-force per square centimeter

Kilogram-force per square centimeter (kgf/cm²) measures pressure. One kilogram-force per square centimeter is 98,066.5 pascals (the technical atmosphere).

Standard and definition

The kilogram-force per square centimeter is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the kilogram-force per square centimeter today

  • Pressure gauges, tire and boiler ratings in older and non-SI specifications
  • Industrial pneumatics and hydraulics in Asia and Europe
