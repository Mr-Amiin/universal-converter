About Converting Inches of Water to Pascals

Inch of water and pascal are both units of pressure. The conventional inch of water is 0.0254 m of water column at the conventional 9,806.65 Pa per meter of water: 249.08891 pascals. The pascal is the SI unit of pressure: one newton per square meter.

Formula

Pa = inH2O × 249.08891

This factor follows the definitions and conventions stated below: 1 inch of water equals 249.08891 pascals, and the reverse factor is 0.00401463076.

1 inch of water = 249.08891 pascals.

Understanding the Inch of water

Inch of water (inH2O) measures pressure. The conventional inch of water is 0.0254 m of water column at the conventional 9,806.65 Pa per meter of water: 249.08891 pascals.

Convention: conventional inch of water, 0.0254 × 9,806.65 Pa = 249.08891 Pa (consistent with this site's meter of water). Values at 4 °C (≈249.082 Pa) or 60 °F (≈248.84 Pa) differ slightly.

Standard and definition

The inch of water is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the inch of water today

  • HVAC duct and fan static pressure
  • Low-pressure gas appliance and furnace specifications

Understanding the Pascal

Pascal (Pa) measures pressure. The pascal is the SI unit of pressure: one newton per square meter.

Standard and definition

The pascal is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the pascal today

  • Scientific and engineering pressure
  • Acoustics and HVAC
