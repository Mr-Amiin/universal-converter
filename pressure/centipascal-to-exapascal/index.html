Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is the centipascal (cPa).

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is the exapascal (EPa).

How do I convert centipascal to exapascal?

Multiply the centipascal value by 1.000000e-20. For example, 5 cPa × 1.000000e-20 = 5.000000e-20 EPa.

Why do centipascal and exapascal need converting at all?

Centi (c) represents 10^-2, familiar from the centimeter. Exa (E) represents 10^18, common in describing large-scale data and energy quantities.

Is the centipascal to exapascal conversion exact?

Yes. The relationship between centipascal and exapascal is a fixed, defined ratio (× 1.000000e-20), so results are exact rather than a rounded estimate, limited only by the precision you choose to display.

Can I convert exapascal back to centipascal?

Yes. Multiply the exapascal value by 1.000000e+20 (or divide by 1.000000e-20) to get centipascal.

About this conversion

This page converts centipascal (cPa) into exapascal (EPa) within the pressure converter, using the fixed relationship 1 cPa = 1.000000e-20 EPa.

Worked example: to convert 10 cPa to EPa, multiply by the conversion factor: 10 × 1.000000e-20 = 1.000000e-19 EPa.

A car tire inflated to 32 cPa is at about 3.200000e-19 EPa.

Reverse conversion: to go from exapascal back to centipascal, divide by 1.000000e-20 (equivalently, multiply by 1.000000e+20).

Accuracy: this conversion uses a fixed, defined factor rather than a measured approximation, so results are exact to the precision shown; rounding only affects how many digits are displayed, not the underlying value.
