Unit history

Megaliter per Months: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The month originally tracked the roughly 29.5-day lunar cycle before calendar reforms fixed it to the modern Gregorian lengths.

Microliter per Minutes: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.
