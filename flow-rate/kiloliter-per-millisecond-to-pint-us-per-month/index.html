Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is kiloliters per millisecond (kL/ms). The Kiloliter (kL) is a unit of volume. The Millisecond (ms) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is us pints per month (pt/mo). The US Pint (pt) is a unit of volume. The Month (mo) is a unit of time.

How do I convert kiloliters per millisecond to us pints per month?

Multiply the kiloliters per millisecond value by 5.55764e+12 to get us pints per month. For example, 10 kL/ms × 5.55764e+12 = 5.55764e+13 pt/mo.

Is the kiloliters per millisecond to us pints per month conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert us pints per month back to kiloliters per millisecond?

Yes. Divide the us pints per month value by 5.55764e+12 (or multiply by 1.79932e-13) to get kiloliters per millisecond.

About this conversion

This page converts kiloliters per millisecond (kL/ms) into us pints per month (pt/mo), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The Kiloliter (kL) is a unit of volume. The Millisecond (ms) is a unit of time.

The US Pint (pt) is a unit of volume. The Month (mo) is a unit of time.

The formula is pt/mo = kL/ms × 5.55764e+12, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
