Unit history

Imperial quart per Days: The imperial quart was set by the 1824 Act as 1/4 of an imperial gallon. The day as a 24-hour period was formalized by the ancient Egyptians and refined by Hellenistic astronomers.

Imperial fluid ounce per Seconds: The imperial fluid ounce was fixed by the 1824 Act as 1/20 of an imperial pint. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.
