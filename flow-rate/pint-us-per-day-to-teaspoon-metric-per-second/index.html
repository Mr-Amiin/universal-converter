Unit history

Pint per Days: The pint has English origins traceable to the 13th century, with US and imperial versions diverging in size after 1824. The day as a 24-hour period was formalized by the ancient Egyptians and refined by Hellenistic astronomers.

Metric teaspoon per Seconds: The metric teaspoon (5 mL) was defined for countries using metric kitchen measurement. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.
