Unit history

Megaliter per Millisecond: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.

Pint per Months: The pint has English origins traceable to the 13th century, with US and imperial versions diverging in size after 1824. The month originally tracked the roughly 29.5-day lunar cycle before calendar reforms fixed it to the modern Gregorian lengths.
