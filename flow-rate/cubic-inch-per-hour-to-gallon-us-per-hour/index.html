Can I convert Gallon per Hours back to Cubic inch per Hours?

When would I use a hour-based flow unit like Cubic inch per Hours?

Cubic inch per Hours expresses volume moved per hour, which is common in engineering, industrial, and utility contexts where flow is measured or billed over that time period.

How accurate is the Cubic inch per Hours to Gallon per Hours conversion?
