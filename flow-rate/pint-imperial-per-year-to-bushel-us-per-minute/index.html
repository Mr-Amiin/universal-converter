Unit history

Imperial pint per Years: The imperial pint was fixed by the 1824 British Weights and Measures Act at 1/8 of an imperial gallon. The year is based on Earth's orbital period; the modern Gregorian calendar year was adopted in 1582.

Bushel per Minutes: The bushel is a medieval English dry-measure unit, standardized for grain trade as early as the 13th century. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.
