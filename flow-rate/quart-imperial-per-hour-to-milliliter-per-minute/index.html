Unit history

Imperial quart per Hours: The imperial quart was set by the 1824 Act as 1/4 of an imperial gallon. The hour's division into 60 minutes also traces to Babylonian sexagesimal timekeeping, formalized in Hellenistic astronomy.

Milliliter per Minutes: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.
