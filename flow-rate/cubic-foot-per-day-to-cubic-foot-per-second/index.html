Frequently asked questions

How do you convert Cubic foot per Days to Cubic foot per Seconds?

Multiply the Cubic foot per Days value by 1.15740740741e-05 to get Cubic foot per Seconds. The formula is ft3/s = ft3/d × 1.15740740741e-05.

How many Cubic foot per Seconds are in one Cubic foot per Days?

One Cubic foot per Days equals 1.15740740741e-05 Cubic foot per Seconds.

Can I convert Cubic foot per Seconds back to Cubic foot per Days?

Yes. Divide the Cubic foot per Seconds value by 1.15740740741e-05, or multiply by 86400, to get Cubic foot per Days.

When would I use a day-based flow unit like Cubic foot per Days?

Cubic foot per Days expresses volume moved per day, which is common in engineering, industrial, and utility contexts where flow is measured or billed over that time period.
