Unit history

Imperial pint per Seconds: The imperial pint was fixed by the 1824 British Weights and Measures Act at 1/8 of an imperial gallon. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.

Cubic Meter per Fortnight: The cubic meter is a direct SI-derived unit built on the meter, defined in 1795 as one ten-millionth of the distance from the pole to the equator. Fortnight is a contraction of the Old English 'feowertyne niht' (fourteen nights), used in Britain since the medieval period.
