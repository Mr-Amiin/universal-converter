Unit history

Imperial pint per Months: The imperial pint was fixed by the 1824 British Weights and Measures Act at 1/8 of an imperial gallon. The month originally tracked the roughly 29.5-day lunar cycle before calendar reforms fixed it to the modern Gregorian lengths.

Hectoliter per Years: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The year is based on Earth's orbital period; the modern Gregorian calendar year was adopted in 1582.
