Unit history

Pint per Weeks: The pint has English origins traceable to the 13th century, with US and imperial versions diverging in size after 1824. The 7-day week has roots in ancient Babylonian astrology and was spread through Judeo-Christian and Roman calendars.

Tablespoon per Years: The tablespoon was standardized alongside the teaspoon as US recipe measurement became formalized. The year is based on Earth's orbital period; the modern Gregorian calendar year was adopted in 1582.

How many Tablespoon per Years are in one Pint per Weeks?

Can I convert Tablespoon per Years back to Pint per Weeks?

Yes. Divide the Tablespoon per Years value by 1669.68, or multiply by 0.000598917157779, to get Pint per Weeks.

Where is a unit like Pint per Weeks actually used?
