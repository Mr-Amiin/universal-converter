About Converting US Gallons per Hour to Liters per Hour

US gallon per hour and liter per hour are both units of volumetric flow rate. One US gallon per hour is 3.785411784 liters per hour. One liter per hour is 1/3,600,000 cubic meters per second.

Formula

L/h = gal/h × 3.785411784

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 US gallon per hour equals 3.785411784 liters per hour, and the reverse factor is 0.2641720524.

Simple example

1 gal/h × 3.785411784 = 3.785411784 L/h

1 US gallon per hour = 3.785411784 liters per hour.

Real-world example
