Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is decaliters per decade (daL/decade). The Decaliter (daL) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is cubic inches per hour (in3/h). The Cubic Inch (in3) is a unit of volume. The Hour (h) is a unit of time.

How do I convert decaliters per decade to cubic inches per hour?

Multiply the decaliters per decade value by 0.00696156 to get cubic inches per hour. For example, 10 daL/decade × 0.00696156 = 0.0696156 in3/h.

Is the decaliters per decade to cubic inches per hour conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert cubic inches per hour back to decaliters per decade?

Yes. Divide the cubic inches per hour value by 0.00696156 (or multiply by 143.646) to get decaliters per decade.

About this conversion

This page converts decaliters per decade (daL/decade) into cubic inches per hour (in3/h), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The Decaliter (daL) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

The Cubic Inch (in3) is a unit of volume. The Hour (h) is a unit of time.

The formula is in3/h = daL/decade × 0.00696156, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
