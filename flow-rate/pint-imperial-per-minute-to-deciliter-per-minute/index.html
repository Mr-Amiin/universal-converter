Unit history

Imperial pint per Minutes: The imperial pint was fixed by the 1824 British Weights and Measures Act at 1/8 of an imperial gallon. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.

Deciliter per Minutes: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.
