Unit history

Pint per Minutes: The pint has English origins traceable to the 13th century, with US and imperial versions diverging in size after 1824. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.

Metric teaspoon per Fortnight: The metric teaspoon (5 mL) was defined for countries using metric kitchen measurement. Fortnight is a contraction of the Old English 'feowertyne niht' (fourteen nights), used in Britain since the medieval period.
