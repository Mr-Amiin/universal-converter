How many Kiloliter per Minutes are in one Cubic foot per Days?

Can I convert Kiloliter per Minutes back to Cubic foot per Days?

Yes. Divide the Kiloliter per Minutes value by 1.96644768e-05, or multiply by 50853.1200789, to get Cubic foot per Days.

When would I use a day-based flow unit like Cubic foot per Days?
