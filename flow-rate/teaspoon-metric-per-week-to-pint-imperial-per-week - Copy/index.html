From definition

Metric teaspoon per Week (metric tsp/wk) is a volumetric flow rate unit that measures how much volume, expressed in metric teaspoon(s), passes a given point every week. About this conversion

This flow rate page converts Metric teaspoon per Week (metric tsp/wk) to Imperial pint per Week (imp pt/wk) using the exact volumetric flow rate factor from the Universal Converter engine. A flow of one metric teaspoon every week is the same physical flow as 0.00879876993196 imperial pint(s) every week, so this page is useful for pipeline sizing, pump and pipe selection, HVAC airflow and liquid handling, irrigation and water-supply planning, and lab or industrial process calculations that mix metric, US customary, or imperial flow units. For example, a pump, pipe, or flow meter running at 1 metric tsp/wk is moving flow equivalent to 0.00879876993196 imp pt/wk — handy when one spec sheet lists flow in metric teaspoon per week and another lists it in imperial pint per week.

Metric flow units are built from SI volume and time units established under the metric system, while US customary flow units combine older trade-derived volume measures (like the gallon or cup) with standard time units, which is why the same physical flow rate looks very different when expressed in each system.
