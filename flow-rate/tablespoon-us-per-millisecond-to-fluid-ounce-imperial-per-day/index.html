From definition

Tablespoon per Millisecond (tbsp/ms) is a volumetric flow rate unit that measures how much volume, expressed in tablespoon(s), passes a given point every millisecond. It is used to compare flow across pipes, pumps, channels, and instruments that report volume moved over that particular time interval.

To definition

Imperial fluid ounce per Day (imp fl oz/d) is a volumetric flow rate unit that measures how much volume, expressed in imperial fluid ounce(s), passes a given point every day. It is used to compare flow across pipes, pumps, channels, and instruments that report volume moved over that particular time interval.

About this conversion

This flow rate page converts Tablespoon per Millisecond (tbsp/ms) to Imperial fluid ounce per Day (imp fl oz/d) using the exact volumetric flow rate factor from the Universal Converter engine. A flow of one tablespoon every millisecond is the same physical flow as 44964405.97 imperial fluid ounce(s) every day, so this page is useful for pipeline sizing, pump and pipe selection, HVAC airflow and liquid handling, irrigation and water-supply planning, and lab or industrial process calculations that mix metric, US customary, or imperial flow units. For example, a kitchen faucet or garden hose running at 1 tbsp/ms is moving flow equivalent to 44964405.97 imp fl oz/d — handy when one spec sheet lists flow in tablespoon per millisecond and another lists it in imperial fluid ounce per day.

Metric flow units are built from SI volume and time units established under the metric system, while US customary flow units combine older trade-derived volume measures (like the gallon or cup) with standard time units, which is why the same physical flow rate looks very different when expressed in each system.
