From definition

Tablespoon per Month (tbsp/mo) is a volumetric flow rate unit that measures how much volume, expressed in tablespoon(s), passes a given point every month. It is used to compare flow across pipes, pumps, channels, and instruments that report volume moved over that particular time interval.

To definition

Cubic foot per Day (ft3/d) is a volumetric flow rate unit that measures how much volume, expressed in cubic foot(s), passes a given point every day. It is used to compare flow across pipes, pumps, channels, and instruments that report volume moved over that particular time interval.

About this conversion

This flow rate page converts Tablespoon per Month (tbsp/mo) to Cubic foot per Day (ft3/d) using the exact volumetric flow rate factor from the Universal Converter engine. A flow of one tablespoon every month is the same physical flow as 1.71564810822e-05 cubic foot(s) every day, so this page is useful for pipeline sizing, pump and pipe selection, HVAC airflow and liquid handling, irrigation and water-supply planning, and lab or industrial process calculations that mix metric, US customary, or imperial flow units. For example, a kitchen faucet or garden hose running at 1 tbsp/mo is moving flow equivalent to 1.71564810822e-05 ft3/d — handy when one spec sheet lists flow in tablespoon per month and another lists it in cubic foot per day.

Metric flow units are built from SI volume and time units established under the metric system, while US customary flow units combine older trade-derived volume measures (like the gallon or cup) with standard time units, which is why the same physical flow rate looks very different when expressed in each system.
