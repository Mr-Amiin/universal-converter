Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is us bushels per decade (bu/decade). The US Bushel (bu) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is metric teaspoons per second (metric tsp/s). The Metric Teaspoon (metric tsp) is a unit of volume. The Second (s) is a unit of time.

How do I convert us bushels per decade to metric teaspoons per second?

Multiply the us bushels per decade value by 2.23336e-5 to get metric teaspoons per second. For example, 10 bu/decade × 2.23336e-5 = 0.000223336 metric tsp/s.

Is the us bushels per decade to metric teaspoons per second conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert metric teaspoons per second back to us bushels per decade?

Yes. Divide the metric teaspoons per second value by 2.23336e-5 (or multiply by 44775.5) to get us bushels per decade.

About this conversion

This page converts us bushels per decade (bu/decade) into metric teaspoons per second (metric tsp/s), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The US Bushel (bu) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

The Metric Teaspoon (metric tsp) is a unit of volume. The Second (s) is a unit of time.

The formula is metric tsp/s = bu/decade × 2.23336e-5, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
