Unit history

Milliliter per Weeks: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The 7-day week has roots in ancient Babylonian astrology and was spread through Judeo-Christian and Roman calendars.

Fluid ounce per Fortnight: The fluid ounce originates in apothecary and English customary measure, later split into US and imperial standards. Fortnight is a contraction of the Old English 'feowertyne niht' (fourteen nights), used in Britain since the medieval period.
