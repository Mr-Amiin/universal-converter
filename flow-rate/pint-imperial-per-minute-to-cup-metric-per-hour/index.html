Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is imperial pints per minute (imp pt/min). The Imperial Pint (imp pt) is a unit of volume. The Minute (min) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is metric cups per hour (metric cup/h). The Metric Cup (metric cup) is a unit of volume. The Hour (h) is a unit of time.

How do I convert imperial pints per minute to metric cups per hour?

Multiply the imperial pints per minute value by 136.383 to get metric cups per hour. For example, 10 imp pt/min × 136.383 = 1363.83 metric cup/h.

Is the imperial pints per minute to metric cups per hour conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert metric cups per hour back to imperial pints per minute?

Yes. Divide the metric cups per hour value by 136.383 (or multiply by 0.00733231) to get imperial pints per minute.

About this conversion

This page converts imperial pints per minute (imp pt/min) into metric cups per hour (metric cup/h), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The Imperial Pint (imp pt) is a unit of volume. The Minute (min) is a unit of time.

The Metric Cup (metric cup) is a unit of volume. The Hour (h) is a unit of time.

The formula is metric cup/h = imp pt/min × 136.383, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
