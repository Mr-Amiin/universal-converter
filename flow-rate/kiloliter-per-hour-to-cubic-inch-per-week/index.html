Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is kiloliters per hour (kL/h). The Kiloliter (kL) is a unit of volume. The Hour (h) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is cubic inches per week (in3/wk). The Cubic Inch (in3) is a unit of volume. The Week (wk) is a unit of time.

How do I convert kiloliters per hour to cubic inches per week?

Multiply the kiloliters per hour value by 1.0252e+7 to get cubic inches per week. For example, 10 kL/h × 1.0252e+7 = 1.0252e+8 in3/wk.

Is the kiloliters per hour to cubic inches per week conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert cubic inches per week back to kiloliters per hour?

Yes. Divide the cubic inches per week value by 1.0252e+7 (or multiply by 9.7542e-8) to get kiloliters per hour.

About this conversion

This page converts kiloliters per hour (kL/h) into cubic inches per week (in3/wk), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The Kiloliter (kL) is a unit of volume. The Hour (h) is a unit of time.

The Cubic Inch (in3) is a unit of volume. The Week (wk) is a unit of time.

The formula is in3/wk = kL/h × 1.0252e+7, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
