Unit history

Megaliter per Minutes: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.

Cubic yard per Days: The cubic yard derives from the yard, defined relative to the English foot since medieval times. The day as a 24-hour period was formalized by the ancient Egyptians and refined by Hellenistic astronomers.
