Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is us quarts per minute (qt/min). The US Quart (qt) is a unit of volume. The Minute (min) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is hectoliters per decade (hL/decade). The Hectoliter (hL) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

How do I convert us quarts per minute to hectoliters per decade?

Multiply the us quarts per minute value by 49773.4 to get hectoliters per decade. For example, 10 qt/min × 49773.4 = 497734 hL/decade.

Is the us quarts per minute to hectoliters per decade conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert hectoliters per decade back to us quarts per minute?

Yes. Divide the hectoliters per decade value by 49773.4 (or multiply by 2.00911e-5) to get us quarts per minute.

About this conversion

This page converts us quarts per minute (qt/min) into hectoliters per decade (hL/decade), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The US Quart (qt) is a unit of volume. The Minute (min) is a unit of time.

The Hectoliter (hL) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

The formula is hL/decade = qt/min × 49773.4, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
