Unit history

Milliliter per Days: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The day as a 24-hour period was formalized by the ancient Egyptians and refined by Hellenistic astronomers.

Cubic inch per Seconds: The cubic inch shares the same English-foot lineage as the cubic foot, formalized in British and US customary systems. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.
