Unit history

Imperial quart per Hours: The imperial quart was set by the 1824 Act as 1/4 of an imperial gallon. The hour's division into 60 minutes also traces to Babylonian sexagesimal timekeeping, formalized in Hellenistic astronomy.

Cubic Millimeter per Millisecond: The 'Cubic' prefix was from the Latin 'mille' (thousand); an original 1795 metric prefix. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.
