Unit history

Megaliter per Years: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The year is based on Earth's orbital period; the modern Gregorian calendar year was adopted in 1582.

Pint per Fortnight: The pint has English origins traceable to the 13th century, with US and imperial versions diverging in size after 1824. Fortnight is a contraction of the Old English 'feowertyne niht' (fourteen nights), used in Britain since the medieval period.
