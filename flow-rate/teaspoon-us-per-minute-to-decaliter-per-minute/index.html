From definition

Teaspoon per Minute (tsp/min) is a volumetric flow rate unit that measures how much volume, expressed in teaspoon(s), passes a given point every minute. It is used to compare flow across pipes, pumps, channels, and instruments that report volume moved over that particular time interval.

About this conversion

This flow rate page converts Teaspoon per Minute (tsp/min) to Decaliter per Minute (daL/min) using the exact volumetric flow rate factor from the Universal Converter engine. A flow of one teaspoon every minute is the same physical flow as 0.000492892159375 decaliter(s) every minute, so this page is useful for pipeline sizing, pump and pipe selection, HVAC airflow and liquid handling, irrigation and water-supply planning, and lab or industrial process calculations that mix metric, US customary, or imperial flow units. For example, a kitchen faucet or garden hose running at 1 tsp/min is moving flow equivalent to 0.000492892159375 daL/min — handy when one spec sheet lists flow in teaspoon per minute and another lists it in decaliter per minute.

Metric flow units are built from SI volume and time units established under the metric system, while US customary flow units combine older trade-derived volume measures (like the gallon or cup) with standard time units, which is why the same physical flow rate looks very different when expressed in each system.
