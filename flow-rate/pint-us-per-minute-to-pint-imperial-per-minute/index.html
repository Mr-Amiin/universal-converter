Unit history

Pint per Minutes: The pint has English origins traceable to the 13th century, with US and imperial versions diverging in size after 1824. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.

Imperial pint per Minutes: The imperial pint was fixed by the 1824 British Weights and Measures Act at 1/8 of an imperial gallon. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.
