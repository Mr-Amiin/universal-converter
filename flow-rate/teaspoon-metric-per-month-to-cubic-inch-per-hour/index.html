Metric teaspoon per Month (metric tsp/mo) is a volumetric flow rate unit that measures how much volume, expressed in metric teaspoon(s), passes a given point every month. About this conversion

This flow rate page converts Metric teaspoon per Month (metric tsp/mo) to Cubic inch per Hour (in3/h) using the exact volumetric flow rate factor from the Universal Converter engine. A flow of one metric teaspoon every month is the same physical flow as 0.000417693341374 cubic inch(s) every hour, so this page is useful for pipeline sizing, pump and pipe selection, HVAC airflow and liquid handling, irrigation and water-supply planning, and lab or industrial process calculations that mix metric, US customary, or imperial flow units. For example, a small hydraulic or fuel-injection system running at 1 metric tsp/mo is moving flow equivalent to 0.000417693341374 in3/h — handy when one spec sheet lists flow in metric teaspoon per month and another lists it in cubic inch per hour.

Metric flow units are built from SI volume and time units established under the metric system, while US customary flow units combine older trade-derived volume measures (like the gallon or cup) with standard time units, which is why the same physical flow rate looks very different when expressed in each system.
