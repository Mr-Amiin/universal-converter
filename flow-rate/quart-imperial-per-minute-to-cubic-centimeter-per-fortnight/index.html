Unit history

Imperial quart per Minutes: The imperial quart was set by the 1824 Act as 1/4 of an imperial gallon. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.

Cubic Centimeter per Fortnight: The 'Cubic' prefix was from the Latin 'centum' (hundred); an original 1795 metric prefix. Fortnight is a contraction of the Old English 'feowertyne niht' (fourteen nights), used in Britain since the medieval period.
