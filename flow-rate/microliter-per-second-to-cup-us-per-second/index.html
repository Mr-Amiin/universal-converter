Unit history

Microliter per Seconds: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.

Cup per Seconds: The US cup emerged from 19th-century American recipe standardization and is fixed at 1/16 of a US gallon. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.
