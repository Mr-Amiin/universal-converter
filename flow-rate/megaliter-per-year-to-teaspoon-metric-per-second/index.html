Unit history

Megaliter per Years: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The year is based on Earth's orbital period; the modern Gregorian calendar year was adopted in 1582.

Metric teaspoon per Seconds: The metric teaspoon (5 mL) was defined for countries using metric kitchen measurement. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.
