Unit history

Megaliter per Months: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The month originally tracked the roughly 29.5-day lunar cycle before calendar reforms fixed it to the modern Gregorian lengths.

Tablespoon per Millisecond: The tablespoon was standardized alongside the teaspoon as US recipe measurement became formalized. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.
