Unit history

Microliter per Months: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The month originally tracked the roughly 29.5-day lunar cycle before calendar reforms fixed it to the modern Gregorian lengths.

Centiliter per Weeks: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The 7-day week has roots in ancient Babylonian astrology and was spread through Judeo-Christian and Roman calendars.
