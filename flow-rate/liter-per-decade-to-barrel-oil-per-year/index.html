Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is liters per decade (L/decade). The Liter (L) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is oil barrels per year (bbl/yr). The Oil Barrel (bbl) is a unit of volume. The Year (yr) is a unit of time.

How do I convert liters per decade to oil barrels per year?

Multiply the liters per decade value by 0.000628981 to get oil barrels per year. For example, 10 L/decade × 0.000628981 = 0.00628981 bbl/yr.

Is the liters per decade to oil barrels per year conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert oil barrels per year back to liters per decade?

Yes. Divide the oil barrels per year value by 0.000628981 (or multiply by 1589.87) to get liters per decade.

About this conversion

This page converts liters per decade (L/decade) into oil barrels per year (bbl/yr), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The Liter (L) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

The Oil Barrel (bbl) is a unit of volume. The Year (yr) is a unit of time.

The formula is bbl/yr = L/decade × 0.000628981, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
