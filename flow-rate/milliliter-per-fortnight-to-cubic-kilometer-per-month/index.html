Unit history

Milliliter per Fortnight: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. Fortnight is a contraction of the Old English 'feowertyne niht' (fourteen nights), used in Britain since the medieval period.

Cubic Kilometer per Months: The 'Cubic' prefix was from the Greek 'khilioi' (thousand); one of the original metric prefixes from 1795. The month originally tracked the roughly 29.5-day lunar cycle before calendar reforms fixed it to the modern Gregorian lengths.
