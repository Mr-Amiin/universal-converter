Unit history

Milliliter per Hours: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The hour's division into 60 minutes also traces to Babylonian sexagesimal timekeeping, formalized in Hellenistic astronomy.

Bushel per Millisecond: The bushel is a medieval English dry-measure unit, standardized for grain trade as early as the 13th century. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.
