Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is metric cups per year (metric cup/yr). The Metric Cup (metric cup) is a unit of volume. The Year (yr) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is cups per fortnight (cup/fn). The Cup (cup) is a unit of volume. The Fortnight (fn) is a unit of time.

How do I convert metric cups per year to cups per fortnight?

Multiply the metric cups per year value by 0.0405036 to get cups per fortnight. For example, 10 metric cup/yr × 0.0405036 = 0.405036 cup/fn.

Is the metric cups per year to cups per fortnight conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert cups per fortnight back to metric cups per year?

Yes. Divide the cups per fortnight value by 0.0405036 (or multiply by 24.6892) to get metric cups per year.

About this conversion

This page converts metric cups per year (metric cup/yr) into cups per fortnight (cup/fn), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The Metric Cup (metric cup) is a unit of volume. The Year (yr) is a unit of time.

The Cup (cup) is a unit of volume. The Fortnight (fn) is a unit of time.

The formula is cup/fn = metric cup/yr × 0.0405036, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
