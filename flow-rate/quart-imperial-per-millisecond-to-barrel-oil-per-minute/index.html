Unit history

Imperial quart per Millisecond: The imperial quart was set by the 1824 Act as 1/4 of an imperial gallon. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.

Oil barrel per Minutes: The 42-US-gallon oil barrel was standardized in 1866 by Pennsylvania oil producers to end disputes over inconsistent container sizes. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.
