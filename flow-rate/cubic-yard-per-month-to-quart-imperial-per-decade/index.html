Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is cubic yards per month (yd3/mo). The Cubic Yard (yd3) is a unit of volume. The Month (mo) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is imperial quarts per decade (imp qt/decade). The Imperial Quart (imp qt) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

How do I convert cubic yards per month to imperial quarts per decade?

Multiply the cubic yards per month value by 80725.7 to get imperial quarts per decade. For example, 10 yd3/mo × 80725.7 = 807257 imp qt/decade.

Is the cubic yards per month to imperial quarts per decade conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert imperial quarts per decade back to cubic yards per month?

Yes. Divide the imperial quarts per decade value by 80725.7 (or multiply by 1.23876e-5) to get cubic yards per month.

About this conversion

This page converts cubic yards per month (yd3/mo) into imperial quarts per decade (imp qt/decade), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The Cubic Yard (yd3) is a unit of volume. The Month (mo) is a unit of time.

The Imperial Quart (imp qt) is a unit of volume. The Decade (decade) is a unit of time.

The formula is imp qt/decade = yd3/mo × 80725.7, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
