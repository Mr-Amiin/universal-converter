Unit history

Imperial pint per Minutes: The imperial pint was fixed by the 1824 British Weights and Measures Act at 1/8 of an imperial gallon. The minute (1/60 of an hour) descends from the Babylonian sexagesimal (base-60) counting system.

Quart per Fortnight: The quart (quarter-gallon) traces to medieval English measures, standardized differently in the US and UK in 1824. Fortnight is a contraction of the Old English 'feowertyne niht' (fourteen nights), used in Britain since the medieval period.
