How many Quart per Minutes are in one Fluid ounce per Hours?

Can I convert Quart per Minutes back to Fluid ounce per Hours?

Yes. Divide the Quart per Minutes value by 0.000520833333333, or multiply by 1920, to get Fluid ounce per Hours.

When would I use a hour-based flow unit like Fluid ounce per Hours?
