Frequently asked questions

What is the source unit in this conversion?

The source unit is metric teaspoons per week (metric tsp/wk). The Metric Teaspoon (metric tsp) is a unit of volume. The Week (wk) is a unit of time.

What is the destination unit in this conversion?

The destination unit is cups per month (cup/mo). The Cup (cup) is a unit of volume. The Month (mo) is a unit of time.

How do I convert metric teaspoons per week to cups per month?

Multiply the metric teaspoons per week value by 0.0918922 to get cups per month. For example, 10 metric tsp/wk × 0.0918922 = 0.918922 cup/mo.

Is the metric teaspoons per week to cups per month conversion exact?

It's exact under the SI-based volume and time definitions used here, since both units convert to cubic meters per second through fixed, non-rounded factors.

Where is this conversion commonly used?

Flow rate conversions like this are used in plumbing, HVAC, irrigation, chemical processing, fuel and water metering, and engineering flow calculations.

Can I convert cups per month back to metric teaspoons per week?

Yes. Divide the cups per month value by 0.0918922 (or multiply by 10.8823) to get metric teaspoons per week.

About this conversion

This page converts metric teaspoons per week (metric tsp/wk) into cups per month (cup/mo), two units of volumetric flow rate.

The Metric Teaspoon (metric tsp) is a unit of volume. The Week (wk) is a unit of time.

The Cup (cup) is a unit of volume. The Month (mo) is a unit of time.

The formula is cup/mo = metric tsp/wk × 0.0918922, based on the fixed relationship between these two flow rate units.

Flow rate is calculated as a volume unit divided by a time unit, so every combination of a volume unit and a time unit forms a valid, exact flow rate unit.
