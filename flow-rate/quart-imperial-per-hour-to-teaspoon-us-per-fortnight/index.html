Unit history

Imperial quart per Hours: The imperial quart was set by the 1824 Act as 1/4 of an imperial gallon. The hour's division into 60 minutes also traces to Babylonian sexagesimal timekeeping, formalized in Hellenistic astronomy.

Teaspoon per Fortnight: The teaspoon became a standardized cooking measure in the US in the late 19th century. Fortnight is a contraction of the Old English 'feowertyne niht' (fourteen nights), used in Britain since the medieval period.
