Understanding the Standard cubic foot per minute

Standard cubic foot per minute (SCFM) measures volumetric flow rate. One standard cubic foot per minute is 1 cubic foot of gas per minute, measured at a stated reference (standard) temperature and pressure; numerically 0.471947443 liters per second.

Convention: the volume is treated as a plain cubic foot per minute, so the conversion to liters per second is valid when both are expressed at the same standard reference conditions (SCFM to standard liters). Different reference temperatures and pressures (for example 60 °F vs 0 °C) change the equivalent flow of actual gas.

Standard and definition

The standard cubic foot per minute is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the standard cubic foot per minute today

  • Compressed-air, gas and ventilation flow ratings
  • Mass-flow controller specifications

Understanding the Liter per second

Liter per second (L/s) measures volumetric flow rate. One liter per second is 0.001 cubic meters per second.

Standard and definition

The liter per second is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).
