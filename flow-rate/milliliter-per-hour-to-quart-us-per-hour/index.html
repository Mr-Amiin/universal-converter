Unit history

Milliliter per Hours: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The hour's division into 60 minutes also traces to Babylonian sexagesimal timekeeping, formalized in Hellenistic astronomy.

Quart per Hours: The quart (quarter-gallon) traces to medieval English measures, standardized differently in the US and UK in 1824. The hour's division into 60 minutes also traces to Babylonian sexagesimal timekeeping, formalized in Hellenistic astronomy.
