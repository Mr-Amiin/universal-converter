Unit history

Pint per Millisecond: The pint has English origins traceable to the 13th century, with US and imperial versions diverging in size after 1824. The second was originally 1/86,400 of a mean solar day; since 1967 it has been defined by a fixed number of caesium-133 atomic transitions.

Fluid ounce per Days: The fluid ounce originates in apothecary and English customary measure, later split into US and imperial standards. The day as a 24-hour period was formalized by the ancient Egyptians and refined by Hellenistic astronomers.
