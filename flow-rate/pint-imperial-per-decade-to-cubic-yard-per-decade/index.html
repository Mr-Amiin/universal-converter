Unit history

Imperial pint per Decades: The imperial pint was fixed by the 1824 British Weights and Measures Act at 1/8 of an imperial gallon. Decade comes from the Greek 'deka' (ten), used generally for any ten-year span.

Cubic yard per Decades: The cubic yard derives from the yard, defined relative to the English foot since medieval times. Decade comes from the Greek 'deka' (ten), used generally for any ten-year span.
