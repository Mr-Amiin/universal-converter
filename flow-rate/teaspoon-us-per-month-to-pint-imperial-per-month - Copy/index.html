About this conversion

This flow rate page converts Teaspoon per Month (tsp/mo) to Imperial pint per Month (imp pt/mo) using the exact volumetric flow rate factor from the Universal Converter engine. A flow of one teaspoon every month is the same physical flow as 0.00867368942322 imperial pint(s) every month, so this page is useful for pipeline sizing, pump and pipe selection, HVAC airflow and liquid handling, irrigation and water-supply planning, and lab or industrial process calculations that mix metric, US customary, or imperial flow units. For example, a kitchen faucet or garden hose running at 1 tsp/mo is moving flow equivalent to 0.00867368942322 imp pt/mo — handy when one spec sheet lists flow in teaspoon per month and another lists it in imperial pint per month.

Metric flow units are built from SI volume and time units established under the metric system, while US customary flow units combine older trade-derived volume measures (like the gallon or cup) with standard time units, which is why the same physical flow rate looks very different when expressed in each system.
