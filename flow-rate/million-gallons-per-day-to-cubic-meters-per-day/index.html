About Converting Million Gallons per Day to Cubic Meters per Day

Million US gallons per day and cubic meter per day are both units of volumetric flow rate. One million US gallons per day (MGD) is 3,785.411784 cubic meters per day, about 43.81 liters per second. One cubic meter per day is 1/86,400 cubic meters per second.

Formula

m³/d = MGD × 3785.411784

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 million US gallons per day equals 3785.411784 cubic meters per day, and the reverse factor is 0.0002641720524.
