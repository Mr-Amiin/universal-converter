Unit history

Milliliter per Weeks: The liter dates to the 1795 French metric system, originally defined as the volume of one kilogram of water. The 7-day week has roots in ancient Babylonian astrology and was spread through Judeo-Christian and Roman calendars.

Cubic inch per Days: The cubic inch shares the same English-foot lineage as the cubic foot, formalized in British and US customary systems. The day as a 24-hour period was formalized by the ancient Egyptians and refined by Hellenistic astronomers.
