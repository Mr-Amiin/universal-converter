Frequently asked questions

How do you convert gigabyte to megabyte?

Use the formula MB = GB × 1000. One gigabyte equals 1000 megabytes.

What is Gigabyte?

A gigabyte is a unit of digital storage equal to one billion bytes.

What is Megabyte?

A megabyte is a unit of digital storage equal to one million bytes.

Is the gigabyte to megabyte conversion exact?

Yes. Gigabyte and Megabyte are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

How do I convert megabyte back to gigabyte?

Use the reverse formula GB = MB ÷ 1000. This site's reverse converter does the same calculation automatically.

About this conversion

This digital storage converter turns gigabyte into megabyte using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. An 8 GB phone data plan gives you about 8000.0 MB. The gigabyte followed the growth of digital storage; the SI-prefix definition (10^9 bytes) is now standard for drive capacities, distinct from the binary gibibyte. The megabyte became a common unit with early personal computer storage in the 1980s and is defined as 10^6 bytes in SI usage. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same gigabyte-to-megabyte factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
