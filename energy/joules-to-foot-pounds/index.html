From definition

The joule (J) is the SI derived unit of energy, equal to the work done when a force of one newton acts over one meter.

To definition

A foot-pound (ft-lb) is the work done by one pound-force acting through a distance of one foot, commonly used in mechanical and engineering calculations.

How the formula works

Both joule and foot-pound measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in joules by 0.7375621493 to get foot-pounds. To reverse the conversion, multiply a foot-pound value by 1.355817948 to get back to joules.

What is Foot-pound (ft-lb)?

A foot-pound (ft-lb) is the work done by one pound-force acting through a distance of one foot, commonly used in mechanical and engineering calculations.

How do I convert joules to foot-pounds?

Multiply the joule value by 0.7375621493 to get the equivalent in foot-pounds. For example, 5 J × 0.7375621493 = 3.687811 ft-lb.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are defined against the SI base unit for this dimension, so the factor 0.7375621493 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Can I convert foot-pounds back to joules?

Yes. Divide the foot-pound value by 0.7375621493, or multiply it by 1.355817948, to get joules.

What is a common mistake when converting Joule to Foot-pound?
