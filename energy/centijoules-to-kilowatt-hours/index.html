How the formula works

Both centijoule and kilowatt-hour measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in centijoules by 2.777777778e-09 to get kilowatt hours. To reverse the conversion, multiply a kilowatt-hour value by 360000000 to get back to centijoules.
