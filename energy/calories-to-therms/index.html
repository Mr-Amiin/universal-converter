How the formula works

Both calorie and us therm measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in calories by 3.966613703e-08 to get therms. To reverse the conversion, multiply a us therm value by 25210420.65 to get back to calories.

Frequently asked questions

What is Calorie (cal)?

A (small) calorie is a thermochemical unit of heat energy equal to 4.184 joules, historically the energy needed to raise 1 gram of water by 1°C.

What is US therm (therm)?

The US therm is a natural-gas billing unit equal to 100,000 BTU, used by utilities to measure gas usage.

How do I convert calories to therms?

Multiply the calorie value by 3.966613703e-08 to get the equivalent in therms. For example, 5 cal × 3.966613703e-08 = 1.983307e-07 therm.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are defined against the SI base unit for this dimension, so the factor 3.966613703e-08 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Can I convert therms back to calories?

Yes. Divide the us therm value by 3.966613703e-08, or multiply it by 25210420.65, to get calories.

What is Calorie used for in energy?

Calorie belongs to the energy family of units. For scale, a typical phone battery stores on the order of 40,000 joules, and a household kettle uses roughly 1,000,000 joules to boil a litre of water.

How precise is the 3.966614e-08 conversion factor?

The figure shown is accurate to the precision displayed. For energy calculations that need more decimal places, use the exact factor 3.966614e-08 directly rather than a rounded intermediate value.

What is a common mistake when converting Calorie to US therm?

The most common mistake is multiplying by the reverse factor instead of 3.966614e-08, or assuming Calorie and US therm sit on the same point of the energy scale. Always confirm the direction of the conversion before applying the formula.
