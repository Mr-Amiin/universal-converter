How the formula works

Both decajoule and terajoule measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in decajoules by 1.000000000e-11 to get terajoules. To reverse the conversion, multiply a terajoule value by 100000000000 to get back to decajoules.

How do I convert decajoules to terajoules?

Can I convert terajoules back to decajoules?

Yes. Divide the terajoule value by 1.000000000e-11, or multiply it by 100000000000, to get decajoules.

What is Decajoule used for in energy?

Why is the converted number so extreme?

What is a common mistake when converting Decajoule to Terajoule?

The most common mistake is multiplying by the reverse factor instead of 1e-11, or assuming Decajoule and Terajoule sit on the same point of the energy scale. Always confirm the direction of the conversion before applying the formula.
