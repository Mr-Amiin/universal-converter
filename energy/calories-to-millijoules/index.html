How the formula works

Both calorie and millijoule measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in calories by 4184 to get millijoules. To reverse the conversion, multiply a millijoule value by 0.0002390057361 to get back to calories.

Frequently asked questions

What is Calorie (cal)?

A (small) calorie is a thermochemical unit of heat energy equal to 4.184 joules, historically the energy needed to raise 1 gram of water by 1°C.

What is Millijoule (mJ)?

A millijoule (mJ) is a metric-prefixed unit of energy equal to 10-3 joules, a fraction of a single joule, using the SI milli prefix.

How do I convert calories to millijoules?

Multiply the calorie value by 4184 to get the equivalent in millijoules. For example, 5 cal × 4184 = 20920 mJ.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are defined against the SI base unit for this dimension, so the factor 4184 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Can I convert millijoules back to calories?

Yes. Divide the millijoule value by 4184, or multiply it by 0.0002390057361, to get calories.

What is Calorie used for in energy?

Calorie belongs to the energy family of units. For scale, a typical phone battery stores on the order of 40,000 joules, and a household kettle uses roughly 1,000,000 joules to boil a litre of water.

How precise is the 4184 conversion factor?

The figure shown is accurate to the precision displayed. For energy calculations that need more decimal places, use the exact factor 4184 directly rather than a rounded intermediate value.

What is a common mistake when converting Calorie to Millijoule?

The most common mistake is multiplying by the reverse factor instead of 4184, or assuming Calorie and Millijoule sit on the same point of the energy scale. Always confirm the direction of the conversion before applying the formula.
