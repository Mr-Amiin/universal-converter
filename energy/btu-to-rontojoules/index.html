From definition

The British thermal unit (BTU) is the energy needed to raise one pound of water by one degree Fahrenheit, widely used for heating and air-conditioning ratings.

To definition

A rontojoule (rJ) is a metric-prefixed unit of energy equal to 10-27 joules, a fraction of a single joule, using the SI ronto prefix.

Formula

rJ = BTU × 1.055055853e+30

How the formula works

Both british thermal unit and rontojoule measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in btu by 1.055055853e+30 to get rontojoules. To reverse the conversion, multiply a rontojoule value by 9.478171203e-31 to get back to btu.

What is British thermal unit (BTU)?

What is Rontojoule (rJ)?

A rontojoule (rJ) is a metric-prefixed unit of energy equal to 10-27 joules, a fraction of a single joule, using the SI ronto prefix.

How do I convert btu to rontojoules?

Multiply the british thermal unit value by 1.055055853e+30 to get the equivalent in rontojoules. For example, 5 BTU × 1.055055853e+30 = 5.275279e+30 rJ.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are defined against the SI base unit for this dimension, so the factor 1.055055853e+30 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Can I convert rontojoules back to btu?

Yes. Divide the rontojoule value by 1.055055853e+30, or multiply it by 9.478171203e-31, to get btu.

What is British thermal unit used for in energy?

Why is the converted number so extreme?

British thermal unit and Rontojoule sit about 27 orders of magnitude apart on the metric scale, so converting between them naturally produces a very large or very small number, even for an everyday input value. This is expected and does not indicate an error.

What is a common mistake when converting British thermal unit to Rontojoule?

The most common mistake is multiplying by the reverse factor instead of 1.055056e+30, or assuming British thermal unit and Rontojoule sit on the same point of the energy scale. Always confirm the direction of the conversion before applying the formula.
