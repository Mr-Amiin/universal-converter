About Converting Tons of TNT to Megajoules

Ton of TNT and megajoule are both units of energy. A ton of TNT is defined by convention as exactly 4.184 gigajoules. A megajoule is one million joules (10⁶ J).

Formula

MJ = tTNT × 4184

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 ton of TNT equals 4184 megajoules, and the reverse factor is 0.0002390057361.

Simple example

1 tTNT × 4184 = 4184 MJ

1 ton of TNT = 4184 megajoules.

Real-world example

10 tTNT × 4184 = 41840 MJ

10 tons of TNT = 41840 megajoules.

Understanding the Ton of TNT

Ton of TNT (tTNT) measures energy. A ton of TNT is defined by convention as exactly 4.184 gigajoules.

Convention: the thermochemical definition of exactly 4.184 × 10⁹ J per ton of TNT (the megaton is 4.184 × 10¹⁵ J).

Standard and definition

The ton of TNT is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the ton of TNT today

  • Expressing explosive yields of blasts and impacts
  • Energy comparisons in geophysics and defence studies

Understanding the Megajoule

Megajoule (MJ) measures energy. A megajoule is one million joules (10⁶ J).

Standard and definition
