About Converting Calorie (IT) to Watt-hours

Calorie (IT) and watt-hour are both units of energy. The International Table calorie is exactly 4.1868 joules. A watt-hour is exactly 3,600 joules.

Formula

Wh = cal IT × 0.001163

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 calorie (IT) equals 0.001163 watt-hours, and the reverse factor is 859.8452279.

Understanding the Calorie (IT)

Calorie (IT) (cal IT) measures energy. The International Table calorie is exactly 4.1868 joules.

Convention: the International Table (IT) calorie of exactly 4.1868 J. The thermochemical calorie (4.184 J), used for food labels, differs by about 0.07 %.

Standard and definition

The calorie (IT) is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the calorie (IT) today

  • Engineering heat and steam tables
  • Older European and Japanese heat-energy specifications

Understanding the Watt-hour

Watt-hour (Wh) measures energy. A watt-hour is exactly 3,600 joules.

Standard and definition

The watt-hour is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the watt-hour today

  • Battery capacity and small-device energy use
  • Energy meters
