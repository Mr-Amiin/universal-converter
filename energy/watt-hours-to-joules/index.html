How the formula works

Both watt-hour and joule measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in watt hours by 3600 to get joules. To reverse the conversion, multiply a joule value by 0.0002777777778 to get back to watt hours.

Frequently asked questions

What is Watt-hour (Wh)?

A watt-hour (Wh) is the energy delivered by one watt of power sustained for one hour, equal to 3,600 joules.

What is Joule (J)?

The joule (J) is the SI derived unit of energy, equal to the work done when a force of one newton acts over one meter.

How do I convert watt hours to joules?

Multiply the watt-hour value by 3600 to get the equivalent in joules. For example, 5 Wh × 3600 = 18000 J.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are defined against the SI base unit for this dimension, so the factor 3600 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Can I convert joules back to watt hours?

Yes. Divide the joule value by 3600, or multiply it by 0.0002777777778, to get watt hours.

What is Watt-hour used for in energy?

Watt-hour belongs to the energy family of units. For scale, a typical phone battery stores on the order of 40,000 joules, and a household kettle uses roughly 1,000,000 joules to boil a litre of water.

How precise is the 3600 conversion factor?

The figure shown is accurate to the precision displayed. For energy calculations that need more decimal places, use the exact factor 3600 directly rather than a rounded intermediate value.

What is a common mistake when converting Watt-hour to Joule?

The most common mistake is multiplying by the reverse factor instead of 3600, or assuming Watt-hour and Joule sit on the same point of the energy scale. Always confirm the direction of the conversion before applying the formula.
