How the formula works

Both terajoule and kilocalorie measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in terajoules by 239005736.1 to get kilocalories. To reverse the conversion, multiply a kilocalorie value by 4.184000000e-09 to get back to terajoules.

Frequently asked questions

What is Terajoule (TJ)?

A terajoule (TJ) is a metric-prefixed unit of energy equal to 1012 joules, using the SI tera prefix.

What is Kilocalorie (kcal)?

A kilocalorie (kcal) equals 1,000 small calories and is the unit behind the food "Calorie" shown on nutrition labels.

How do I convert terajoules to kilocalories?

Multiply the terajoule value by 239005736.1 to get the equivalent in kilocalories. For example, 5 TJ × 239005736.1 = 1.195029e+09 kcal.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are defined against the SI base unit for this dimension, so the factor 239005736.1 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Can I convert kilocalories back to terajoules?

Yes. Divide the kilocalorie value by 239005736.1, or multiply it by 4.184000000e-09, to get terajoules.

What is Terajoule used for in energy?

Terajoule belongs to the energy family of units. For scale, a typical phone battery stores on the order of 40,000 joules, and a household kettle uses roughly 1,000,000 joules to boil a litre of water.

Why is the converted number so extreme?

Terajoule and Kilocalorie sit about 12 orders of magnitude apart on the metric scale, so converting between them naturally produces a very large or very small number, even for an everyday input value. This is expected and does not indicate an error.

What is a common mistake when converting Terajoule to Kilocalorie?

The most common mistake is multiplying by the reverse factor instead of 2.390057e+08, or assuming Terajoule and Kilocalorie sit on the same point of the energy scale. Always confirm the direction of the conversion before applying the formula.
