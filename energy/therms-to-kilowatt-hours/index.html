How the formula works

Both us therm and kilowatt-hour measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in us therms by 29.30011111 to get kilowatt-hours. To reverse the conversion, multiply a kilowatt-hour value by 0.03412956341 to get back to us therms.

Frequently asked questions

What is US therm (therm)?

The US therm is a natural-gas billing unit equal to 100,000 BTU, used by utilities to measure gas usage.

What is Kilowatt-hour (kWh)?

A kilowatt-hour (kWh) is the standard billing unit for electricity, equal to 1,000 watts sustained for one hour.

How do I convert us therms to kilowatt-hours?

Multiply the us therm value by 29.30011111 to get the equivalent in kilowatt-hours. For example, 5 therm × 29.30011111 = 146.500556 kWh.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are defined against the SI base unit for this dimension, so the factor 29.30011111 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Can I convert kilowatt-hours back to us therms?

Yes. Divide the kilowatt-hour value by 29.30011111, or multiply it by 0.03412956341, to get us therms.
