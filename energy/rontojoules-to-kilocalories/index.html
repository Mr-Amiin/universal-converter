How the formula works

Both rontojoule and kilocalorie measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in rontojoules by 2.390057361e-31 to get kilocalories. To reverse the conversion, multiply a kilocalorie value by 4.184000000e+30 to get back to rontojoules.

How do I convert rontojoules to kilocalories?

Can I convert kilocalories back to rontojoules?

Yes. Divide the kilocalorie value by 2.390057361e-31, or multiply it by 4.184000000e+30, to get rontojoules.

What is Rontojoule used for in energy?

Why is the converted number so extreme?

What is a common mistake when converting Rontojoule to Kilocalorie?
