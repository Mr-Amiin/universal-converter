How the formula works

Both attojoule and petajoule measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in attojoules by 1.000000000e-33 to get petajoules. To reverse the conversion, multiply a petajoule value by 1.000000000e+33 to get back to attojoules.

What is Petajoule (PJ)?

How do I convert attojoules to petajoules?

Multiply the attojoule value by 1.000000000e-33 to get the equivalent in petajoules. For example, 5 aJ × 1.000000000e-33 = 5.000000e-33 PJ.

Is this conversion exact?

Can I convert petajoules back to attojoules?

Yes. Divide the petajoule value by 1.000000000e-33, or multiply it by 1.000000000e+33, to get attojoules.
