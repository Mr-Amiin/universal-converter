About Converting Megawatt-hours to Gigajoules

Megawatt-hour and gigajoule are both units of energy. A megawatt-hour is one million watt-hours, or 3.6 gigajoules. A gigajoule is one billion joules (10⁹ J).

Formula

GJ = MWh × 3.6

This factor comes from the defined size of each unit: 1 megawatt-hour equals 3.6 gigajoules, and the reverse factor is 0.2777777778.

Simple example

1 MWh × 3.6 = 3.6 GJ

1 megawatt-hour = 3.6 gigajoules.

Real-world example

10 MWh × 3.6 = 36 GJ

10 megawatt-hours = 36 gigajoules.

Understanding the Megawatt-hour

Megawatt-hour (MWh) measures energy. A megawatt-hour is one million watt-hours, or 3.6 gigajoules.

Standard and definition

The megawatt-hour is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the megawatt-hour today

  • Utility-scale electricity generation, trading and billing
  • Grid storage capacity and industrial load reporting

Understanding the Gigajoule

Gigajoule (GJ) measures energy. A gigajoule is one billion joules (10⁹ J).

Standard and definition

The gigajoule is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the gigajoule today

  • Large energy quantities such as fuel, heating and gas supply
  • Energy statistics and utility billing
