How the formula works

Both kilocalorie and microjoule measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in kilocalories by 4184000000 to get microjoules. To reverse the conversion, multiply a microjoule value by 2.390057361e-10 to get back to kilocalories.

Frequently asked questions

What is Kilocalorie (kcal)?

A kilocalorie (kcal) equals 1,000 small calories and is the unit behind the food "Calorie" shown on nutrition labels.

What is Microjoule (uJ)?

A microjoule (uJ) is a metric-prefixed unit of energy equal to 10-6 joules, a fraction of a single joule, using the SI micro prefix.

How do I convert kilocalories to microjoules?

Multiply the kilocalorie value by 4184000000 to get the equivalent in microjoules. For example, 5 kcal × 4184000000 = 2.092000e+10 uJ.

Is this conversion exact?

Yes. Both units are defined against the SI base unit for this dimension, so the factor 4184000000 is an exact, non-rounded value.

Can I convert microjoules back to kilocalories?

Yes. Divide the microjoule value by 4184000000, or multiply it by 2.390057361e-10, to get kilocalories.

What is Kilocalorie used for in energy?

Kilocalorie belongs to the energy family of units. For scale, a typical phone battery stores on the order of 40,000 joules, and a household kettle uses roughly 1,000,000 joules to boil a litre of water.

Why is the converted number so extreme?

Kilocalorie and Microjoule sit about 6 orders of magnitude apart on the metric scale, so converting between them naturally produces a very large or very small number, even for an everyday input value. This is expected and does not indicate an error.

What is a common mistake when converting Kilocalorie to Microjoule?

The most common mistake is multiplying by the reverse factor instead of 4.184e+09, or assuming Kilocalorie and Microjoule sit on the same point of the energy scale. Always confirm the direction of the conversion before applying the formula.
