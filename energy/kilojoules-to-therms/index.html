How the formula works

Both kilojoule and us therm measure the same physical quantity, so converting between them is a single multiplication. Multiply a value in kilojoules by 0.00000948043428 to get therms. To reverse the conversion, multiply a us therm value by 105480.4 to get back to kilojoules.

How do I convert kilojoules to therms?

Can I convert therms back to kilojoules?
