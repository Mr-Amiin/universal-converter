About Converting Barrels of Oil Equivalent to Gigajoules

Barrel of oil equivalent and gigajoule are both units of energy. A barrel of oil equivalent is the energy released by burning one barrel of crude oil, about 6.1179 gigajoules. A gigajoule is one billion joules (10⁹ J).

GJ = boe × 6.1178632

Understanding the Barrel of oil equivalent

Barrel of oil equivalent (boe) measures energy. A barrel of oil equivalent is the energy released by burning one barrel of crude oil, about 6.1179 gigajoules.

Convention: 5.8 million BTU (59 °F) per barrel ≈ 6.1178632 GJ ≈ 1,699 kWh, the US convention. Other agencies use slightly different values (for example 6.12 GJ or tonne-of-oil-equivalent based factors).

Standard and definition

The barrel of oil equivalent is covered by NIST Special Publication 811 — Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI).

Uses of the barrel of oil equivalent today

  • Comparing oil, gas and coal reserves and production
  • Corporate and national energy statistics

Understanding the Gigajoule

Gigajoule (GJ) measures energy. A gigajoule is one billion joules (10⁹ J).

Standard and definition

The gigajoule is covered by BIPM — The International System of Units (SI Brochure).

Uses of the gigajoule today

  • Large energy quantities such as fuel, heating and gas supply
  • Energy statistics and utility billing
